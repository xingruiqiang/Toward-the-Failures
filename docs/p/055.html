宗庆后：42岁才骑着三轮车卖冰棍的人

Zong Qinghou: The Man Who Was Still Selling Ice Pops from a Tricycle at Forty-Two

——“首富，只是虚名；共富，才有意义”

—"The 'richest man' is only an empty title; shared prosperity is what has meaning"

2026年，“35岁危机”几乎成了社会共识。很多人在35岁前后开始焦虑：是不是已经被时代淘汰了？是不是该认命了？

In 2026, the "crisis of turning thirty-five" has become an almost universal social consensus. Many people begin to panic around the age of thirty-five: have I already been made obsolete by the times? Should I just accept my fate?

但1987年，有一个人42岁才白手起家。他借了14万元，接手一家连年亏损的校办企业经销部，骑着三轮车挨家挨户地卖冰棍和文具。一根冰棍卖四分钱，只能赚几厘钱。

But in 1987, one man started from nothing at the age of forty-two. He borrowed 140,000 yuan, took over a distribution office under a school-run enterprise that had lost money year after year, and rode a tricycle from door to door selling ice pops and stationery. An ice pop went for four fen; the profit on each was only a fraction of a fen.

他叫宗庆后。几年后，他做出了“娃哈哈”；二十多年后，他三次登上中国首富的位置。而他一年的个人花销，据说不超过5万元。

His name was Zong Qinghou. A few years later he built Wahaha; more than twenty years later he topped the list of China's richest people three times. And his personal spending, it is said, came to no more than 50,000 yuan a year.

一、被时代耽误的十五年

I. Fifteen Years Held Back by the Times

1945年，宗庆后出生在江苏宿迁，幼年随家人迁居杭州。

Zong Qinghou was born in Suqian, Jiangsu, in 1945, and moved with his family to Hangzhou in his early childhood.

因为家里穷——父亲没有工作，母亲40块钱的工资要养五个孩子，有时候吃了上顿没下顿——他初中毕业就开始四处打工。17岁以后，他到舟山的农场和绍兴的茶场劳动，一干就是14年。

Because the family was poor—his father had no job, and his mother's wage of forty yuan had to feed five children, so that there were times when one meal was eaten with no certainty of the next—he began taking odd jobs the moment he finished middle school. After he turned seventeen he went to labor on a farm in Zhoushan and a tea plantation in Shaoxing, and there he stayed for fourteen years.

1978年，母亲提前退休，33岁的他顶职回到杭州，在邮电路小学的校办工厂当工人。那一年，改革开放的消息已经传出，但形势还不明朗。他后来回忆：“我那时唯一的梦想就是有一家自己的企业，干自己想干的事业，我也一直在寻找着这样一个机会。”

In 1978 his mother retired early, and at thirty-three he took over her post and returned to Hangzhou to work as a laborer in the school-run factory of Youdian Road Primary School. That year news of reform and opening had already spread, but the situation was still unclear. He recalled later: "My only dream back then was to have an enterprise of my own, to do the work I wanted to do, and I was always searching for such an opportunity."

这个机会，他等了整整9年。

That opportunity took him a full nine years to find.

为了生计，他辗转于几家校办企业之间：做过推销员，蹬着三轮车卖冰棍、卖文具，背着几台落地电扇挤在绿皮火车上，在简陋的招待所里打地铺，在广交会大门外摆地摊。

To make a living he moved among several school-run enterprises: he worked as a salesman, pedaled a tricycle to sell ice pops and stationery, squeezed onto the old green-painted trains with several standing fans strapped to his back, slept on the floor of shabby guesthouses, and set up a street stall outside the gates of the Canton Fair.

二、1987年：42岁的“冒险”

II. 1987: A Forty-Two-Year-Old's "Gamble"

1987年，上城区文教局要对下属的校办企业经销部采用承包经营的方式，公开选拔负责人，要求当年创利4万元。

In 1987 the Shangcheng District education bureau decided to lease out its subordinate school-run distribution office under a contracting arrangement, openly selecting a manager, with the requirement that it turn a profit of 40,000 yuan that year.

宗庆后毛遂自荐，借了14万元，承包下这个经销部。他当时承诺：当年创利10万元。“大家都觉得不可思议，但我心里是有底的。”

Zong Qinghou put himself forward, borrowed 140,000 yuan, and took on the contract for the office. He promised at the time to turn a profit of 100,000 yuan that year. "Everyone thought it was inconceivable, but I had confidence in my heart."

创业的第一天，他就骑着三轮车把练习本送到学校。最初的员工只有他和两名退休教师，一共3个人。他每天骑着三轮车卖冰棍、文具——一根冰棍卖4分钱，利润只有几厘。但他说，娃哈哈几十年的发展，就是从这几分几厘开始的。

On the very first day of the venture he rode his tricycle to deliver exercise books to schools. His first employees were just himself and two retired teachers, three people in all. Every day he rode the tricycle out to sell ice pops and stationery—an ice pop went for four fen, with a profit of only a fraction of a fen. But he said that the decades of Wahaha's growth began with exactly those few fen and li.

在卖货的过程中，他发现不少孩子存在食欲不振、营养不良的问题。这让他看到了商机。

In the course of selling goods, he noticed that many children had poor appetites and were malnourished. This showed him a business opportunity.

1988年，娃哈哈推出国内第一款专门为儿童设计的营养液。那时候，“上门做广告的企业像大熊猫一样稀罕”，宗庆后却坚持一个个城市地跑报社、电视台。最后，“喝了娃哈哈，吃饭就是香”这句广告语火遍全国。

In 1988 Wahaha launched the country's first nutritional drink designed specifically for children. At the time, "companies that came knocking to advertise were as rare as giant pandas," yet Zong Qinghou insisted on going city by city to newspaper offices and television stations. In the end, the slogan "Drink Wahaha, and every meal tastes good" spread across the whole country.

此后，销售额从第一年的488万元，飞涨到第三年的9800多万元。

After that, sales soared from 4.88 million yuan in the first year to more than 98 million yuan in the third.

1989年，企业改名为“杭州娃哈哈营养食品厂”，娃哈哈品牌正式诞生。

In 1989 the enterprise was renamed the Hangzhou Wahaha Nutritional Food Factory, and the Wahaha brand was officially born.

三、小鱼吃大鱼

III. The Small Fish Swallows the Big Fish

1991年，娃哈哈做了一件在当年引起巨大争议的事。

In 1991, Wahaha did something that caused enormous controversy at the time.

只有146人的杭州娃哈哈营养食品厂，有偿兼并了拥有2000多名职工、已经资不抵债的国营老厂——杭州罐头食品厂。

The Hangzhou Wahaha Nutritional Food Factory, with only 146 employees, paid to merge with an old state-owned factory that had more than 2,000 workers and was already insolvent—the Hangzhou Canned Food Factory.

“民营小鱼”吃掉“国有大鱼”，当时引起轩然大波。宗庆后被一些报纸批评“挖社会主义墙角”，双方企业的员工也怨声迭起。

A "small private fish" swallowing a "big state-owned fish" stirred up a storm at the time. Some newspapers criticized Zong Qinghou for "undermining the foundations of socialism," and employees on both sides raised a chorus of complaints.

面对质疑，他跟几千名员工“掏心窝子”地谈。三个月后，原本亏损的罐头厂扭亏为盈；第二年，销售收入达4亿元，利润7000多万元。

Faced with the doubts, he spoke from the heart with several thousand employees. Three months later, the once-loss-making cannery had turned a profit; the following year its sales revenue reached 400 million yuan, with profits of more than 70 million.

这一步，让娃哈哈从一家小厂走上了规模经营之路。

This step set Wahaha on the path from a small factory to large-scale operation.

四、“布鞋首富”的两面

IV. Two Sides of the "Cloth-Shoe Tycoon"

此后的娃哈哈一路高歌猛进。宗庆后独创的“产销联合体”模式，成了中国消费品企业做经销商渠道的模本。他陆续打造出AD钙奶、营养快线、娃哈哈纯净水、“非常可乐”等一个又一个爆款。

After that, Wahaha charged ahead. Zong Qinghou's original "production-and-sales alliance" model became the template for Chinese consumer-goods companies building distributor channels. One after another he created hit products: AD Calcium Milk, Nutrition Express, Wahaha purified water, "Future Cola," and more.

2010年、2012年、2013年，他三次登上福布斯中国富豪榜首位。

In 2010, 2012, and 2013, he topped the Forbes China rich list three times.

但有意思的是，这位“首富”的形象，却是“一袭白衬衫、一双黑布鞋”。他的个人年花销，据说不超过5万元。他有一句名言：“所谓的'首富'不过只是虚名一个，什么也带不来，什么也带不走。”

But what is interesting is that this "richest man" cut the figure of "a plain white shirt and a pair of black cloth shoes." His personal spending, it is said, came to no more than 50,000 yuan a year. He had a famous saying: "The so-called 'richest man' is nothing but an empty title; it brings nothing and takes nothing away."

另一次电视节目里，主持人握着一瓶娃哈哈矿泉水，连问他三个问题：“瓶口有几圈螺纹？瓶身有几道棱？瓶盖有几个齿？”他不假思索地回答：“四圈螺纹，八道棱，十八个齿。”当场验证，全部答对。

On another television program, the host held a bottle of Wahaha mineral water and fired three questions at him: "How many threads are there on the neck? How many ridges on the body? How many teeth on the cap?" Without hesitation he answered: "Four threads, eight ridges, eighteen teeth." Verified on the spot, every answer was correct.

他对产品的这种“颗粒度”把握，来自几十年一直跑在市场第一线。

This fine-grained grasp of his products came from decades spent on the front lines of the market.

而在对待员工上，他又是出了名的大方——某年娃哈哈给全体员工发放的年终奖总额约6亿元。

Yet in his treatment of employees he was famously generous—one year Wahaha paid out about 600 million yuan in year-end bonuses to its entire staff.

2006年到2009年，娃哈哈还与合资方达能打了一场被称为“改革开放以来影响最大的国际商战”。三年间，诉讼打了29场，最终娃哈哈胜出。宗庆后对这件事的总结很朴素：“合作要平等，信守契约精神。”

From 2006 to 2009, Wahaha also fought a battle with its joint-venture partner Danone that was called "the most consequential international commercial war since reform and opening." Over three years, twenty-nine lawsuits were fought, and in the end Wahaha prevailed. Zong Qinghou summed it up plainly: "Cooperation must be equal, and the spirit of contract must be honored."

2024年2月25日，宗庆后因病医治无效在杭州逝世，享年79岁。

On February 25, 2024, Zong Qinghou died in Hangzhou after medical treatment failed, at the age of seventy-nine.

五、致2026：关于“起点”和“年龄”

V. To 2026: On "Starting Points" and "Age"

2026年，我们这个时代特别强调“起跑线”。好像过了某个年纪还没有成就，人生就已经定型了。

In 2026, our era places enormous emphasis on the "starting line." It seems that if you reach a certain age without achievements, your life is already fixed.

宗庆后的故事，是对这种焦虑最直接的一次反驳。

Zong Qinghou's story is the most direct rebuttal of that anxiety.

他33岁才回城当工人，42岁才借钱创业，还是从“卖冰棍”这种最不起眼的生意做起。他没有名校背景，没有风口上的技术，没有资本的加持，靠的只是“把一件小事做到极致”的耐心。

He returned to the city to work as a laborer only at thirty-three, borrowed money to start a business only at forty-two, and began with the humblest trade of all—selling ice pops. He had no elite-school pedigree, no technology riding the latest trend, no backing from capital; all he had was the patience to "do one small thing to the utmost."

他自己一辈子强调的，也是这一点。他多次说：“我自己是一个普通人，从底层崛起的凡人。幸运的是，我生于这一个大时代。”

This was what he himself stressed throughout his life. He said many times: "I am an ordinary person, a common man who rose from the bottom. Fortunately, I was born into this great era."

对我们这代人来说，宗庆后提供的是一个很“实”的参照：人生不是只有一次发令枪。 你可能在35岁被裁，在40岁换行，在42岁才开始真正想做的事——只要你还愿意骑上那辆“三轮车”，去把每一件小事一件一件地送出去。

For our generation, Zong Qinghou offers a very concrete reference point: life does not have only one starting gun. You may be laid off at thirty-five, change careers at forty, and only at forty-two begin what you truly want to do—as long as you are still willing to get on that "tricycle" and deliver each small task, one by one.

还有一层更难得的东西：他那种“从几分几厘开始”的耐心。今天太多人想做“大生意”——一上来就要高毛利、高增长、高估值，看不起卖冰棍、送练习本这种“小钱”。但宗庆后说得很清楚：娃哈哈几十年，就是从这几分几厘开始的。不是因为他只能赚小钱，而是因为他知道，能把小钱赚明白的人，才配谈大钱。

There is one more layer that is even harder to come by: his patience in "starting from a few fen and li." Too many people today want to do "big business"—they want high margins, high growth, and high valuations from the outset, and they look down on "small money" like selling ice pops and delivering exercise books. But Zong Qinghou put it plainly: the decades of Wahaha began with exactly those few fen and li. Not because he could only earn small money, but because he knew that only someone who can earn small money properly deserves to talk about big money.

宗庆后晚年喜欢引用陶渊明的一句话：“纵浪大化中，不喜亦不惧。应尽便须尽，无复独多虑。”这大概也是他一生的注脚——不狂喜，不畏惧，该做的事就把它做完。对今天在焦虑中打转的我们来说，这种“朴素的定力”，可能比任何方法论都珍贵。

In his later years Zong Qinghou liked to quote a line from Tao Yuanming: "Let me drift on the great transformation; neither rejoicing nor fearing. When a thing must be done, let it be done; do not brood over it alone." This may be the footnote to his whole life—no wild elation, no fear, and when something ought to be done, do it to the end. For those of us spinning in anxiety today, this "plain steadiness" may be more precious than any methodology.
