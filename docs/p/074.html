李清照：前半生“赌书泼茶”，后半生国破家亡

Li Qingzhao: "Betting on Books, Spilling the Tea" in Her First Half of Life; a Fallen Nation and a Broken Home in the Second

——“寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚。”

—"Seeking, seeking; searching, searching; cold, so cold; quiet, so quiet; bitter, bitter, sad, sad, grieving, grieving."

2026年，李清照是很多人心中“中国第一才女”。课本里选她的词，博物馆里展她的《金石录》，网络上流传着她和赵明诚的爱情故事。

In 2026, Li Qingzhao is, in many people's minds, "China's foremost woman of talent." Textbooks select her ci poems, museums display her Records on Metal and Stone (Jinshi Lu), and her love story with Zhao Mingcheng circulates online.

可真实的李清照，绝不是一段才子佳人的甜文。她活了七十多岁，人生被一场战争劈成两半：前一半，是书香门第里那个敢和丈夫比记忆力的少女；后一半，是一个带着几箱文物、在乱世里仓皇南逃、晚年再嫁又迅速离异的孤苦老人。

But the real Li Qingzhao is anything but a sweet tale of a gifted scholar and a beautiful lady. She lived more than seventy years, and her life was split in two by a war: the first half, a girl from a scholarly household who dared to compete with her husband over memory; the second half, a lonely old woman carrying a few cases of antiquities, fleeing in panic to the south through a chaotic age, and in her later years remarrying and then swiftly divorcing.

她经历过抄家、逃难、丧夫、诬陷、家暴、官司、牢狱。她把这一切咽下去，然后写成词。

She lived through the ransacking of her home, refugee flight, the death of her husband, false accusation, domestic violence, lawsuits, imprisonment. She swallowed all of it, and wrote it into ci.

一、章丘李家有女

I. A Daughter of the Li Family of Zhangqiu

1084年，李清照出生在齐州章丘（今山东济南章丘）的一个书香门第。她的父亲李格非，是苏轼的学生，官至礼部员外郎，家里藏书极多。她的母亲也出身名门，能文能诗。

In 1084, Li Qingzhao was born into a scholarly family in Zhangqiu, Qizhou (present-day Zhangqiu, Jinan, Shandong). Her father, Li Gefei, was a disciple of Su Shi, rose to the post of Vice Director of the Ministry of Rites, and kept a great many books at home. Her mother likewise came from a distinguished family and could write prose and poetry.

李清照从小就不是那种只学女红的姑娘。她读书、写词、喝酒、赌钱（她最爱的是一种叫“打马”的博戏），胆子极大。她十几岁时写下的《如梦令》——“常记溪亭日暮，沉醉不知归路”——就透着一股旁人少有的洒脱。

From childhood Li Qingzhao was not the sort of girl who learned only needlework. She read, wrote ci, drank wine, and gambled (her favorite pastime was a board game called dama, or "horse-beating"); she was remarkably bold. The ci poem "Ru Meng Ling" (As If in a Dream) that she wrote in her teens—"I often remember the dusk at the brookside pavilion, / deep in drink, not knowing the way home"—already shows a free-spiritedness rare in others.

在“女子无才便是德”的年代，李清照活成了一个异数。 而她幸运的是，她的家庭没有拦着她。

In an age that held that "a woman's lack of talent is her virtue," Li Qingzhao lived as an anomaly. And she was fortunate: her family did not hold her back.

二、赌书泼茶

II. Betting on Books, Spilling the Tea

1101年，18岁的李清照嫁给了21岁的赵明诚。赵明诚出身官宦之家，父亲赵挺之后来官至宰相。更重要的是，赵明诚和她一样，痴迷于金石书画。

In 1101, at eighteen, Li Qingzhao married the twenty-one-year-old Zhao Mingcheng. Zhao came from an official family; his father, Zhao Tingzhi, would later rise to be chancellor. More importantly, Zhao Mingcheng shared her passion for epigraphy, calligraphy, and painting.

这段婚姻，是中国文学史上最被称道的一段姻缘。两人志趣相投，一起收集碑帖、古籍、字画，一起品评、抄录、考订。他们的生活并不总是富裕——赵明诚一度在外地做小官，两人分居，靠书信往来。

This marriage is among the most celebrated unions in the history of Chinese literature. The two shared the same tastes, collected rubbings of stone inscriptions, rare books, and calligraphy and paintings together, and together appraised, copied, and collated them. Their life was not always comfortable—for a time Zhao Mingcheng held a minor post elsewhere, and the couple lived apart, keeping in touch by letter.

李清照在《金石录后序》里回忆过他们最快乐的日子：饭后烹茶，指着满屋的书，说出某件事在某本书第几卷第几页第几行，谁说得对，谁就先喝茶。赢的人常常高兴得把茶泼在怀里，茶还没喝到嘴里，人先笑倒了。

In the Postscript to Records on Metal and Stone (Jinshi Lu Houxu), Li Qingzhao recalled their happiest days: after a meal they would brew tea, point at the books filling the room, and name some event and precisely which book, which volume, which page, which line recorded it. Whoever got it right drank first. The winner was often so delighted that she spilled the tea down her front—before the tea ever reached her lips, she had already laughed herself over.

“赌书泼茶”，从此成为中国文化里夫妻相得的最高写照。

"Betting on books, spilling the tea" has since become, in Chinese culture, the supreme image of a couple in perfect accord.

三、靖康之变

III. The Jingkang Catastrophe

1127年，金兵南下，北宋灭亡。这就是“靖康之变”。赵明诚和李清照的家，也在劫难中瓦解。

In 1127, the Jin armies swept south and the Northern Song fell. This was the "Jingkang Catastrophe." The home of Zhao Mingcheng and Li Qingzhao was torn apart in the calamity as well.

那几年，是他们一生中最狼狈的时光。他们带着十几车金石文物南逃。战乱之中，很多东西要么丢了，要么烧了，要么被人抢了。赵明诚辗转各地任职，先后在几个地方做官。

Those years were the most wretched of their lives. They fled south with more than a dozen cartloads of metal-and-stone antiquities. In the chaos of war many of these things were lost, burned, or seized by others. Zhao Mingcheng moved from post to post, serving as an official in several places in turn.

1129年，赵明诚任江宁（今南京）知府。当时城中发生兵变，赵明诚却从城墙上用绳子偷偷逃走。这件事让李清照极为失望。她写下《夏日绝句》：

In 1129, Zhao Mingcheng was prefect of Jiangning (present-day Nanjing). When a mutiny broke out in the city, Zhao Mingcheng slipped away down the city wall by a rope. This left Li Qingzhao bitterly disappointed. She wrote "Jueju on a Summer Day" (Xiari Jueju):

生当作人杰，死亦为鬼雄。

In life, be a hero among men; in death, be a champion among ghosts.

至今思项羽，不肯过江东。

Even now I think of Xiang Yu, who would not cross the river east.

四句二十字，字字是指责。一个女子，用最刚烈的诗，讽刺了自己丈夫的胆怯。

Four lines, twenty characters, every one of them an accusation. A woman, with the most unyielding of poems, satirized her own husband's cowardice.

同年，赵明诚在赴任途中病逝，年仅四十九岁。李清照成了寡妇，也彻底失去了依靠。

That same year, Zhao Mingcheng fell ill and died on the way to a new posting, aged only forty-nine. Li Qingzhao became a widow and lost her last support entirely.

四、带着文物逃难的日子

IV. Days of Flight, Carrying the Antiquities

赵明诚死后，李清照的生活变成了一场漫长的逃难。

After Zhao Mingcheng's death, Li Qingzhao's life became one long flight.

她带着剩下的文物一路南撤，从南京到杭州，从越州到台州，从温州到衢州，几乎把东南沿海跑了一遍。金兵追到哪里，她就逃到哪里。她手中的文物越逃越少——有的被乱兵抢走，有的被房东偷走，有的被偷运到别处。

She withdrew southward with the antiquities that remained, from Nanjing to Hangzhou, from Yuezhou to Taizhou, from Wenzhou to Quzhou, covering nearly the whole southeast coast. Wherever the Jin soldiers pursued, there she fled. The antiquities in her keeping dwindled as she fled—some seized by marauding soldiers, some stolen by landlords, some smuggled away elsewhere.

她后来在《金石录后序》里详细记下了这二十多年“得之难而失之易”的痛。那篇文章，不只是一篇序，而是一个人眼睁睁看着自己的整个世界一点点流失的记录。

She later recorded in detail, in the Postscript to Records on Metal and Stone, the pain of those twenty-odd years—how hard such things were to gain and how easily lost. That piece is not merely a preface but the record of a person watching her entire world slip away piece by piece.

五、再嫁与离婚

V. Remarriage and Divorce

1132年，49岁的李清照做了一件在当时惊世骇俗的事：她再嫁了，丈夫叫张汝舟。

In 1132, at forty-nine, Li Qingzhao did something that scandalized her age: she remarried. Her new husband was Zhang Ruzhou.

关于这次婚姻，后世争议极大。有人认为她确实再嫁，有人（尤其是明清一些文人）出于“维护才女形象”的目的，坚称她没嫁。现代学界的主流看法是：她确实再嫁了。 部分否定再嫁的说法，来自后世对女性再嫁的偏见，而不是史实本身。

This marriage has been fiercely disputed by later generations. Some hold that she did remarry; others—especially certain literati of the Ming and Qing—insisted she did not, in order to "preserve the image of a woman of talent." The mainstream view in modern scholarship is that she did indeed remarry. The denials of her remarriage arose in part from later prejudice against women remarrying, not from the historical record itself.

这段婚姻很快失败了。张汝舟娶她，很可能图的是她手里的文物。发现所得有限后，他开始对她施加暴力。李清照不肯忍，她选择了告发——状告张汝舟在科举中有舞弊行为。

The marriage quickly failed. Zhang Ruzhou very likely married her to get at the antiquities in her possession. When he found there was little to be had, he began to do violence to her. Li Qingzhao would not endure it; she chose to lay a charge—accusing Zhang Ruzhou of fraud in the imperial examinations.

这在中古的法律里，是一条极其凶险的路。按照宋代法律，妻子告丈夫，即使告赢了，妻子也要被判入狱两年。

Under the law of that era, this was an extremely perilous course. By Song law, if a wife accused her husband, then even if she won her case she herself was sentenced to two years in prison.

她还是告了。结果张汝舟被治罪，李清照也入了狱。 幸好有翰林学士綦崇礼等亲友营救，她只被关押了九天就获释。

She brought the charge anyway. Zhang Ruzhou was convicted, and Li Qingzhao went to prison as well. Fortunately, friends and relatives—among them the Hanlin academician Qi Chongli—came to her rescue, and she was released after only nine days.

一个年近五十的寡妇，宁愿坐牢，也要跟一个打她的丈夫彻底了断。这件事的分量，放在今天的语境里，依然沉甸甸的。

A widow nearing fifty would rather go to prison than remain bound to a husband who beat her. The weight of that act still feels heavy even in today's terms.

六、她还敢批评所有人

VI. And She Dared to Criticize Everyone

李清照不只是写词，她还写过一篇《词论》，系统地评价了当时几乎所有重要的词人。

Li Qingzhao did not merely write ci; she also wrote a piece titled "On Ci" (Cilun), systematically assessing nearly every important ci poet of her day.

她说柳永的词“词语尘下”，说苏轼的词“皆句读不葺之诗耳”——意思是苏轼那些东西，根本就是句子长短不齐的诗，不算真正的词。她还批评了晏殊、欧阳修、王安石等人。

She said Liu Yong's ci had "language that is vulgar," and said Su Shi's ci were "nothing but poems with uncorrected punctuation"—meaning that Su Shi's work was simply poetry with lines of uneven length, not true ci at all. She also criticized Yan Shu, Ouyang Xiu, Wang Anshi, and others.

一个生活在十二世纪的女作者，敢对当代最顶级的男性文人逐一评判，这在文学史上几乎是孤例。

For a woman writer living in the twelfth century to dare to judge, one by one, the foremost male literati of her time is almost unique in literary history.

今天回看，她的判断未必全部公允。但她那种“我懂，所以我敢说”的底气，恰恰说明她不是凭天赋随口写写，而是有一套完整的、经过深思的词学主张。她是中国词史上第一个既有创作、又有理论、还敢开火的人。

In hindsight, her judgments were not all necessarily fair. But the confidence behind her attitude—"I understand, therefore I dare to speak"—shows precisely that she did not write casually on talent alone, but held a complete, carefully considered doctrine of ci. She was the first person in the history of Chinese ci to combine creation, theory, and the nerve to open fire.

值得一提的是，她批评苏轼，恰恰说明她站得够高——她不是在“抬杠”，而是在为“词”争取一个独立的艺术地位：词不该只是诗的边角料，它有它自己的规矩和尊严。

It is worth noting that her criticism of Su Shi actually shows how high she stood—she was not merely being contrarian, but fighting for ci to have an independent artistic standing: ci should not be a mere leftover of poetry; it has its own rules and its own dignity.

七、一个人的晚年

VII. A Solitary Old Age

此后的李清照，几乎是一个人过完的。

From then on, Li Qingzhao lived out her life almost entirely alone.

她没有子女。她继续整理赵明诚留下的《金石录》，并为它写下了那篇著名的后序。她写词、喝酒、偶尔和友人往来。她的词风变了，从早年的明快清丽，变成了深沉的、几乎令人窒息的凄楚。

She had no children. She went on editing the Records on Metal and Stone that Zhao Mingcheng had left behind, and wrote for it that famous postscript. She wrote ci, drank wine, and occasionally exchanged visits with friends. Her style had changed—from the bright clarity of her early years to a profound, almost suffocating desolation.

寻寻觅觅，冷冷清清，凄凄惨惨戚戚。

Seeking, seeking; searching, searching; cold, so cold; quiet, so quiet; bitter, bitter, sad, sad, grieving, grieving.

乍暖还寒时候，最难将息。

When it turns warm and then cold again, rest is hardest to come by.

这十四个叠字，写尽了国破、家亡、夫死、飘零之后，一个人内心那种无处安放的空白。约1155年前后，李清照在孤独中去世，享年七十余岁。

These fourteen reduplicated characters lay bare, after a ruined country, a broken home, a dead husband, and years of drifting, that inner blankness a person has nowhere to place. Around 1155, Li Qingzhao died in loneliness, aged over seventy.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

李清照的故事，经常被讲成“一个女人失去了丈夫”。这是把她看小了。

Li Qingzhao's story is often told as "a woman who lost her husband." That is to see her too small.

她失去的不只是丈夫。她失去的是整个秩序——一个国家、一个家、一屋子藏品、一种她以为会持续下去的生活。她后来做的每一件事——整理《金石录》、告发张汝舟、写《词论》批评当时所有的大词人——都是一个人在废墟上，固执地维持着自己的尊严。

What she lost was not only a husband. She lost an entire order—a country, a home, a roomful of collections, a way of life she had believed would go on. Everything she did afterward—editing Records on Metal and Stone, laying charges against Zhang Ruzhou, writing "On Ci" to criticize all the great ci poets of her day—was one person stubbornly holding on to her dignity amid the ruins.

她最了不起的地方，不是“有才”，而是“不服”。

Her greatest quality was not "talent" but refusal to submit.

不服丈夫逃城，不服丈夫早逝，不服被欺凌，不服被世界遗忘。她把不服气，全都写进了词里，然后这些词活了一千年。

She refused to accept that her husband had fled the city; refused to accept his early death; refused to be bullied; refused to be forgotten by the world. She poured all that refusal into her ci, and those ci lived for a thousand years.

今天，当我们谈“独立女性”的时候，李清照早就在那里了。她不是谁的贤内助，也不是谁的宠妃。她就是一个在乱世里，努力做自己的人。她的词，至今仍是很多人心里“关于失去”最好的表达。

Today, when we talk about the "independent woman," Li Qingzhao was already there long ago. She was no one's virtuous helpmate, no one's favored consort. She was simply a person, in a chaotic age, doing her best to be herself. Her ci remain, for many people, the finest expression of "loss."

她的晚年是孤独的。但也正是在这孤独里，她没有变成一个只会哀怨的人。她整理丈夫留下的金石著录，为它写下那篇著名的后序；她写词，评词，点评当世的大家。她活到七十多岁，在那个年代已算长寿。她用后半生证明了一件事：一个失去一切的人，依然可以靠一支笔，把自己立住。

Her old age was lonely. But it was precisely in this loneliness that she did not become merely a woman of complaint. She edited the epigraphic catalogue her husband had left and wrote for it that famous postscript; she wrote ci, judged ci, and appraised the great figures of her age. She lived to over seventy, which in that era counted as long life. With the second half of her life she proved one thing: a person who has lost everything can still, with a single brush, hold herself upright.

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：李清照，1084年生于齐州章丘（今山东济南章丘），约1155年卒。父李格非为苏轼门生。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Li Qingzhao, born in 1084 in Zhangqiu, Qizhou (present-day Zhangqiu, Jinan, Shandong), died around 1155. Her father, Li Gefei, was a disciple of Su Shi. Established historical fact.

2. 婚姻：1101年与赵明诚结婚。夫妻共好金石书画，“赌书泼茶”出自她本人的《金石录后序》。为确证史实。

2. Marriage: married Zhao Mingcheng in 1101. The couple shared a love of epigraphy, calligraphy, and painting; "betting on books, spilling the tea" comes from her own Postscript to Records on Metal and Stone (Jinshi Lu Houxu). Established historical fact.

3. 靖康之变与南渡：1127年北宋灭亡，夫妻南逃；1129年赵明诚弃江宁城逃走并同年病逝。为确证史实。《夏日绝句》通常被认为针对此事，但这是后世的主流解读，不能完全排除其他创作背景。

3. The Jingkang Catastrophe and the flight south: the Northern Song fell in 1127 and the couple fled south; in 1129 Zhao Mingcheng abandoned Jiangning and fled, and died the same year. Established historical fact. "Jueju on a Summer Day" is usually taken to refer to this event, but this is the mainstream reading of later ages; other contexts of composition cannot be entirely ruled out.

4. 再嫁张汝舟：据宋代文献（如《建炎以来系年要录》等）记载，1132年李清照再嫁张汝舟，后讼离。辨析：明清部分文人否认其再嫁，认为系“诬蔑”；现代学界（如王仲闻、黄盛璋等）经过考订，普遍认为再嫁属事实。本文采信后者。

4. Remarriage to Zhang Ruzhou: according to Song-dynasty sources (such as the Yaolu, Essential Records of the Jianyan Period, and others), Li Qingzhao remarried Zhang Ruzhou in 1132 and later sued for divorce. Analysis: some Ming and Qing literati denied the remarriage, calling it "slander"; modern scholars (such as Wang Zhongwen, Huang Shengzhang, and others), after examination, generally hold that the remarriage is a fact. This essay follows the latter.

5. 入狱九日：宋律“妻告夫”即便胜诉，妻亦须受罚。多得綦崇礼等人营救，仅系狱九日获释。为通行记载，具体天数各资料记载略有出入，但“经人营救、短期获释”这一基本事实可信。

5. Nine days in prison: under Song law, when "a wife accuses her husband," even if she wins she must also be punished. With the help of Qi Chongli and others, she was released after only nine days in custody. This is the common account; the exact number of days varies slightly among sources, but the basic fact—that she was rescued by others and released after a short time—is credible.

6. 卒年：史无确载，一般推定在1155年前后。本文采用“约1155年”的表述，不编造确切日期。

6. Year of death: no precise record survives; it is generally placed around 1155. This essay uses the wording "around 1155" and does not invent a precise date.

7. 词作归属：《词论》为其重要词学批评文献，其中对苏轼等人词作的评价历来有争议。本文不展开其学术争论。

7. Attribution of works: "On Ci" (Cilun) is an important document of her ci criticism, and its appraisals of Su Shi and others' ci have long been disputed. This essay does not enter into the scholarly debate.
