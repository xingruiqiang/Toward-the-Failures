陈景润：在六平方米的屋子里，他“移动了群山”

Chen Jingrun: In a Six-Square-Meter Room, He "Moved Mountains"

——“在科学的道路上，我只是翻过了一个小山包。”

— "On the road of science, I have only crossed a small hill."

2026年，我们谈“科研条件”，谈的是超算、是大平台、是国际合作。但中国现代科学史上，有一座里程碑，是在一间六平方米的小屋里立起来的。

In 2026, when we talk about "research conditions," we mean supercomputers, big platforms, international collaboration. Yet in the history of modern Chinese science there is a milestone that was raised inside a room of six square meters.

那间屋子原本是个锅炉房，没有锅炉，角落里杵着一根烟囱，进门左边放一张单人床，床就当书桌用。一个人伏在床上，借着一盏煤油灯，用掉了几麻袋的草稿纸——他在那里攻克了一个困扰世界两百多年的数学难题。

The room had once been a boiler room. There was no boiler; a chimney pipe stood in the corner. To the left of the door was a single bed, and the bed served as a desk. A man bent over it by the light of a kerosene lamp and used up several sacks of scratch paper—there he conquered a mathematical problem that had troubled the world for more than two hundred years.

这个人叫陈景润。他做出的成果，被国际数学界称为“陈氏定理”。

The man's name was Chen Jingrun. The result he produced is known in the international mathematical community as Chen's theorem.

一、不爱说话的瘦弱孩子

I. A Thin, Quiet Child

1933年5月，陈景润出生在福建省福州市。他从小就是一个瘦弱、内向、不爱说话的孩子。

Chen Jingrun was born in Fuzhou, Fujian Province, in May 1933. From early childhood he was a thin, inward boy who spoke little.

别的孩子在外面玩，他喜欢一个人演算数学题。那些在旁人看来枯燥无味的代数方程式，对他来说，却充满了乐趣。

While other children played outside, he liked to work through mathematics on his own. The algebraic equations that seemed dull to everyone else were, to him, full of delight.

高中时，老师讲了一个故事：1742年，德国数学家哥德巴赫发现，每一个大偶数似乎都可以写成两个素数的和——但没人能证明它。两百多年过去了，它仍然只是一个“猜想”。这个猜想，被称为“数学皇冠上的明珠”。

In high school, a teacher told a story: in 1742 the German mathematician Goldbach noticed that every large even number seems to be expressible as the sum of two primes—but no one could prove it. More than two hundred years had passed, and it was still only a conjecture. This conjecture was called "the jewel in the crown of mathematics."

这个故事，陈景润记了一辈子。

Chen Jingrun remembered that story for the rest of his life.

1953年，他从厦门大学数学系毕业。之后他做过教师，也在厦门大学工作。他在数论方面的出色研究，引起了数学家华罗庚的注意。华罗庚曾对人说：你们待在我身边，倒让一个素不相识的青年改进了我的工作。

In 1953 he graduated from the mathematics department of Xiamen University. He later taught school and worked at Xiamen University. His outstanding research in number theory drew the attention of the mathematician Hua Luogeng. Hua once remarked to those around him: You all stay close to me, and yet a young man I have never met has improved my work.

1957年，华罗庚把陈景润调到了中国科学院数学研究所。

In 1957, Hua Luogeng had Chen Jingrun transferred to the Institute of Mathematics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.

二、那间刀把形的屋子

II. The Knife-Handle Room

在数学所，陈景润被安排进了一间六平方米的屋子。

At the institute, Chen Jingrun was assigned a room of six square meters.

那是一间“刀把形”的锅炉房。没有锅炉，但角落里有一根突起的烟囱占着显眼的位置。屋里正好放一张单人床，一张断了腿的凳子横着放倒，正好当座位。床，就是他的书桌。

It was a "knife-handle-shaped" boiler room. There was no boiler, but a protruding chimney pipe occupied a conspicuous spot in the corner. The room was just big enough for a single bed, and a stool with a broken leg, laid on its side, made a seat. The bed was his desk.

1964年前后，他开始正式向哥德巴赫猜想中“1+2”的难关发起冲击。

Around 1964 he began his full assault on the "1+2" barrier within Goldbach's conjecture.

1966年，他发表了相关的论文，但他自己不满意——证明过程过于冗杂，不够简洁明了。他上了山顶，但路线还不清晰。他要重新走一遍，把路修好。

In 1966 he published a paper on it, but he was not satisfied—the proof was too cluttered, not clean or clear enough. He had reached the summit, but the route was still unclear. He would walk it again and repair the road.

而就在这时，“文革”开始了。

And just then, the Cultural Revolution began.

三、浊流声中的演算

III. Calculation in the Roar of Muddy Waters

那段时期，陈景润也受到了冲击。有人往他脸上吐唾沫，他揩掉了，然后继续低头算他的数学。他不善于申辩，受了天大的委屈，也只是忍着。

Chen Jingrun was attacked during those years too. Someone spat in his face; he wiped it off and went back to his mathematics, head down. He was not good at arguing his case. No matter how unjustly he was treated, he simply endured it.

后来，数学所绝大多数人都被下放到“五七干校”，体弱多病的陈景润意外被留了下来。楼道一到夜晚就空无一人，安静得发慌。

Later, almost everyone at the Institute of Mathematics was sent down to a May Seventh Cadre School. Chen Jingrun, frail and often ill, was unexpectedly allowed to stay. At night the corridors emptied of all people, quiet enough to make the heart uneasy.

他房间里的电线被人剪断了。他自己买了两盏煤油灯，一盏亮着，另一盏放在墙角，随时准备替换。

The wiring in his room was cut by someone. He bought two kerosene lamps himself—one lit, the other set in the corner against the wall, ready to replace it at any moment.

于是，在那间六平方米的小屋里，就出现了一盏煤油灯的光。 外面是震天的喧闹，屋里是一个人在纸上一遍遍地推演。

And so, in that room of six square meters, there was the light of a kerosene lamp. Outside raged a deafening clamor; inside, one man worked the same derivations over and over on paper.

他是在“被人忘记”的那几年里，完成了那件让世界记住他的事。

It was in those years of being forgotten that he finished the work that made the world remember him.

四、1973年

IV. 1973

1973年，陈景润发表了“1+2”的详细证明。

In 1973 Chen Jingrun published the detailed proof of "1+2."

这个结果，在国际数学界引起了轰动。它被公认为对哥德巴赫猜想研究的重大贡献，是筛法理论的光辉顶点。

The result caused a sensation in the international mathematical community. It has been recognized as a major contribution to research on Goldbach's conjecture, the brilliant summit of sieve-method theory.

简单地说：他证明了这个命题——任何一个足够大的偶数，都可以表示为一个素数与一个“不超过两个素数乘积的数”之和。 距离最终的“1+1”（哥德巴赫猜想本身），只差最后一步。

Put simply, he proved this proposition: any sufficiently large even number can be expressed as the sum of a prime and a number that is "the product of no more than two primes." Only one final step remained to the ultimate "1+1"—Goldbach's conjecture itself.

有数学家给他写信，只写了一句：“你移动了群山。”

A mathematician wrote him a letter. It contained a single line: "You have moved mountains."

这个成果，被国际数学界命名为“陈氏定理”。至今，它仍然是哥德巴赫猜想研究中最重要的成果之一，被写进世界各国的数论专著。

The international mathematical community named the result Chen's theorem. To this day it remains one of the most important achievements in research on Goldbach's conjecture, written into number-theory monographs around the world.

一个关于他忘我钻研的故事广为流传：他一边走路一边想数学，有一次一头撞在路边的树上，他连忙道歉，抬头一看，才发现那是一棵白杨树。

A story about his self-oblivious devotion is widely told: walking along thinking about mathematics, he once bumped headlong into a roadside tree. He apologized at once, then looked up and realized it was a poplar.

五、从“六平方米”到“抢救陈景润”

V. From "Six Square Meters" to "Saving Chen Jingrun"

1978年，作家徐迟的报告文学《哥德巴赫猜想》发表。这篇作品让数学家陈景润的名字传遍了大江南北。

In 1978 the writer Xu Chi published his reportage "The Goldbach Conjecture." The piece made the name of the mathematician Chen Jingrun known throughout the land.

一夜之间，他成了全中国最有名的科学家之一。 那个躲在六平方米小屋里的数学痴人，成了一个时代的偶像。无数年轻人因为这篇文章，立志去学数学。

Overnight he became one of the most famous scientists in all of China. The mathematics-obsessed man hidden away in a six-square-meter room became an idol of his era. Countless young people, because of that essay, resolved to study mathematics.

这里有一个重要的背景：是那一篇报告文学，让国家意识到，这样一个做出了世界级成果的科学家，竟然住在这样的环境里。 随后，“抢救陈景润”成了一个专门的提法——国家决定改善他的生活和工作条件，分给他更好的住处。

There is an important backdrop here: it was that piece of reportage that made the state realize a scientist who had produced world-class results was living in such conditions. "Saving Chen Jingrun" then became a phrase of its own—the state decided to improve his living and working conditions and assign him better housing.

1981年，他当选中科院学部委员（即后来的院士）。他获得了国家自然科学奖一等奖、华罗庚数学奖等重要荣誉。

In 1981 he was elected a member of the Academic Divisions of the Chinese Academy of Sciences—what later became academician. He received major honors including the first prize of the State Natural Science Award and the Hua Luogeng Mathematics Prize.

六、最后的岁月

VI. The Final Years

常年的透支，让陈景润的身体很早就出了问题。他患上了帕金森氏综合征，一病就是十多年。

Years of running himself into the ground took their toll on Chen Jingrun's health early. He developed Parkinson's syndrome, and the illness lasted more than a decade.

但他没有停下来。在生命的最后阶段，他仍然在尝试向“1+1”发起冲击。他知道自己大概完不成这最后一步——他和他所在的那个世纪，可能都够不着了。 但他仍然在算。

But he did not stop. In the last stage of his life he was still trying to mount an assault on "1+1." He knew he would probably never finish that final step—that neither he nor the century he belonged to might reach it. And still he kept calculating.

1996年3月19日，陈景润在北京去世，享年六十三岁。他做的最后一个决定，是把遗体捐献给医院做解剖。

On March 19, 1996, Chen Jingrun died in Beijing at the age of sixty-three. The last decision he made was to donate his body to a hospital for dissection.

他把自己用了六十三年、最后被疾病折磨的身体，也交了出去。

He gave away even the body he had used for sixty-three years, the one illness had tormented to the end.

一个一辈子在纸上推演数字的人，最后用这种方式，为医学留下了一点有用的东西。这很像他：凡是他有的，他都愿意拿出来，放到能起作用的地方。

A man who had spent a lifetime working out numbers on paper left medicine, in the end and in this way, something useful. It was very like him: whatever he had, he was willing to give over and put where it could do some good.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

陈景润的故事，在今天读来有特别的意义。

Chen Jingrun's story carries a particular meaning read today.

我们这一代人，习惯了“条件”。做不好一件事，可以归因于资源不够、平台不好、环境太差。这些因素当然真实存在。

Our generation has grown used to "conditions." When we fail at something, we can blame insufficient resources, a poor platform, a bad environment. Those factors of course really exist.

但陈景润是在一间六平方米的锅炉房里，用煤油灯和几麻袋草稿纸，做出了世界级的成果。他不是没有困难，他的困难比我们大得多。他只是“不看困难，只看问题”。

But Chen Jingrun produced world-class results in a six-square-meter boiler room, with a kerosene lamp and several sacks of scratch paper. It is not that he had no difficulties—his were far greater than ours. He simply "paid no attention to the difficulties, only to the problem."

他有一句话，最能说明他的想法：“时间是个常数，花掉一天等于浪费24小时。”

One remark of his captures his thinking best: "Time is a constant; to spend a day is to waste twenty-four hours."

2026年，我们每天被无数信息切割着时间。陈景润提醒我们：专注，本身就是一种极其稀缺的能力。 一个人能不能做出点什么，很多时候不取决于他有多少时间，而取决于他能不能把时间，真正地用在一件事上。

In 2026, our time is sliced apart every day by a torrent of information. Chen Jingrun reminds us: concentration is itself an extremely scarce ability. Whether a person can accomplish anything often depends not on how much time he has, but on whether he can truly put his time into a single thing.

他被后人记住，不是因为“苦”，而是因为在那样苦的条件里，他仍然把一件事做到了世界第一。 这才是“失败者”这个词，在他身上完成反转的方式。

He is remembered not because of the hardship, but because under such hard conditions he still brought one thing to first place in the world. That is how the word "failure" is turned on its head in his case.

也要看到：陈景润在很长时间里，被认为“不正常”——他不善交往，不修边幅，走路撞树，说话磕巴。在一套以“会来事”为标准的社会评价里，他这样的人往往是被边缘的。 可他证明了另一件事：世界的进步，有时候恰恰要靠这些“不太合群”的人。 他们可能不会应酬，不会包装自己，但他们能把一件事做到极致——而这，往往才是最稀缺的能力。

One should also see this: for a long time Chen Jingrun was considered "abnormal"—he was socially awkward, careless about his appearance, walked into trees, stammered when he spoke. In a set of social judgments that take "knowing how to work people" as the standard, a man like him is often pushed to the margins. But he proved something else: the world's progress sometimes depends precisely on these "ill-fitting" people. They may not know how to make the rounds, may not know how to package themselves, but they can push one thing to its limit—and that, often, is the scarcest ability of all.

他的一生，也折射出一个时代的转变：从“知识分子不被重视”，到“科学家成为全民偶像”，中间只隔了一篇报告文学和一次国家层面的反省。一个人可以改变数学的边界，但他能不能被善待，取决于他所在的社会愿不愿意低头看一眼。陈景润最终被看见了，这是他的幸运，也是那个时代的自我修正。

His life also reflects a shift in an era: between "intellectuals are not valued" and "scientists become national idols" lay only a single piece of reportage and one moment of national self-examination. A person can change the boundaries of mathematics, but whether he is treated well depends on whether the society he lives in is willing to lower its head and look. Chen Jingrun was seen at last—his good fortune, and also that era's self-correction.
