麦哲伦：第一个绕地球一圈的人，死在半路上

Magellan: The First Man to Circle the Globe, Who Died Along the Way

——“教会总是说地球是平的，可我在海上，看见的是圆的。”

—"The Church always said the earth was flat, but I was at sea, and what I saw was round."

2026年，“地球是圆的”是幼儿园的常识。我们用一张机票、一次转机，就能完成一次环球旅行。

In 2026, "the earth is round" is kindergarten common sense. With one plane ticket and one layover, we can complete a trip around the world.

但五百多年前，没有一个人真正证明过这件事。人们知道你住在“地上”，却不知道这个“地”到底是什么形状——是平的？是圆的？走到尽头会不会掉下去？

But more than five hundred years ago, no one had actually proven it. People knew you lived on "the earth," but no one knew what shape that earth was—flat? Round? Would you fall off if you walked to the edge?

有一个人，决定用船队去撞这个答案。他带着五艘船、两百多人出发，穿过前所未见的狂风巨浪，横渡一片几乎不可能生还的大洋。

One man decided to hurl a fleet of ships at that question. He set out with five ships and more than two hundred men, through winds and waves unlike anything seen before, across an ocean that seemed almost impossible to survive.

他最终没有回来。他死在了一个小岛上的部落冲突里。

He never came back. He died in a tribal conflict on a small island.

但他出发时的那支船队，最后有十八个人，绕了地球整整一圈，回到了出发的港口。人类第一次知道：这个世界，是圆的。

But of the fleet that set out with him, eighteen men finally circled the entire globe and returned to the port they had left. For the first time, humanity knew: this world is round.

一、一个被自己国家拒之门外的葡萄牙人

I. A Portuguese Turned Away by His Own Country

麦哲伦约1480年出生在葡萄牙的一个小贵族家庭。他很早就进了葡萄牙海军，参加过远征印度的行动，在东方、非洲的海面上跑了十几年。

Magellan was born around 1480 into a minor noble family in Portugal. He joined the Portuguese navy early, took part in expeditions to India, and spent more than a decade sailing the waters off the East and Africa.

他相信一件事：只要找到一条向西的航线，就能绕过非洲，直接到达盛产香料的“香料群岛”（今印尼马鲁古群岛）。 当时香料在欧洲比黄金还贵，谁掌握了这条航线，谁就掌握了财富。

He believed one thing: that if a westward route could be found, it would bypass Africa and reach the spice-rich "Spice Islands" (today's Maluku Islands in Indonesia) directly. At the time, spices were worth more than gold in Europe, and whoever controlled that route controlled a fortune.

他把这个计划提交给葡萄牙国王，请求支持。国王拒绝了。他又请求提高待遇和年金，也被拒绝了。

He submitted the plan to the King of Portugal and asked for support. The king refused. He then asked for better pay and a pension, and was refused again.

于是，1517年，他做了一个在当时极其“叛国”的决定：放弃葡萄牙，转投西班牙。

So in 1517 he made a decision that was, at the time, thoroughly "treasonous": he gave up Portugal and took his service to Spain.

他和宇宙学家法莱罗一起，向西班牙国王查理一世（即后来的神圣罗马皇帝查理五世）提出了环球航行计划。国王批准了。

Together with the cosmographer Rui Faleiro, he presented a plan for a voyage around the world to King Charles I of Spain (later Holy Roman Emperor Charles V). The king approved it.

二、五艘船，两百七十人

II. Five Ships, Two Hundred and Seventy Men

1519年9月20日，麦哲伦的船队从西班牙的桑卢卡尔港出发。五艘船，约两百七十名船员，来自九个不同的国家。

On September 20, 1519, Magellan's fleet left the port of Sanlúcar in Spain. Five ships, about two hundred and seventy crew, from nine different countries.

他们先横渡大西洋，沿着南美洲东海岸一路南下。到了1520年3月，船队在南美的圣胡利安港过冬，遇上了麻烦——几艘船的船长发动叛乱，要求返航。

They first crossed the Atlantic and sailed south along the eastern coast of South America. By March 1520 the fleet was wintering at Port San Julián in South America when trouble struck—the captains of several ships mutinied and demanded that they turn back.

麦哲伦的手段非常强硬。他平定了叛乱，处死或放逐了首恶。这次镇压，保住了这次航行，也让所有人都明白了：谁也不能动摇船长。

Magellan's response was harsh. He put down the mutiny, executing or marooning its ringleaders. The crackdown saved the voyage and made one thing clear to everyone: no one could shake the captain.

1520年10月，船队终于发现了一条通往西面海洋的狭窄水道——后来被命名为“麦哲伦海峡”。他们在狂风巨浪里走了二十八天，才穿过去。

In October 1520 the fleet finally found a narrow passage to the western ocean—later named the Strait of Magellan. It took twenty-eight days of gales and heavy seas to get through.

穿出去的那一刻，眼前是一片一望无际、风平浪静的海。麦哲伦给它起了个名字：太平洋。

The moment they emerged, a boundless, calm sea opened before them. Magellan gave it a name: the Pacific.

三、九十八天，没有陆地

III. Ninety-Eight Days Without Land

“太平洋”这个名字，其实是一个巨大的误会。它一点都不“太平”。

The name "Pacific" was, in fact, a huge misunderstanding. There was nothing "peaceful" about it.

船队进入太平洋后，连续航行了几十天，却碰不到一块陆地。船上带的食物早就吃光了，饼干变成了粉块，爬满蛆虫；饮水发臭变质。船员们开始吃牛皮（用来做缆绳的）、吃木屑、甚至吃舱里的老鼠。坏血病在船上蔓延，很多人牙齿脱落、皮肤溃烂。

After entering the Pacific, the fleet sailed for dozens of days without touching a single piece of land. The food they had brought was long gone; the biscuit had turned to powder crawling with maggots, and the drinking water had gone foul. The crew began to eat oxhide (the kind used for rigging), sawdust, even the rats in the hold. Scurvy spread through the ships; many lost their teeth and broke out in open sores.

一百多天的航行，没有补给，没有陆地，没有人知道还有多远。

More than a hundred days of sailing, no supplies, no land, and no one knew how far was left.

有记载说，麦哲伦本人在那段日子里，曾说过一句话：他即使在最绝望的时候，也从未担心过自己不会到达。

One account says that during those days Magellan himself remarked that even in his most desperate moments, he never once doubted that they would arrive.

1521年3月，船队终于到达了关岛一带，随后进入菲律宾群岛。从西向东、从东向西，他终于用一条航线，把地球连成了一个圆。

In March 1521 the fleet finally reached the region of Guam and then entered the Philippine Islands. West to east, east to west—with a single route he had finally joined the globe into a circle.

一个细节

A Detail

船队到达东方之后，麦哲伦从马六甲带来的一个会说马来语的仆人亨利，竟然能跟当地人对话。这意味着：船队从“西边”一路走来，绕了大半个地球，最终抵达的，正是葡萄牙人从“东边”到过的同一片海域。

After the fleet reached the East, Enrique—a servant Magellan had brought from Malacca who spoke Malay—was able to talk with the locals. That meant the fleet, having come all the way from "the west" around most of the globe, had arrived in the very same waters the Portuguese had reached "from the east."

那一刻，麦哲伦确信：他证明了地球是圆的。他完成了他出发时想要完成的事。

At that moment Magellan was certain: he had proven that the earth was round. He had done what he set out to do.

四、麦克坦岛

IV. Mactan Island

但麦哲伦没有停手。

But Magellan did not stop.

到了菲律宾群岛之后，他卷入了当地部落之间的纷争。宿务岛的酋长受洗成为基督徒，但邻近的麦克坦岛酋长拉普拉普拒绝臣服。麦哲伦决定出兵。

Once in the Philippine Islands, he was drawn into quarrels among the local tribes. The chief of Cebu was baptized a Christian, but Lapulapu, the chief of nearby Mactan Island, refused to submit. Magellan decided to send in his troops.

1521年4月27日，他带着六十来名装备火枪的士兵，乘小船登上麦克坦岛。他以为凭借火器和铠甲，可以轻松取胜。但岛上的战士人数众多，且熟悉地形地势。火枪装填缓慢，铠甲在浅水和丛林里反而成了负担。

On April 27, 1521, he landed on Mactan Island with some sixty soldiers armed with muskets, ferried in small boats. He expected firearms and armor to make short work of it. But the island's warriors were many and knew the terrain. The muskets took forever to reload, and the armor became a burden in the shallow water and jungle.

麦哲伦在混战中被杀死。 据说他腿上中了毒箭，最后被围攻砍倒。他没有完成自己的环球航行——他倒在了离终点只差最后一段路的地方。

Magellan was killed in the melee. It is said he took a poisoned arrow in the leg and was finally surrounded and cut down. He never completed his own voyage around the world—he fell with only the last stretch of the road still ahead.

后来那两座碑

The Two Monuments

在麦克坦岛上，至今矗立着两座纪念碑：一座纪念麦哲伦，一座纪念拉普拉普。它们相距不远，甚至就在同一条街的两侧，彼此对望。

On Mactan Island two monuments still stand today: one to Magellan, one to Lapulapu. They are not far apart—in fact they sit on opposite sides of the same street, facing each other.

同一个人，一方是“伟大的航海家”，另一方是“抵抗侵略者的民族英雄”。 历史给同一次事件，留下了完全相反的两份判词。

The same man: to one side a "great navigator," to the other a "national hero who resisted the invaders." History has left two entirely opposite verdicts on a single event.

五、十八个人

V. Eighteen Men

麦哲伦死后，船队由塞巴斯蒂安·埃尔卡诺接手。他们烧掉了一艘破烂的船，带着剩下的两艘，装满了香料，穿过马六甲海峡，经印度洋，绕过好望角，继续往回走。

After Magellan's death the fleet was taken over by Sebastián Elcano. They burned one unseaworthy ship, kept the remaining two, loaded them with spices, passed through the Strait of Malacca, crossed the Indian Ocean, rounded the Cape of Good Hope, and kept on homeward.

1522年9月6日，“维多利亚号”回到了西班牙的桑卢卡尔港——三年前出发的那个港口。

On September 6, 1522, the Victoria returned to the port of Sanlúcar in Spain—the same port they had left three years earlier.

出发时的五艘船，只剩下一艘。出发时的两百七十人，只剩下十八个。

Of the five ships that set out, one remained. Of the two hundred and seventy men, eighteen remained.

这十八个人，是人类历史上第一批绕地球走完一整圈的人。

Those eighteen men were the first in human history to travel all the way around the globe.

有意思的是，他们上岸后发现，自己的航海日志比西班牙本土的日期少了一天。这个“丢了一天”的现象，正是地球自转的直接证据——比任何理论推导都更有说服力。

Curiously, once ashore they found that their ship's log was a day behind the date kept in Spain. This "lost day" was direct evidence of the earth's rotation—more convincing than any theoretical proof.

六、写给2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

麦哲伦的故事，有一个让人不太舒服的地方：他本人也是个殖民者。 他到菲律宾之后，想的是把那里变成西班牙的领地，是征服、是传教、是扩张。他的死，本质上是一场镇压行动的失败。

There is something uncomfortable in Magellan's story: he was himself a colonizer. After reaching the Philippines, what he had in mind was turning the place into Spanish territory—conquest, conversion, expansion. His death was, at bottom, the failure of a punitive expedition.

所以，我们不能把他简单地讲成“纯粹的英雄”。麦哲伦是那个扩张时代的产物，他身上既有极其伟大的探索精神，也有极其冷酷的野心。

So we cannot simply tell him as a "pure hero." Magellan was a product of that age of expansion: in him lived both an extraordinary spirit of exploration and an extraordinarily cold ambition.

但这不妨碍承认另一件事：他和他那支船队做到的事情，改变了人类对自身所处世界的认知。

But that does not prevent us from acknowledging something else: what he and his fleet accomplished changed how humanity understood the world it lived in.

五百年前，一个人带着五艘船出发，用自己的死和十八个人的归航，回答了“我们住在什么样的世界上”这个问题。

Five hundred years ago, a man set out with five ships and, through his own death and the homecoming of eighteen men, answered the question of what kind of world we live in.

2026年的我们，已经很少有这种“整个世界都是未知”的机会了。但麦哲伦留下的那种东西还在：当一个答案还没被验证时，你敢不敢押上全部，去把它试出来？

For us in 2026, chances to face a world that is entirely unknown have grown rare. But what Magellan left behind is still here: when an answer has not yet been proven, do you dare to stake everything and go find it out?

他没能回来。但“地球是圆的”这件事，从此再也不是猜想。

He did not come back. But from then on, the fact that the earth is round was never a guess again.

值得一提的是，那十八个完成环球航行的人里，有一个叫皮加费塔的年轻人。他一路记日记，后来写成了一部航行报告。正是这份记录，让后人得以知道这次航行的细节。也就是说，麦哲伦的死、坏血病的折磨、“太平洋”这个名字的由来，都靠一支笔留了下来。探险和记录，从来是一件事的两面。

Worth noting: among the eighteen who completed the voyage was a young man named Antonio Pigafetta. He kept a journal all along and later turned it into a report of the voyage. It is thanks to that record that later generations came to know the details of the journey. In other words, Magellan's death, the torment of scurvy, the origin of the name "Pacific"—all of it survived through a single pen. Exploration and record-keeping have always been two sides of one thing.

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：麦哲伦（Ferdinand Magellan，葡萄牙语名Fernão de Magalhães），约1480年生于葡萄牙，1521年4月27日卒于菲律宾麦克坦岛。为通行记载。其出生年份学界有不同考订（约1480年前后）。

1. Basic biographical facts: Ferdinand Magellan (Portuguese name Fernão de Magalhães), born in Portugal around 1480, died April 27, 1521, on Mactan Island in the Philippines. Standard account. Scholars differ on his birth year (around 1480).

2. 早年与转投西班牙：曾参加葡萄牙海军远征印度，1517年前后因未获重用而转投西班牙。为确证史实。

2. Early years and transfer to Spain: he took part in Portuguese naval expeditions to India and, around 1517, transferred his service to Spain after failing to gain advancement. Established fact.

3. 船队规模与出发：1519年9月20日出发，5艘船，约270人（一说265人、270余人）。为通行记载，具体人数各资料略有差异。

3. Fleet size and departure: departed September 20, 1519, with 5 ships and about 270 men (some sources say 265, others "more than 270"). Standard account; the exact number varies slightly among sources.

4. 圣胡利安港平叛：1520年在南美圣胡利安港过冬期间发生叛乱，麦哲伦以强硬手段平定。为确证史实。

4. Suppression of the mutiny at Port San Julián: a mutiny broke out during the winter at Port San Julián in South America in 1520, and Magellan put it down by force. Established fact.

5. 麦哲伦海峡与“太平洋”命名：1520年10月发现海峡，28天穿越后进入大洋并命名“太平洋”。为确证史实。

5. The Strait of Magellan and the naming of the Pacific: the strait was discovered in October 1520, crossed in 28 days, after which the fleet entered the ocean and named it the "Pacific." Established fact.

6. 横渡太平洋：约98—110天未见陆地，船员饱受饥饿与坏血病折磨，有“吃牛皮、吃老鼠”的记载。为通行记载。

6. Crossing the Pacific: roughly 98–110 days without sighting land; the crew suffered severely from hunger and scurvy, with records of eating oxhide and rats. Standard account.

7. 麦克坦岛之死：1521年4月27日，麦哲伦率约60人进攻麦克坦岛，被拉普拉普部众击杀。为确证史实。其死亡细节（中毒箭、被砍杀）来自随行人员和船队史料的记载，可能有加工。

7. Death on Mactan Island: on April 27, 1521, Magellan attacked Mactan Island with about 60 men and was killed by Lapulapu's warriors. Established fact. The details of his death (the poisoned arrow, being cut down) come from the accounts of those who accompanied him and from fleet records, and may be embellished.

8. 归航：由埃尔卡诺率队，1522年9月6日返回西班牙，仅“维多利亚号”一艘、18人（一说17名欧洲人加4名印第安人一类的不同统计口径）。为通行记载。本文采用最常见的“1艘船、18人”。

8. The return: led by Elcano, the fleet returned to Spain on September 6, 1522, with only the Victoria and 18 men (other counts exist, such as 17 Europeans plus 4 Indians). Standard account. This book adopts the most common version: "1 ship, 18 men."

9. 两座纪念碑：麦克坦岛上麦哲伦纪念碑与拉普拉普纪念碑并存，为确证事实。本文借此说明历史评价的多面性，不偏向任何一方。

9. The two monuments: the Magellan monument and the Lapulapu monument stand side by side on Mactan Island. Established fact. This book uses them to illustrate the many-sidedness of historical judgment, favoring neither side.

10. 人类学评价：麦哲伦的航行同时具有伟大的探索意义和殖民扩张性质。本文明确不把他简化为“纯粹英雄”，以保持历史的完整。

10. Assessment: Magellan's voyage carried both great significance for exploration and the character of colonial expansion. This book deliberately does not reduce him to a "pure hero," in order to keep history whole.
