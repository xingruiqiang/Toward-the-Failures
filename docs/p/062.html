Chapter 062 · Part 3

雷军：金山16年，上市即“破发”，40岁才重新出发

Lei Jun: Sixteen Years at Kingsoft, an IPO That Broke Below Its Price, and a Fresh Start at Forty

——“站在台风口，猪都能飞上天”

—"Stand at the mouth of a typhoon, and even a pig will fly."

2026年，很多人在35岁前后经历一次“幻灭”：在一个地方埋头干了十几年，以为只要够努力，总会等到一个体面的回报。结果发现，努力和结果之间，并不成正比。

In 2026, many people go through a "disillusionment" around the age of thirty-five: they work away in one place for a dozen years or more, believing that if they just try hard enough, a decent reward will eventually come. Then they discover that effort and outcome are not proportional.

有一个人，22岁进金山，一干就是16年，把青春全砸了进去。2007年公司终于在香港上市，市值却只有6亿多港元——而同年上市的阿里巴巴，是15亿美元。

There was a man who joined Kingsoft at twenty-two and stayed sixteen years, pouring all his youth into it. In 2007 the company finally listed in Hong Kong, yet its market value was only a little over 600 million Hong Kong dollars—while Alibaba, listed the same year, was worth 1.5 billion US dollars.

他在40岁生日那天对自己说：“开始干吧！”第二年，他创办了小米。

On his fortieth birthday he told himself, "Time to start!" The next year he founded Xiaomi.

他叫雷军。他有一句名言，后来被无数创业者引用，也常被误读：“站在台风口，猪都能飞上天。”

His name is Lei Jun. He has a famous line that countless entrepreneurs have since quoted—and often misread: "Stand at the mouth of a typhoon, and even a pig will fly."

一、“好孩子”的16年

I. Sixteen Years as a "Good Boy"

1969年，雷军出生在湖北仙桃。18岁，他考入武汉大学计算机系，成绩优异；靠稿费和奖学金，他从大二起就实现了经济独立。

Lei Jun was born in 1969 in Xiantao, Hubei. At eighteen he was admitted to the computer science department at Wuhan University with excellent grades; from his sophomore year he was financially independent, supporting himself with writing income and scholarships.

1992年，22岁的他加入金山软件，成为公司第六名员工。从那以后，“劳模”和“老好人”的名声一直跟着他——他工作极其勤奋，为人又厚道，在业内口碑极好。

In 1992, at twenty-two, he joined Kingsoft as the company's sixth employee. From then on the reputation of "model worker" and "nice guy" followed him—he worked extremely hard and was generous by nature, with an excellent name in the industry.

1998年，29岁的他升任金山软件总经理。年少得志，意气风发。

In 1998, at twenty-nine, he was promoted to general manager of Kingsoft. Young and successful, riding high.

但金山所处的赛道，是一个“夹缝”。用雷军自己的话说是：“前有微软，后有盗版。” 做办公软件，上有微软Office，下有泛滥的盗版，几乎赚不到钱。金山被迫一次次转型——从办公软件到词霸、毒霸，再到游戏和互联网。

But Kingsoft's track was a "crack" between two forces. In Lei Jun's own words: "Microsoft ahead, piracy behind." In office software, Microsoft Office pressed from above and rampant piracy from below, and there was almost no money to be made. Kingsoft was forced to reinvent itself again and again—from office software to PowerWord and Antivirus, then on to games and the internet.

16年里，金山5次冲击IPO。2007年10月，公司终于在香港挂牌上市。

Over sixteen years, Kingsoft made five runs at an IPO. In October 2007, the company finally listed on the Hong Kong exchange.

二、上市那天，他在黯然神伤

II. On Listing Day, He Was Quietly Sorrowful

这是雷军人生中一个被反复讲述的场景。

This is one of the most retold scenes in Lei Jun's life.

2007年10月，金山在香港上市，众人都很振奋。雷军却高兴不起来。他后来发现，自己和同伴耗费16年、5840天奋斗，换来的市值只有6.26亿港元——而同年上市的阿里巴巴是15亿美元，2005年在纳斯达克上市的百度是39.58亿美元。

In October 2007, Kingsoft listed in Hong Kong, and everyone was elated. Lei Jun could not share the joy. He later realized that he and his colleagues had spent sixteen years—5,840 days—of struggle to earn a market value of just 626 million Hong Kong dollars, while Alibaba, listed the same year, was worth 1.5 billion US dollars, and Baidu, which went public on Nasdaq in 2005, was worth 3.958 billion US dollars.

眼看着比自己出道晚的“小兄弟们”功成名就，他心里的滋味可想而知。

Watching "little brothers" who had entered the game after him achieve fame and fortune, one can imagine what he felt.

两个月后，他在隆冬中离开了金山。临走前，他说了一句话：“从22岁到38岁，我在金山整整疯了16年，中间的压力很难表达，就像马拉松一样。”

Two months later, in the depths of winter, he left Kingsoft. Before going, he said one thing: "From twenty-two to thirty-eight, I went mad at Kingsoft for a full sixteen years. The pressure in between is hard to put into words—it was like a marathon."

“我的青春，我的金山。” 这句话里，有16年的全部重量。

"My youth, my Kingsoft." All sixteen years of weight are in that sentence.

他开始反复追问自己几个问题：“为什么有人付出100%的努力，只能换回20%的增长？反之，有人付出20%的努力，却能获得100%的回报？”“金山有中国最优秀的一批工程师，大家都很团结，执行力也非常强，可为什么最后上市依靠的反而是网游业务？”

He began asking himself the same questions over and over: "Why can someone give 100 percent effort and get back only 20 percent growth? Conversely, why can someone give 20 percent effort and get a 100 percent return?" "Kingsoft has some of China's best engineers; everyone is united, and the execution is very strong—so why, in the end, did the listing depend on the online gaming business instead?"

他的答案是：“金山就像是在盐碱地里种草。为什么不在台风口放风筝呢？” 于是有了后来那句著名的“站在台风口，猪都能飞上天”。

His answer was: "Kingsoft was like planting grass in saline-alkali soil. Why not fly a kite at the mouth of a typhoon?" And so came the later famous line, "Stand at the mouth of a typhoon, and even a pig will fly."

三、40岁，重新当一次“创业者”

III. At Forty, Becoming a "Founder" Again

离开金山后，雷军做了三年天使投资，投了三十多家公司，其中不少做得不错。

After leaving Kingsoft, Lei Jun spent three years as an angel investor, backing more than thirty companies, many of which did well.

但投资成功填不满他心里的那个洞——他一直想做出真正属于自己的事业。

But investment success could not fill the hole in his heart—he had always wanted to build something truly his own.

2009年底，40岁生日那天，他对自己说：“开始干吧！”

At the end of 2009, on his fortieth birthday, he told himself, "Time to start!"

2010年4月6日，小米科技在北京中关村成立。最初的团队非常“豪华”：创业者来自各个领域的精英，最初的56名员工总共投资了1100万美元，平均每人投20万美元。

On April 6, 2010, Xiaomi Tech was founded in Zhongguancun, Beijing. The initial team was remarkably "star-studded": the founders were elite figures from various fields, and the first fifty-six employees invested a total of 11 million US dollars, an average of 200,000 each.

但这在当时的科技圈里，引来的是普遍的质疑： 一个40岁、做了16年软件的人，凭什么去做手机？那时候中国的手机市场，是苹果、三星、华为、中兴、酷派、联想混战的战场。

But in the tech circles of the time, this drew widespread skepticism: a forty-year-old man who had spent sixteen years in software—what gave him the right to make phones? China's phone market then was a battlefield of Apple, Samsung, Huawei, ZTE, Coolpad, and Lenovo.

2011年8月16日，雷军发布第一款小米手机，售价1999元。这个价格对当时的智能手机来说几乎是“史无前例”的低。37小时卖出40万部，销售额8亿元。到2012年，小米全年销售719万部。

On August 16, 2011, Lei Jun launched the first Xiaomi phone at 1,999 yuan. For a smartphone at the time, that price was almost "unprecedentedly" low. In thirty-seven hours, 400,000 units sold, for 800 million yuan in revenue. By 2012, Xiaomi was selling 7.19 million units a year.

四、跌下来，再爬起来

IV. Fall Down, Then Get Up Again

小米也不是一路向上。

Xiaomi's path was not straight upward either.

2015到2016年，小米遭遇成立以来最大的危机：冲击高端市场却流失了用户，2016年业绩断崖式下滑，手机销量大跌，公司进入低谷。

In 2015 and 2016, Xiaomi hit the worst crisis since its founding: pushing into the premium market cost it users, and in 2016 its performance fell off a cliff, phone sales collapsed, and the company entered a trough.

素来严苛的雷军，那一次反而对团队说：“去掉KPI”“开心就好”。同时，他亲自整顿供应链，布局线下渠道和海外市场。

Lei Jun, usually so demanding, this time told his team the opposite: "Drop the KPIs," "Just be happy." At the same time, he personally overhauled the supply chain and laid out offline channels and overseas markets.

有意思的是，小米那几年的低谷，很大程度上是因为“冲高端”这一步走得太急。雷军后来复盘时说过类似的话：问题不是方向错了，而是能力还没跟上野心。这句话其实很值得琢磨——它和“顺势而为”是一对：方向对了，不代表你现在就有资格做成，该等的时候得等，该补的课得补。

Interestingly, Xiaomi's trough in those years was largely because the step into the premium market was taken too hastily. In later reflections Lei Jun said something along these lines: the problem was not that the direction was wrong, but that capability had not yet caught up with ambition. That sentence is worth pondering—it pairs with "go with the current": having the right direction does not mean you are now entitled to succeed. When you should wait, you wait; when you must catch up, you catch up.

局势在2017年渐渐明朗。2018年7月，小米在香港上市。而在上市之前的一份公开信里，雷军写下了那句后来被反复引用的话——小米的使命，是“感动人心、价格厚道”的好产品，“让全球每个人都能享受科技带来的美好生活”。

The situation gradually clarified in 2017. In July 2018, Xiaomi listed in Hong Kong. And in an open letter before the listing, Lei Jun wrote the line that would be quoted again and again—that Xiaomi's mission is good products that "move the heart and are honestly priced," to "let everyone in the world enjoy a better life through technology."

五、致2026：关于“努力了却没结果”

V. To 2026: On "Working Hard and Getting Nothing"

2026年，我们这代人最普遍的困惑，大概是：“我明明很努力，为什么没有用？”

In 2026, the most common confusion of our generation is probably this: "I clearly worked so hard—why did it come to nothing?"

雷军在金山那16年，给了这个问题一个很诚恳的答案：努力不一定有结果，很可能是因为你站在了错误的地形上。

Lei Jun's sixteen years at Kingsoft give this question a very honest answer: effort does not always produce results, and it may well be because you are standing on the wrong terrain.

他不是不努力——他是出了名的“劳模”。但他在“前有微软、后有盗版”的夹缝里努力了16年，最后还是没能把金山做成一家值钱的公司。这是他人生中最痛的一课。

He was not lacking in effort—he was famous as a "model worker." But he worked for sixteen years in that crack between "Microsoft ahead, piracy behind," and in the end still could not make Kingsoft into a valuable company. This was the most painful lesson of his life.

他后来总结了自己40岁前的五点体会，其中第二点是：“顺势而为，不要做逆天的事情。”

He later summed up five insights from before he turned forty, the second of which is: "Go with the current; don't do things that defy the heavens."

这不是让人放弃努力，而是让人先判断方向，再投入强度。对我们这代人来说，也许最该学的是这一点：

This is not a call to give up effort, but to judge the direction first and only then commit intensity. For our generation, this may be the most important thing to learn:

当你发现自己的努力长期没有回报时，先别急着加倍努力，先问问自己——我是不是站在一片盐碱地里？

When you find that your effort has long gone unrewarded, don't rush to double down—first ask yourself: am I standing in a patch of saline-alkali soil?

换一块地，也许比更快地跑，更重要。

Changing the ground may matter more than running faster.

雷军还有一句话，和他那句“台风口”是一体的：“不要用战术上的勤奋，掩盖战略上的懒惰。” 这句话戳中的是很多“努力型选手”的痛处——我们太容易沉浸在“我很忙、我很拼”的自我感动里，却很少停下来问一句：“我这么拼的这件事，本身对不对？”对2026年正在加班的你来说，也许最该留给自己的一点时间，就是用来回答这个问题。

Lei Jun has another line that is one with his "typhoon" line: "Don't use tactical diligence to cover up strategic laziness." That sentence hits the sore spot of many "hard-working competitors"—we are too easily immersed in the self-moved feeling of "I'm so busy, I'm working so hard," and rarely stop to ask: "Is this very thing I'm grinding away at actually right?" For you, working late in 2026, perhaps the bit of time you most need to set aside for yourself is to answer this question.
