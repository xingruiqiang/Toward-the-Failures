图灵：被“化学阉割”的计算机之父

Turing: The "Chemically Castrated" Father of the Computer

——他救了整个英国，却被自己的国家判了罪

He saved all of Britain, and his own country convicted him.

2026年，你手机里每一次对话式的AI，都在悄悄回答一个七十多年前由他提出的问题：“机器能思考吗？”

In 2026, every conversational AI on your phone is quietly answering a question he posed more than seventy years ago: "Can machines think?"

图灵测试到今天仍是人工智能最著名的思想实验。今天所有的大模型发布会，几乎都要变着法子重提这个问题。可提出这个问题的人，41岁那年死在自己的床上，床边放着一只咬了一口的苹果。而就在他死前两年，这个拯救过英国的人，被英国政府以“严重猥亵”罪定罪，被迫接受激素注射，身体被改变，名誉被剥光。

The Turing Test remains the most famous thought experiment in artificial intelligence to this day. Nearly every launch event for a large model today finds some new way to raise the question again. But the man who asked it died in his own bed at forty-one, with a half-eaten apple beside him. And just two years before he died, this man who had saved Britain was convicted by the British government of "gross indecency," forced to undergo hormone injections—his body altered, his reputation stripped bare.

他一生破解了敌人的密码，却没能破解自己时代对一个人的偏见。

All his life he cracked the codes of his enemies, yet he could never crack his own era's prejudice against one man.

一、那个总在算数的人

I. The Man Who Was Always Counting

1912年6月23日，艾伦·图灵出生在伦敦。从小，他就是那种“聪明得让老师为难”的孩子。骑自行车时链条经常掉，他不去修车，而是自己蹲下来琢磨——发现链条总是在踏到某个固定的圈数时滑落。于是他记住了那圈数，每次骑到临界点就猛地一停；后来干脆在脚踏旁装了个小计数器，到点就停，边骑边想别的问题。旁人看得目瞪口呆，以为他在耍杂技。

On June 23, 1912, Alan Turing was born in London. From childhood he was the kind of boy who was "too clever for his teachers' comfort." When his bicycle chain kept slipping, he did not take the bike to be repaired; he crouched down and puzzled it out himself—and found that the chain always slipped after a fixed number of pedal revolutions. So he memorized that number, and each time he reached the critical point he braked sharply; eventually he simply fitted a small counter beside the pedals, stopping the moment it hit the mark, thinking about other problems all the while he rode. Onlookers stared, dumbfounded, taking him for a circus performer.

1936年，24岁的图灵写出了一篇后来被奉为计算机科学开山之作的论文《论可计算数》。他在里面构想了一台假想的机器——后来被称为“图灵机”——用一条无限长的纸带、一个读写头，去定义“什么叫做可计算”。今天所有计算机的理论底座，都隔着将近一个世纪立在这篇论文之上。

In 1936, at the age of twenty-four, Turing wrote a paper that would later be revered as the founding work of computer science, "On Computable Numbers." In it he conceived an imaginary machine—later called the "Turing machine"—using an infinitely long paper tape and a read-write head to define what it means for something to be computable. The theoretical foundation of every computer today rests upon that paper, across a span of nearly a century.

二、二战：一个人顶得上几个师

II. World War II: One Man Worth Several Divisions

1939年，战争爆发。图灵被召入英国布莱切利园的秘密机构，任务是破解德军的“恩尼格玛”（Enigma）密码机。

In 1939 the war broke out. Turing was summoned to a secret establishment at Bletchley Park in England, tasked with breaking the German Enigma cipher machine.

恩尼格玛每天换密钥，组合方式是一个天文数字。图灵没有靠蛮力，而是把逻辑、数学和一点点机械装置结合起来，设计出“炸弹机”（Bombe），让机器去排除千万种可能，直到剩下唯一正确的那个。

Enigma changed its keys every day, and the number of possible combinations was astronomical. Rather than relying on brute force, Turing combined logic, mathematics, and a little mechanical machinery to design the "Bombe," letting the machine eliminate millions of possibilities until only the single correct one remained.

这场看不见的战争，历史学界普遍认为，把二战的进程缩短了以年计，救了无数生命。可因为它是绝密，图灵所有的功劳，在他活着的时候，都锁在保险柜里——外面的人，一个字都不知道。

Historians generally agree that this invisible war shortened the course of World War II by years and saved countless lives. But because it was top secret, all of Turing's achievements were locked in a safe during his lifetime—not a word of them reached the outside world.

三、1950年，他问了一个让世界不安的问题

III. In 1950, He Asked a Question That Unsettled the World

战后，图灵把兴趣转向了“机器能否思考”。1950年，他发表论文《计算机器与智能》，提出了那个著名的问题，并给出了操作性的回答：如果一个人在隔着一堵墙的文字对话里，分不出对面是人还是机器，那我们就该说这台机器会“思考”。这就是“图灵测试”。

After the war, Turing turned his interest to whether machines could think. In 1950 he published the paper "Computing Machinery and Intelligence," raising that famous question and giving it an operational answer: if a person conversing in text through a wall cannot tell whether the other side is a human or a machine, then we ought to say that the machine can think. This is the "Turing Test."

在那个年代，说“机器会思考”的人，会被人当成疯子。但图灵早就看得很远，他在别处说过一句话，大意是：“我认为，几十年后，机器智能超越人类是完全可以预期的。”

In that era, anyone who said machines could think was taken for a madman. But Turing had long seen far ahead. Elsewhere he remarked, in substance, "I believe that in a few decades' time it is entirely reasonable to expect machine intelligence to surpass that of humans."

四、1952：因为爱一个人，他被自己的国家定罪

IV. 1952: For Loving a Man, He Was Convicted by His Own Country

图灵是同性恋。在那个年代的英国，这不是个人隐私，而是“严重亵渎罪”。

Turing was gay. In the Britain of that era, this was not a private matter but a "grave crime."

1952年，他家中失窃，报警后，警方在查案过程中发现了他的同性关系。图灵没有撒谎，也没有辩解——他甚至用漂亮的花体字，写了长达五页的供述，坦然地承认了一切。他天真地以为自己在做正确的事，以为王室很快就会让同性恋合法化。

In 1952 his home was burgled. After he reported it, the police discovered his homosexual relationship while investigating the case. Turing did not lie, and did not make excuses—he even wrote a five-page statement in elegant calligraphy, calmly admitting everything. He naively believed he was doing the right thing, and that the Crown would soon legalize homosexuality.

法庭给了他两个选项：坐牢，或者接受雌激素注射“治疗”。他选了后者。

The court gave him two options: prison, or estrogen injections as "treatment." He chose the latter.

这种后来被称为“化学阉割”的注射持续了整整一年。它没有改变他的性取向，却让他身体浮肿、乳房发育、性功能受损——一个曾经热爱长跑、差点跑进国家队的运动员，被药物在身心上彻底击垮。

These injections, later known as "chemical castration," continued for a full year. They did not change his sexual orientation, but they left him bloated, caused his breasts to develop, and impaired his sexual function—an athlete who had loved long-distance running and nearly made the national team was utterly broken in body and mind by the drug.

五、1954，那只没人检测过的苹果

V. 1954: The Apple No One Ever Tested

1954年6月7日夜，图灵死在家中，死因是氰化物中毒。床边，放着一只被咬过几口的苹果。

On the night of June 7, 1954, Turing died at home of cyanide poisoning. Beside his bed lay an apple with a few bites taken out of it.

验尸官判定为自杀。但真相从来没有那么简单，也从来没有那么干净：

The coroner ruled it suicide. But the truth was never that simple, and never that clean:

- 他去世时，激素治疗其实已经结束了一年多，朋友们都说他近来心情不错，研究也顺利；

- By the time he died, the hormone treatment had in fact ended more than a year earlier, and his friends all said he had been in good spirits lately, with his research going well;

- 他留下了一份“待办研究清单”，正准备回大学过周末；

- He left behind a "to-do list" of research and was getting ready to return to the university for the weekend;

- 他没有留下任何遗书，反而写好了一封打算寄给皇家学会、接受会议邀请的信；

- He left no suicide note; instead he had written a letter, ready to post to the Royal Society, accepting an invitation to a conference;

- 他的书房里有个小实验室，里面就有氰化钾溶液——他喜欢在家做化学电镀实验，房间里弥漫着苦杏仁味。

- In his study there was a small laboratory containing a solution of potassium cyanide—he liked to do chemical electroplating experiments at home, and the room was filled with the smell of bitter almonds.

最关键的一点是：那只苹果，从来没有人拿去检测过它是否含有氰化物。验尸报告显示他体内有低浓度氰化物，但这究竟从何而来，档案里没有答案。

Most crucially: the apple was never tested for cyanide. The post-mortem report showed a low concentration of cyanide in his body, but where it came from, the files offer no answer.

因此，三种解释至今并存：官方的“自杀”、他母亲坚持的“实验意外吸入”、以及阴谋论者说的“被灭口”。我们唯一能确定的是：1954年6月7日的夜里，现代计算机的奠基人，41岁，死于氰化物。

So three explanations coexist to this day: the official "suicide," the "accidental inhalation during an experiment" that his mother insisted on, and the "silencing" claimed by conspiracy theorists. The one thing we can be certain of is this: on the night of June 7, 1954, the founder of the modern computer died of cyanide at the age of forty-one.

六、他死后，英国花了六十年才向他低头

VI. After His Death, It Took Britain Sixty Years to Bow to Him

图灵去世后，故事没有结束。

After Turing died, the story did not end.

2009年，在三万多人签名的请愿下，英国首相布朗在报纸上撰文，正式向图灵道歉，说“我们太无情了”。2013年，英国女王正式赦免了他的“罪名”。2017年，英国通过所谓“图灵法”，为历史上因同性恋被定罪的人平反。

In 2009, after a petition signed by more than thirty thousand people, the British Prime Minister Gordon Brown wrote in a newspaper formally apologizing to Turing, saying, "We were heartless." In 2013, Queen Elizabeth II formally pardoned his "crime." In 2017, Britain passed the so-called "Turing Law," clearing the records of those convicted of homosexuality in the past.

但也有更尖锐的声音：为图灵平反“错得离谱”——因为他本来就一点错都没有。当年和他一样因同性恋被定罪的英国人有七万五千人，其中一万五千人至今在世，他们中的许多人，可能永远不会被赦免。

But there were sharper voices too: pardoning Turing was "absurdly wrong," because he had never done anything wrong in the first place. Seventy-five thousand Britons were convicted for homosexuality in those years, as he was; fifteen thousand of them are still alive today, and many of them may never be pardoned.

而那只苹果，后来被无数人当作苹果公司标志的灵感来源。苹果公司从来没有承认过。它可能什么都不是，只是一个巧合——就像图灵本人，被后世过度浪漫化，又被他的时代粗暴地抹去。

As for that apple, countless people later took it as the inspiration for the Apple Inc. logo. Apple has never acknowledged it. It may be nothing at all, just a coincidence—like Turing himself, over-romanticized by later generations and crudely erased by his own era.

七、致2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

图灵的一生，是一个“贡献与待遇严重错位”的样本。

Turing's life is a case study in a grave mismatch between what one gives and what one receives.

他为人类争取到了战争里的几年时间，为今天的数字世界打下了地基，却因为一件完全属于个人私生活的事，被剥夺了工作、身体和尊严。而且这一切发生时，他还是一个对世界近乎天真的、没有防备的人。

He won humanity several years of war and laid the foundation for today's digital world, and yet for something that belonged entirely to his private life he was stripped of his work, his body, and his dignity. And all of this happened to a man who remained almost naive before the world, entirely unguarded.

对今天的我们，图灵留下两句话：

Turing left two sayings for us today:

第一句关于做事——他说，他只是被好奇心驱动，关心的是问题本身，而不是它有什么用。正是这种“不务正业”的纯粹，造就了计算机科学。

The first is about work—he said he was driven only by curiosity, caring about the problem itself rather than what use it might have. It was precisely this pure, "unproductive" curiosity that gave rise to computer science.

第二句关于时代——你做得对不对，和时代承不承认你，常常是两码事。图灵那个年代错的不是他，是那条法律，和那些沉默的人。所以当我们今天讨论AI、讨论隐私、讨论什么该被允许、什么该被禁止时，请记得：二十年后的常识，往往就是今天被误解的那些人的坚持。

The second is about the times—whether you are right and whether your times recognize it are often two different things. In Turing's day the wrong was not his; it was that law, and the people who stayed silent. So when we today discuss AI, discuss privacy, discuss what should be permitted and what should be forbidden, remember this: the common sense of twenty years from now is often the insistence of those who are misunderstood today.

他没能等到赦免，我们替他等到了。但我们更应该做的，是让下一个图灵，不必再等。

He did not live to see a pardon; we saw it on his behalf. But what we ought to do, above all, is to make sure the next Turing does not have to wait.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

8. “图灵咬毒苹果自杀”——证据不足。床边确有咬过的苹果，但该苹果从未被检测是否含氰化物（法医未做此检测）。在他去世前一年多激素治疗已结束，且他近期精神状态良好、留下待办研究清单，并正准备寄出接受会议邀请的回信，因此母亲坚持的“实验意外吸入”解释长期存在。三种解释（自杀/意外/阴谋）学界与民间并存，文中如实并列，未采单一结论。

8. "Turing bit a poisoned apple and killed himself"—the evidence is insufficient. There was indeed a bitten apple by his bed, but that apple was never tested for cyanide (the forensic examiner did not perform such a test). More than a year before his death the hormone treatment had ended, and he had recently been in good mental health, had left a to-do list of research, and was about to post a reply accepting an invitation to a conference; hence the explanation his mother insisted on—"accidental inhalation during an experiment"—has long persisted. The three explanations (suicide / accident / conspiracy) coexist in academic and popular accounts; this article sets them side by side as they are, without adopting any single conclusion.

9. “苹果公司的标志源自图灵的苹果”——无据可查，苹果公司从未承认。CNN等媒体已澄清为“美丽的误会”。

9. "The Apple logo derives from Turing's apple"—unverifiable, and Apple has never acknowledged it. Media outlets such as CNN have clarified it as a "beautiful misconception."

10. “他被定罪是因为同性恋本身触犯法律”——需注明历史背景：当时英国法律将同性性行为定为刑事罪，图灵是依当时的法律被定罪判刑的，这也是2009年英国政府正式道歉、2013年予以赦免的原因。

10. "He was convicted because homosexuality itself violated the law"—the historical context must be noted: British law at the time criminalized homosexual acts, and Turing was convicted and sentenced under the law as it then stood, which is why the British government formally apologized in 2009 and granted a pardon in 2013.

11. 后世常称图灵“吃苹果自杀后只活了41岁”，其中“吃苹果致死”不能作为已证事实；“41岁去世”无误。

11. Later generations often say that Turing "lived only to forty-one after eating the apple and killing himself"; the "died from eating the apple" part cannot be treated as proven fact; "died at forty-one" is correct.
