纳兰性德：宰相家里的公子，却写尽了人间的愁

Nalan Xingde: A Prime Minister's Son Who Wrote All the Sorrows of the World

——“人生若只如初见，何事秋风悲画扇。”

— "If only life were as it was when we first met, why should the autumn wind grieve over a painted fan?"

2026年，如果你在社交媒体上搜“古风金句”或者“最伤感的词”，《木兰花·拟古决绝词》里的那两句几乎必然会跳出来。写下它们的人，是一个生在顶级权贵之家、二十出头就中了进士、皇帝身边的贴身侍卫。

In 2026, if you search social media for "classical-style quotes" or "the saddest ci poems," the two lines from Mu Lan Hua (Magnolia Flower): A Song of Final Parting in the Ancient Style are almost certain to surface. The man who wrote them was born into a family of the highest nobility, passed the imperial examination in his early twenties, and served as a personal guard at the emperor's side.

按世俗标准，他什么都赢了。他是那个时代最有资格“幸福”的人。

By worldly standards, he had won at everything. He was the person of his age most entitled to be called "happy."

可他只活到31岁。他一生写下的，几乎全是离别、怀念、孤独和遗憾。

Yet he lived only to the age of thirty-one. Almost everything he wrote over his lifetime was of parting, remembrance, solitude, and regret.

他叫纳兰性德。

His name was Nalan Xingde.

一、含着金钥匙出生的少年

I. A Boy Born with a Silver Spoon

1655年1月19日，纳兰性德出生在北京。他原名成德，字容若。

On January 19, 1655, Nalan Xingde was born in Beijing. His original given name was Chengde, and his courtesy name was Rongruo.

他的父亲，是纳兰明珠——康熙朝权倾一时的大臣，官至大学士，是当时最有权势的人之一。他的家族是满洲正黄旗，出身显赫。用今天的话说，他既是“官二代”，也是“学二代”。

His father was Mingzhu—a minister of unmatched influence in the reign of the Kangxi Emperor, who rose to the post of Grand Secretary and was one of the most powerful men of his time. His family belonged to the Plain Yellow Banner of the Manchu Eight Banners, of illustrious birth. In today's terms, he was both a "second-generation official" and a "second-generation scholar."

但纳兰性德跟一般纨绔子弟不一样。他读书极多，汉学功底极深。他熟读经史，也工书法，擅骑射——这在满洲贵族子弟里，是标准配置，却被他做到了最好。

But Nalan Xingde was not like the usual pampered sons of the rich. He read enormously and had a deep grounding in Chinese scholarship. He knew the classics and histories thoroughly, was skilled at calligraphy, and excelled at horseback archery—all standard accomplishments for a young Manchu nobleman, yet he carried them to their highest pitch.

1676年，他考中进士，名次很高。康熙皇帝很欣赏他，把他留在身边，授三等侍卫，后来升到一等侍卫。他的差事，是跟着皇帝出巡、值夜、护卫。 这在当时是极大的恩宠，是许多人挤破头也求不到的出路。

In 1676 he passed the imperial examination with a high rank. The Kangxi Emperor thought highly of him and kept him close, appointing him a third-class imperial guard, later promoting him to first-class. His duties were to accompany the emperor on tours, stand night watch, and serve as a bodyguard. This was an extraordinary mark of favor at the time—a path many would have fought tooth and nail to obtain.

二、卢氏

II. Lady Lu

1674年，纳兰性德娶了卢氏。她是两广总督卢兴祖的女儿，知书达理，与他感情极好。

In 1674, Nalan Xingde married Lady Lu. She was the daughter of Lu Xingzu, Governor-General of Guangdong and Guangxi, learned and sensible, and deeply devoted to him.

那几年，是纳兰性德一生中最明亮的时光。夫妻二人吟诗、论画、赏春、看花。他后来写下大量追忆这段日子的词，字字都是温柔。

Those years were the brightest of Nalan Xingde's life. Husband and wife composed poems, discussed paintings, admired the spring, and looked at flowers together. He later wrote a great many ci poems recalling those days, every word of them tender.

但这段幸福只持续了三年。1677年，卢氏在生下一个孩子后病逝，年仅二十出头（一说是难产，一说为产后受寒）。那一年，纳兰性德二十二岁。

But this happiness lasted only three years. In 1677, Lady Lu died of illness after giving birth to a child, still only in her early twenties (one account says from a difficult labor, another from taking cold after childbirth). That year, Nalan Xingde was twenty-two.

他从此陷进了一个再也走不出来的洞。他写下了中国文学史上最令人心碎的一批悼亡词：

From then on he sank into a pit from which he could never climb out. He wrote some of the most heartbreaking elegies for a deceased wife in the history of Chinese literature:

谁念西风独自凉，萧萧黄叶闭疏窗，沉思往事立残阳。

Who pities the west wind, chilling me alone? / Rustling yellow leaves, the latticed window closed; / Lost in thought of things past, I stand in the setting sun.

被酒莫惊春睡重，赌书消得泼茶香，当时只道是寻常。

Warmed by wine, do not wake my heavy spring sleep; / Betting on lines, we spilled the fragrance of tea— / Back then I only thought it all ordinary.

最后那五个字——“当时只道是寻常”——被无数后来的读者奉为写尽“失去”的名句。它不是控诉，不是呐喊，而是一种迟到的、无用的醒悟：原来那些平淡的日子，就是一生中最好的日子。

Those last words—"Back then I only thought it all ordinary"—have been enshrined by countless later readers as the lines that say all there is to say about loss. It is not an accusation, not a shout, but a belated, useless awakening: those plain, uneventful days were the best days of a whole life.

三、他其实并不想做那个侍卫

III. In Truth, He Never Wanted to Be That Guard

很多人以为，纳兰性德的人生是“顺遂的贵公子”。这是一个误解。

Many people assume that Nalan Xingde's life was that of a "fortunate young nobleman." This is a misunderstanding.

他真正的痛苦，在于他的位置和他的内心，是完全冲突的。他喜欢的是汉族文人的那种生活——吟诗、读书、交朋友、自由自在。可他偏偏要穿着盔甲，站在皇帝身边，一年里有大量时间跟着御驾出塞、南巡、围猎。

His real suffering lay in the fact that his position and his inner self were in total conflict. What he loved was the life of a Han Chinese scholar—chanting poems, reading books, making friends, being free and at ease. Yet he was obliged to wear armor and stand at the emperor's side, spending much of each year following the imperial carriage beyond the passes, on southern tours, and on hunting expeditions.

他写过这样的句子：“我是人间惆怅客，知君何事泪纵横。”——他把自己定位成一个“人间的过客”，而不是一个“御前的红人”。

He once wrote: "I am but a melancholy guest in this world; I know why your tears run in streams."—He cast himself as a "passerby in the world," not a "favorite before the throne."

所以，他真正放松的时候，是跟顾贞观、严绳孙、姜宸英这些汉族文人在一起的时候。他们在他家里结社、唱和。他用自己的身份，替这些汉族才子挡风遮雨，也从他们那里，找到了自己在御前找不到的东西。

So the times when he truly relaxed were those spent with Han Chinese literati such as Gu Zhenguan, Yan Shengsun, and Jiang Chenying. They formed a literary society and exchanged poems at his home. He used his standing to shelter these Han talents from wind and rain, and from them he found what he could never find before the throne.

四、他为一个陌生人奔走

IV. He Went to Great Lengths for a Stranger

纳兰性德最让人意外的，不只是他的词，还有他做过的事。

What is most surprising about Nalan Xingde is not only his poetry but also the things he did.

他的好友顾贞观，有一位知己叫吴兆骞——江南才子，因为科场案被流放到宁古塔（今黑龙江一带），苦寒之地，一待就是二十多年。顾贞观为救他四处奔走，写下了两首著名的《金缕曲》，字字血泪。

His good friend Gu Zhenguan had a bosom friend named Wu Zhaoqian—a talented man of Jiangnan who, because of an examination-hall scandal, was exiled to Ningguta (in present-day Heilongjiang), a bitterly cold land where he remained for more than twenty years. Gu Zhenguan moved heaven and earth to save him, writing two famous poems to the tune Jin Lü Qu (Golden Thread Song), every word of them tears and blood.

纳兰性德读了这两首词，大为震动。他答应顾贞观：这事我管到底，十年之内，一定把他救回来。 后来，他动用家族的力量，经过多方周旋，终于让吴兆骞得以从宁古塔生还。

When Nalan Xingde read these two poems, he was deeply shaken. He promised Gu Zhenguan: I will see this through to the end; within ten years I will certainly bring him back. In time he brought the power of his family to bear and, after much maneuvering, finally obtained Wu Zhaoqian's release alive from Ningguta.

这件事和一个“满洲贵公子”该有的生活方式完全不符。他本可以只写词、只当差、只享受荣华。但他选择了替一个被朝廷流放的汉族文人，押上自己的信誉和家族的资源。

This affair was utterly at odds with the way of life expected of a "Manchu young nobleman." He could have simply written his poems, done his duty, and enjoyed his wealth and honor. Instead he chose to stake his own reputation and his family's resources for a Han Chinese literatus exiled by the court.

所谓“仗义”，在那个年代是要冒政治风险的。 后人记住他的词，其实也该记住这份义气。

What is called "standing up for justice" meant taking real political risk in that era. Later generations remember his poetry; they ought also to remember this sense of honor.

五、“侧帽”与“饮水”

V. "The Tilted Cap" and "Drinking Water"

纳兰性德的两部词集，名字都很讲究。

Nalan Xingde's two collections of ci poems both have carefully chosen names.

《侧帽集》取自北朝独孤信的典故——他打猎归来，帽子被风吹歪了，路人觉得这样很潇洒，纷纷模仿。“侧帽”这个词，透着一种满不在乎的风流。

Ce Mao Ji (The Tilted Cap) takes its name from an anecdote about Dugu Xin of the Northern Dynasties—returning from a hunt, his cap was blown askew by the wind, and passersby, finding this dashing, rushed to imitate him. The phrase "tilted cap" breathes an air of carefree elegance.

后来他把词集改名为《饮水词》。“饮水”二字出自佛家语：“如人饮水，冷暖自知。”意思是，一杯水到底是凉是热，只有喝的人自己知道。

Later he renamed the collection Yin Shui Ci (Drinking Water Lyrics). The words "drinking water" come from a Buddhist saying: "As when one drinks water, only the drinker knows whether it is cold or warm." That is, whether a cup of water is cold or hot, only the one who drinks it can know.

这两个书名，几乎就是他的一生：表面是“侧帽”——风光、潇洒、人人羡慕；内里是“饮水”——孤独、冷暖、无人可诉。

These two titles are almost his whole life: on the surface "The Tilted Cap"—glamorous, dashing, envied by all; within, "Drinking Water"—lonely, cold and warm, with no one to tell.

他写下的大多是短调小令，篇幅不长，但胜在真挚。他不用生僻典故堆砌，也不故意炫耀学问，而是把最普通的话——“当时只道是寻常”“人生若只如初见”——放进最恰当的位置。这就是为什么三百多年后，他的词仍然能在朋友圈里被一遍遍转发：他写的是所有人的心事。

Most of what he wrote were short tunes and brief lyrics, not long in form but all the more winning for their sincerity. He did not pile up obscure allusions or show off his learning; instead he placed the plainest phrases—"Back then I only thought it all ordinary," "If only life were as it was when we first met"—in exactly the right places. This is why, more than three hundred years later, his poems are still forwarded again and again on social media: he wrote the hearts of everyone.

六、一生只写了31年

VI. A Life That Lasted Only Thirty-One Years

1685年，纳兰性德病倒了。

In 1685, Nalan Xingde fell ill.

那一年他才三十一岁。据记载，他得的是“寒疾”——一场来势凶猛的病。他在病中与朋友饮酒，之后病情恶化。1685年7月1日（康熙二十四年五月三十日），他去世了，年仅31岁。

He was only thirty-one that year. According to records, he contracted a "cold illness"—a disease that came on with great violence. While ill he drank with friends, and afterward his condition worsened. On July 1, 1685 (the thirtieth day of the fifth month of the twenty-fourth year of Kangxi), he died, only thirty-one years old.

他的死，跟他妻子的忌日是同一天。 这个巧合，后来被无数人当作宿命的注脚。

His death fell on the same day as his wife's death anniversary. This coincidence was later taken by countless people as a footnote of fate.

他生前就出版了词集，名为《侧帽集》，后改《饮水词》。当时已经有“家家争唱饮水词”的说法——一个活着就成名、就被传唱的满族词人，这在清代是非常罕见的。

In his lifetime his collection was already published, titled Ce Mao Ji (The Tilted Cap), later renamed Yin Shui Ci (Drinking Water Lyrics). By then there was already a saying that "every household vies to sing Drinking Water Lyrics"—a Manchu ci poet who became famous and was sung while still alive, which was extremely rare in the Qing dynasty.

五、写给2026年的我们

V. To Us in 2026

纳兰性德的故事，最容易被误读成“一个富贵闲人在无病呻吟”。

Nalan Xingde's story is most easily misread as "an idle rich man moaning about nothing."

但如果仔细读他的词，会发现它一点也不“闲”。他写的愁，是一种结构性的愁：他所在的位置，和他想成为的人，永远无法重合。 他有最好的出身、最好的差事、最好的朋友圈，可他每一天都在扮演一个不是自己的角色。

But read his poems carefully and you will find there is nothing idle about them. The sorrow he wrote was a structural sorrow: the position he occupied and the person he wished to become could never coincide. He had the finest birth, the finest post, the finest circle of friends—yet every day he played a role that was not himself.

这其实是很多现代人的处境。我们被家庭、被学历、被行业、被期待推到一个位置上，然后我们用很多年去适应，却发现那个位置从来不属于自己。

This is in fact the situation of many people today. We are pushed into a position by family, by education, by profession, by others' expectations, and then we spend many years adapting to it, only to find that it never belonged to us.

纳兰性德的回答，是把这种撕裂写下来。他不假装满意，也不假装痛苦。他只是老老实实地承认：我在这个世界上，是个“惆怅客”。

Nalan Xingde's answer was to write that rift down. He did not pretend to be content, nor pretend to be in pain. He simply and honestly admitted: in this world, I am a "melancholy guest."

在一个所有人都劝你“知足”的世界里，敢于承认自己不快乐，本身就需要巨大的诚实。

In a world where everyone urges you to "be content," daring to admit that you are unhappy takes enormous honesty in itself.

31年，几百首词，他就给中国留下了一个关于“身在福中而不自知”的、最温柔的忏悔。

Thirty-one years, several hundred ci poems—and he left China the gentlest of confessions about "not knowing one is in the midst of blessings."

王国维在《人间词话》里说，纳兰性德“以自然之眼观物，以自然之舌言情”，自他之后，“北宋以来，一人而已”。这个评价极高，却也点到了要害：他的好，不在技巧，而在真。 他没有把自己活成一个“合格的贵公子”，他把自己活成了一个会疼的人。而恰恰是这一点，让三百年后的我们，仍然觉得他是同类。他留下的不是一部“贵族词集”，而是一份人人都能对号入座的、关于失去的记录。

Wang Guowei wrote in Ren Jian Ci Hua (Poetic Remarks in the Human World) that Nalan Xingde "regards things with the eye of nature and expresses feeling with the tongue of nature," and that since him, "in all the years since the Northern Song, there has been only this one man." This is the highest of praise, and it hits the mark: his excellence lies not in technique but in truth. He did not live his life as a "qualified young nobleman"; he lived it as a man who could feel pain. And it is precisely this that makes us, three hundred years later, still feel he is one of us. What he left behind is not a "nobleman's collection of ci poems" but a record of loss in which everyone can find themselves.

他去世的时候，他的父亲纳兰明珠还在，权位仍在。可那个曾经显赫到极点的家族，后来也因为党争而败落。纳兰性德没有赶上家族的衰落，他先走了。有人说这是不幸，也有人说这是另一种幸运。但无论如何，一个只活过31年的人，留下了几百首被传唱三百多年的词——这笔账，怎么算都不亏。

When he died, his father Mingzhu was still alive, still holding power. But that family, once so dazzlingly prominent, later fell into decline amid factional strife. Nalan Xingde did not live to see the family's fall; he went first. Some call this misfortune, others a different kind of fortune. But in any case, a man who lived only thirty-one years left several hundred ci poems that have been sung for more than three centuries—by any reckoning, that is no loss.

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：纳兰性德（原名成德，字容若），1655年1月19日（顺治十一年十二月十二日）生于北京，1685年7月1日（康熙二十四年五月三十日）卒，年31岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Nalan Xingde (original given name Chengde, courtesy name Rongruo), born in Beijing on January 19, 1655 (the twelfth day of the twelfth month of the eleventh year of Shunzhi), died July 1, 1685 (the thirtieth day of the fifth month of the twenty-fourth year of Kangxi), aged thirty-one. Considered confirmed historical fact.

2. 家世：父纳兰明珠为康熙朝大学士、权臣；属满洲正黄旗。为确证史实。

2. Family background: his father Mingzhu was a Grand Secretary and powerful minister under Kangxi; the family belonged to the Plain Yellow Banner of the Manchu. Considered confirmed historical fact.

3. 科举与任职：1676年（康熙十五年）中进士，授三等侍卫，后升至一等侍卫，常随康熙出巡。为确证史实。

3. Examination and office: he passed the imperial examination in 1676 (the fifteenth year of Kangxi); appointed third-class imperial guard, later promoted to first-class; often accompanied Kangxi on tours. Considered confirmed historical fact.

4. 卢氏：1674年成婚，1677年病逝。辨析：关于其死因，有“难产”与“产后受寒”两种记载，具体情形史料语焉不详，本文并列呈现，不武断。其“同年同月同日去世”的说法，指的是纳兰性德去世之日与其妻忌日为同一天（五月三十日），两人生卒年份不同，切勿混淆。

4. Lady Lu: married in 1674, died of illness in 1677. Analysis: as to the cause of death, there are two records, "difficult labor" and "taking cold after childbirth"; the historical sources are vague on the specifics, and this book presents both side by side without dogmatism. The claim that they "died on the same year, month, and day" means that the day of Nalan Xingde's death coincided with his wife's death anniversary (the thirtieth day of the fifth month); the two died in different years, and the two must not be confused.

5. 词集：《侧帽集》《饮水词》为其主要词集，生前即有流传。为确证史实。

5. Poetry collections: Ce Mao Ji (The Tilted Cap) and Yin Shui Ci (Drinking Water Lyrics) are his principal collections, both in circulation during his lifetime. Considered confirmed historical fact.

6. “寒疾”：史载其病为“寒疾”，具体病名今已难确考，不应臆断为某种现代医学病名。

6. "Cold illness": the histories record his illness as a "cold illness"; the specific disease can no longer be determined today and should not be arbitrarily identified with any modern medical term.

7. “家家争唱饮水词”：此语出自同时代人（如顾贞观等）的记述，形容其词在当时流传之广，属当时人的评价，可采信其大意。

7. "Every household vies to sing Drinking Water Lyrics": this phrase comes from accounts by his contemporaries (Gu Zhenguan and others); it describes how widely his poems circulated at the time. It is a contemporary's evaluation, and its general sense may be accepted.
