阮籍：驾车走到没路的地方，就大哭一场回来

Ruan Ji: Driving Until the Road Runs Out, Then Weeping All the Way Home

——“时无英雄，使竖子成名。”

—"In an age without heroes, the small men have made their names."

← 返回主页 Home第四辑　用痛苦喂养的灵魂80 / 100约 3050 字 · 3050 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，很多人有过这样的时刻：加完班，一个人开车在城市里漫无目的地走，走到高架桥的尽头、走到河边、走到没有路的地方，然后停下来，什么也不想做，只想哭一会儿。

In 2026, many people have had moments like this: after working overtime, driving alone through the city with no destination, going on until the elevated road ends, until the river, until there is no road left, and then stopping, wanting to do nothing, only to cry for a while.

一千七百多年前，有一个人每天都在做这件事。他驾着车，不选路，随便走。走到车再也走不动的地方，他就放声大哭，然后掉头回家。

More than seventeen hundred years ago, a man did this very thing every day. He drove his carriage, choosing no road, going wherever it went. When the carriage could go no farther, he wept aloud, then turned around and went home.

他哭的不是某一件具体的事。他哭的是——这个世界，没有路可走了。

What he wept over was no particular thing. He wept because—there was no road left in this world.

他叫阮籍。

His name was Ruan Ji.

一、三岁丧父的才子

I. A Talented Boy Who Lost His Father at Three

阮籍，字嗣宗，210年生于陈留尉氏（今河南开封一带）。他的父亲阮瑀，是“建安七子”之一，在曹操手下做过官。但阮瑀在阮籍三岁时就去世了，阮籍是由母亲一手带大的。

Ruan Ji, courtesy name Sizong, was born in 210 in Weishi, Chenliu (in present-day Kaifeng, Henan). His father, Ruan Yu, was one of the Seven Masters of the Jian'an Era and had served under Cao Cao. But Ruan Yu died when Ruan Ji was three, and Ruan Ji was raised by his mother alone.

孤儿寡母的处境，塑造了阮籍的性格：敏感、内省、早熟。史书上说他“志气宏放，傲然独得，任性不羁，而喜怒不形于色”——心里翻江倒海，脸上什么也看不出来。

The situation of widow and orphan shaped Ruan Ji's character: sensitive, inward-looking, precocious. The histories describe him as "of grand and expansive spirit, proudly self-contained, unrestrained by temperament, and never showing joy or anger on his face"—a storm inside, nothing on the surface.

少年时代的阮籍，其实是个有抱负的人。他熟读儒家经典，也练过武，梦想着建功立业。他曾登上广武山，看着楚汉相争的古战场，长叹一声：

In his youth Ruan Ji was in fact a man of ambition. He read the Confucian classics thoroughly and also trained in arms, dreaming of winning merit and making a career. Once he climbed Mount Guangwu, looked out over the ancient battlefield where Chu and Han had contended, and gave a long sigh:

“时无英雄，使竖子成名。”

"In an age without heroes, the small men have made their names."

意思是：这个时代没有真正的英雄，才让那些小人得了势。这句话里有一种极其骄傲的失望——他不是不想做事，他是觉得，能配得上他做事的人，根本不存在。

The meaning: this age has no true heroes, and so those petty men have come to power. There is an extraordinarily proud disappointment in these words—it was not that he had no wish to act, but that he felt no one worthy to act alongside him existed at all.

二、夹缝里的二十多年

II. Twenty-Plus Years Caught in the Middle

阮籍真正的人生难题，是“站队”。

Ruan Ji's real life problem was "taking sides."

他生活在曹魏与司马氏斗争最激烈的年代。他本人在政治上倾向于曹魏，但司马氏一天天得势。高平陵之变后，司马氏大杀异己，牵连者极多。

He lived in the years of the fiercest struggle between Cao Wei and the Sima clan. Politically he leaned toward Cao Wei, but the Sima clan grew stronger by the day. After the Incident at the Gaoping Tombs, the Sima clan killed off their opponents on a grand scale, implicating very many people.

阮籍的应对方式是：不表态。

Ruan Ji's way of coping was: not to declare a position.

他先后在司马懿、司马师、司马昭手下做过“从事中郎”这样的官。这个位置很奇怪——他是司马家的属官，却不参与任何实质的政治活动。他像一片漂在水面上的叶子，看起来在体制里，实际上从没真正下水。

He served in turn under Sima Yi, Sima Shi, and Sima Zhao in a post called attendant gentleman (congshi zhonglang). The position was odd—he was an officer of the Sima household, yet took part in no substantive political activity. He was like a leaf floating on the surface of the water, seemingly inside the system, but never truly getting wet.

司马昭的心腹钟会，多次来找他聊天，想套他的话，好抓住把柄。阮籍的办法是：喝酒。 钟会一来，他就喝；喝到醉得说不出话，钟会什么也问不出来。

Zhong Hui, Sima Zhao's confidant, came many times to chat with him, hoping to draw him out and catch him in something. Ruan Ji's method was: drink. Whenever Zhong Hui came, he drank; he drank until he was too drunk to speak, and Zhong Hui could get nothing out of him.

司马昭本人也试探过他的政见，他“发言玄远，口不臧否人物”——说些跟现实毫不沾边的话，从不评论具体的人。司马昭最后只能说：这个人“至慎”（谨慎到了极点）。

Sima Zhao himself also sounded out his political views, and he "spoke in remote and abstract terms, never passing judgment on people"—saying things that touched reality not at all, never commenting on any particular person. In the end Sima Zhao could only say that the man was "extremely cautious" (cautious to the utmost).

三、大醉六十天

III. Sixty Days Dead Drunk

最能说明阮籍处境的一件事，是“拒婚”。

The episode that best illustrates Ruan Ji's predicament is his "refusal of marriage."

司马昭想跟阮籍联姻——把自己的女儿（一说为司马昭之女）嫁给阮籍的儿子。这在当时是天大的恩宠，答应下来，就等于彻底绑上了司马家的船。

Sima Zhao wanted a marriage alliance with Ruan Ji—to marry his own daughter (some say a daughter of Sima Zhao) to Ruan Ji's son. At the time this was an honor of the highest order; to agree would have meant tying himself completely to the Sima family's boat.

阮籍不想答应。但他也不敢直接拒绝。于是，他做了一个极其荒诞的决定：连醉六十天。

Ruan Ji did not want to agree. But he did not dare refuse outright either. So he made an utterly absurd decision: to stay drunk for sixty days straight.

媒人来了很多次，每次来，阮籍都醉得不省人事，根本没法开口谈正事。最后，司马昭也就作罢了。

The matchmaker came many times, and each time Ruan Ji was dead drunk, quite unable to discuss serious business. In the end Sima Zhao let the matter drop.

六十天。一个人要用连续两个月的烂醉，才能既不点头、也不得罪，把一桩婚事拖过去。 这里面有多少恐惧、多少屈辱，今天的我们很难完全体会。

Sixty days. To neither nod nor offend, to drag a marriage proposal to death, a man had to spend two months in a continuous stupor. How much fear, how much humiliation there was in this, we today can hardly fully comprehend.

四、青白眼

IV. Black Eyes and White Eyes

阮籍有一双“会说话的”眼睛。

Ruan Ji had a pair of eyes that "could speak."

他对于喜欢的人，用“青眼”（正眼相看，黑眼珠对着人）；对于讨厌的人，用“白眼”（翻白眼看人）。

For people he liked, he used the "dark eye" (looking straight at them, the dark pupil turned toward them); for people he disliked, he used the "white eye" (rolling his eyes so that only the whites showed).

最有名的一次，是在他母亲去世的时候。嵇康的哥哥嵇喜来吊唁——嵇喜是个在司马氏体制里做事的人，阮籍看不上他，就以白眼相对，这让嵇喜很不高兴。后来嵇康带着酒和琴来了，阮籍立刻换成青眼。

The most famous instance came at the time of his mother's death. Ji Kang's elder brother Ji Xi came to offer condolences—Ji Xi was a man who worked within the Sima system, and Ruan Ji held him in contempt, so he met him with the white eye, which greatly displeased Ji Xi. Later, when Ji Kang came with wine and a zither, Ruan Ji at once switched to the dark eye.

一个“白眼”，就是一次沉默的表态。 阮籍在司马氏的体制里活了半辈子，他没有一次明着反对，但他用眼睛、用酒、用沉默，把立场表达得清清楚楚。

One "white eye" was a silent declaration of position. Ruan Ji lived half his life inside the Sima system, and never once opposed it openly, yet with his eyes, his wine, and his silence he made his stance perfectly clear.

五、母亲的葬礼上，他吃了肉

V. At His Mother's Funeral, He Ate Meat

阮籍最惊世骇俗的行为之一，发生在他母亲的葬礼上。

One of Ruan Ji's most shocking acts took place at his mother's funeral.

按照礼法，丧期要禁酒禁肉，要哭得死去活来。可阮籍在母亲去世时，照样喝酒吃肉，甚至在司马昭的宴席上也毫不避讳。

By ritual law, during mourning one was to abstain from wine and meat and to weep oneself half to death. But when his mother died, Ruan Ji went on drinking wine and eating meat as usual, and made no attempt to hide it even at Sima Zhao's banquets.

很多人骂他不孝。但实际上——母亲下葬那天，阮籍“举声一号，吐血数升”，身体差点就垮了。他后来“毁瘠骨立”，瘦得不成人形。

Many people called him unfilial. But in fact—on the day of his mother's burial, Ruan Ji "gave a single great cry and spat up several pints of blood," and nearly collapsed. Afterwards he "wasted away to skin and bone," so thin he scarcely looked human.

他拒绝的是礼法的形式，付出的却是最真实的悲痛。 阮籍自己说过一句很有名的话：“礼岂为我辈设也？”（礼法难道是给我们这种人定的吗？）

What he rejected was the form of ritual; what he gave was the truest grief. Ruan Ji himself once said a famous thing: "Were the rites made for the likes of us?" (Were ritual laws made for people like us?)

他真正反对的，是司马氏把“孝”当成政治工具的那套虚伪。当“名教”变成了杀人的刀，一个真性情的人，只能把形式砸掉，保住里面那点真心。

What he truly opposed was the hypocrisy of the Sima clan's making "filial piety" into a political tool. When the "doctrine of names" (moral teaching) became a knife for killing, a man of true feeling had no choice but to smash the form in order to preserve the little sincerity inside it.

六、穷途之哭

VI. Weeping at the End of the Road

所以，就有了那个最著名的画面。

And so came that most famous image.

阮籍常常一个人驾车出门，不选方向，让车随便走。走到路断了、走不了了，他就“恸哭而返”。

Ruan Ji often drove out alone, choosing no direction, letting the carriage go where it would. When the road ended and it could go no farther, he would "weep bitterly and turn back."

这不是矫情，这是一个清醒的人，对现实的最后反应。 他看得很清楚：司马氏篡位已成定局，曹魏将亡；他既不能反对，也不愿投靠；他读过那么多书，有那么大的抱负，可他什么都做不了。

This was not affectation; it was the final response of a lucid man to reality. He saw clearly: the Sima usurpation was now settled, Cao Wei would fall; he could neither oppose it nor bring himself to join it; he had read so many books, had such great ambitions, and could do nothing at all.

所有的路都堵死了。还能怎么办？只能痛哭一场，然后掉头回去，继续喝他的酒。

Every road was blocked. What else could he do? Only weep his fill, turn around, and go on drinking his wine.

263年，阮籍去世，年五十三四岁。就在他去世前一两个月，司马昭晋封为晋公，需要公卿大臣上“劝进表”。这篇表，是阮籍被指派执笔的。据说他喝得大醉，被人催稿，就趴在桌上勉强写完了。

In 263 Ruan Ji died, aged fifty-three or fifty-four. Just a month or two before his death, Sima Zhao was enfeoffed as Duke of Jin, and the high ministers and officials were required to submit a "memorial urging him to ascend." This memorial was assigned to Ruan Ji to write. It is said that he was dead drunk, and being pressed for the draft, he sprawled over his desk and barely finished it.

他一生最想远离的那件事，最后落到了他的笔上。 写完不久，他就死了。

The one thing he had most wanted to keep far from all his life ended up falling to his pen. Not long after he finished it, he died.

七、他留下了什么

VII. What He Left Behind

阮籍最重要的作品，是《咏怀诗》八十二首。

Ruan Ji's most important work is the eighty-two poems of "Songs of My Heart" (Yonghuai shi).

这些诗有一个共同的特点：不敢直说。 里面全是隐喻、比兴、典故。他写飞鸟，写草木，写夜色，写古事，但每一句背后，都藏着对时局的忧惧。

The poems share one trait: they dare not speak plainly. They are full of metaphor, allusion, and indirection. He writes of birds in flight, of grass and trees, of the night, of ancient events, but behind every line is hidden a dread of the current state of things.

夜中不能寐，起坐弹鸣琴。

In the night I cannot sleep; / I rise and sit, and play the singing zither.

薄帷鉴明月，清风吹我襟。

The thin curtain mirrors the bright moon; / the clear wind blows through my lapels.

这四句写的是一个人在深夜里睡不着，起来弹琴。可你越读，越觉得那不是失眠，那是一个人被巨大的无力和恐惧包围，只能靠弹琴撑到天亮。

These four lines describe a man unable to sleep late at night, rising to play the zither. But the more you read them, the more you feel it is not insomnia—it is a man surrounded by immense helplessness and fear, holding himself up with the zither until daybreak.

他还写过《大人先生传》，用极其激烈的语言批判虚伪的礼法。阮籍在中国文学史上是第一个把“组诗”写成一个庞大整体的人，此后左思、陶渊明、陈子昂、李白，都从他的《咏怀》里接过火。

He also wrote "Biography of Master Great Man," attacking hypocritical ritual law in the most vehement language. In the history of Chinese literature Ruan Ji was the first to write a "poem cycle" as one vast whole; afterwards Zuo Si, Tao Yuanming, Chen Zi'ang, and Li Bai all took up the flame from his "Songs of My Heart."

八、写给2026年的我们

VIII. To Us in 2026

阮籍和嵇康，是那对最让人感慨的朋友。嵇康选择了硬扛，结果是死；阮籍选择了装醉，结果是活。

Ruan Ji and Ji Kang were the pair of friends who move people most. Ji Kang chose to stand firm, and the result was death; Ruan Ji chose to feign drunkenness, and the result was life.

两条路都很苦。一条苦在痛快地死，一条苦在屈辱地活。

Both roads were bitter. One was bitter in dying cleanly; the other, in living in humiliation.

后人常常抬高嵇康、贬低阮籍，说他“太软弱”。但如果把自己放进那个年代，我们大概会理解：在一个说错一句话就满门抄斩的环境里，能靠六十天的醉、一双翻白的眼、一场没路的痛哭，把心里的东西守住，已经需要极大的勇气。

Later generations often exalt Ji Kang and belittle Ruan Ji, saying he was "too weak." But if we put ourselves into that era, we would probably understand: in an environment where one wrong sentence could bring the extermination of an entire household, to guard what was in one's heart by means of sixty days of drunkenness, a pair of rolled-up eyes, and a road-ending fit of weeping already required enormous courage.

今天，我们不必经历那种生死的抉择。但阮籍提醒我们：有时候，一个人“什么都没做”，并不等于他什么都没承受。 那些沉默、那些酒、那些深夜的琴声，都是一个人在极限处境里的抵抗。

Today we need not face that choice of life and death. But Ruan Ji reminds us: sometimes a person "doing nothing" does not mean he has borne nothing. That silence, that wine, that zither music late at night—all were one man's resistance in an extreme situation.

他没能改变那个时代。但他把那个时代，写进了八十二首诗里，让一千七百年后的我们，还能听见那声哭。

He could not change his age. But he wrote that age into eighty-two poems, so that we, seventeen hundred years later, can still hear that cry.

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：阮籍（字嗣宗），210年生于陈留尉氏（今河南开封一带），263年卒，年约53—54岁。父阮瑀为“建安七子”之一。为确证史实。《晋书·阮籍传》为其主要传记来源。

1. Basic biographical facts: Ruan Ji (courtesy name Sizong), born in 210 in Weishi, Chenliu (in present-day Kaifeng, Henan), died in 263, aged about 53–54. His father, Ruan Yu, was one of the Seven Masters of the Jian'an Era. This is a well-attested fact. "The Biography of Ruan Ji" in the Jinshu (Book of Jin) is the main source for his life.

2. “时无英雄，使竖子成名”：出自《晋书》等。关于这句话的对象（项羽？刘邦？司马氏？），后世理解不一，本文取“对时局的失望”这一较宽泛的解读。

2. "In an age without heroes, the small men have made their names": from the Jinshu and elsewhere. Later readers disagree about whom the remark targets (Xiang Yu? Liu Bang? the Sima clan?). This essay takes the broader reading of "disappointment with the state of the times."

3. 任职：曾任司马懿、司马师、司马昭的从事中郎，后任步兵校尉（故称“阮步兵”）。为确证史实。

3. Official posts: he served as attendant gentleman (congshi zhonglang) under Sima Yi, Sima Shi, and Sima Zhao, and later as Colonel of Infantry (hence "Ruan the Infantryman"). This is a well-attested fact.

4. 钟会试探、以醉避祸：为通行记载。“司马昭欲联姻，阮籍大醉六十日”亦为通行记载，具体天数（六十日）为史书所载，但属笔记性描述，本文保留其原貌。

4. Zhong Hui's sounding him out and his use of drink to avoid trouble: the standard account. That "Sima Zhao wished a marriage alliance and Ruan Ji was dead drunk for sixty days" is also the standard account; the specific number of days (sixty) is recorded in the histories but is "notes"-type description, and this essay preserves it as given.

5. 青白眼：出自《晋书·阮籍传》，为确证记载（鲁迅《魏晋风度及文章与药及酒之关系》有专门讨论）。

5. The dark eye and the white eye: from "The Biography of Ruan Ji" in the Jinshu, a well-attested record (Lu Xun discusses it specifically in "The Relation of Wei-Jin Manner and Literature to Medicine and Wine").

6. 母丧饮酒食肉、吐血数升：出自《晋书》等。为通行记载。“吐血”属于史书对极端悲痛的描述性记载，其真实性无法用现代医学核实，本文如实呈现为“史书记载”。

6. Drinking wine and eating meat during mourning for his mother, and spitting up several pints of blood: from the Jinshu and elsewhere. The standard account. The "spitting blood" is the histories' descriptive record of extreme grief; its reality cannot be checked by modern medicine, and this essay presents it as given, "as the histories record."

7. 穷途之哭：《晋书·阮籍传》载：“时率意独驾，不由径路，车迹所穷，辄恸哭而反。”为确证记载。

7. Weeping at the end of the road: "The Biography of Ruan Ji" in the Jinshu records: "He would often drive out alone on impulse, following no path; when the carriage tracks ran out, he would weep bitterly and turn back." A well-attested record.

8. “劝进表”：景元四年（263年）司马昭受封晋公，阮籍被指定作劝进表，完成后不久去世。为确证史实。《世说新语》记载其醉中属稿，细节可能有加工。

8. The "memorial urging him to ascend": in the fourth year of Jingyuan (263), Sima Zhao was enfeoffed as Duke of Jin; Ruan Ji was assigned to write the memorial urging him to ascend, and died soon after completing it. This is a well-attested fact. The Shishuo Xinyu (A New Account of Tales of the World) records that he drafted it while drunk; the details may be embellished.

9. 《咏怀诗》八十二首：为其代表作，非一时一地之作，而是长期累积的组诗。本文对“组诗开创性”的评价，属于文学史的公论。

9. The eighty-two poems of "Songs of My Heart": his representative work, not composed at one time or place but a cycle accumulated over a long period. This essay's judgment of its "pioneering" quality as a poem cycle is the common view in literary history.
