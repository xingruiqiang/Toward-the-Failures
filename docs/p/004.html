孔子：周游列国的“丧家之狗”

Confucius: The "Homeless Dog" Who Wandered the States

In 2026, when we speak of Confucius, what comes to mind may be "the model teacher for all ages," "the sage Confucius." But if you traveled back 2,500 years and stood on a dusty road in the late Spring and Autumn period, the Confucius you saw would be no lofty sage at all, but an out-and-out "failure."

在2026年的今天，当我们谈论孔子时，脑海中浮现的或许是“万世师表”，是“孔圣人”。但如果你穿越回2500年前，站在春秋末期的黄土路上，你看到的孔子，绝不是一个高高在上的圣人，而是一个彻头彻尾的“失败者”。

In 2026, when we speak of Confucius, what comes to mind may be "the model teacher for all ages," "the sage Confucius." But if you traveled back 2,500 years and stood on a dusty road in the late Spring and Autumn period, the Confucius you saw would be no lofty sage at all, but an out-and-out "failure."

作为1980年代出生的我们，习惯了社会的飞速变迁，也习惯了用“成功学”来衡量一切。我们害怕被时代抛弃，害怕成为“孔乙己”那样的迂腐者。其实，这种对“旧思想”的嘲讽和打压，并非今日独有。近百年来，从民国时期对“孔孟之道”的激烈批判，视其为阻碍进步的旧枷锁，到特殊时期对孔子思想的全面否定，孔子曾无数次被推下神坛，被踩在泥里。

Born in the 1980s, we are used to the rapid transformation of society and to measuring everything by the "science of success." We fear being left behind by the times, fear becoming a pedant like Kong Yiji. In fact, this mockery and suppression of "old ideas" is nothing new. For nearly a century now—from the Republican era's fierce denunciation of "the way of Confucius and Mencius" as an old shackle blocking progress, to the wholesale rejection of Confucian thought during a later period of upheaval—Confucius has been pushed off his altar and trampled into the mud time and again.

然而，历史总是惊人的相似。每当社会经历动荡与迷茫，当物质的繁华无法填补精神的空虚，人们总会想起这位老人的话。今天，让我们剥去圣人的光环，还原一个真实的、狼狈的、却无比坚韧的孔子。

And yet history has a striking way of repeating itself. Whenever society goes through turmoil and confusion, whenever material abundance cannot fill a spiritual void, people turn again to the words of this old man. Today, let us strip away the halo of the sage and restore a real Confucius—bedraggled, and yet unbelievably resilient.

一、出身微寒：被遗忘的“野人”

I. Of Humble Origin: The Forgotten "Wild Man"

在成为那个“知其不可而为之”的圣人之前，孔子首先是一个为了生存而挣扎的贫苦少年。

Before he became the sage who "knew it could not be done and yet did it," Confucius was first a poor boy struggling to survive.

公元前551年，孔子出生在鲁国陬邑。虽然祖上是宋国的贵族，但到了他这一代，家道早已中落。他的父亲叔梁纥在他三岁时就去世了，正妻施氏将年幼的孔子与他的母亲颜征在赶出家门。孤儿寡母相依为命，生活极其贫困。

In 551 BCE, Confucius was born in Zou, a town in the State of Lu. Though his ancestors had been nobles of the State of Song, by his generation the family had long since declined. His father, Shuliang He, died when Confucius was three, and the principal wife, Lady Shi, drove the boy and his mother, Yan Zhengzai, out of the household. Widow and orphan depended on each other, living in extreme poverty.

孔子后来曾坦率地承认：“吾少也贱，故多能鄙事。”（我小时候地位低下，所以学会了许多粗鄙的技艺。）为了养活母亲，他做过管理仓库的“委吏”，把账目算得清清楚楚；也做过管理牛羊的“乘田”，把牲畜养得膘肥体壮。他甚至可能做过在丧礼上吹吹打打的“儒”（乐手）。

Confucius later admitted frankly: "I was of lowly station when young, and so acquired many humble skills." (As a boy I held a low position, and therefore learned many menial crafts.) To support his mother, he served as a "granary clerk," keeping the accounts scrupulously, and as a "herdsman," tending cattle and sheep until they grew fat and sleek. He may even have worked as a "ru"—a musician who played at funerals.

17岁时，母亲去世。孔子想将父母合葬，却连父亲的墓地在哪儿都不知道，只能四处打听。在得知父亲葬于“防”地后，他毅然将母亲的遗体迁去合葬。这在当时是违背礼制的行为，但他不在乎。

When he was seventeen, his mother died. Confucius wanted to bury his parents together, but he did not even know where his father's grave was, and had to ask around. When he learned that his father was buried at Fang, he resolutely moved his mother's body there to join him. At the time this violated ritual propriety, but he did not care.

17岁那年，他还遭遇了人生第一次巨大的羞辱。鲁国权贵季孙氏宴请士人，孔子身穿丧服前去赴宴，却被季孙氏的家臣阳虎拦在门外，并当众嘲讽：“季氏宴请的是士人，不是请你这种人！”孔子默默地转身离开，将这份屈辱深埋心底。

That same year, at seventeen, he suffered the first great humiliation of his life. Jisun, a powerful nobleman of Lu, held a banquet for the gentry, and Confucius, still in mourning dress, went to attend. But Yang Hu, a retainer of the Jisun family, blocked him at the door and mocked him before the crowd: "The Jisun family is entertaining the gentry, not the likes of you!" Confucius turned and walked away in silence, burying the humiliation deep in his heart.

这段贫困、卑微且备受歧视的少年时光，塑造了孔子坚韧不拔的性格。他自称“野人”，并说：“先进于礼乐，野人也。后进于礼乐，君子也。如用之，则吾从先进。”（先学习礼乐的，是野人。后学习礼乐的，是君子。如果要用人，我选择野人。）这既是他对自己出身的坦然接受，也是他对那些高高在上的“君子”的无声反抗。

This boyhood of poverty, lowliness, and constant discrimination shaped Confucius's tenacious character. He called himself a "wild man" and said: "Those who first mastered ritual and music were the wild men; those who later mastered them were the nobles. If I were to employ men, I would follow the former." (Those who learned ritual and music first were the wild men; those who learned it later were the gentleman. If one were to make use of men, I would choose the wild men.) This was at once his honest acceptance of his origins and his silent rebellion against those lofty "gentlemen."

二、短暂的辉煌与彻底的失败

II. A Brief Glory and a Total Failure

在孔子周游列国的十四年里，他在当时主流政治圈的眼中，是一个彻头彻尾的“失败者”。但鲜为人知的是，在他流亡之前，他曾短暂地站在权力的巅峰，试图力挽狂澜。

During the fourteen years Confucius wandered from state to state, the mainstream political circles of his day saw him as an out-and-out "failure." But few know that before his exile he briefly stood at the pinnacle of power, trying to turn back the tide.

公元前501年，51岁的孔子被任命为中都宰（相当于现在的县长）。这在当时是一个不起眼的职位，但孔子却干出了惊天动地的成绩。仅仅一年，中都就被治理得井井有条，路不拾遗，夜不闭户，成为了鲁国乃至天下诸侯学习的榜样。因为政绩卓著，孔子被迅速提拔为司空，随后又升任大司寇（相当于司法部长兼最高法院院长）。

In 501 BCE, the fifty-one-year-old Confucius was appointed magistrate of Zhongdu (roughly a county head today). It was an unremarkable post, yet Confucius achieved earthshaking results. Within a single year, Zhongdu was governed so well that nothing dropped on the road was picked up and doors were left unbolted at night, and it became a model for all of Lu and even for the feudal lords. For his outstanding administration he was quickly promoted to Minister of Works, and then to Minister of Crime (equivalent to a minister of justice who also headed the supreme court).

这是他人生中最接近“成功”的时刻。公元前500年，孔子作为鲁国的相礼（外交顾问），陪同鲁定公与齐景公在夹谷会盟。面对强大的齐国，孔子不仅据理力争，还做好了军事准备，最终迫使齐国归还了侵占鲁国的土地。这一仗，让孔子赢得了极大的政治声望，也让鲁国在列国中扬眉吐气。

This was the moment when he came closest to "success." In 500 BCE, as Lu's master of ceremonies (diplomatic adviser), Confucius accompanied Duke Ding of Lu to the Jiagu meeting with Duke Jing of Qi. Facing the powerful State of Qi, Confucius argued his case firmly and made military preparations as well, finally forcing Qi to return the lands it had seized from Lu. This victory won Confucius great political prestige and let Lu hold its head high among the states.

然而，真正的考验随之而来。当时的鲁国，政权长期被“三桓”（孟孙氏、叔孙氏、季孙氏）三家贵族把控，他们的封邑城墙高大坚固，甚至超过了国君的规格，严重威胁着公室的权力。公元前498年，孔子提出了一个大胆的政治改革方案——“堕三都”，即拆除这三家贵族封邑的城墙。

Then the real test arrived. In Lu at that time, power had long been in the grip of the "Three Huan"—the Mengsun, Shusun, and Jisun clans. The walls of their fief towns were so high and thick that they exceeded even the duke's own specifications, gravely threatening the authority of the ducal house. In 498 BCE, Confucius proposed a bold political reform: "razing the three capitals," that is, tearing down the walls of these three noble fief towns.

这个计划初期得到了三桓的支持，因为他们也深受家臣叛乱的困扰。孔子的弟子子路当时担任季孙氏的家宰，积极推行此事。费邑（季孙氏封邑）和郈邑（叔孙氏封邑）的城墙被顺利拆除。但在拆除成邑（孟孙氏封邑）时，遭到了孟孙氏家臣公敛处父的强烈抵制。他警告孟孙氏：“拆了成邑，齐国军队就会打到北门。成邑是孟氏的保障，没有成邑，就没有孟氏。”孟孙氏动摇了，暗中支持家臣反抗。

At first the plan won the support of the Three Huan, since they too were plagued by rebellions among their retainers. Confucius's disciple Zilu, then steward to the Jisun family, actively pushed the matter forward. The walls of Bi (the Jisun fief) and Hou (the Shusun fief) were duly torn down. But when it came to Cheng (the Mengsun fief), it met fierce resistance from the Mengsun retainer Gonglian Chufu. He warned the Mengsun: "If you tear down Cheng, the Qi army will come straight to the north gate. Cheng is the Meng clan's protection; without Cheng, there is no Meng clan." The Mengsun wavered and secretly backed his retainer's resistance.

“堕三都”的计划半途而废。这不仅标志着孔子政治改革的彻底失败，也让他与三桓的关系彻底破裂。曾经的支持者变成了敌人，曾经的理想变成了泡影。不久后，齐国为了阻止鲁国强大，送给鲁定公80名美女和120匹骏马。鲁定公沉迷于声色，连续三天不上朝。孔子失望至极，知道自己在鲁国已无立足之地。公元前497年，55岁的孔子带着弟子们，离开了父母之邦，开始了他长达14年的流亡生涯。

The plan to "raze the three capitals" was abandoned halfway. This marked not only the total failure of Confucius's political reform but also the complete rupture of his relations with the Three Huan. Former supporters became enemies, former ideals became bubbles. Not long afterward, to keep Lu from growing strong, the State of Qi sent Duke Ding of Lu eighty beauties and 120 fine horses. Duke Ding gave himself over to pleasure and did not hold court for three days running. Confucius was deeply disillusioned and knew he had no place left to stand in Lu. In 497 BCE, at the age of fifty-five, he left the land of his parents with his disciples and began a fourteen-year exile.

三、十四年流浪路线图：一个“失败者”的足迹

III. A Route Map of Fourteen Years of Wandering: The Footprints of a "Failure"

孔子的周游列国，并非一次潇洒的旅行，而是一场充满屈辱、危险与绝望的漫长逃亡。我们可以沿着他的足迹，看看这位“失败者”走过的路。

Confucius's journey through the states was no carefree tour but a long flight full of humiliation, danger, and despair. Let us follow his footprints and see the road this "failure" traveled.

第一站：卫国（前497年-前496年）

Stop One: The State of Wei (497–496 BCE)

离开鲁国后，孔子的第一站是卫国。卫国与鲁国文化相近，且有弟子子路的亲戚在卫国做官，孔子本以为这里能成为一个施展抱负的平台。然而，卫国却成了他“失败”生涯中最具讽刺意味的一站。

After leaving Lu, Confucius's first stop was the State of Wei. Wei's culture was close to Lu's, and his disciple Zilu had relatives serving as officials there, so Confucius thought this might be a platform for realizing his ambitions. Yet Wei turned out to be the most ironic stop in his career of "failure."

卫灵公表面上对孔子非常客气，不仅给予他“奉粟六万”的高薪俸禄，还亲自到郊外迎接。但这份礼遇仅仅停留在表面，卫灵公只把孔子当作一个装点门面的“吉祥物”，并不打算真正重用他。孔子在卫国无所事事，只能靠结交卫国的贤人来打发时间，这反而引起了卫国大夫的嫉妒。不久，有人在卫灵公面前进谗言，说孔子是鲁国的奸细。卫灵公立刻变脸，派兵在孔子住处外拉起警戒线，名为保护，实为监视。孔子意识到自己已不被信任，在卫国待了十个月后，只能无奈离开。

Duke Ling of Wei was outwardly very courteous to Confucius, granting him a generous stipend of "sixty thousand measures of grain" and even coming out to the outskirts to welcome him in person. But the courtesy was only on the surface; Duke Ling treated Confucius as a mascot to decorate his court and had no intention of truly employing him. With nothing to do in Wei, Confucius could only pass the time by befriending the worthy men of the state—which in turn aroused the envy of Wei's grandees. Before long someone slandered him to Duke Ling, claiming that Confucius was a spy for Lu. Duke Ling's face changed at once, and he sent troops to cordon off the area around Confucius's lodgings—ostensibly for protection, in fact to keep watch. Realizing he was no longer trusted, Confucius, after ten months in Wei, had no choice but to leave.

第二站：匡地被囚（前496年）

Stop Two: Imprisoned at Kuang (496 BCE)

孔子离开卫国，准备前往陈国，途中经过匡地（今河南长垣）。匡人曾遭受过鲁国阳虎的劫掠，而孔子的相貌与阳虎相似。加上孔子的弟子颜刻曾为阳虎驾车，匡人误以为孔子就是阳虎，将他们团团围住，囚禁了五天五夜。弟子们惊恐万分，孔子却镇定自若，说：“文王既没，文不在兹乎？天之将丧斯文也，后死者不得与于斯文也；天之未丧斯文也，匡人其如予何？”（周文王死后，周朝的礼乐文化不就在我这里吗？如果上天要毁灭这种文化，那我就不会掌握它；如果上天不想毁灭它，匡人能把我怎么样？）最终，误会解除，孔子得以脱险。

Leaving Wei, Confucius set out for the State of Chen and on the way passed through Kuang (present-day Changyuan, Henan). The people of Kuang had once been plundered by Yang Hu of Lu, and Confucius resembled Yang Hu in appearance. What was more, Confucius's disciple Yan Ke had once driven Yang Hu's chariot, so the people of Kuang mistook Confucius for Yang Hu, surrounded them, and held them prisoner for five days and nights. The disciples were terrified, but Confucius remained calm and said: "Since King Wen is dead, is the culture not here with me? If Heaven meant to destroy this culture, I would not have been given it. If Heaven does not mean to destroy it, what can the people of Kuang do to me?" (After King Wen of Zhou died, is not the ritual and music culture of Zhou here with me? If Heaven meant to destroy this culture, then I would not have been able to master it; if Heaven does not mean to destroy it, what can the men of Kuang do to me?) In the end the misunderstanding was cleared up and Confucius escaped.

第三站：重返卫国与“子见南子”（前495年-前493年）

Stop Three: Return to Wei and "Confucius Meets Nanzi" (495–493 BCE)

孔子离开匡地后，又因战乱辗转返回卫国。这一次，他遭遇了人生中最尴尬的时刻——“子见南子”。南子是卫灵公的宠妃，名声不好，却掌握着卫国的实权。她想见孔子，孔子本想拒绝，但为了能有机会向卫灵公推行自己的政治主张，只能硬着头皮去见。南子在帷帐中接见孔子，身上的玉佩首饰叮当作响，言语轻佻。事后，孔子对弟子们解释：“我本来不想见她，既然见了，就须以礼相待。”但生性耿直的子路对此非常不满，认为老师有失身份。孔子无奈之下，只能指天发誓：“予所否者，天厌之！天厌之！”（如果我做了什么不对的事，上天厌弃我！上天厌弃我！）这一幕，让孔子在弟子心中的形象大打折扣，也让他陷入了深深的委屈之中。

After leaving Kuang, Confucius was driven back to Wei by the upheavals of war. This time he met the most embarrassing moment of his life: "Confucius meets Nanzi." Nanzi was the favorite consort of Duke Ling of Wei; her reputation was poor, yet she held real power in the state. She wished to see Confucius. He would have preferred to refuse, but for a chance to press his political views on Duke Ling he steeled himself and went. Nanzi received him behind a curtain, her jade ornaments jingling, her words frivolous. Afterward Confucius explained to his disciples: "I did not wish to see her, but having seen her, I had to treat her with propriety." But the blunt Zilu was greatly displeased, feeling that his teacher had demeaned himself. At a loss, Confucius could only swear to Heaven: "If I have done anything wrong, may Heaven forsake me! May Heaven forsake me!" (If I have done anything improper, may Heaven reject me! May Heaven reject me!) This scene badly damaged Confucius's image in the eyes of his disciples and plunged him into deep grievance.

更让孔子感到羞辱的是，卫灵公与南子出游时，让南子坐在主车上，自己作陪，却让孔子乘坐第二辆车，像个随从一样跟在后面，招摇过市。孔子看着这一幕，心中充满了悲凉，感叹道：“吾未见好德如好色者也。”（我没见过像喜好美色那样喜好德行的人。）这次经历后，孔子彻底对卫国失望，再次离开。

What humiliated Confucius even more was that when Duke Ling and Nanzi went out, the duke seated Nanzi in the first carriage and rode with her, while Confucius was put in the second carriage, trailing behind like a mere attendant as they paraded through the streets. Watching this, Confucius felt a deep desolation and sighed: "I have never seen one who loves virtue as much as he loves beauty." (I have not seen anyone who loves virtue as much as he loves beauty.) After this experience Confucius lost all hope in Wei and left once more.

第四站：过宋遭逐（前492年）

Stop Four: Driven Out of Song (492 BCE)

孔子离开卫国，前往宋国。在宋国，他与弟子们在一棵大树下演习礼仪。宋国司马桓魋（tuí）想杀死孔子，派人拔掉了那棵大树。弟子们催促孔子快走，孔子却说：“天生德于予，桓魋其如予何？”（上天把德行赋予了我，桓魋能把我怎么样？）他从容不迫地离开了宋国。

Confucius left Wei for the State of Song. There, under a large tree, he and his disciples practiced the rites. Huan Tui, the minister of war of Song, wanted to kill Confucius and sent men to uproot the tree. The disciples urged Confucius to flee quickly, but he said: "Heaven gave me this virtue—what can Huan Tui do to me?" (Heaven endowed me with virtue; what can Huan Tui do to me?) He left the State of Song unhurried and unafraid.

第五站：郑国东门与“丧家之狗”（前492年）

Stop Five: The East Gate of Zheng and the "Homeless Dog" (492 BCE)

孔子到达郑国都城时，与弟子走散，独自站在东门。一个郑国人看到他的样子，对子贡说：“东门有个人，他的额头像尧，脖子像皋陶，肩膀像子产，但这都不重要。重要的是，他疲惫憔悴的样子，真像一条‘丧家之狗’。”当子贡找到孔子，把这话转告给他时，孔子欣然笑曰：“然哉！然哉！”（说得对啊！说得对啊！）

When Confucius reached the capital of Zheng, he became separated from his disciples and stood alone at the east gate. A man of Zheng, seeing how he looked, said to Zigong: "There is a man at the east gate whose forehead is like Yao's, whose neck is like Gao Yao's, whose shoulders are like Zichan's—but none of that matters. What matters is that he looks so weary and haggard, truly like a 'homeless dog.'" When Zigong found Confucius and repeated the words to him, Confucius laughed with delight and said: "Exactly so! Exactly so!" (How right he is! How right he is!)

第六站：陈蔡之厄（前489年）

Stop Six: The Peril Between Chen and Cai (489 BCE)

孔子到达陈国后，正值吴国攻打陈国，楚国出兵救陈。楚国听说孔子在陈蔡之间，便派人去聘请他。陈蔡两国的大夫害怕孔子被楚国重用后会对自己不利，于是联手派兵将孔子及其弟子围困在荒野之中。他们断绝了孔子的粮食供应，导致孔子一行人断粮七天七夜。弟子们饿得站不起来，子路愤怒地质问：“君子亦有穷乎？”孔子却依然抚琴而歌，并说出了那句千古名言：“君子固穷，小人穷斯滥矣。”

When Confucius reached the State of Chen, the State of Wu was attacking Chen, and Chu sent troops to rescue it. Hearing that Confucius was between Chen and Cai, Chu sent men to invite him. The grandees of Chen and Cai, fearing that a Confucius employed by Chu would harm them, joined forces to send troops and surround Confucius and his disciples in the wilderness. They cut off his food supply, leaving Confucius and his followers without grain for seven days and nights. So weak from hunger that they could not stand, Zilu angrily demanded: "Must even the gentleman suffer such want?" Yet Confucius went on strumming his lute and singing, and spoke those words that have echoed through the ages: "The gentleman remains steadfast in poverty; the petty man, in poverty, grows reckless."

第七站：负函与叶公问政（前488年-前484年）

Stop Seven: Fuhan and the Duke of She Asks About Government (488–484 BCE)

孔子在陈蔡之间脱险后，辗转来到负函（今河南信阳），受到楚国大夫沈诸梁（即叶公）的接待。叶公向孔子请教为政之道，孔子留下了“近者悦，远者来”的名言。然而，楚国令尹子西忌惮孔子的才能，阻止楚昭王重用他。孔子在楚国边境徘徊良久，最终未能得到任用，只能返回卫国。

After escaping the danger between Chen and Cai, Confucius made his way to Fuhan (present-day Xinyang, Henan), where he was received by the Chu grandee Shen Zhuliang—that is, the Duke of She. The Duke of She asked Confucius about the way of government, and Confucius left behind the famous words "make those near you happy, and those far off will come." Yet Zi Xi, the prime minister of Chu, feared Confucius's talents and blocked King Zhao of Chu from employing him. Confucius lingered a long time on the borders of Chu but in the end gained no appointment and could only return to Wei.

第八站：再返卫国与归鲁（前484年）

Stop Eight: Back to Wei and Home to Lu (484 BCE)

孔子最后一次回到卫国时，卫灵公已死，其孙卫出公在位。孔子对卫国父子争位的混乱局面感到失望，最终在弟子冉求的帮助下，于公元前484年回到了阔别14年的鲁国。此时，他已68岁。

When Confucius returned to Wei for the last time, Duke Ling was dead and his grandson, Duke Chu of Wei, was on the throne. Disillusioned by the chaos of father and son contending for the throne, Confucius at last, with the help of his disciple Ran Qiu, returned in 484 BCE to Lu, which he had left fourteen years before. By now he was sixty-eight.

四、至暗时刻：狼狈与绝望的巅峰

IV. The Darkest Hour: The Peak of Bedragglement and Despair

孔子的流浪生涯中，有两个瞬间最能体现他作为“失败者”的狼狈。

In Confucius's years of wandering, two moments best reveal how bedraggled he was as a "failure."

公元前492年，孔子60岁。在郑国都城，他与弟子走散了。想象一下那个画面：一个身高九尺（约合现在1.9米以上）的老头，须发皆白，孤零零地站在城东门，眼神迷茫。这时，有个郑国人看到了他，对正在找人的子贡说：“东门有个人，他的额头像尧，脖子像皋陶，肩膀像子产，但这都不重要。重要的是，他疲惫憔悴的样子，真像一条‘丧家之狗’。”当子贡找到孔子，小心翼翼地把这话转告给他时，孔子是什么反应？他生气了吗？没有。孔子欣然笑曰：“形状，末也。而谓似丧家之狗，然哉！然哉！”（意思是：他说我长得像圣人，那是瞎扯。但说我像条丧家之狗，太对了！太对了！）这一刻，孔子不是圣人，他是一个承认自己生活一地鸡毛的中年人。他用自嘲消解了命运的嘲弄，这种幽默感，是只有真正经历过绝望的人才拥有的。

In 492 BCE, Confucius was sixty. In the capital of Zheng he became separated from his disciples. Picture the scene: an old man nine chi tall (over 1.9 meters today), hair and beard all white, standing alone at the east gate of the city, his eyes bewildered. A man of Zheng saw him and said to Zigong, who was searching for him: "There is a man at the east gate whose forehead is like Yao's, whose neck is like Gao Yao's, whose shoulders are like Zichan's—but none of that matters. What matters is that he looks so weary and haggard, truly like a 'homeless dog.'" When Zigong found Confucius and cautiously repeated the words to him, how did Confucius react? Was he angry? No. Confucius laughed with delight and said: "As for my looks, that is a trivial matter. But to say I resemble a homeless dog—exactly so! Exactly so!" (He said I looked like a sage—that was nonsense. But that I looked like a homeless dog—how right he was! How right he was!) At that moment Confucius was not a sage; he was a middle-aged man admitting that his life was a mess. He dissolved fate's mockery with self-mockery, and that sense of humor belongs only to those who have truly known despair.

公元前489年，孔子63岁。在陈国和蔡国之间，他被两国派兵围困在荒野之中。这不是演习，是真实的绝境。粮食断绝，随行弟子饿得站不起来。一向直率的子路终于忍不住了，带着怨气问孔子：“君子亦有穷乎？”（老师，难道君子也会混到这种穷途末路的地步吗？）面对弟子的质疑和死亡的威胁，孔子在做什么？他在抚琴而歌。他告诉子路：“君子固穷，小人穷斯滥矣。”（君子在困境中依然坚守节操，小人在困境中才会胡作非为。）这七天七夜的断粮，是孔子肉体最痛苦的时刻，也是他精神最闪光的时刻。他证明了，一个人的尊严，不取决于他拥有多少财富或权力，而取决于他在绝境中是否还能守住内心的“道”。

In 489 BCE, Confucius was sixty-three. Between the states of Chen and Cai, troops sent by both states surrounded him in the wilderness. This was no drill; it was real extremity. The grain ran out, and his disciples were too weak from hunger to stand. The ever-blunt Zilu finally could not hold back and asked Confucius, full of resentment: "Must even the gentleman come to such a dead end?" (Teacher, must even a gentleman be reduced to such a hopeless strait?) Facing his disciple's doubt and the threat of death, what was Confucius doing? He was strumming his lute and singing. He told Zilu: "The gentleman remains steadfast in poverty; the petty man, in poverty, grows reckless." (The gentleman keeps his integrity even in adversity; only the petty man runs wild when in straits.) Those seven days and nights without food were the most painful time for Confucius's body and the most radiant time for his spirit. He proved that a person's dignity does not depend on how much wealth or power he holds, but on whether he can still hold fast to the "Way" within his heart in the depths of despair.

五、师生情谊：流浪路上的温暖

V. The Bond Between Teacher and Disciples: Warmth on the Road

在这漫长的流浪途中，孔子与弟子们的互动，更像是一个落魄大家长带着几个性格迥异的孩子在闯荡江湖。这些细节，让孔子的形象更加立体。

On this long road of wandering, the interactions between Confucius and his disciples were more like a down-and-out patriarch leading a few children of very different temperaments through the world. These details give Confucius a fuller, more three-dimensional image.

子路是孔子最忠实的保镖，也是最爱发牢骚的“刺头”。除了陈蔡之厄时的质问，平日里子路也常给孔子出难题。有一次，孔子路过一处荒野，让子路去向正在耕田的隐士长沮、桀溺问路。结果这两位隐士不但不指路，反而借机嘲讽孔子是“辟人之士”（逃避坏人的人），劝子路不如跟着他们这些“辟世之士”（逃避整个社会的人）归隐田园。子路碰了一鼻子灰，回来把话原封不动地告诉了孔子。孔子听后，怅然若失地说：“鸟兽不可与同群，吾非斯人之徒与而谁与？”（人不能和鸟兽生活在一起，我不和天下人在一起，又和谁在一起呢？）这一幕，展现了孔子在遭遇误解时，依然坚持入世救人的孤独与坚定，而子路则在一旁默默听着，虽然不懂老师的深意，却始终不离不弃。

Zilu was Confucius's most loyal bodyguard and also his most grumbling "thorn in the side." Besides his challenge during the peril between Chen and Cai, Zilu often put his teacher on the spot at other times. Once, when Confucius passed through the wilderness, he sent Zilu to ask directions from two recluses who were plowing the fields, Chang Ju and Jie Ni. Not only did the two recluses refuse to give directions; they used the occasion to mock Confucius as one who "avoids men" (flees from the wicked), and advised Zilu to join them, who "avoid the world" (flee from society altogether) and withdraw to the fields. Zilu came back with his nose out of joint and reported their words to Confucius exactly as they were spoken. Hearing this, Confucius said with a sense of loss: "One cannot herd with birds and beasts. If I do not associate with my fellow men, with whom shall I associate?" (A man cannot live among birds and beasts; if I do not join with the people of the world, whom shall I join?) This scene reveals Confucius's loneliness and resolve in engaging with the world to save others even when misunderstood; and Zilu, listening silently at his side, though he did not grasp his teacher's deeper meaning, never left him.

颜回是孔子最得意的门生，也是孔子在绝望中最大的慰藉。在陈蔡绝粮最艰难的时候，大家都饿得东倒西歪，颜回却在一旁为大家讨米煮饭。饭快熟时，孔子偶然瞥见颜回从锅里抓了一把饭吃。孔子心里很不是滋味，但他没有当场发作。等饭熟了，颜回请孔子吃饭，孔子故意说：“我刚才梦见祖先了，这饭要先祭祖。”颜回一听，急忙解释：“老师，这饭不能祭祖。刚才有煤灰掉进锅里，弄脏了一些饭，我把它抓出来吃了，怕浪费。”孔子这才恍然大悟，感叹道：“人可信的是眼睛，但眼睛也有不可靠的时候。”这段插曲，不仅展现了颜回的诚实与节俭，更体现了孔子作为老师，在困境中依然保持着对人性的审慎与反思。

Yan Hui was Confucius's most cherished disciple and his greatest comfort in despair. During the hardest days of the hunger between Chen and Cai, when everyone was too weak from hunger to stand straight, Yan Hui was off begging rice and cooking it for everyone. When the rice was nearly done, Confucius happened to glimpse Yan Hui taking a handful of rice from the pot and eating it. Confucius was uneasy but did not confront him on the spot. When the rice was ready and Yan Hui invited Confucius to eat, Confucius deliberately said: "I have just dreamed of my ancestors; this rice must first be offered to them." Hearing this, Yan Hui hurried to explain: "Teacher, this rice cannot be offered to the ancestors. Just now some soot fell into the pot and soiled part of the rice; I scooped it out and ate it so as not to waste it." Only then did Confucius understand, and he sighed: "What one can trust are the eyes, but the eyes too can be unreliable." This episode shows not only Yan Hui's honesty and frugality but also Confucius, as a teacher, keeping a cautious and reflective stance toward human nature even in hardship.

还有一次，孔子带弟子们登上农山，让大家各言其志。子路豪气干云，说要率领军队冲锋陷阵，夺回千里失地；子贡则说要在两国交战之际，凭三寸不烂之舌化解干戈。轮到颜回时，他却说希望能辅佐一位圣明君主，让天下太平，铸剑为犁，那样就不需要子路去打仗，也不需要子贡去游说了。孔子听后感叹道：“美哉！大士。”并说如果颜回能实现这个理想，他愿意背着行李跟在颜回身后。这段对话，让我们看到了孔子与弟子之间亦师亦友的深厚情谊，以及他们对理想的不同追求。

Once more, Confucius led his disciples up Mount Nong and had each of them speak his aspirations. Zilu, full of swaggering spirit, said he would lead an army into battle and recover a thousand li of lost territory; Zigong said he would, when two states were at war, use his silver tongue to dissolve the conflict. When it came to Yan Hui's turn, he said he hoped to assist an enlightened ruler in bringing peace to the world and beating swords into plowshares, so that there would be no need for Zilu to fight or for Zigong to go on diplomatic missions. Hearing this, Confucius sighed: "How admirable! A great scholar." And he said that if Yan Hui could realize this ideal, he would carry the luggage and follow behind Yan Hui. This exchange shows the deep bond of both teacher and friend between Confucius and his disciples, and the different ideals they each pursued.

六、最后的岁月：落叶归根与孤独离世

VI. The Final Years: A Leaf Falling to Its Root and a Lonely Death

公元前484年，68岁的孔子在弟子冉求的努力下，终于回到了阔别14年的鲁国。鲁哀公和季康子以“国老”的规格接待他，向他咨询国政。然而，此时的孔子已心灰意冷，他不再寻求出仕，而是将全部精力投入到整理古籍和教育弟子中。他删《诗》《书》，订《礼》《乐》，赞《周易》，作《春秋》，为后世留下了宝贵的文化遗产。

In 484 BCE, with the efforts of his disciple Ran Qiu, the sixty-eight-year-old Confucius at last returned to Lu after fourteen years away. Duke Ai of Lu and Ji Kangzi received him with the honors due a state elder and consulted him on state affairs. But by now Confucius's heart had gone cold; he no longer sought office, but poured all his energy into editing the ancient texts and teaching his disciples. He compiled the Shi (Book of Songs) and the Shu (Book of Documents), ordered the Li (Rites) and Yue (Music), praised the Zhou Yi (Book of Changes), and wrote the Chunqiu (Spring and Autumn Annals), leaving behind a precious cultural legacy for later generations.

然而，晚年的孔子接连遭受沉重的精神打击。公元前481年，他最得意的弟子颜回英年早逝。孔子悲痛欲绝，哭喊着：“天丧予！天丧予！”（上天要我的命啊！上天要我的命啊！）次年，他最忠诚的弟子子路在卫国内乱中战死，并被敌人剁成肉酱。孔子听闻噩耗，命人倒掉家中的肉酱，从此一病不起。

Yet in his later years Confucius suffered one heavy spiritual blow after another. In 481 BCE his most cherished disciple, Yan Hui, died young. Confucius was overwhelmed with grief and cried out: "Heaven is destroying me! Heaven is destroying me!" (Heaven is taking my life! Heaven is taking my life!) The next year his most loyal disciple, Zilu, was killed in a civil strife in Wei, his body hacked to pieces by the enemy. When Confucius heard the news, he ordered the meat sauce in his house to be thrown out, and from then on he took to his bed and never rose healthy again.

公元前479年，孔子病重。子贡前来看望，孔子拄着拐杖在门口徘徊，唱道：“泰山其颓乎！梁木其坏乎！哲人其萎乎！”（泰山要崩塌了吗！梁木要朽坏了吗！哲人要凋零了吗！）七天后，孔子在孤独与悲痛中去世，享年73岁。

In 479 BCE Confucius fell gravely ill. Zigong came to visit, and Confucius, leaning on a staff, paced at the doorway and sang: "Mount Tai is crumbling! The great beam is breaking! The wise man is withering!" (Is Mount Tai about to collapse? Is the roof beam about to rot? Is the wise man about to fade?) Seven days later, Confucius died in loneliness and sorrow at the age of seventy-three.

他去世后，弟子们为他守丧三年，子贡更是守了六年。鲁哀公为他撰写悼词，但孔子生前并未得到真正的重用。他带着未竟的理想和满身的伤痕，走完了自己“失败”而又伟大的一生。

After his death his disciples mourned him for three years, and Zigong for six. Duke Ai of Lu wrote a eulogy for him, yet in life Confucius had never truly been given weight. Carrying unfulfilled ideals and a body full of scars, he completed a life that was at once "failed" and great.

七、结语：致每一个“丧家之狗”

VII. Conclusion: To Every "Homeless Dog"

为什么孔子在当时如此“失败”，却能影响中国两千多年？因为他是一个在礼崩乐坏的时代，唯一坚持推行“仁政”的孤独行者。他明知理想难以实现，却依然坚持“知其不可而为之”。这种精神，不是愚蠢，而是一种超越时代的孤勇。

Why, though he was such a "failure" in his own time, has Confucius been able to influence China for more than two thousand years? Because he was, in an age when rites had collapsed and music was in ruins, the one solitary traveler who kept pressing for "benevolent government." Knowing full well that his ideal was hard to realize, he still held to "knowing it cannot be done and yet doing it." This spirit is not foolishness but a lonely courage that transcends its age.

关于“孔乙己”的误解：鲁迅笔下的孔乙己，是封建科举制度的受害者，是迂腐的代名词。但真正的孔子，绝不是孔乙己。孔乙己只会死读书，而孔子是“有教无类”的开创者，是打破贵族垄断教育的革命者。

On the misunderstanding about "Kong Yiji": the Kong Yiji of Lu Xun's story is a victim of the feudal examination system, a byword for pedantry. But the real Confucius is in no way Kong Yiji. Kong Yiji could only read books to death; Confucius was the pioneer of "education for all without regard to class," a revolutionary who broke the aristocracy's monopoly on education.

关于思想的沉浮：孔子思想在近现代的两度被打压（民国与文革），恰恰证明了它的强大生命力。它像一颗种子，即便被压在巨石之下，一旦春风拂过（改革开放），依然能破土而出，成为支撑民族精神的脊梁。

On the rise and fall of an idea: that Confucian thought was suppressed twice in modern times (in the Republican era and the Cultural Revolution) proves precisely its powerful vitality. Like a seed, even crushed beneath a boulder, once the spring wind blows (the reform and opening-up), it can still break through the soil and become the backbone that supports the spirit of a nation.

在2026年，我们或许也曾像孔子一样，在职场中碰壁，在生活中迷茫，被嘲笑为“不合时宜”，被贴上“失败者”的标签。我们可能觉得自己像条“丧家之狗”，找不到归属感，看不清未来。

In 2026, we too may have run into walls at work like Confucius, felt lost in life, been mocked as "out of step with the times," been labeled a "failure." We may feel like a "homeless dog," unable to find a sense of belonging, unable to see the future clearly.

但请记得，孔子也曾是那条“丧家之狗”。他没有因为狼狈而放弃理想，没有因为嘲笑而停止脚步。他用十四年的流浪，告诉我们：所谓的失败，只是暂时的境遇；而内心的坚守，才是永恒的胜利。

But remember: Confucius too was once that "homeless dog." He did not abandon his ideals because he was bedraggled, nor stop walking because he was mocked. Through fourteen years of wandering he tells us: so-called failure is only a temporary circumstance; an inner steadfastness is the eternal victory.

愿你在感到狼狈时，也能像孔子一样，笑着说一句：“然哉！然哉！”然后，拍拍身上的尘土，继续前行。

May you, when you feel bedraggled, also laugh like Confucius and say: "Exactly so! Exactly so!"—then dust yourself off and walk on.
