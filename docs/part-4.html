第四辑　用痛苦喂养的灵魂

Part Four · Souls Fed on Pain

有些人的失败，藏在作品里。

For some, the failure is hidden inside the work.

海明威写了一辈子硬汉，最后把枪对准了自己；茨威格亲眼看着自己的世界毁灭；老舍走进了太平湖；傅雷和妻子一起走了；萧红三十一岁死在异乡；海子二十五岁卧在铁轨上；顾城闭上了自己的眼睛；李清照后半生国破家亡；徐渭九次自杀未遂；八大山人画了一辈子翻白眼的鸟……

Hemingway wrote tough men his whole life, then turned the gun on himself. Zweig watched his own world destroyed before his eyes. Lao She walked into Taiping Lake. Fu Lei left the world together with his wife. Xiao Hong died in a foreign place at thirty-one. Haizi lay down on the rails at twenty-five. Gu Cheng closed his own eyes. Li Qingzhao spent her later years with her country broken and her family gone. Xu Wei attempted suicide nine times and failed each time. Bada Shanren painted birds that rolled their eyes in disdain, and painted them for a lifetime…

他们的人生大都不幸。可他们留下的东西，让后来的人一遍遍地读、听、看。

Their lives were mostly unhappy. Yet what they left behind makes later generations read, and listen, and look, again and again.
