Chapter 092 · Part 5

张志新：她坚持了自己认为对的事

Zhang Zhixin: She Held to What She Believed Was Right

——“一个党员，应该有自己独立思考的权利。”

—"A Party member should have the right to think independently."

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人92 / 100约 3072 字 · 3072 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，我们谈论“独立思考”的时候，往往把它当成一种能力训练——怎么不盲从、怎么有自己的判断。但在半个多世纪前，对一个普通干部来说，“独立思考”是一件可能付出生命的事。

In 2026, when we talk about "independent thinking," we usually treat it as a kind of skill to be trained—how not to follow blindly, how to form a judgment of one's own. But more than half a century ago, for an ordinary cadre, "independent thinking" was something that could cost a person her life.

张志新就是那个付出了生命的人。她是一名共产党员，是辽宁省委宣传部的一名干部。在那个特殊的年代里，她坚持了自己认为正确的观点，拒绝认错，拒绝改口，最后被处决。

Zhang Zhixin was the one who paid with her life. She was a member of the Communist Party of China, a cadre in the Publicity Department of the Liaoning Provincial Party Committee. In that extraordinary era, she held to the views she believed to be correct, refused to admit error, refused to take back her words, and was in the end executed.

她死的时候，四十五岁。

She was forty-five when she died.

而四年之后，中共辽宁省委为她彻底平反昭雪，追认她为革命烈士。这个国家最终承认：她是对的。

And four years later, the Liaoning Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China completely rehabilitated her and cleared her name, and posthumously recognized her as a revolutionary martyr. The country at last acknowledged: she was right.

一、一个普通的青年党员干部

I. An Ordinary Young Party Cadre

1930年12月20日，张志新出生在天津。她的家庭是知识分子家庭，她从小就读书、爱思考，性格直率。

Zhang Zhixin was born in Tianjin on December 20, 1930. Hers was an intellectual family; from childhood she read books and loved to think, and she had a straightforward temperament.

1950年，她参加工作；1955年，她加入了中国共产党。此后她在宣传系统工作，后来调到辽宁省委宣传部，做理论方面的干事。

In 1950 she began working; in 1955 she joined the Communist Party of China. After that she worked in the propaganda system, and later was transferred to the Publicity Department of the Liaoning Provincial Party Committee, where she worked as an officer on theoretical matters.

从她的履历看，她是一个典型的、优秀的青年干部。 她工作认真，有理论水平，也有自己的想法。如果不是那场运动，她大概会像很多人一样，平平淡淡地工作一辈子。

By her record, she was a typical and outstanding young cadre. She worked conscientiously, had a grounding in theory, and had ideas of her own. Had it not been for that movement, she would probably, like so many others, have worked quietly for a lifetime.

但“文化大革命”开始了。

But the "Cultural Revolution" began.

二、她说了“不该说的话”

II. She Said "What Should Not Be Said"

在那场运动中，张志新对当时的一些做法产生了疑问。她是一个受过训练的、习惯用逻辑思考问题的人。当周围人都跟着喊口号、跟着表态的时候，她提出了自己的看法。

During that movement, Zhang Zhixin began to have doubts about some of the practices of the time. She was a trained person, accustomed to working through problems with logic. When everyone around her was chanting slogans and falling into line, she put forward views of her own.

她认为，有些事不对。

She believed that some things were wrong.

这些看法，在今天看来，是常识。但在当时，它们被认定为“罪行”。1969年，她因此被捕。

These views, seen today, are common sense. But at the time they were deemed "crimes." In 1969 she was arrested because of them.

三、她其实是一个很普通的人

III. She Was, in Fact, a Very Ordinary Person

在成为“烈士”之前，张志新首先是一个普通的女人。

Before she became a "martyr," Zhang Zhixin was first of all an ordinary woman.

她有家庭，有孩子。她的丈夫也是干部，他们一起抚育着子女。她会弹琴，喜欢音乐，据说她还能拉一手不错的小提琴。她关心孩子，也关心丈夫。

She had a family, and children. Her husband was a cadre too, and together they raised their children. She could play the piano and loved music; it is said she played a decent violin as well. She cared for her children, and for her husband.

这一点很重要，因为它让这个故事的重量完全不同。 她不是一个“天生就不怕死”的英雄。她是一个有牵挂、有软肋、有牵肠挂肚的家的人。她做出那个选择的时候，是知道自己会失去什么的。

This matters, because it makes the weight of the story entirely different. She was not a hero "born without fear of death." She was a person with attachments, with weaknesses, with a family she could never stop worrying about. When she made that choice, she knew what she was going to lose.

在关押期间，她的家人受到了牵连。她的丈夫最终与她办理了离婚。这些细节，读起来比任何豪言壮语都更让人心疼：一个人为了守住自己认为对的东西，不仅失去了自由和生命，还失去了整个家。

During her detention, her family was implicated as well. Her husband in the end divorced her. These details are more painful to read than any grand declaration: for holding to what she believed was right, a person lost not only her freedom and her life, but her whole family.

四、在狱中

IV. In Prison

在狱中，审讯者反复要求她认错、要求她“交代”、要求她放弃自己的观点。给出的条件很明确：只要认错，就可以活。

In prison, her interrogators repeatedly demanded that she admit error, demanded that she "come clean," demanded that she abandon her views. The terms offered were plain: admit you were wrong, and you could live.

她拒绝了。

She refused.

据记载，她在狱中坚持自己的判断，写下了材料，继续表达她的观点。她没有用“我错了”来换取宽大。她还曾说过，一个党员应该有独立思考的权利，应该讲真话。

According to the records, she held to her own judgment in prison, wrote down material, and went on expressing her views. She did not trade "I was wrong" for leniency. She also said, it is recorded, that a Party member should have the right to think independently and should speak the truth.

那几年，她遭受了身体和精神上的严重折磨。她的家人也受到了牵连。但她始终没有改口。

In those years she suffered severe physical and mental torment. Her family was implicated as well. But she never took back her words.

一个人要在那样的处境里坚持“我说的是对的”，需要一种几乎超出常人的意志。

To insist, in circumstances like those, that "what I said is right" requires a will almost beyond the ordinary.

更值得琢磨的是她“坚持”的方式。她不是在搞什么激烈的对抗，她只是“不走回头路”——不改口、不认错、不为了活命而说违心的话。这种坚持看起来是“消极”的，实际上需要最大的主动性。 因为认错只需要一句话，而坚持要一天一天地熬。

What is more worth pondering is the manner of her "holding on." She was not waging any fierce confrontation; she simply would not "turn back"—she would not take back her words, would not admit error, would not say things she did not believe in order to stay alive. This kind of holding on looks "passive," but in fact it demands the greatest initiative. For admitting error takes only one sentence, while holding on means enduring day after day.

五、1975年4月4日

V. April 4, 1975

1975年4月4日，张志新被处决。她四十五岁。

On April 4, 1975, Zhang Zhixin was executed. She was forty-five.

关于她生命的最后阶段，有一些记载令人难以平静。但在这里，我们更愿意把重点放在另一件事上：她不是因为做了什么“惊天动地”的大事，而是因为坚持了几句“她认为正确的话”。

Some accounts of the last stage of her life are hard to remain calm about. But here we would rather put the emphasis on something else: it was not because she had done some "earth-shaking" deed, but because she held to a few "words she believed to be right."

她不是英雄叙事里的那种“力挽狂澜”的人物。她只是一个干部，一个母亲，一个读过书、会思考的人。她的“不服从”，朴素到了极点——她只是不肯说“我错了”，而她并不认为自己错。

She was not the kind of figure in heroic narratives who "turns back the raging tide." She was just a cadre, a mother, a person who had read books and could think. Her "disobedience" was utterly plain—she simply would not say "I was wrong," and she did not believe she was wrong.

六、四年之后的平反

VI. Rehabilitation Four Years Later

1976年，那段历史结束了。

In 1976, that span of history came to an end.

1978年之后，中国开始系统地为一大批在运动中受冤屈的人平反。1979年3月，中共辽宁省委作出决定，为张志新彻底平反昭雪，追认她为革命烈士。

After 1978, China began systematically to rehabilitate a great many people who had been wronged during the movement. In March 1979, the Liaoning Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China made a decision to completely rehabilitate Zhang Zhixin and clear her name, and posthumously recognized her as a revolutionary martyr.

从她被杀，到被承认为“烈士”，只隔了四年。

From her being killed to her being acknowledged as a "martyr," only four years passed.

这个平反的意义，不只是“为一个人恢复名誉”。它意味着：这个国家和她的党，承认在那段历史里犯过错误，也愿意为错误承担责任。 承认错误，从来比坚持“永远正确”更难，也更需要勇气。

The significance of this rehabilitation was not merely "restoring one person's reputation." It meant that the country and its Party acknowledged that mistakes had been made in that span of history, and were willing to bear responsibility for them. Acknowledging error has always been harder, and has required more courage, than insisting on being "always right."

张志新的名字，被写进了辽宁的烈士名录，也被写进了许多关于那个年代的记述里。她成了一个符号——一个关于“一个人可以坚持到什么程度”的符号。

Zhang Zhixin's name was entered in Liaoning's register of martyrs, and also in many accounts of that era. She became a symbol—a symbol of how far a person can hold on.

她的平反，在当时并不是一件小事。它和一大批冤假错案的纠正一起，构成了那个转折年代里最重要的底色。一个社会能不能进步，很大程度上取决于它敢不敢承认自己曾经错过。 为张志新平反，就是这种承认的一部分。

Her rehabilitation was no small matter at the time. Together with the correction of a great many unjust, false, and wrong cases, it formed the most important undertone of that era of transition. Whether a society can progress depends to a great extent on whether it dares to admit that it has once been wrong. The rehabilitation of Zhang Zhixin was part of that admission.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

张志新的故事，最让人难以平静的地方在于：她所坚持的东西，其实非常简单。

What makes Zhang Zhixin's story hardest to remain calm about is this: what she held to was actually very simple.

她不是要推翻什么，也不是要建立什么。她只是觉得，有些事应该讲道理，有些人应该有独立思考的权利。这在今天，几乎是每个人都默认的东西。

She was not trying to overthrow anything, nor to establish anything. She simply felt that some things should be reasoned out, and that people should have the right to think independently. Today this is almost something everyone takes for granted.

而恰恰是这种“最简单的东西”，在她的年代，需要用命去换。

And it was precisely this "simplest thing" that, in her era, had to be bought with a life.

2026年，我们生活在一个可以自由讨论、自由表达的时代。这个时代不是凭空出现的，它是从张志新们所经历的年代里走出来的。我们今天可以随口说出的话，是有人曾经用生命换来的。

In 2026, we live in an era in which we can discuss freely and speak freely. This era did not appear out of nowhere; it came out of the age that Zhang Zhixin and people like her lived through. The words we can now say offhand were bought by someone with her life.

所以，纪念张志新，不只是为了哀悼一个人。更是为了记住：“可以独立思考”这件事，从来不是理所当然的。它是一份遗产，需要每一代人自己去守住。

So to remember Zhang Zhixin is not only to mourn one person. It is, more, to remember this: the ability to "think independently" was never a matter of course. It is an inheritance that each generation must guard for itself.

也要看到，这种“守住”在今天有了新的形态。我们不必再面对生死的考验，但我们依然要面对另一种压力：在信息如此拥挤、观点如此容易被裹挟的时代，你是选择跟着人群走，还是选择自己判断？ 张志新当年面临的，是“要不要为了活命改口”；我们面临的，是“要不要为了合群放弃判断”。难度不同，但性质相通。

We should also see that this "guarding" has taken a new form today. We no longer have to face the test of life and death, but we still face another kind of pressure: in an age when information is so crowded and opinions so easily swept along, do you choose to follow the crowd, or do you choose to judge for yourself? What Zhang Zhixin faced was "whether to take back her words in order to live"; what we face is "whether to give up judgment in order to fit in." The difficulty differs, but the nature is the same.

她用生命给出的答案是：不改。 这两个字，是这本书里最重的一笔。

The answer she gave with her life was: do not change. These two words are the heaviest stroke in this book.

回过头看，张志新身上最值得敬畏的，不是她“敢”，而是她“信”。她相信讲道理是有意义的，相信一个人应该对自己的判断负责，相信“党员”这个身份本身就意味着要讲真话。正因为她真心相信这些东西，她才会在所有人都在改口的时候，坚持不改。 她的“不服从”，恰恰来自她最深的那种“相信”。这大概是她和那些只想撇清关系的人，最根本的区别。一个人可以因为怀疑而反抗，也可以因为相信而反抗；张志新属于后者，而后者往往更难，因为它没有“反正这个世界不值得”这种轻松可以依靠。

Looking back, what is most to be revered in Zhang Zhixin is not that she "dared," but that she "believed." She believed that reasoning things out was meaningful, believed that a person should be accountable for her own judgment, believed that the very identity of "Party member" meant telling the truth. It was precisely because she truly believed these things that, when everyone was taking back their words, she held firm. Her "disobedience" came, in fact, from her deepest "belief." This is probably the most fundamental difference between her and those who only wanted to distance themselves. A person can rebel out of doubt, or rebel out of belief; Zhang Zhixin belonged to the latter, and the latter is often harder, because it has no easy comfort of "this world isn't worth it anyway" to fall back on.

她的一生，其实没有留下多少著作，也没有留下什么理论体系。她留下的，是一个很单纯的姿态：面对一个要她改口的世界，她说“不”。这个“不”字，在1975年要了她的命，在1979年被这个国家重新认定为“对”。剩下的，就交给每一个后来的人自己去想了。

Her life left behind, in truth, few writings and no theoretical system. What she left was a very simple stance: facing a world that demanded she take back her words, she said "no." That "no" cost her her life in 1975, and in 1979 was reaffirmed by the country as "right." The rest is left for each person who comes after to think through for themselves.

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：张志新，1930年12月20日生于天津，1975年4月4日被处决，年45岁。为通行记载。

1. Basic biographical facts: Zhang Zhixin, born December 20, 1930, in Tianjin; executed on April 4, 1975, aged 45. Commonly recorded account.

2. 政治身份与职务：1950年参加工作，1955年加入中国共产党；曾在辽宁省委宣传部任干事（一说为宣传部干事、理论干部）。为通行记载。

2. Political status and position: began working in 1950; joined the Communist Party of China in 1955; served as an officer in the Publicity Department of the Liaoning Provincial Party Committee (some sources say a publicity department officer, a theoretical cadre). Commonly recorded account.

3. 被捕与狱中情况：1969年被捕；在关押期间坚持自己的观点，拒绝认错。为通行记载。

3. Arrest and imprisonment: arrested in 1969; during detention she held to her views and refused to admit error. Commonly recorded account.

4. 平反：1979年3月，中共辽宁省委为其彻底平反昭雪，追认其为革命烈士。为确证史实（这是官方作出的正式决定）。

4. Rehabilitation: in March 1979, the Liaoning Provincial Committee of the Communist Party of China completely rehabilitated her and cleared her name, and posthumously recognized her as a revolutionary martyr. Well-established historical fact (this was a formal decision made by the authorities).

5. 表述原则：本文依据中国官方公开的平反决定与主流媒体报道的历史事实进行叙述，聚焦于“坚持真理”“独立思考”“后来被党和政府平反昭雪”这一主线。对于具体受刑细节等，本文不作渲染性描述，以免偏离“致敬其精神”的写作目的。

5. Principle of presentation: this article narrates according to the publicly released official rehabilitation decision of China and the historical facts reported by the mainstream media, focusing on the main thread of "holding to the truth," "independent thinking," and "being rehabilitated by the Party and the government afterward." Regarding specific details such as the punishments she suffered, this article offers no sensationalized description, so as not to stray from the purpose of "paying tribute to her spirit."

6. 关于文首引语：“一个党员，应该有自己独立思考的权利”——此类表述见于关于张志新事迹的各类记述中，属于对其观点的概括性转述，并非逐字引用的原文。本文以“她说过的意思”处理，标注为概括而非原文。

6. About the quotation at the beginning: "A Party member should have the right to think independently"—phrasing of this kind appears in various accounts of Zhang Zhixin's deeds; it is a summary paraphrase of her views, not a word-for-word quotation. This article treats it as "the sense of what she said," marking it as a paraphrase rather than an original text.

7. 历史定位：张志新与遇罗克、林昭等人，都是那场运动中因坚持自己的判断而付出生命、并在后来被平反的人。他们的共同点是：最终都被这个国家承认为“错的是当时，不是他们”。

7. Historical placement: Zhang Zhixin, along with Yu Luoke, Lin Zhao, and others, were people who gave their lives during that movement for holding to their own judgment, and who were later rehabilitated. What they share is this: in the end the country acknowledged that "what was wrong was the time, not them."
