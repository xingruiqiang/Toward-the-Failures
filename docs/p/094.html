Chapter 094 · Part 5

遇罗克：一篇《出身论》，一条二十七岁的命

Yu Luoke: One Essay, “On Family Background,” and a Life of Twenty-Seven Years

——“家庭出身，不能决定一个人的好坏。”

— “Family background cannot determine whether a person is good or bad.”

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人94 / 100约 3068 字 · 3068 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，我们很少有人会因为“父母是谁”，而被剥夺上学、工作、结婚的权利。这已经是我们默认的社会底线。

In 2026, few of us would be deprived of the right to go to school, to work, or to marry because of “who our parents are.” This has become a baseline we take for granted.

但在半个多世纪前，中国曾经有过一段历史：一个人的命运，在很多场合，是由他的“家庭出身”决定的。你父母是什么成分，你就可能被打上什么标签。这就是所谓的“血统论”。

But more than half a century ago, China went through a period in which a person's fate was, in many settings, decided by his “family origin.” Whatever class status your parents carried, you might be branded with the same label. This was the so-called “bloodline theory.”

有一个年轻人，用一篇《出身论》，正面挑战了这个逻辑。他因此在二十七岁那年被处决。

One young man challenged this logic head-on with an essay called “On Family Background” (Chushen Lun). For that he was executed at the age of twenty-seven.

九年后，北京市中级人民法院宣告他无罪。

Nine years later, the Beijing Intermediate People's Court declared him not guilty.

他叫遇罗克。

His name was Yu Luoke.

一、一个“出身不好”的年轻人

I. A Young Man of “Bad Origin”

1942年5月1日，遇罗克出生在北京。他的父亲是工程技术人员，母亲做过教师。在那个年代，这样的家庭背景，在某些政治标准下，属于“有问题”的一类。

Yu Luoke was born in Beijing on May 1, 1942. His father was an engineer and technician, and his mother had been a teacher. In those years, under certain political standards, a family background like theirs fell into the category of “having problems.”

遇罗克从小聪明、爱读书、爱思考。他学习成绩很好，也很有正义感。但他很快就意识到一件事：在这个社会里，有些门槛，不是靠成绩能跨过去的，而是靠“出身”。

From childhood Yu Luoke was bright, fond of reading, and given to thinking. He did well in school and had a strong sense of justice. But he soon realized something: in this society, some thresholds could not be crossed by good marks, but only by “origin.”

在升学、就业、参军等很多方面，“家庭成分”成了一个决定性的因素。一个人无论多努力、多优秀，只要“出身不好”，就可能被挡在门外。

In further schooling, employment, military service, and many other areas, “family class status” became a decisive factor. However hard a person worked, however outstanding he was, if his “origin was bad,” he could be shut out.

这种把“父母的身份”直接等同于“子女的品性”的逻辑，就是“血统论”。它的极端表达，就是那句在“文革”初年广为流传的口号——“老子英雄儿好汉，老子反动儿混蛋”。

This logic, which equates “the status of the parents” directly with “the character of the children,” is the “bloodline theory.” Its most extreme expression was the slogan widely circulated in the early years of the Cultural Revolution: “If the father is a hero, the son is a brave man; if the father is a reactionary, the son is a bastard.”

遇罗克不能接受这句话。

Yu Luoke could not accept that sentence.

二、《出身论》

II. “On Family Background”

1966年，遇罗克写下了《出身论》。

In 1966, Yu Luoke wrote “On Family Background” (Chushen Lun).

在这篇文章里，他提出了一个极其简单、也极其有力的观点：一个人的出身，不能决定他的品格、能力和价值。 一个人的好与坏，要看他自己做了什么，而不是看他父母是谁。

In the essay he advanced a view that was extremely simple and extremely powerful: a person's origin cannot determine his character, his abilities, or his worth. Whether a person is good or bad depends on what he himself has done, not on who his parents are.

他还指出，那种“血统论”的逻辑，本质上是把封建时代的“等级制”换了个说法搬了回来。它不符合新社会的平等原则。

He also pointed out that the logic of “bloodline theory” was in essence the old “hierarchy” of feudal times brought back under a new name. It did not accord with the new society's principle of equality.

1967年，这篇文章以“首都红卫兵革命造反总司令部”的名义印发，随后被很多地方转载、传抄。在那个年代，它像一颗石子，扔进了一潭被口号搅浑的水里，激起了巨大的涟漪。

In 1967 the essay was printed and distributed in the name of the “Capital Red Guards Revolutionary Rebel General Headquarters,” and it was then reprinted and copied by hand in many places. In those years it was like a stone thrown into a pool muddied by slogans, raising great ripples.

对很多人来说，这篇文章说出了他们心里憋了很久却不敢说的话。也正因为如此，遇罗克成了某些人的眼中钉。

For many people, the essay said aloud what they had long held back and dared not say. And for that very reason, Yu Luoke became a thorn in the side of certain people.

三、1968年的被捕

III. The Arrest of 1968

1968年1月，遇罗克被捕。

In January 1968, Yu Luoke was arrested.

他面临的压力是巨大的。作为一个“有问题”的年轻人，他做了一件“挑战主流”的事。而这篇文章的影响越大，他就越危险。

The pressure on him was enormous. As a young man who “had problems,” he had done something that “challenged the mainstream.” And the greater the essay's influence, the more dangerous he became.

据记载，在关押期间，他被反复审讯。他坚持自己的观点，没有改口。

According to the records, he was interrogated again and again during his detention. He held to his views and never changed his story.

他并不是没有机会。 如果他愿意低头、愿意承认“自己错了”，也许还有转圜的可能。但他没有。

He was not without a chance. Had he been willing to bow his head and admit that “he was wrong,” there might still have been room to turn things around. But he did not.

1970年3月5日，遇罗克在北京的一处体育场被处决。他二十七岁。

On March 5, 1970, Yu Luoke was executed at a stadium in Beijing. He was twenty-seven.

一个只写了七千多字的年轻人，用一条命，为这篇文章付了账。

A young man who had written just over seven thousand characters paid for that essay with his life.

四、九年后的那份判决

IV. That Verdict Nine Years Later

1976年之后，随着对历史错误的系统清理，遇罗克的案子被重新审视。

After 1976, as historical errors were systematically reviewed and corrected, Yu Luoke's case was reexamined.

1979年11月21日，北京市中级人民法院作出判决，宣告遇罗克无罪。

On November 21, 1979, the Beijing Intermediate People's Court issued a ruling declaring Yu Luoke not guilty.

从他被处决到被宣告无罪，只隔了九年。

Only nine years separated his execution from the declaration of his innocence.

九年在历史里很短，但对一个已经死去的二十七岁的年轻人来说，这九年是一个国家重新认识自己、承认自己错误的过程。

Nine years is very short in history, but for a young man of twenty-seven who was already dead, those nine years were a country's process of coming to know itself anew and admitting its own mistakes.

遇罗克的《出身论》，后来被重新刊印、被反复讨论。他提出来的那个观点——“出身不能决定一个人的价值”——如今已经成为中国社会的普遍共识。

Yu Luoke's “On Family Background” was later reprinted and repeatedly discussed. The view he put forward—that “origin cannot determine a person's worth”—has now become a common consensus in Chinese society.

五、他真正“击中”了什么

V. What He Really Struck At

今天回头看，《出身论》的价值，不只在“反对血统论”这件事上。

Looking back today, the value of “On Family Background” lies not only in opposing bloodline theory.

它真正击中的，是一种思维方式：把人按“类别”划分，然后用类别去判断个体。 这种思维，古今中外都有。它的危险在于：它让一个人不必为自己负责，也不必为自己负责——因为你“属于”哪一类，就已经决定了一切。

What it truly struck was a way of thinking: sorting people into “categories” and then judging the individual by the category. This way of thinking exists in every age and every country. Its danger lies in this: it spares a person from having to answer for himself—because the category you “belong to” has already decided everything.

遇罗克反对的，正是这种“类别决定论”。他要说的是：每个人都是一个具体的人，都有自己的选择，也都要为自己的选择负责。

What Yu Luoke opposed was precisely this “category determinism.” What he wanted to say was: every person is a concrete individual, each with his own choices, and each answerable for those choices.

这个观点，在1970年是危险的；在2026年，仍然值得被反复确认。因为今天我们依然会看到，有人把“地域”“学历”“行业”“性别”当成一个标签，去定义一个具体的人。

This view was dangerous in 1970; in 2026 it is still worth affirming again and again. Because today we still see people take “region,” “education,” “profession,” or “gender” as a label and use it to define a concrete human being.

遇罗克用命换来的那句话，其实一直没说完：不要用标签去看人。

The sentence Yu Luoke bought with his life has in fact never been fully said: do not look at people through labels.

值得注意的是，《出身论》并不是一篇“情绪化”的文章。它讲的是逻辑，用的是分析。遇罗克不是靠喊口号去反对口号，他是用说理去对抗不讲理。这一点，在那个年代尤其难得——在一场以情绪和立场为主导的运动里，一个年轻人选择了讲道理。这本身就是最有力量的反抗。

It is worth noting that “On Family Background” is not an “emotional” essay. It argues by logic and proceeds by analysis. Yu Luoke did not oppose slogans by shouting slogans; he fought unreason with reasoning. That was especially rare in those years—in a movement driven by emotion and position, a young man chose to argue. That in itself was the most powerful form of resistance.

而且，他要反对的那套逻辑，其实也不只属于某一个年代。只要我们还习惯用“你是什么出身”“你在哪个群体”“你属于哪一类”，去提前判断一个人的好坏，那套逻辑就还在。遇罗克用生命守住的那条底线，需要每一代人自己去重新守住。

And the logic he opposed does not really belong to one era alone. As long as we are still in the habit of judging a person in advance by “what his origin is,” “what group he is in,” “what category he belongs to,” that logic is still with us. The line Yu Luoke held with his life has to be held again, by each generation for itself.

六、一篇七千字的文章，为什么重要

VI. Why a Seven-Thousand-Character Essay Matters

今天回头看，《出身论》的篇幅并不长，思想也不算复杂。它讲的道理，几乎每个受过教育的人都会同意。

Looking back today, “On Family Background” is not long, and its ideas are not especially complex. The reasoning in it would be accepted by almost anyone with an education.

但它出现的时间，决定了他的分量。

But the moment when it appeared decided its weight.

它出现在一个全社会都在用“出身”给人排队的年代。它出现在一个连很多“出身好”的人都默认这套逻辑的年代。它出现在一个说错一句话就可能万劫不复的年代。

It appeared in an era when the whole society lined people up by “origin.” It appeared in an era when even many people of “good origin” tacitly accepted this logic. It appeared in an era when one wrong sentence could mean utter ruin.

在这样一个年代里，一个“出身不好”的年轻人，站出来说“你们这套逻辑是错的”——这不是学术研讨，这是拿命在赌。

In such an era, a young man of “bad origin” stood up and said, “Your logic is wrong”—that was not academic discussion; it was a wager with his life.

而且他赌的不是“我一定能赢”。他大概从一开始就知道，自己很可能会输。他知道会输，还是站了出来。 这才是《出身论》真正的重量所在。

And what he wagered was not that “I am sure to win.” He probably knew from the start that he was likely to lose. He knew he would lose, and he stood up anyway. That is where the true weight of “On Family Background” lies.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

遇罗克的故事，最让人心痛的地方在于：他提出的观点，本不该需要有人用生命去证明。

What hurts most about Yu Luoke's story is this: the view he put forward should never have required anyone to prove it with his life.

“出身不能决定一个人的好坏”——这句话，听起来如此普通，普通到今天几乎没有人会反对它。可就是这句话，在三十多年前，要用一个二十七岁年轻人的命来换。

“Origin cannot decide whether a person is good or bad”—that sentence sounds so ordinary, so ordinary that today almost no one would oppose it. Yet it was precisely that sentence, more than thirty years ago, that had to be bought with the life of a young man of twenty-seven.

2026年的我们，享受着“平等”这两个字带来的日常便利：可以考学、可以找工作、可以凭本事竞争，而不必先交代父母是谁。这份“普通”，是有代价的。

We in 2026 enjoy the everyday conveniences that the word “equality” brings: we can take examinations, look for work, and compete on our own merits without first having to explain who our parents are. That “ordinariness” came at a price.

所以，记住遇罗克，不只是为了哀悼。更是为了提醒自己：不要轻易用任何标签去定义一个人。 这不是什么高深的道理，这是一个人用二十七年的生命，留下的最朴素的一句话。

So remembering Yu Luoke is not only for mourning. It is also to remind ourselves: do not lightly define a person with any label. This is not a profound idea; it is the plainest sentence a man left behind with his twenty-seven years of life.

而在今天，这句话还有另一层意思：面对任何一套“看起来很有道理”的归类逻辑，都别忘了先问一句——它对不对？ 遇罗克当年就是这么问的。他没有被那句响亮的标语唬住，他只是停下来，用脑子想了一遍。这个动作看起来很小，但在某些年代，它就是一个人能做的全部抵抗。

And today that sentence carries another meaning: faced with any system of classification that “looks very reasonable,” do not forget to ask one thing first—is it right? That is how Yu Luoke asked it back then. He was not overawed by that resounding slogan; he simply stopped and thought it through with his own head. The gesture looks very small, but in certain eras it was all the resistance one person could offer.

《出身论》后来被一次次重印，被一遍遍讨论。人们在文章里读到的，其实不只是“反对血统论”这一个观点，而是一种态度：面对任何被当成“天经地义”的说法，你都可以停下来，用自己的脑子想一遍。这个动作并不需要多大的勇气——除非它恰好出现在一个不让你想的年代。

“On Family Background” was later reprinted again and again and discussed over and over. What people read in it was not only the single idea of “opposing bloodline theory,” but an attitude: in the face of anything taken as “self-evidently true,” you can stop and think it through with your own head. That gesture does not require much courage—unless it happens to occur in an era that does not let you think.

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：遇罗克，1942年5月1日生于北京，1970年3月5日被处决，年27岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Yu Luoke, born in Beijing on May 1, 1942, executed on March 5, 1970, aged 27. Confirmed historical fact.

2. 《出身论》：写于1966年，1967年以“首都红卫兵革命造反总司令部”名义印发（一说为该组织下属部门）。为通行记载。

2. “On Family Background” (Chushen Lun): written in 1966, printed and distributed in 1967 in the name of the “Capital Red Guards Revolutionary Rebel General Headquarters” (some accounts say a subordinate department of that organization). Generally accepted record.

3. 被捕与处决：1968年1月被捕；1970年3月5日在北京被处决。为通行记载。

3. Arrest and execution: arrested in January 1968; executed in Beijing on March 5, 1970. Generally accepted record.

4. 平反：1979年11月21日，北京市中级人民法院宣告遇罗克无罪。为确证史实。

4. Redress: on November 21, 1979, the Beijing Intermediate People's Court declared Yu Luoke not guilty. Confirmed historical fact.

5. 表述原则：本文依据中国司法机关的正式平反结论及国内公开的史料叙述展开，聚焦“提出观点—遭受冤屈—国家正式平反”这一主线，不采用境外政治化叙述框架，不作煽情渲染。

5. Principle of presentation: this essay is based on the formal redress conclusions of China's judicial authorities and on openly available domestic historical accounts. It focuses on the main line—putting forward a view, suffering injustice, and formal state redress—and does not adopt an overseas politicized narrative framework or engage in sensationalism.

6. 关于《出身论》的字数与具体文本：《出身论》全文数千字（一说七千余字），曾被多方转载。其文本在流传中有不同版本，本文只引用其核心观点，不逐字引用争议性表述。

6. On the length and specific text of “On Family Background” (Chushen Lun): the full essay runs to several thousand characters (some accounts say just over seven thousand) and was reprinted by many parties. Its text circulated in different versions; this essay quotes only its core views and does not reproduce disputed formulations word for word.

7. 遇罗克之妹遇罗锦，后来也曾因相关事件经历坎坷，并留下回忆文字。其家族的其他遭遇，本文不作展开。

7. Yu Luoke's younger sister, Yu Luojin, later went through hardships connected with these events and left behind recollections. This essay does not go into the other experiences of the family.

8. 历史定位：遇罗克、张志新、林昭是那一时期因坚持自己的判断而付出生命、并在后来被国家正式平反的代表人物。他们被平反这件事本身，说明了这个国家最终给出的答案是：错的不是他们。

8. Historical place: Yu Luoke, Zhang Zhixin, and Lin Zhao are representative figures of that period who gave their lives for holding to their own judgment and who were later formally rehabilitated by the state. The very fact of their rehabilitation shows the answer the country ultimately gave: they were not the ones who were wrong.
