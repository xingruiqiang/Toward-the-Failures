科马洛夫：他坐上了一条回去不了的飞船

Komarov: He Boarded a Spacecraft That Could Not Come Home

——“联盟一号”的最后一次飞行。

—The Last Flight of Soyuz 1.

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人88 / 100约 3053 字 · 3053 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，我们看航天发射直播，习惯了“零失误”的仪式感。有故障就推迟，有问题就返回，载人航天的第一原则是“安全”。

In 2026 we watch rocket launches live and have grown used to the ceremony of "zero failure." A fault means a delay; a problem means turning back. For human spaceflight, the first principle is safety.

可在1967年的苏联，事情不是这样的。那一年，为了赶在十月革命纪念日之前拿出成绩，为了在太空竞赛里“不能让美国抢先”，一个已经知道有严重隐患的飞船，还是被发射了。

But in the Soviet Union of 1967, things were not like that. That year, in order to produce results before the anniversary of the October Revolution, and so as not to "let the Americans get ahead" in the space race, a spacecraft already known to have serious hazards was launched anyway.

坐上去的那个人，叫弗拉基米尔·科马洛夫。他四十岁。

The man who boarded it was named Vladimir Komarov. He was forty.

他知道那条船有问题。他还是坐了上去。

He knew the ship had problems. He boarded it all the same.

一、从试飞员到航天员

I. From Test Pilot to Cosmonaut

1927年3月16日，科马洛夫出生在莫斯科。他从小就喜欢飞机，后来成了一名出色的空军飞行员和试飞员。

Komarov was born in Moscow on March 16, 1927. He loved aircraft from childhood, and later became an outstanding air force pilot and test pilot.

试飞员这个职业，本身就是和风险打交道的——他要驾驶还没有人飞过的新飞机，去验证它们能不能飞。一个人选择做试飞员，就已经说明了他对“风险”这件事的态度。

The profession of test pilot is by its very nature a dealing with risk—flying new aircraft that no one has flown, to determine whether they can fly at all. That a person chooses to become a test pilot already says something about his attitude toward "risk."

1960年，他入选了苏联的航天员队伍。1964年，他作为指令长，乘坐“上升1号”飞船进入太空——那是人类第一次多人同时进入太空的飞行。科马洛夫因此成为苏联的功勋航天员。

In 1960 he was selected for the Soviet cosmonaut corps. In 1964, as commander, he flew into space aboard the Voskhod 1 spacecraft—the first flight in which more than one person entered space at the same time. Komarov thereby became a decorated cosmonaut of the Soviet Union.

但真正把他写进历史的，是1967年的那次飞行。

But what truly wrote him into history was the flight of 1967.

二、“联盟一号”的问题

II. The Problems of Soyuz 1

1967年，苏联正在研制新一代的“联盟号”飞船。按照计划，“联盟1号”要先发射，与随后发射的“联盟2号”在轨道上对接，进行首次太空对接试验。

In 1967 the Soviet Union was developing a new generation of spacecraft, the Soyuz. Under the plan, Soyuz 1 would launch first and then dock in orbit with the Soyuz 2 launched afterward, to conduct the first space docking test.

问题是，“联盟号”的测试极不顺利。飞船在无人试飞中多次失败，技术缺陷一大堆。参与研制的工程师和航天员都清楚：这艘船还没准备好。

The problem was that the Soyuz testing had gone extremely badly. The spacecraft failed repeatedly in unmanned test flights, and technical defects were piled up everywhere. The engineers and cosmonauts involved were all clear: this ship was not ready.

但当时的政治压力极大。苏联在太空竞赛中屡遭挫折，急于拿出一项重大成果。而“联盟1号”的发射日期，被设定在了一个重要的政治节点之前。

But the political pressure at the time was enormous. The Soviet Union had suffered repeated setbacks in the space race and was eager for a major achievement. And the launch date of Soyuz 1 was set before an important political milestone.

有材料显示，科马洛夫本人在发射前就清楚这艘船的问题。他把自己的一些心事托付给了朋友，也做过一些像是在“告别”的事。

Some materials indicate that Komarov himself was clear about the ship's problems before launch. He entrusted certain private concerns to friends, and did things that seemed like a "farewell."

但任务还是执行了。

But the mission went ahead anyway.

三、天上的故障

III. Failure in the Sky

1967年4月23日，“联盟1号”发射升空。

On April 23, 1967, Soyuz 1 lifted off.

一进入轨道，故障就接连出现。最致命的问题是：飞船的太阳能电池板没有正常展开，导致供电严重不足。姿态控制也出现严重问题，飞船无法正常调整方向。

The moment it entered orbit, failures came one after another. The most deadly problem was that the spacecraft's solar panels did not deploy properly, leaving the power supply severely short. Attitude control also failed badly, and the ship could not properly adjust its orientation.

地面和科马洛夫想了很多办法，试图手动控制飞船，试图修复——都没有成功。原定的“联盟2号”对接任务被取消。

The ground and Komarov tried many methods, attempting to control the spacecraft by hand, attempting repairs—none succeeded. The originally planned docking with Soyuz 2 was cancelled.

飞船在天上出了故障，而他能做的，只有等待返回的那一刻。

The spacecraft had failed up in the sky, and all he could do was wait for the moment of return.

四、降落伞没有打开

IV. The Parachute Did Not Open

1967年4月24日，科马洛夫执行返回程序。

On April 24, 1967, Komarov carried out the return procedure.

返回舱要穿过大气层，然后依次打开引导伞、减速伞和主伞，最后软着陆。这是整套流程里最关键的环节。

The descent module had to pass through the atmosphere, then deploy, in sequence, the drogue parachute, the braking parachute, and the main parachute, before a soft landing. This was the most critical step in the entire sequence.

但主伞没有正常打开。备用伞也被卡住了——据后来的调查，降落伞系统存在设计缺陷。

But the main parachute did not open properly. The backup parachute was jammed as well—according to the later investigation, the parachute system had a design flaw.

返回舱以极高的速度，直接砸向地面。

The descent module plunged straight into the ground at enormous speed.

科马洛夫殉难。他是人类第一位在执行航天任务中牺牲的航天员。

Komarov was killed. He was the first human being to die in the course of a space mission.

有报道称，在坠地前的最后时刻，地面监听站（一说土耳其的监听站）听到了科马洛夫愤怒的声音。但这些说法存在争议，官方从未确认。我们能确定的，是那声巨大的撞击，和一位四十岁航天员的死亡。

There are reports that in the final moments before impact, a ground listening station—one account says a station in Turkey—heard Komarov's angry voice. But these accounts are disputed, and the authorities never confirmed them. What we can be certain of is that enormous impact, and the death of a forty-year-old cosmonaut.

五、一场盛大的国葬

V. A Grand State Funeral

科马洛夫去世后，苏联为他举行了隆重的国葬。他的骨灰被安放进克里姆林宫的墙内。他被追授为“苏联英雄”，被官方称为“为科学献身的烈士”。

After Komarov's death, the Soviet Union held an elaborate state funeral for him. His ashes were placed in the Kremlin Wall. He was posthumously awarded the title Hero of the Soviet Union, and officially called a "martyr who gave his life for science."

但很多人心里清楚：他不仅是“献身”，他更像是被一套不容置疑的机器推上去的。

But many people knew in their hearts: he did not merely "give his life"; he was, rather, pushed up there by a machine that would not be questioned.

这一点，让他的死格外沉重。他不是在不知道危险的情况下冒险，而是在清楚危险的情况下，被要求去执行。“牺牲”和“被牺牲”，是两件不同的事。

This is what makes his death so heavy. He did not take a risk without knowing the danger; he was required to carry out the mission while fully aware of the danger. "Sacrifice" and "being sacrificed" are two different things.

他的死，也迫使苏联重新审视“联盟号”的设计。此后，“联盟号”经过了大量改进，成为世界上最可靠、服役时间最长的载人飞船之一——至今仍在飞。科马洛夫用性命换来的教训，被写进了后来的每一艘飞船里。

His death also forced the Soviet Union to re-examine the design of the Soyuz. Afterward, the Soyuz underwent extensive improvements and became one of the most reliable and longest-serving crewed spacecraft in the world—it is still flying today. The lesson Komarov bought with his life was written into every spacecraft that followed.

六、写给2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

科马洛夫的故事，最难面对的地方在于：它不是一个“英雄壮烈牺牲”的简单故事。

The hardest thing about Komarov's story is this: it is not a simple story of a "hero dying gloriously."

如果只说他“明知有危险仍执行任务、为国捐躯”，就把其中真正值得警惕的东西盖住了。他坐上去，是因为那个系统不允许他说“不”。在一个“不能失败”的氛围里，个人的安危，往往是最先被牺牲掉的东西。

If we say only that he "carried out the mission knowing the danger and died for his country," we cover up what is truly worth guarding against. He boarded because the system did not permit him to say no. In an atmosphere where "failure is not allowed," a person's own safety is often the first thing sacrificed.

2026年，我们身边也有类似的结构：一个项目明明知道有风险，但“领导要求”“节点已定”“不能掉链子”，于是问题被压下去，直到它爆发。

In 2026 there are similar structures around us: a project known to carry risk, but "the boss requires it," "the deadline is fixed," "we can't drop the ball"—so the problem is suppressed until it blows up.

科马洛夫提醒我们：把一个人推到不安全的船上，然后称他为英雄，这不是致敬，这是一种转嫁。

Komarov reminds us: to push a person onto an unsafe ship and then call him a hero is not a tribute; it is a shifting of blame.

而真正对他最好的纪念，是那艘后来被反复改进、飞了半个多世纪、再也没有出过类似事故的“联盟号”。让悲剧不再重演，比把悲剧写成颂歌，更有意义。

And the truest memorial to him is that Soyuz which was repeatedly improved, which flew for more than half a century, and which never suffered a similar accident again. To keep a tragedy from repeating is more meaningful than to turn it into a hymn of praise.

最后还有一点值得记住：科马洛夫不是被动地被“送上”那艘船的。他是一名试飞员，是一名航天员，他比任何人都清楚那艘船的问题。他选择上去，有他自己的专业判断和责任意识——哪怕这个选择里有无奈，也有他不愿意让别人替他承担的部分。这让他既是一个受害者，也是一个有尊严的执行者。 这两件事同时成立，才是完整的他。

One last thing worth remembering: Komarov was not passively "sent" onto that ship. He was a test pilot, a cosmonaut, and he understood the ship's problems better than anyone. His choice to go up carried his own professional judgment and sense of responsibility—even though that choice held helplessness, and a part he was unwilling to let others bear in his place. This makes him both a victim and a dignified executor. Both things are true at once, and only then is he whole.

今天，“联盟号”仍然是往返国际空间站最主要的载人飞船，被公认为世界上最可靠的载人航天系统之一。每次它平安升空、平安返回，某种意义上，都是对那一次事故最好的回应。

Today the Soyuz is still the main crewed spacecraft carrying people to and from the International Space Station, and is widely regarded as one of the most reliable human spaceflight systems in the world. Each time it lifts off safely and returns safely, in a sense it is the best possible answer to that one accident.

七、后记

VII. Afterword

科马洛夫的名字，被刻在了月球背面的一座环形山上，和加加林、和无数为太空献身的人一起。在地球上，也有一颗小行星以他的名字命名。这些纪念当然都不足以补偿一个四十岁的生命，但它们至少说明：人类没有忘记他是怎么死的，也没有忘记那一次死亡之后，世界为此做出的改变。

Komarov's name is carved on a crater on the far side of the Moon, together with Gagarin and countless others who gave their lives to space. On Earth, an asteroid is also named after him. These memorials can of course never make up for a forty-year-old life, but they at least show that humanity has not forgotten how he died, nor forgotten the changes the world made afterward because of that death.

每一年4月24日，俄罗斯的航天人都会举行纪念活动。国际航天界也把他列为人类航天史上最重要的殉难者之一。这些纪念当然不能改变任何已经发生的事，但它们至少在反复提醒后来的人：安全，是用代价换来的；而每一个代价，都曾经是一个具体的人。

Every year on April 24, Russian space workers hold commemorative events. The international space community also lists him among the most important martyrs in the history of human spaceflight. These memorials can of course change nothing that has already happened, but they at least repeatedly remind those who come after: safety is bought with a price, and every price was once a specific human being.

科马洛夫的名字，就这样留在了太空探索的纪念碑上——不是因为他成功了，而是因为他在一个不允许说“不”的位置上，仍然选择了自己去承担。这份承担，比任何一枚勋章都更有分量。

Komarov's name thus remains on the monument of space exploration—not because he succeeded, but because, in a position where saying no was not allowed, he still chose to bear it himself. That bearing carries more weight than any medal.

他留下的，是一份沉重的“资产”。正是因为他的死，苏联对“联盟号”做了彻底的重新设计：降落伞系统、生命保障系统、供电系统，几乎全部推倒重来。此后半个多世纪，“联盟号”成了世界上最可靠的载人飞船。用一个人的命，换回一整套安全标准——这句话听起来残酷，但它就是航天史真实发生过的逻辑。

What he left behind was a heavy "asset." Precisely because of his death, the Soviet Union completely redesigned the Soyuz: the parachute system, the life-support system, the power system—almost everything was rebuilt from scratch. Over the following half century, the Soyuz became the most reliable crewed spacecraft in the world. To trade one person's life for an entire set of safety standards—the sentence sounds cruel, but that is the logic that actually played out in the history of spaceflight.

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：弗拉基米尔·科马洛夫（Vladimir Komarov），1927年3月16日生于莫斯科，1967年4月24日殉难于“联盟1号”返回途中，年40岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic facts of his life: Vladimir Komarov, born March 16, 1927, in Moscow; died April 24, 1967, during the return of Soyuz 1, aged 40. Well-established historical fact.

2. 早年与试飞员经历：曾任空军飞行员、试飞员，1960年入选航天员。为确证史实。

2. Early years and test-pilot career: served as an air force pilot and test pilot; selected as a cosmonaut in 1960. Well-established historical fact.

3. “上升1号”飞行：1964年执行，为人类首次多人航天飞行。为确证史实。

3. The Voskhod 1 flight: carried out in 1964, the first multi-person spaceflight in human history. Well-established historical fact.

4. “联盟1号”任务：1967年4月23日发射，原计划与“联盟2号”对接。为确证史实。

4. The Soyuz 1 mission: launched April 23, 1967, originally planned to dock with Soyuz 2. Well-established historical fact.

5. 故障：太阳能电池板未正常展开、姿态控制故障。为确证史实（事后调查有明确记载）。

5. Failures: solar panels failing to deploy properly, and attitude-control failure. Well-established historical fact (clearly recorded in the post-flight investigation).

6. 降落伞未打开：返回时主伞与备用伞均失效，返回舱高速坠地，科马洛夫殉难。为确证史实。降落伞系统的设计缺陷在后来的调查中被确认。

6. Parachute failure: on return, both the main and backup parachutes failed, the descent module hit the ground at high speed, and Komarov was killed. Well-established historical fact. The design flaw in the parachute system was confirmed in the later investigation.

7. “他事先知道飞船有问题”：这一说法广为流传，主要来源包括后来苏联航天员及部分当事人的回忆（如赫鲁晓夫之子谢尔盖·赫鲁晓夫的回忆等）。辨析——这些回忆有一定的可信度，但属于事后追述，具体细节（如他是否明确表达过“必死”的判断）无法严格确证。本文采用“他很清楚这艘船的问题”这一相对克制的表述。

7. "He knew beforehand that the spacecraft had problems": this claim is widely circulated, with sources mainly including the later recollections of Soviet cosmonauts and some of those involved (such as the recollections of Sergei Khrushchev, son of Khrushchev). Assessment—these recollections have a certain credibility, but they are retrospective accounts, and specific details (such as whether he explicitly expressed a judgment that he would surely die) cannot be strictly confirmed. This text adopts the relatively restrained formulation "he was very clear about this ship's problems."

8. 关于他临终前的“声音”：有报道称监听站收到过他的声音（甚至有具体内容），来源多为外媒转述，无法核实，官方亦未确认。本文明确标注“存在争议”，不作为事实采用。

8. Regarding his "voice" before death: some reports say the listening station received his voice (with even specific content), but the sources are mostly secondhand accounts from foreign media, unverifiable, and never confirmed by the authorities. This text explicitly marks it as "disputed" and does not adopt it as fact.

9. 评价定位：本文不采用“单纯英雄牺牲”的叙事框架，因为该框架会掩盖当时决策机制的系统性问题。同时，也不否定科马洛夫本人的专业素养与勇气。

9. Evaluative positioning: this text does not adopt the narrative frame of "simple heroic sacrifice," because that frame would obscure the systemic problems of the decision-making mechanism of the time. At the same time, it does not deny Komarov's own professional competence and courage.
