尼采：生前无人问津的“疯子”

Nietzsche: The “Madman” Nobody Noticed in His Lifetime

——他说“上帝已死”，然后被世界当成了疯子

— He said “God is dead,” and the world took him for a madman

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者17 / 100约 2347 字 · 2347 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

在2026年的今天，尼采是那种被引用最多、也最容易被误读的思想家。“上帝已死”“超人”“那些杀不死我的使我更强大”——这些话被印在T恤上、贴在健身房里、写进无数鸡汤语录。可如果你走进1880年代欧洲的廉价旅馆，你会看到一个完全不同的人：一个长期偏头痛到呕吐、视力近乎崩溃、靠微薄退休金漂泊流浪、一本书也卖不出去的落魄学者。

In 2026, Nietzsche is the kind of thinker who is quoted more than anyone and misread more easily than anyone. “God is dead,” “the overman,” “what does not kill me makes me stronger”—these phrases are printed on T-shirts, taped up in gyms, written into countless inspirational quotes. But if you walked into a cheap boardinghouse in 1880s Europe, you would see a completely different person: a down-and-out scholar with migraines so severe they made him vomit, eyesight on the verge of collapse, drifting from place to place on a meager pension, unable to sell a single book.

他写出了要影响整个二十世纪的思想，却没等到任何一个读者。

He wrote the ideas that would shape the whole of the twentieth century—yet he never lived to see a single reader.

一、24岁当上教授，35岁失去一切

I. A Professor at Twenty-Four, Stripped of Everything at Thirty-Five

1844年10月15日，尼采出生在普鲁士萨克森州的勒肯，一个牧师家庭。1849年，父亲去世；不久，弟弟也夭折。他从小在一群女性——母亲、祖母、姑母、妹妹——的包围中长大。

On October 15, 1844, Nietzsche was born in Röcken, in the Prussian province of Saxony, into a pastor's family. In 1849 his father died; not long after, his younger brother also died. He grew up surrounded by women—his mother, grandmother, aunts, and sister.

1869年，24岁的尼采还没拿到博士学位，就被巴塞尔大学聘为古典语文学教授。这在学术史上几乎是奇迹。

In 1869, at twenty-four, before he had even earned his doctorate, Nietzsche was appointed professor of classical philology at the University of Basel. In the history of scholarship this was little short of a miracle.

可十年后，1879年，他因为严重的偏头痛、呕吐、视力崩溃，辞去了教授职务。

But ten years later, in 1879, severe migraines, vomiting, and failing eyesight forced him to resign his professorship.

此后十年，他成了一个欧洲的流浪者：靠一点微薄的退休金，在瑞士、意大利、法国的廉价旅馆之间辗转。没有家，没有职业，没有弟子，也没有读者。

For the next ten years he became a wanderer across Europe: on a little pension, he drifted among cheap lodgings in Switzerland, Italy, and France. No home, no profession, no students, no readers.

二、他亲手推倒了曾经的偶像

II. He Tore Down His Own Idols with His Own Hands

年轻时，他把作曲家瓦格纳当作精神导师。后来，他看清了瓦格纳身上的宗教化、民族主义和反犹倾向，最终与之决裂。

As a young man he took the composer Wagner as his spiritual mentor. Later, he came to see the religiosity, the nationalism, and the antisemitism in Wagner, and finally broke with him.

“亲手推倒自己曾经最崇拜的人”——这件事，尼采做过不止一次。他的一生，就是不断地追问、否定、再重建。

“Tearing down with his own hands the person he had once most admired”—this was something Nietzsche did more than once. His whole life was a ceaseless process of questioning, negating, and rebuilding.

三、十年，写出了整个二十世纪的思想

III. Ten Years That Wrote the Thought of an Entire Century

1883到1885年，他用诗一样的语言写下了《查拉图斯特拉如是说》。

From 1883 to 1885 he wrote Thus Spoke Zarathustra in language as dense as poetry.

之后是《善恶的彼岸》《论道德的谱系》《偶像的黄昏》《反基督》……

Then came Beyond Good and Evil, On the Genealogy of Morality, Twilight of the Idols, The Antichrist…

“上帝已死”——他在《快乐的科学》里写下这句话。这不是庆祝，是警告：当支撑整个西方价值体系的那根柱子倒了，人，会掉进虚无。

“God is dead”—he wrote those words in The Gay Science. This was not a celebration but a warning: when the pillar that upholds the entire Western value system falls, human beings will drop into nihilism.

“那些杀不死我的，使我更强大。”

“What does not kill me makes me stronger.”

“与怪物战斗的人，应当小心自己不要成为怪物。”

“Whoever fights monsters should see to it that in the process he does not become a monster.”

这些句子，如今被无数人挂在嘴边。可在他写下的当时，这些书，几乎没有人读。

These lines are now on everyone's lips. But when he wrote them, almost no one read these books.

四、1889年1月：都灵街头的崩溃

IV. January 1889: The Collapse on a Turin Street

1889年1月3日，尼采在意大利都灵的街头出事，被警察带走了。那天到底发生了什么，至今没有人能确定。

On January 3, 1889, Nietzsche broke down on a street in Turin, Italy, and was taken away by the police. What actually happened that day, no one has ever been able to establish.

流传最广的一个版本是：他看见一匹马被马夫鞭打，冲上去抱住马的脖子失声痛哭，喊着“我受苦受难的兄弟啊”，然后瘫倒在地。

The most widely circulated version is this: he saw a horse being whipped by its driver, rushed up, threw his arms around the horse's neck, and wept aloud, crying, “My suffering brother!”—then collapsed to the ground.

但必须说清楚：这个故事没有确凿的证据。

But it must be said plainly: there is no solid evidence for this story.

它唯一的来源，是1900年——也就是事发十一年后——一篇意大利文章里，转述房东菲诺“据说”告诉某位记者的话。而房东在自己详细的回忆里，从来没提过马的事；尼采同时代的好友奥韦尔贝克，也没有提过。

Its only source is a 1900 Italian article—eleven years after the event—relaying what the landlord Fino was said to have told a journalist. Yet in the landlord's own detailed memoir, the horse is never mentioned; nor did Nietzsche's contemporary friend Overbeck mention it.

这是一则极其动人的传说。它成了后世电影、小说反复书写的意象。但它不是史实。

This is an extraordinarily moving legend. It became an image endlessly rewritten by later films and novels. But it is not historical fact.

可以确定的是：接下来的几天里，尼采寄出了一批“疯癫的信”。这些信署名“狄俄尼索斯”，有的还寄给了俾斯麦和瓦格纳的遗孀。朋友们读到信，赶紧赶到都灵，把他接回了巴塞尔。

What can be established is this: over the days that followed, Nietzsche sent out a batch of “mad letters.” They were signed “Dionysus,” and some were addressed to Bismarck and to Wagner's widow. When his friends read them, they hurried to Turin and brought him back to Basel.

五、最后十一年，和一个篡改他的妹妹

V. The Last Eleven Years, and a Sister Who Rewrote Him

1889到1900年，尼采精神失常，由母亲和妹妹照顾，最后在魏玛度过余生。

From 1889 to 1900 Nietzsche was mentally incapacitated, cared for by his mother and sister, and spent his final years in Weimar.

1893年，妹妹伊丽莎白从巴拉圭回来——她的殖民计划彻底失败，丈夫在那里自杀。此后，她一步步掌控了尼采全部著作的出版权。

In 1893 his sister Elisabeth returned from Paraguay—her colonial venture had failed utterly, and her husband had taken his own life there. After that, she gradually took control of the publication rights to all of Nietzsche's writings.

她做了什么？

What did she do?

她篡改、拼接、编造，硬生生拼出了一本尼采从未写过的书《权力意志》；她把他包装成一个反犹主义者、民族主义者——也就是后来纳粹思想的先驱。

She altered, spliced, and fabricated, piecing together a book Nietzsche had never written, The Will to Power; she packaged him as an antisemite and a nationalist—that is, as a forerunner of later Nazi ideology.

这是哲学史上最大的误读之一。尼采本人，明确反对反犹主义，也反对民族主义。

This is one of the greatest misreadings in the history of philosophy. Nietzsche himself was emphatically opposed to antisemitism, and to nationalism as well.

1900年8月25日，尼采死于肺炎。

On August 25, 1900, Nietzsche died of pneumonia.

而他的名声，恰恰是在这最后十年、在他已经无法再思考的时候，才开始到来的。

And his fame arrived precisely in those last ten years, when he could no longer think.

六、结语：致2026年，每一个“不被看见”的人

VI. Conclusion: To Everyone in 2026 Who Is “Not Seen”

尼采的一生，是一场彻底的“失败”：

Nietzsche's life was a thoroughgoing “failure”:

他24岁登上学术顶峰，35岁失去一切；

at twenty-four he reached the summit of academia; at thirty-five he lost everything;

他写出了要影响一个世纪的书，却一本也卖不动；

he wrote books that would shape a century, yet could not sell a single one;

他最后十一年精神失常，等到成名时，人已不识；

he spent his last eleven years insane, and by the time he became famous, he no longer recognized anyone;

更残忍的是，他死后，被他最亲近的妹妹改造、曲解，成了他一生最反对的那种东西的招牌。

more cruelly still, after his death he was reshaped and distorted by his closest kin, his own sister, and made into a banner for the very thing he had opposed all his life.

但他还是赢了。二十世纪最重要的那些思想——从存在主义到后现代主义——几乎都要回到他那里去找答案。

Yet he still won. The most important ideas of the twentieth century—from existentialism to postmodernism—almost all have to go back to him for answers.

2026年，我们活在一个“算法只看当下”的时代。没有即时反馈，人就容易觉得自己已经失败了：发了文没人看，做了事没人赞，努力的成果三个月都看不到回响。

In 2026, we live in an age when “the algorithm only looks at the present.” Without instant feedback, people easily come to feel they have already failed: a post no one reads, work no one praises, effort whose results show no echo for three months.

尼采告诉你：伟大的东西，往往在它诞生时无人问津。

Nietzsche tells you: great things are often unnoticed at the moment of their birth.

真正可怕的，从来不是“现在没人看见”，而是“为了被看见，你把自己改掉了”。

What is truly terrifying was never “no one sees you now,” but “in order to be seen, you changed yourself.”

他生前没被读懂。但时间，最终站在了他这边。

In his lifetime he was never understood. But time, in the end, took his side.

Appendix: Sources and Notes

主要史料

Primary Sources

1. 尼采著作：《查拉图斯特拉如是说》（1883—1885）、《善恶的彼岸》（1886）、《论道德的谱系》（1887）、《快乐的科学》（1882/1887）、《偶像的黄昏》《反基督》（1888）。

1. Nietzsche's works: Thus Spoke Zarathustra (1883–1885), Beyond Good and Evil (1886), On the Genealogy of Morality (1887), The Gay Science (1882/1887), Twilight of the Idols and The Antichrist (1888).

2. 尼采“疯癫信件”（Wahnbriefe，1889年1月）——寄给布克哈特、科西玛·瓦格纳等人，署名“狄俄尼索斯”，是精神崩溃的直接物证。

2. Nietzsche's “mad letters” (Wahnbriefe, January 1889)—sent to Burckhardt, Cosima Wagner, and others, signed “Dionysus,” the direct material evidence of his mental collapse.

3. 弗兰茨·奥韦尔贝克（Franz Overbeck）、彼得·加斯特等人的记录。

3. Records by Franz Overbeck, Peter Gast, and others.

生卒与事实

Dates and Facts

4. 生于1844年10月15日勒肯，卒于1900年8月25日魏玛。1869年（24岁）任巴塞尔大学古典语文学教授；1879年因健康辞职；1889年1月精神崩溃；1889—1900年由母亲、妹妹照料。

4. Born October 15, 1844, in Röcken; died August 25, 1900, in Weimar. In 1869 (at twenty-four) appointed professor of classical philology at the University of Basel; resigned in 1879 for health reasons; mental collapse in January 1889; cared for by his mother and sister from 1889 to 1900.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

5. “都灵抱马痛哭”——未经证实的传说。唯一来源为1900年《新文选》（Nuova Antologia）一篇转述房东达维德·菲诺之言的匿名文章；菲诺本人详细回忆中未提及，同时代友人（如奥韦尔贝克）亦未提及。

5. The “Turin horse” episode—an unverified legend. Its sole source is an anonymous 1900 article in Nuova Antologia relaying the words of the landlord Davide Fino; Fino himself never mentions it in his detailed memoir, nor do contemporary friends such as Overbeck.

6. 死因：长期被归为梅毒，近年受质疑，有研究提出CADASIL（遗传性脑血管病）等可能，学界无定论。

6. Cause of death: long attributed to syphilis, this has been questioned in recent years, and some researchers have proposed CADASIL (a hereditary cerebrovascular disease) and other possibilities; no scholarly consensus exists.

7. 《权力意志》——并非尼采本人完成之书，系其妹伊丽莎白（及其合作者）于身后拼凑、编排而成，并带有明显的意识形态加工，不可直接当作尼采原意采信。

7. The Will to Power—not a book completed by Nietzsche himself, but assembled and arranged after his death by his sister Elisabeth (and her collaborators), with clear ideological manipulation; it cannot be taken directly as Nietzsche's own meaning.
