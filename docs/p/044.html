亨利·福特：前两次创业都倒掉的“汽车疯子”

Henry Ford: The “Car Fanatic” Whose First Two Ventures Collapsed

——他被投资者赶出自己创办的公司，第三次才把汽车开进千家万户

—Thrown out of his own company by his investors, he only got cars into ordinary homes on his third try

2026年，“创业失败”是互联网上最常见的日常。第一次就成的传奇，其实少之又少。

In 2026, “startup failure” is the most ordinary thing on the internet. Legends of succeeding on the first try are, in truth, very few.

而一百多年前，那个后来让全美国人都买得起汽车的人，前两家汽车公司都倒了：第一家公司解散，第二家公司，他被投资人赶出了自己创办的厂子，连名字都没能留下。

Yet more than a century ago, the man who would later make cars affordable to all Americans saw his first two car companies both fall: the first was dissolved, and from the second he was thrown out by his investors from the very plant he had founded, unable to leave even his name behind.

他叫亨利·福特。他没有停下来。第三次，他办了福特汽车公司。这一次，他改写了整个工业时代。

His name was Henry Ford. He did not stop. On the third try he founded the Ford Motor Company. And this time, he rewrote an entire industrial age.

他的“失败”不是一次，是两次，而且是那种最尴尬的失败：不是市场不要他，是股东不要他。

His “failure” came not once but twice, and of the most embarrassing kind: it was not the market that rejected him, but his shareholders.

一、一个农场少年的机械梦

I. A Farm Boy’s Dream of Machines

1863年，亨利·福特出生在美国密歇根州的一个农场。他从小对机械着迷——别的孩子在田里干活，他更喜欢拆钟表、修机器。

In 1863, Henry Ford was born on a farm in Michigan. From childhood he was obsessed with machinery—while other boys worked in the fields, he preferred taking apart clocks and fixing engines.

十六岁那年，他做了一个在农家看来很“出格”的决定：离开农场，去底特律当机械学徒。他先在一家机械厂做工，后来进了爱迪生照明公司当工程师。

At sixteen he made a decision that seemed outrageous by farm standards: he left the farm to become a machinist’s apprentice in Detroit. He first worked in a machine shop, and later joined the Edison Illuminating Company as an engineer.

1896年，他造出了自己的第一辆“四轮车”（Quadricycle）——一台装在马车型车架上的汽油车。据说，车造好那天，因为门太窄，他干脆把墙拆了，才把车推出去。这辆车的诞生，让当时的发明家爱迪生注意到他，并鼓励他继续做下去。

In 1896 he built his first “quadricycle”—a gasoline vehicle mounted on a carriage-like frame. It is said that on the day it was finished, because the door was too narrow, he simply tore down a wall to push it out. The birth of this vehicle drew the attention of the inventor Thomas Edison, who encouraged him to keep going.

二、两次失败：先倒闭，再被赶走

II. Two Failures: First a Collapse, Then an Ejection

1899年，在底特律一批投资人的支持下，福特参与创办了底特律汽车公司（Detroit Automobile Company）。

In 1899, with the backing of a group of Detroit investors, Ford helped found the Detroit Automobile Company.

问题是：福特对“量产”极其执着，车的质量和成本反复调整；而投资人想的是赶快做出车来卖钱。双方的矛盾越来越大。这家公司撑了不到两年，1901年就解散了。

The problem: Ford was obsessed with mass production and kept adjusting the car’s quality and cost, while the investors wanted cars finished quickly to sell for money. The conflict between them grew. The company lasted less than two years and was dissolved in 1901.

1901年，他又和一批投资人成立了亨利·福特公司（Henry Ford Company）。这一次，冲突更激烈。1902年，投资人请来的顾问亨利·利兰（后来凯迪拉克的创始人）与福特理念冲突，最终投资人把福特赶了出去。

In 1901 he founded the Henry Ford Company with another group of investors. This time the conflict was sharper. In 1902, the consultant the investors brought in—Henry Leland, later the founder of Cadillac—clashed with Ford in his approach, and in the end the investors forced Ford out.

那家公司的名字被换掉，后来变成了“凯迪拉克”。而亨利·福特，被自己创办的第二家公司扫地出门，连公司都改了姓。

The company’s name was changed, and it later became “Cadillac.” And Henry Ford, swept out of the second company he had founded, saw even the firm renamed out from under him.

三、第三次，他找对了伙伴

III. The Third Time, He Found the Right Partners

1903年，福特第三次创业，成立了福特汽车公司。

In 1903, Ford launched his third venture, the Ford Motor Company.

这一次，他吸取了前两次的教训：他找来了一个愿意与他长期配合的伙伴——商人马尔科姆森，并请机械天才哈罗德·威尔斯设计车型。他们还找来了一个关键人物：一个懂“账”和“规模”的年轻人——詹姆斯·库森。

This time he had learned from the first two attempts: he brought in a partner willing to work with him over the long term—the businessman Alexander Malcomson—and had the mechanical genius Harold Wills design the car. They also brought in a key figure: a young man who understood “accounts” and “scale”—James Couzens.

1908年，福特公司推出了T型车。这款车结构简单、皮实耐用、容易修理，价格还能一降再降。它成了美国历史上第一款真正意义上的“大众汽车”。

In 1908, Ford introduced the Model T. Simple in design, sturdy and durable, easy to repair, and with a price that could keep falling, it became the first true “car for the masses” in American history.

四、他改变了“工人”的定义

IV. He Changed the Definition of “Worker”

福特真正的革命，不在车，而在生产方式。

Ford’s real revolution was not in the car but in the way it was made.

1913年，他在工厂里建起了流水线（装配线）：把汽车装配分成一系列简单的动作，让工件在传送带上移动，工人固定在自己的位置上，重复一个动作。

In 1913 he set up an assembly line in his factory: auto assembly was broken into a series of simple motions, the work moved along on a conveyor, and the worker stayed fixed in one place, repeating a single action.

结果：装配一辆车的时间，从原来的十几个小时，缩短到了几十分钟。效率提升了几倍。汽车的售价一路下降，从最初的八百多美元，降到后来的三百美元以下——一个普通工人也能买得起了。

The result: the time to assemble one car fell from more than ten hours to a few tens of minutes. Efficiency multiplied severalfold. The price of a car kept falling, from more than eight hundred dollars at first to less than three hundred later—within reach of an ordinary worker.

1914年，他做了一个震惊全美的决定：把工人的日薪提高到5美元（几乎是当时的两倍），同时把工作日从9小时减到8小时。

In 1914 he made a decision that stunned the whole country: he raised workers’ daily pay to five dollars (nearly double what it had been) while cutting the workday from nine hours to eight.

这个决定在当时被骂成“疯了”。但福特的逻辑很清醒：如果工人买不起自己造的车，那汽车就永远只是富人的玩具。他要用流水线降低成本，再用高工资让工人成为顾客。

The decision was called “insane” at the time. But Ford’s logic was clear-headed: if workers could not afford the cars they built, then cars would forever be toys for the rich. He would use the assembly line to cut costs, and high wages to turn workers into customers.

他还很早就雇佣了残疾人和黑人工人，为他们在体力和工种上做安排。这一点在当时的工业界并不常见。

He also hired disabled workers and Black workers early on, making arrangements for them in terms of physical demands and job types. This was uncommon in industry at the time.

五、他的另一面

V. His Other Side

但福特不是完人，甚至有不少污点：

But Ford was no saint, and he had no shortage of stains:

- 他晚年在许多问题上极其固执，反对工会，动用暴力手段压制工人组织；

- In his later years he was extremely stubborn on many issues, opposed unions, and used violent means to suppress workers’ organizing;

- 他长期在自己办的报纸上发表反犹文章，这些内容后来甚至被纳粹德国引用；

- He long published anti-Semitic articles in his own newspaper, content that was later even cited by Nazi Germany;

- 他对自己的儿子埃兹尔长期压制，父子关系紧张。

- He long dominated his son Edsel, and their relationship was strained.

他不是一个可以简单赞美的“偶像”。他是一个能力惊人、同时毛病也很重的人。

He is not an “icon” one can simply praise. He was a man of astonishing ability and equally serious flaws.

1947年，亨利·福特在故乡去世，享年83岁。

In 1947, Henry Ford died in his hometown at the age of eighty-three.

六、致2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

福特的故事，讲的是“失败”和“选择伙伴”的关系。

Ford’s story is about the relationship between “failure” and “choosing partners.”

第一，他前两次失败，不是因为他不够聪明，而是因为他找错了合伙人。第一次，投资人只想要短期回报；第二次，投资人干脆把他踢出去。到第三次，他才找到愿意容忍他“固执”、补齐他短板的人。很多时候，一件事做不成，不是你的问题，是“和谁一起做”的问题。

First, his first two failures were not because he was not clever enough but because he chose the wrong partners. The first time, the investors wanted only short-term returns; the second time, they simply kicked him out. Only on the third try did he find people willing to tolerate his “stubbornness” and make up for his weaknesses. Often, a thing fails not because of you but because of “who you do it with.”

第二，最大的创新，往往不在产品，而在“让更多人用得起”。流水线不是炫技，它的目的是：把昂贵的东西，变成普通人也能拥有的东西。这个思路，今天仍然适用于很多行业。

Second, the greatest innovation is often not in the product but in “making it affordable to more people.” The assembly line was not a show of technique; its purpose was to turn something expensive into something ordinary people could own. That way of thinking still applies to many industries today.

第三，一个人的伟大和他的缺陷，可以是同时存在的。福特在提高工人待遇、让汽车平民化方面贡献巨大，同时在反犹、压制工会方面留下了无法回避的污点。我们敬佩一个人做的事，但不必为他做错的事辩护。这才是看待历史人物应有的态度。

Third, a person’s greatness and their flaws can exist at the same time. Ford contributed enormously to raising workers’ conditions and democratizing the automobile, and at the same time left unavoidable stains in his anti-Semitism and his suppression of unions. We can admire what a person did without defending what they did wrong. That is the attitude we should bring to historical figures.

他被自己的公司赶出去两次，第三次，他把整个世纪都开上了轮子。

Twice he was thrown out of his own company; on the third try, he put an entire century on wheels.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

7. “福特被自己的第二家公司赶走，该公司后来变成凯迪拉克”——亨利·福特公司确在福特离开后被改名，后经亨利·利兰等人运作，演变为凯迪拉克（1902年）。“赶出去”是通俗概括，实质是投资人以股权/控制权方式排挤他，文中已表述为“投资人把他赶了出去”。

7. “Ford was forced out of his second company, which later became Cadillac”—the Henry Ford Company was indeed renamed after Ford’s departure and, through the efforts of Henry Leland and others, evolved into Cadillac (1902). “Forced out” is a popular summary; in substance the investors squeezed him out through equity and control. The main text phrases it as “the investors forced Ford out.”

8. “第一辆四轮车造好后拆墙推出”——此说流传很广（福特本人传记与后世叙述均有提及），细节难以精确核实，属著名的轶事，文中已用“据说”限定。

8. “Tearing down a wall to get the first quadricycle out”—this account is widely circulated (it appears in Ford’s own biography and in later narratives), but the details are hard to verify precisely. It is a famous anecdote, and the main text qualifies it with “it is said.”

9. “5美元日薪让工人买得起车”——提高日薪（1914年，从约2.34美元提到5美元）是史实；但需注意，该政策附有条件（要求工人符合一定的“品行”与生活标准审查），并非无条件普惠。文中已表述为“把工人的日薪提高到5美元”，未将其美化为纯粹的福利。

9. “The five-dollar workday let workers afford cars”—the pay raise (in 1914, from about $2.34 to $5.00) is historical fact; but note that the policy came with conditions (workers had to pass a review of a certain “character” and living standard). It was not an unconditional benefit. The main text phrases it as “raised workers’ daily pay to five dollars” and does not glorify it as pure welfare.

10. “他为残疾人和黑人工人提供工作”——福特公司确实较早雇佣这两类工人，是史实；但不能据此否定他在反犹、压制工会等方面的严重问题，文中已如实并列其两面。

10. “He provided work for disabled and Black workers”—it is historical fact that Ford’s company hired these two groups relatively early; but this cannot be used to deny his serious problems in anti-Semitism and union suppression. The main text sets both sides side by side faithfully.
