约翰·富兰克林：一支129人的探险队，再也没有回来

John Franklin: An Expedition of 129 Men That Never Came Back

——“找到西北航道。”

—“Find the Northwest Passage.”

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人85 / 100约 3153 字 · 3153 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，我们用卫星地图可以精确看到北冰洋上每一块浮冰的位置。可在一百八十年前，北极还是一片几乎完全的空白。地图上那一大片白色里，藏着一个让整个大英帝国着迷的谜题：西北航道。

In 2026, satellite maps let us see the exact position of every floe of ice in the Arctic Ocean. Yet a hundred and eighty years ago, the Arctic was still an almost total blank. Inside that vast expanse of white on the map lay a puzzle that fascinated the whole British Empire: the Northwest Passage.

传说中，有一条海路可以从大西洋穿过北美北部的群岛，直接通到太平洋。谁能找到它，谁就为国家打开了一条黄金商路。

Legend held that a sea route ran from the Atlantic through the islands north of North America straight on to the Pacific. Whoever found it would open a golden trade route for his country.

1845年，英国派出了当时最精锐的一支北极探险队。两艘改装过的军舰，一百二十九名官兵，三年的物资，最先进的蒸汽动力和供暖设备。

In 1845, Britain sent out the finest Arctic expedition of its day. Two refitted warships, a hundred and twenty-nine officers and men, three years of supplies, and the most advanced steam power and heating equipment then available.

然后，他们全部消失了。 一个都没有回来。

And then they all vanished. Not one of them came back.

一、一个“吃靴子”的老人

I. An Old Man Who “Ate His Boots”

约翰·富兰克林并不是一个缺乏经验的指挥官。恰恰相反，他在北极吃过最惨的苦。

John Franklin was no inexperienced commander. Quite the opposite—he had endured the worst hardships the Arctic could offer.

1819年到1822年，他率队从陆路探索加拿大北极海岸线。那次远征堪称灾难：食物严重不足，队员们被迫吃地衣、吃皮革，甚至有“吃自己的靴子”的记载。十几个人死去，还发生了谋杀队友的严重事件。富兰克林因此得了个外号——“吃靴子的人”（the man who ate his boots）。

From 1819 to 1822 he led an overland expedition to chart the Arctic coastline of Canada. That journey was a disaster: food ran desperately short, the men were forced to eat lichen and leather, and there are even records of them “eating their own boots.” More than a dozen died, and a murder of one of their own took place. Franklin earned a nickname from it—“the man who ate his boots.”

但这次惨烈的经历，反而让他成为英国海军里最有北极经验的将领之一。到1845年，他已经五十九岁，两鬓花白。海军部其实并不太想让他带队，但最后还是在多方因素下把这个任务交给了他。

But this brutal experience ended up making him one of the most Arctic-seasoned officers in the Royal Navy. By 1845 he was fifty-nine, greying at the temples. The Admiralty was in fact reluctant to put him in command, but in the end, for a tangle of reasons, the task was given to him.

一个曾经在北极靠吃靴子活下来的人，要去完成寻找西北航道的最重要一搏。 这个开头，已经带着一种不祥的意味。

A man who had once survived the Arctic by eating his boots was now to make the great push to find the Northwest Passage. The beginning alone already carried an ominous note.

二、史上最好的装备

II. The Best Equipment in History

有意思的是，1845年的这支探险队，装备是当时最顶级的。

Curiously enough, the equipment of this 1845 expedition was the finest of its time.

两艘船——“埃里伯斯号”（Erebus）和“恐惧号”（Terror）——曾在南极服役，船首加装了厚厚的木板和铁皮以抵御浮冰。两艘船都装上了蒸汽机，甲板下是全新的供暖系统，还能自己制造淡水。船上带了三年的罐装食品、大量的衣物、甚至有图书和乐器的配置。

The two ships—HMS Erebus and HMS Terror—had served in the Antarctic, and their bows were reinforced with thick planks and iron plating to withstand the ice. Both were fitted with steam engines, a brand-new heating system below decks, and the ability to make their own fresh water. They carried three years of canned food, quantities of clothing, and even a supply of books and musical instruments.

出发时的气氛，是自信而昂扬的。船队从英国出发，先在格陵兰补给，随后进入兰开斯特海峡。

The mood at departure was confident and high-spirited. The ships set out from Britain, resupplied in Greenland, and then entered Lancaster Sound.

1845年7月末，两艘捕鲸船在巴芬湾最后一次看到他们。从那以后，再也没有欧洲人见过这两艘船上的任何一个人。

At the end of July 1845, two whaling ships saw them for the last time in Baffin Bay. After that, no European ever laid eyes on a single man from either ship again.

三、消失的线索

III. The Vanishing Clues

接下来的十几年，是英国海军史上规模最大、持续最久的一次搜救。

The following dozen years saw the largest and longest search-and-rescue effort in the history of the Royal Navy.

搜救队一批批被派出去，先后有几十支队伍、上千人次参与。他们在冰原上找到了零星的遗物、几座坟墓、一些营地痕迹。

Search parties were dispatched one after another; dozens of teams and thousands of people took part. On the ice they found scattered relics, several graves, and traces of camps.

最关键的线索，是1859年（一说1850年代）由搜救队在一处石堆里发现的一份文件（“胜利角文件”）。文件上写着：

The most crucial clue was a document that a search party found in a cairn in 1859 (some accounts place it in the 1850s)—the “Victory Point record.” It read:

1846年9月，“埃里伯斯号”和“恐惧号”被浮冰困住。1846—1847年，船员们在威廉王岛附近度过了第一个冬天。1847年6月11日，约翰·富兰克林去世。 到1848年4月，已经有二十四人死亡（包括富兰克林）。剩余的105人决定弃船，徒步向南，前往加拿大内陆的贸易站。

In September 1846, HMS Erebus and HMS Terror were trapped in the ice. Through 1846–1847, the crews spent their first winter near King William Island. On 11 June 1847, John Franklin died. By April 1848, twenty-four men had died (including Franklin). The remaining 105 decided to abandon the ships and walk south on foot toward a trading post in the Canadian interior.

从威廉王岛到最近的人类定居点，是一千多公里。 那片冰原上，没有补给，没有人烟。

From King William Island to the nearest human settlement lay more than a thousand kilometres. On that ice field there was no supply, no human habitation.

105个人，走不完这段路的。

A hundred and five men could not walk that distance.

四、因纽特人看见的

IV. What the Inuit Saw

后来，通过与当地因纽特人的接触，事情的全貌一点点拼了出来。

Later, through contact with the local Inuit, the full picture was pieced together bit by bit.

因纽特人讲述：他们看见过一群脸色苍白、身形消瘦的外来男人，拖着雪橇往南走。雪橇上拖着的，是一些奇怪而沉重的东西——银器、书籍，甚至还有一张写字桌。

The Inuit told of seeing a group of pale, gaunt strangers dragging sledges southward. On the sledges were strange, heavy things—silverware, books, even a writing desk.

这些人快饿死了，却不肯丢掉那些没用的重物。 因纽特人还说，他们看见这些白人一个个倒下，有的还发生了人吃人的事。

These men were starving to death, yet would not throw away those useless heavy loads. The Inuit added that they saw the white men fall one by one, and that in some cases men ate one another.

1854年，探险家约翰·雷把因纽特人关于“食人”的说法带回英国，引起了轩然大波。当时的英国舆论无法接受“皇家海军军官吃自己的同伴”这个说法。查尔斯·狄更斯甚至专门写文章反驳，坚称这是污蔑。

In 1854 the explorer John Rae brought the Inuit accounts of “cannibalism” back to Britain, and a storm of outrage followed. British opinion at the time could not accept the idea that “Royal Navy officers ate their own companions.” Charles Dickens even wrote an article to refute it, insisting it was a slander.

但现代科学站在了因纽特人这一边。

But modern science has come down on the Inuit’s side.

五、骨头里的真相

V. The Truth in the Bones

二十世纪后期以来，研究者对在威廉王岛等地出土的遗骨做了系统的科学分析，结果触目惊心。

Since the late twentieth century, researchers have subjected the remains unearthed on King William Island and elsewhere to systematic scientific analysis, with shocking results.

首先，骨头上有明显的切割痕迹和刮削痕迹——这与人被剥去皮肉、取食骨髓的行为相符。因纽特人的说法被证实了。

First, the bones bear clear cut marks and scrape marks—consistent with the act of stripping flesh from a body and taking the marrow for food. The Inuit account was confirmed.

其次，遗骨中的铅含量极高。研究者推测，这些铅很可能来自船上的罐装食品——当时的罐头密封用的是含铅焊料。长期食用这些罐头，会导致严重的铅中毒，进而引起精神错乱、体力衰竭、免疫力下降。他们在被冻死、饿死之前，可能已经被自己的食物毒害了。

Second, the lead content in the remains is extremely high. Researchers suspect this lead came from the ships’ canned food—the cans of the day were sealed with lead solder. Eating such food over a long period could cause severe lead poisoning, and in turn delirium, physical collapse, and a weakened immune system. Before they froze or starved to death, they may already have been poisoned by their own food.

此外，还有坏血病、结核、锌缺乏等多种问题。最终杀死这一百二十九个人的，是一整套叠加在一起的因素：寒冷、饥饿、疾病、中毒、判断失误，以及运气不好。

There were also scurvy, tuberculosis, zinc deficiency, and other problems besides. What finally killed those hundred and twenty-nine men was a whole set of overlapping factors: cold, hunger, disease, poisoning, errors of judgement, and plain bad luck.

六、迟到了一百七十年的答案

VI. An Answer a Hundred and Seventy Years Late

富兰克林的船队失败了。全队129人无一生还。

Franklin’s expedition failed. Not one of its 129 men survived.

但它留下的东西，却以一种奇怪的方式延续了下来。

Yet what it left behind endured in a strange way.

在之后的几十年里，正是为了搜救富兰克林，英国和其他国家的探险队一次次深入北极，把加拿大北极群岛的海岸线几乎测绘了一遍。换句话说，是这场惨败，把北极的地图补全了。 1906年，挪威人阿蒙森终于驾船完整通过了西北航道——比富兰克林出发晚了六十一年。

In the decades that followed, it was precisely the search for Franklin that sent British and other expeditions deep into the Arctic again and again, charting the coastlines of the Canadian Arctic Archipelago almost in their entirety. In other words, it was this disastrous failure that filled in the map of the Arctic. In 1906 the Norwegian Amundsen at last sailed a ship through the Northwest Passage in full—sixty-one years after Franklin set out.

而富兰克林的两艘船，一直沉在北极的海底。

And Franklin’s two ships lay on the seabed of the Arctic all the while.

2014年，“埃里伯斯号”的残骸被找到。2016年，“恐惧号”的残骸也被找到。 距离它们消失，已经过去了一百七十年。

In 2014 the wreck of HMS Erebus was found. In 2016 the wreck of HMS Terror was found too. A hundred and seventy years had passed since they vanished.

一个细节

One Small Detail

被找到的“恐惧号”，保存得出奇完好。舱门关着，里面的摆设还保持着当年的样子。潜水员发现，有些船员的床铺上，还放着叠好的衣物。

The recovered HMS Terror was remarkably well preserved. Its doors were shut, and the things inside still looked as they had that year. Divers found folded clothes laid on some of the crew’s bunks.

在船被冰封、主人已经死去的那些年里，这些东西就一直静静地待在那里，等着有人来看。

Through all the years the ship lay sealed in ice and its owners were dead, these things simply waited quietly in place for someone to come and look.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

富兰克林的故事，是关于“准备”最沉重的一课。

Franklin’s story is the most sobering lesson there is about “preparation.”

他们带的是当时最好的装备，最先进的船，最充足的食物。可恰恰是那些“最先进”的罐头，很可能毒害了他们；恰恰是那两艘看起来最结实的船，一旦被冰困住，就变成了两座移动的坟墓。

They carried the best equipment of the age, the most advanced ships, the most abundant food. Yet it was precisely those “most advanced” cans that may well have poisoned them; it was precisely those two seemingly sturdiest ships that, once trapped in the ice, became two moving tombs.

在极端环境里，没有“绝对安全”这回事。再好的准备，也可能被没想到的东西击穿。

In extreme environments there is no such thing as “absolute safety.” The best preparation can still be broken through by something no one thought of.

但另一件事同样值得记住：这一百二十九个人，在最绝望的时候，依然在拖着那些沉重的银器和书籍往南走。那是没有意义的负重，可那是他们作为文明人最后的体面。 他们知道自己可能走不出去，但他们没有因此变成野兽——至少，没有全部变成野兽。

But something else is worth remembering too: at their most desperate hour, these 129 men were still dragging those heavy silverware and books southward. It was a meaningless burden, but it was the last dignity they had as civilized men. They knew they might not make it out, and yet they did not become beasts because of it—at least, not all of them became beasts.

2026年，我们习惯了用“投入产出比”衡量一切。富兰克林船队提醒我们：有些东西的价值，不在它有没有用，而在它证明了你是什么人。

In 2026 we have grown used to measuring everything by its return on investment. Franklin’s expedition reminds us that some things have value not because they are useful, but because they prove what kind of people we are.

他们的船沉在冰海里，可一百七十年后，还有人潜下去，替他们看最后一眼。

Their ships lie sunk in the icy sea, and yet a hundred and seventy years later there are still people who dive down to take one last look on their behalf.

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：约翰·富兰克林（John Franklin），1786年4月16日生于英国，1847年6月11日卒于加拿大北极地区（据“胜利角文件”记载）。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: John Franklin, born in Britain on 16 April 1786, died in the Canadian Arctic on 11 June 1847 (according to the Victory Point record). Established fact.

2. 1819—1822年陆上探险：食物短缺，有“吃靴子”的记载，队员死亡并发生谋杀事件。为确证史实。富兰克林的“吃靴子的人”称号由此而来。

2. The 1819–1822 overland expedition: food shortages, records of “eating boots,” deaths among the men and a murder. Established fact. Franklin’s nickname “the man who ate his boots” came from this.

3. 1845年远征：1845年5月出发，两艘船（Erebus、Terror），共129人（一说128名军官与士兵，加富兰克林等，各资料统计口径略有差异）。为确证史实。

3. The 1845 expedition: departed in May 1845 with two ships (Erebus and Terror) and 129 men in all (some accounts count 128 officers and men plus Franklin and others; sources differ slightly in how they tally). Established fact.

4. 最后一次被目击：1845年7月末在巴芬湾被两艘捕鲸船看到。为确证史实。

4. The last sighting: seen in Baffin Bay by two whaling ships in late July 1845. Established fact.

5. “胜利角文件”：记录了1846年9月船被冰困、1847年6月11日富兰克林去世、1848年4月105人弃船南下等关键信息。为确证史实（该文件为搜救队发现的重要一手材料）。

5. The Victory Point record: it documents key facts—the ships trapped in the ice in September 1846, Franklin’s death on 11 June 1847, and the 105 men abandoning the ships and heading south in April 1848. Established fact (the document is an important primary source found by a search party).

6. 因纽特人的证词与“食人”指控：1854年约翰·雷（John Rae）带回相关说法，引发巨大争议（狄更斯曾撰文反驳）。辨析：现代骨骼考古研究的切割痕迹支持“部分船员曾取食同伴遗体”的结论，但因纽特叙述的具体细节（如人数、地点）无法逐一核实。

6. Inuit testimony and the “cannibalism” accusation: in 1854 John Rae brought back the relevant accounts, triggering enormous controversy (Dickens wrote an article in rebuttal). Note: cut marks found in modern skeletal research support the conclusion that “some crew members consumed the remains of their companions,” but the specific details of the Inuit accounts (such as numbers and locations) cannot be verified one by one.

7. 铅中毒：遗骨铅含量显著偏高，研究者多认为与含铅焊料的罐头食品有关。辨析：“铅中毒为主因”是重要假说，但并非唯一致死因素；现代学界普遍认为死因是多重叠加（寒冷、饥饿、坏血病、结核、中毒等）。本文采用“多重因素叠加”的表述。

7. Lead poisoning: lead levels in the remains are markedly elevated, and most researchers link this to canned food sealed with lead solder. Note: “lead poisoning as the main cause” is an important hypothesis, but it is not the only cause of death; modern scholarship generally holds that the causes were multiple and overlapping (cold, hunger, scurvy, tuberculosis, poisoning, and so on). This article adopts the framing of “overlapping multiple factors.”

8. 残骸发现：2014年发现“埃里伯斯号”，2016年发现“恐惧号”。为确证史实。

8. Discovery of the wrecks: HMS Erebus found in 2014, HMS Terror in 2016. Established fact.

9. 关于“恐惧号保存完好、床铺上还放着衣物”的细节：出自近年潜水考察的报道，为可信但需注意属“最新考察发现”，本文以描述性语言呈现，不作过度渲染。

9. The detail that HMS Terror was well preserved with clothes still lying on the bunks: it comes from reports of recent dive surveys. It is credible, but it should be noted that it is a “recent survey finding”; this article presents it in descriptive language without over-dramatizing.
