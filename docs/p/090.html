白求恩：一个在本国“不受待见”的医生，把命留在了中国

Bethune: A Doctor Unwelcome at Home, Who Left His Life in China

——“最近两年，是我平生最愉快、最有意义的日子。”

—"These past two years have been the happiest and most meaningful of my life."

2026年，几乎每个中国人都知道白求恩。他出现在课本里、纪念文章里、医学院的墙上。毛泽东为他写的那篇《纪念白求恩》，是几代人都背过的文章。

In 2026, almost every Chinese person knows Bethune. He appears in textbooks, in memorial articles, on the walls of medical schools. "In Memory of Norman Bethune," the essay Mao Zedong wrote for him, is a text several generations have committed to memory.

但如果把时钟拨回1938年，白求恩在加拿大和英国，其实并不算一个“主流”的医生。他是出色的胸外科专家，但性格直率、脾气暴躁，在医学界和同行里都不算讨人喜欢。他信仰共产主义，在那个年代，这让他显得非常“异类”。

But if we turn the clock back to 1938, Bethune in Canada and Britain was in fact not quite a "mainstream" doctor. He was an outstanding thoracic surgeon, but he was blunt and hot-tempered, and neither in the medical establishment nor among his colleagues was he especially liked. He believed in communism, and in that era this made him seem a great oddity.

一个在自己的世界里不太被接纳的人，最后在一个他从未生活过的国家，被永远记住了。

A man not quite accepted in his own world was in the end remembered forever in a country he had never lived in.

一、一个脾气不好的胸外科专家

I. A Thoracic Surgeon with a Bad Temper

1890年3月3日，诺尔曼·白求恩出生在加拿大安大略省的格雷文赫斯特镇。他毕业于多伦多大学医学院，逐渐成为一位技术精湛的胸外科医生。

On March 3, 1890, Norman Bethune was born in the town of Gravenhurst, Ontario, Canada. He graduated from the medical school of the University of Toronto and gradually became a highly skilled thoracic surgeon.

他不是一个安分的人。他做过很多事：当过军医，开过私人诊所，也投入了大量精力研究肺结核的外科治疗。他发明的几种手术器械，在当时的胸外科界是有价值的贡献。

He was not a settled man. He did many things: he served as a military doctor, ran a private clinic, and poured great energy into the surgical treatment of tuberculosis. Several surgical instruments he invented were valuable contributions to thoracic surgery at the time.

但他的性格，让他在体制里待不长。他说话直接，不留情面，容易得罪人；他同情底层患者，看不惯医疗的商业化。这些特质，让他成为一个“好医生”，却不是一个“受欢迎的人”。

But his temperament kept him from lasting long within institutions. He spoke bluntly, sparing no one's feelings, and easily made enemies; he sympathized with poor patients and could not stomach the commercialization of medicine. These traits made him a "good doctor," but not a "popular man."

1935年，他加入了加拿大共产党。1936年，他去了西班牙，参加反法西斯的斗争，在那里创办了一套“移动输血”的服务，把血液送到前线去。

In 1935 he joined the Communist Party of Canada. In 1936 he went to Spain to take part in the anti-fascist struggle, where he set up a "mobile blood transfusion" service, delivering blood to the front.

二、1938年，他来到中国

II. In 1938, He Came to China

1937年，中国全面抗战爆发。白求恩在北美为西班牙募捐时，看到了关于中国的报道。他说：真正的战斗是在中国。

In 1937, full-scale war of resistance broke out in China. While raising funds in North America for Spain, Bethune saw reports about China. He said: the real battle is in China.

1938年1月，他受加拿大共产党和美国共产党的派遣，率医疗队来华。他带着足够装备几个医疗队的药品和器械，从温哥华出发，辗转到达中国。

In January 1938, dispatched by the Communist Party of Canada and the Communist Party of the USA, he led a medical team to China. Carrying enough medicine and equipment to outfit several medical teams, he set out from Vancouver and, by a roundabout route, reached China.

1938年3月底，他到达延安。第二天，毛泽东就接见了他。

At the end of March 1938 he arrived in Yan'an. The very next day, Mao Zedong received him.

关于这次见面，白求恩在日记里写下了他的印象。他本以为会见到一个高高在上的领袖，结果毛泽东穿着打补丁的黑布棉衣，脚上是普通的破棉鞋，和普通士兵没什么两样。两人从中国谈到世界，从军事谈到卫生，一谈就是几个小时。

Bethune wrote down his impressions of the meeting in his diary. He had expected to meet a lofty leader; instead Mao Zedong wore a black quilted cotton jacket with patches on it, and on his feet were ordinary worn cotton shoes, no different from any common soldier's. The two talked from China to the world, from military affairs to health, and the conversation ran on for hours.

白求恩写道：他“是一个巨人，是我们世界上最伟大的人物之一”。

Bethune wrote: he was "a giant, one of the greatest figures in our world."

在延安，他感受到了一种他在自己的世界里从未感受到的东西——被当成“自己人”。

In Yan'an he felt something he had never felt in his own world—being treated as one of their own.

三、“我要到前线去”

III. "I Want to Go to the Front"

在延安，白求恩提出了一个要求：他要到抗日前线去。

In Yan'an, Bethune made a request: he wanted to go to the front of the war of resistance.

然后他穿越太行山、渡过黄河、冲过封锁线，来到了晋察冀军区。司令员聂荣臻聘请他做军区卫生顾问。

And so he crossed the Taihang Mountains, forded the Yellow River, and broke through blockade lines to reach the Jin-Cha-Ji Military Region. Its commander, Nie Rongzhen, engaged him as medical adviser to the region.

他看到的，是一个他从未想象过的医疗条件：手术室里只有几把剪子、止血钳和手术刀，截肢的工具甚至是木工锯改造的。药品极度缺乏。

What he saw was a set of medical conditions he had never imagined: in the operating room there were only a few scissors, hemostats, and scalpels; the tools for amputation had been converted from a carpenter's saw. Medicine was desperately scarce.

但白求恩没有抱怨条件，他说了另一句话。 他握着后方医院院长的手说：你们在这样艰苦的条件下工作，太了不起了。

But Bethune did not complain about the conditions; he said something else instead. Taking the hand of the rear hospital's director, he said: for you to work under such harsh conditions is truly remarkable.

他立刻开始工作：把他在西班牙熟练掌握的输血技术带进来（当时在野战条件下输血，几乎没人敢想）；组织战地输血队；提出把手术室设到离前线更近的地方。

He began work at once: bringing in the transfusion technique he had mastered in Spain (at the time, transfusing blood under field conditions was something almost no one dared to attempt); organizing a battlefield transfusion unit; and proposing that operating rooms be set up closer to the front.

他把“手术台”搬到了离火线更近的地方。 他的理由是：离火线远了，伤员在路上耽误的时间就长，死亡率就会增高。他说：“战士在火线上都不怕危险，我们怕什么危险？”

He moved the "operating table" closer to the line of fire. His reasoning: the farther from the firing line, the longer the wounded are delayed on the road, and the higher the death rate. He said: "If the soldiers on the firing line don't fear danger, what danger have we to fear?"

四、那些数字

IV. Those Numbers

白求恩在中国工作了不到两年，但留下的数字是惊人的。

Bethune worked in China for less than two years, but the numbers he left behind are astonishing.

1938年11月到1939年2月，他率医疗队在山西雁北和冀中前线工作。据记载，他曾在40个小时里连续做了71台手术；在齐会战斗中，他曾连续工作69小时，为115名伤员做手术。

From November 1938 to February 1939 he led his medical team at the front in Yanbei, Shanxi, and in central Hebei. According to the records, he once performed 71 operations in 40 consecutive hours; at the battle of Qihui he worked 69 hours without a break, operating on 115 wounded men.

还有一次，他率队在4个月里行程750公里，做手术300余次，建立手术室和包扎所13处。

On another occasion he led his team on a march of 750 kilometers over four months, performing more than 300 operations and establishing 13 operating rooms and dressing stations.

他是一个把自己的体力当成消耗品在用的人。 他还创办了卫生学校，编写战地医疗教材，自制医疗器械——他给一个药驮子起名叫“卢沟桥”，用来纪念中国的抗战。

He was a man who spent his own physical strength as if it were something to be used up. He also founded a medical school, wrote field-medical teaching materials, and made his own medical instruments—he named a medicine pack "Marco Polo Bridge," as a memorial to China's war of resistance.

他还把自己的衣服、食物都分给伤员。有人问他为什么，他说：我在这里，找到了最富有人性的同志。

He also gave away his own clothes and food to the wounded. When someone asked him why, he said: here I have found the most humane of comrades.

五、1939年10月

V. October 1939

1939年10月，日军对根据地发动冬季“扫荡”。

In October 1939, the Japanese army launched a winter "mopping-up" campaign against the base areas.

白求恩本来已经打算回国，去为中国的抗战募集经费、药品和器械。但战斗开始了，他决定留下。他对聂荣臻说：我不能在战斗的时候离开部队。

Bethune had already intended to return home, to raise funds, medicine, and equipment for China's war of resistance. But when the fighting began, he decided to stay. He told Nie Rongzhen: I cannot leave the troops at a time of battle.

他带医疗队赶往涞源摩天岭前线，在靠近火线的一个小庙里设立手术室。在一次手术中，他的左手中指被划破，局部发炎肿胀。

He led his medical team to the front at Motianling in Laiyuan, setting up an operating room in a small temple close to the firing line. During one operation his left middle finger was cut, and the wound became inflamed and swollen.

11月1日，他为一名患有颈部丹毒合并蜂窝组织炎的伤员做手术时，带伤的手指受到了致命的感染。 病菌迅速扩散，他高烧不退，但仍然坚持做手术、写讲义。

On November 1, while operating on a wounded man suffering from erysipelas of the neck complicated by cellulitis, the injured finger became fatally infected. The bacteria spread rapidly, his fever would not come down, and yet he still insisted on performing operations and writing lecture notes.

后来，在多次命令下，他终于答应去后方医院治疗。但感染已经发展成败血症，左臂变成了黑色。当时的条件，已经无力回天。

Later, after repeated orders, he finally agreed to go to a rear hospital for treatment. But the infection had developed into septicemia, and his left arm had turned black. Under the conditions of the time, there was no longer anything that could save him.

1939年11月12日凌晨，白求恩在河北省唐县黄石口村去世，年仅四十九岁。他在前一天用最后的力气，给聂荣臻写了一封长信，信里还在惦记着要买多少奎宁、多少铁剂，还在提醒“千万不要到保定、平津去买药，那边比沪港贵两倍”。

In the small hours of November 12, 1939, Bethune died at Huangshikou village in Tangxian county, Hebei province, only forty-nine years old. The day before, with his last strength, he had written a long letter to Nie Rongzhen, in which he was still fretting over how much quinine to buy, how much iron supplement, and still reminding him: "Whatever you do, don't buy medicine in Baoding or in Beiping and Tianjin—prices there are twice what they are in Shanghai and Hong Kong."

一个快死的人，最后想的还是怎么给伤员省钱。

A dying man, and his last thoughts were still about how to save money for the wounded.

六、他为什么被记住

VI. Why He Is Remembered

1939年12月21日，毛泽东写下了《纪念白求恩》。

On December 21, 1939, Mao Zedong wrote "In Memory of Norman Bethune."

这篇文章里的话，几乎每个中国学生都读过：“一个人能力有大小，但只要有这点精神，就是一个高尚的人，一个纯粹的人，一个有道德的人，一个脱离了低级趣味的人，一个有益于人民的人。”

Almost every Chinese student has read the words in that essay: "A man's ability may be great or small, but if he has this spirit, he is already noble-minded and pure, a man of moral integrity and above vulgar interests, a man who is of value to the people."

白求恩被记住了。但值得思考的是：他为什么会被记住？

Bethune is remembered. But what is worth thinking about is this: why is he remembered?

不是因为他“完美”。 他脾气不好，他在自己的国家不太受欢迎，他有过失败和挫折。他被记住，是因为他做了一个选择：他把自己最擅长的东西——医术，放到了一个最需要它的地方。

Not because he was "perfect." He had a bad temper, he was not very popular in his own country, and he had his failures and setbacks. He is remembered because he made a choice: he took the thing he did best—his medical skill—and put it where it was needed most.

他本可以在加拿大过安稳的日子。他选择了中国。

He could have lived a settled life in Canada. He chose China.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

白求恩的故事，有一个容易被忽略的角度：“被需要”这件事，本身就有巨大的力量。

Bethune's story has an angle that is easily overlooked: being needed is itself a tremendous force.

他在自己的世界里，是个“异类”——信仰、脾气、性格，都让他格格不入。他来到中国，在晋察冀的破庙里，第一次感到自己被完整地接纳了。他自己说，“最近两年，是我平生最愉快、最有意义的日子”。

In his own world he was an "oddity"—his beliefs, his temper, his character all made him out of place. He came to China, and in a broken temple in the Jin-Cha-Ji region, he felt for the first time that he was completely accepted. As he himself said, "These past two years have been the happiest and most meaningful of my life."

一个人最难过的，往往不是辛苦，而是“我这一身本事，没地方使”。 白求恩找到了那个地方。

What troubles a person most is often not hardship, but the feeling that "all my skills have nowhere to be used." Bethune found that place.

2026年的我们，很多人也在寻找那个“地方”：一个能让自己的价值真正落地、真正被需要的地方。白求恩的答案很朴素：不一定是你出生的地方。 你要看的是，哪里的人最需要你手里的东西。

Many of us in 2026 are likewise searching for that "place": a place where our worth can truly take root, where we are truly needed. Bethune's answer is very plain: it need not be the place where you were born. What you have to look at is where the people most need what you have in your hands.

他用四十九年的生命，在异国的土地上，给了这句话一个最沉的注解。

With his forty-nine years of life, on the soil of a foreign land, he gave that thought its gravest annotation.

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：诺尔曼·白求恩（Norman Bethune），1890年3月3日生于加拿大安大略省格雷文赫斯特镇，1939年11月12日卒于中国河北省唐县黄石口村，年49岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic facts of his life: Norman Bethune, born March 3, 1890, in Gravenhurst, Ontario, Canada; died November 12, 1939, at Huangshikou village, Tangxian county, Hebei province, China, aged 49. Well-established historical fact.

2. 医学身份：毕业于多伦多大学医学院，著名胸外科医生。为确证史实。

2. Medical identity: a graduate of the medical school of the University of Toronto; a prominent thoracic surgeon. Well-established historical fact.

3. 政治身份：1935年加入加拿大共产党。为确证史实。

3. Political affiliation: joined the Communist Party of Canada in 1935. Well-established historical fact.

4. 西班牙经历：1936年赴西班牙参加反法西斯斗争，创办移动输血服务。为确证史实。

4. Spanish experience: went to Spain in 1936 to join the anti-fascist struggle and established a mobile blood transfusion service. Well-established historical fact.

5. 来华：1938年1月受加拿大共产党和美国共产党派遣来华，3月底到达延安，毛泽东接见。为确证史实。

5. Coming to China: dispatched to China in January 1938 by the Communist Party of Canada and the Communist Party of the USA; reached Yan'an at the end of March; received by Mao Zedong. Well-established historical fact.

6. 到达晋察冀：1938年6月前后到达晋察冀边区，被聘为军区卫生顾问。为确证史实。

6. Arrival in Jin-Cha-Ji: reached the Jin-Cha-Ji border region around June 1938 and was engaged as medical adviser to the military region. Well-established historical fact.

7. 手术数字：1938年11月至1939年2月期间的“40小时71台手术”“4个月行程750公里、手术300余次”等数据，见于中方权威记载（如中国军网、央视等官方报道）。辨析：这些数字出自当时的记录与后来的整理材料，属于官方权威叙述，本文如实引用。

8. 感染与去世：1939年10月左手中指受伤感染，11月1日为丹毒合并蜂窝组织炎的伤员手术时受致命感染，转为败血症，11月12日去世。为确证史实。

8. Infection and death: in October 1939 his left middle finger was injured and infected; on November 1, while operating on a wounded man with erysipelas complicated by cellulitis, he suffered a fatal infection; it turned into septicemia, and he died on November 12. 9. The last letter: his letter to Nie Rongzhen (November 11, 1939) is an important primary source, and its contents are confirmed. The passage on "saving money on medicine" cited in this text comes from that letter.

10. 《纪念白求恩》：毛泽东写于1939年12月21日，为确定史实。本文所引为该文广为人知的段落。

11. His "imperfections": Bethune was blunt and prone to conflict, and he was not a popular figure in the medical circles of his own country. This aspect is widely recorded in his biographies and source materials (for example, Canadian research), and this text presents it in order to restore a real person rather than a flat idol.
