Chapter 098 · Part 5

邓稼先：隐姓埋名二十八年，临终才被世人知道

Deng Jiaxian: Twenty-Eight Years Under a Hidden Name, Known to the World Only at the End

——“我不入地狱，谁入地狱。”

— "If I do not enter hell, who will?"

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人98 / 100约 3042 字 · 3042 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，中国的核武器早已是国之大器，关于“两弹一星”的故事也被讲了很多年。但要真正理解那段历史有多难，你得先想一想：一个人，可以让自己的名字，从所有公开记录里消失二十八年吗？

In 2026, China's nuclear weapons have long been a pillar of national strength, and the story of "two bombs, one satellite" has been told for many years. But to truly grasp how hard that history was, you have to think first about this: can a person make his own name vanish from every public record for twenty-eight years?

邓稼先做到了。

Deng Jiaxian did.

他是中国第一颗原子弹和第一颗氢弹的理论设计负责人。他从1958年开始隐姓埋名，直到1986年去世前，他的身份才被公开。

He was the head of theoretical design for China's first atomic bomb and its first hydrogen bomb. From 1958 he lived under a hidden name, and his identity was not made public until shortly before his death in 1986.

他把自己的一生，藏进了一个代号里。

He hid his whole life inside a code name.

而在那二十八年里，他的家人不知道他在做什么，他的妻子只能靠零星的见面和极少的通信与他联系。他的同事、邻居、朋友，都以为他只是“工作调动了”。一个人可以消失得这么彻底，靠的不是隐忍，而是一种把“我”彻底放下的自觉。

During those twenty-eight years his family did not know what he was doing; his wife could keep in touch with him only through scattered meetings and the rarest of correspondence. His colleagues, neighbors, and friends all assumed he had merely been "transferred." That a man can vanish so completely rests not on endurance alone, but on a kind of discipline that sets "I" down entirely.

一、从西南联大到普渡大学

I. From National Southwestern Associated University to Purdue

1924年6月25日，邓稼先出生在安徽怀宁。他成长在一个知识分子家庭，从小接受良好的教育。

Deng Jiaxian was born on June 25, 1924, in Huaining, Anhui. He grew up in an intellectual family and received a good education from childhood.

1945年，他毕业于西南联合大学物理系。那是一所在战火中成立的大学，条件极其艰苦，却培养出了中国后来一大批顶尖的科学家。

In 1945 he graduated from the physics department of National Southwestern Associated University. That was a university founded amid the fires of war, under extremely harsh conditions, and yet it produced a large number of China's later leading scientists.

1948年，邓稼先赴美国普渡大学留学，攻读物理学。1950年，他获得了物理学博士学位——那一年他只有二十六岁，被同学们称为“娃娃博士”。

In 1948 Deng Jiaxian went to Purdue University in the United States to study physics. In 1950 he received a doctorate in physics—he was only twenty-six that year, and his classmates called him the "baby doctor."

拿到学位后，他几乎没有停留。1950年，他回到中国。

He barely lingered after getting his degree. In 1950 he returned to China.

当时，美国方面有人劝他留下，给出的条件是优厚的。他还是回来了。

At the time, some on the American side urged him to stay and offered generous terms. He came back all the same.

二、1958年，他“消失”了

II. 1958: He "Disappeared"

1958年，一项绝密任务找到了邓稼先。

In 1958, a top-secret assignment came to Deng Jiaxian.

当时，中国决定自己研制原子弹。他被选为这项工程的理论设计负责人。任务的性质决定了：他必须从所有公开的、可以被查到的地方消失。

At the time, China had decided to develop an atomic bomb on its own. He was chosen to head the theoretical design of the project. The nature of the task dictated it: he had to disappear from everywhere that was public and could be looked up.

他对妻子许鹿希说的话，后来被反复引用：他要调动工作了，以后可能顾不上家；通信会不方便；如果他做成了这件事，这一生就值了，死了也值得。

What he said to his wife, Xu Luxi, was quoted again and again afterward: he was being transferred; from then on he might not be able to look after the family; correspondence would be inconvenient; and if he succeeded in this undertaking, his life would have been worth it—worth it even if he died.

从那一天起，“邓稼先”这个名字，在中国的公开记载里消失了。他成了一个代号，一个岗位，一个不能对家人说、也不能对朋友说的秘密。

From that day on, the name "Deng Jiaxian" disappeared from China's public records. He became a code name, a post, a secret he could tell no family member and no friend.

接下来的二十八年里，他带着一批平均年龄二十几岁的年轻科研人员，在戈壁滩和实验室里，从零开始，一步步推导、计算、试验。

Over the next twenty-eight years he led a group of young researchers averaging in their twenties, on the Gobi and in the laboratory, starting from zero, deriving, calculating, and testing step by step.

三、1964年10月16日

III. October 16, 1964

1964年10月16日，中国第一颗原子弹在罗布泊爆炸成功。

On October 16, 1964, China's first atomic bomb detonated successfully at Lop Nur.

中国成为世界上第五个拥有核武器的国家。

China became the fifth country in the world to possess nuclear weapons.

三年后的1967年6月17日，中国第一颗氢弹爆炸成功。从原子弹到氢弹，中国只用了两年零八个月——这个速度，在世界上是极其惊人的。

Three years later, on June 17, 1967, China's first hydrogen bomb detonated successfully. From atomic bomb to hydrogen bomb, China took only two years and eight months—a pace that was extraordinary by any measure in the world.

这两次成功的背后，有无数人的付出。但作为理论设计的负责人，邓稼先是那个把最核心的难题，一肩扛下来的人。

Behind these two successes lay the efforts of countless people. But as head of theoretical design, Deng Jiaxian was the one who shouldered the most central difficulty alone.

他做的不是“执行”，而是“决定怎么做”——在没有图纸、没有经验、没有外援的条件下，从最基本的物理原理出发，把理论框架搭起来。

What he did was not "carrying out" but "deciding how"—with no blueprints, no experience, no outside help, starting from the most basic principles of physics and raising up the theoretical framework.

四、1979年，那次失败的试验

IV. 1979: The Test That Failed

1979年，一次核试验中，空投的核弹没有正常爆炸。

In 1979, during a nuclear test, an air-dropped bomb failed to detonate properly.

这是一个极其危险的情况。核弹残骸散落在一片区域里，里面的材料和数据必须被人亲自去取回来。谁进去，谁就要面对大量的辐射。

It was an extremely dangerous situation. The wreckage of the bomb lay scattered across an area; the materials and data inside had to be recovered by hand. Whoever went in would face heavy radiation.

邓稼先去了。 他亲自走进那片区域，把散落的碎片捡了起来。

Deng Jiaxian went. He walked into that area himself and picked up the scattered fragments.

从那一刻起，他的身体就开始被辐射侵蚀。检查结果显示，他的尿液中带有放射性物质，肝脏也受到了损伤。

From that moment his body began to be eaten away by radiation. Tests showed radioactive material in his urine and damage to his liver as well.

他心里很清楚，这意味着什么。 但他没有停下来。此后几年，他继续主持核武器的研制和试验工作。

He knew clearly in his heart what it meant. But he did not stop. In the years that followed he went on directing the development and testing of nuclear weapons.

五、1985年，他倒下了

V. 1985: He Collapsed

1985年，邓稼先被确诊为直肠癌。

In 1985 Deng Jiaxian was diagnosed with rectal cancer.

他住进了医院，接受了手术。但他的身体已经无法承受更多的治疗。1986年，他的病情急剧恶化，出现了全身大出血。

He was admitted to the hospital and underwent surgery. But his body could no longer bear further treatment. In 1986 his condition deteriorated sharply, and he suffered massive hemorrhaging throughout his body.

就在这一年，他的身份被公开了。 人们终于知道，那个从中国公开记录里消失了二十八年的科学家，就是“两弹元勋”邓稼先。

That same year, his identity was made public. People finally learned that the scientist who had vanished from China's public records for twenty-eight years was Deng Jiaxian, the "founding father of the two bombs."

1986年7月29日，邓稼先去世，享年六十二岁。

On July 29, 1986, Deng Jiaxian died at the age of sixty-two.

从身份公开到去世，只有几个月。 他隐姓埋名了二十八年，被世人知道的时间，不到半年。

From the disclosure of his identity to his death was only a few months. He had lived under a hidden name for twenty-eight years; the time the world knew him was less than half a year.

1986年，他被追授“全国劳动模范”称号。1999年，他被追授“两弹一星功勋奖章”。

In 1986 he was posthumously named a National Model Worker. In 1999 he was posthumously awarded the Medal of Merit for the Two Bombs and One Satellite.

六、他留下了什么

VI. What He Left Behind

邓稼先留下的，当然首先是中国自己的核能力。在一个曾经被核威胁笼罩的年代，这件事的意义，不是“厉害”，而是“不受制于人”。

What Deng Jiaxian left behind was, first of all, China's own nuclear capability. In an era once shadowed by nuclear threats, the meaning of this was not that China was "formidable," but that it was no longer at the mercy of others.

但他留下的，还有一种更难的东西：在那个年代，有一批最聪明的年轻人，把自己最好的二十八年，交给了一个不能对任何人说的任务。

But he also left behind something harder: in that era, a group of the brightest young people gave the best twenty-eight years of their lives to a task they could not speak of to anyone.

他们没有署名权，没有掌声，没有曝光度。他们的成果属于国家，他们的名字属于机密。他们的家人不知道他们在做什么，他们的朋友以为他们“失踪”了。

They had no right of authorship, no applause, no exposure. Their achievements belonged to the state; their names were classified. Their families did not know what they were doing; their friends thought they had "gone missing."

一个人愿意把自己的存在感彻底交出去，只为了让自己的国家不再被威胁——这件事的难度，已经不能用“奉献”两个字概括了。

That a person would give away his own sense of existing entirely, only so that his country would no longer be threatened—the difficulty of this can no longer be summed up by the word "dedication."

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

邓稼先的故事，在2026年读来，会让人产生一种复杂的感受。

Read in 2026, Deng Jiaxian's story stirs a complicated feeling.

我们今天生活在一个极度强调“个人品牌”“可见度”“影响力”的时代。一个人做了事，恨不得让全世界都知道。而邓稼先做的，恰恰是相反的选择：他做了最重要的贡献，却主动让自己“不存在”。

We live today in an age that stresses "personal brand," "visibility," and "influence" to the extreme. Having done something, a person wants the whole world to know. What Deng Jiaxian did was precisely the opposite choice: he made the most important contribution, and deliberately made himself "not exist."

这两种选择，哪个对？没有标准答案。时代不同，处境不同，人的追求也不同。

Which of these two choices is right? There is no standard answer. The times differ, the circumstances differ, and so do people's aspirations.

但邓稼先至少告诉我们一件事：世界上真的存在这样一种人——他们做一件事，不是为了被看见。

But Deng Jiaxian tells us at least one thing: there really are people in this world who do a thing not in order to be seen.

2026年的我们，也许不需要像他那样隐姓埋名。但我们可以从他身上学到一种东西：当你决定做一件事的时候，先问一问，你是为了这件事本身，还是为了这件事带来的名声？

We in 2026 may not need to live under hidden names as he did. But we can learn one thing from him: when you decide to do a thing, ask first—are you doing it for the thing itself, or for the fame the thing brings?

他用了二十八年，回答了这个问题的另一半。

He spent twenty-eight years answering the other half of that question.

还有一点，是关于“记住”这件事的。邓稼先隐姓埋名了二十八年，可今天，几乎每一个中国学生都知道他的名字。这说明历史其实是有公道的：它可能会迟到，但它不会真的忘掉那些真正重要的人。 你在没人看见的时候做的事，历史最终会替你打开那盏灯。

One more point, about "remembering." Deng Jiaxian lived under a hidden name for twenty-eight years, yet today almost every Chinese student knows his name. This shows that history in fact has a kind of justice: it may come late, but it will not truly forget those who really mattered. What you do when no one is watching—history will finally light the lamp for you.

所以，邓稼先这个名字，本身就是一份回答。它回答的是很多人心里那个疑问：如果一件事做了没人知道，还有意义吗？ 他的答案是：有。因为一件事的意义，从来不由“有没有人看见”决定，而由“它到底重不重要”决定。他做的那些事，重要到足以让一个国家挺直腰杆——这就够了。

So the very name Deng Jiaxian is itself an answer. It answers the question in many people's minds: if a thing is done and no one knows, does it still have meaning? His answer is: yes. Because the meaning of a thing is never decided by whether anyone sees it, but by whether it truly matters. What he did mattered enough to let a country stand straight—and that is enough.

他去世后，他的妻子许鹿希长期住在他们曾经一起住过的那间老房子里。有人不解，她只说习惯了。这段婚姻里，他们真正在一起生活的时间并不长——因为他的工作，不能让家人知道。一个人的“隐姓埋名”，其实是整个家庭陪着他一起承担的。

After his death, his wife, Xu Luxi, lived for a long time in the old house they had once shared. People did not understand; she said only that she was used to it. In that marriage, the time they truly spent living together was not long—because his work could not be told to the family. One man's "hidden name" was, in fact, borne together with his whole family.

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：邓稼先，1924年6月25日生于安徽怀宁，1986年7月29日去世，年62岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Deng Jiaxian was born on June 25, 1924, in Huaining, Anhui, and died on July 29, 1986, at the age of sixty-two. Confirmed historical fact.

2. 教育经历：1945年毕业于西南联合大学物理系；1948年赴美国普渡大学留学，1950年获物理学博士学位。为确证史实。

2. Education: graduated from the physics department of National Southwestern Associated University in 1945; went to Purdue University in the United States in 1948; received a doctorate in physics in 1950. Confirmed historical fact.

3. 回国：1950年获博士学位后即回国。为确证史实。

3. Return to China: returned immediately upon receiving his doctorate in 1950. Confirmed historical fact.

4. 隐姓埋名：1958年起参与核武器研制并隐姓埋名，约28年。为确证史实。

4. Hidden name: from 1958 he took part in nuclear-weapons development and lived under a hidden name for about twenty-eight years. Confirmed historical fact.

5. 关键节点：1964年10月16日中国第一颗原子弹爆炸成功；1967年6月17日第一颗氢弹爆炸成功。为确证史实。

5. Key milestones: China's first atomic bomb detonated successfully on October 16, 1964; the first hydrogen bomb on June 17, 1967. Confirmed historical fact.

6. 1979年核试验事故：一次空投试验未成功，他亲自进入现场收集残骸，受到辐射。为确证史实（广为记载）。辨析：关于他“抱回碎片”的具体过程，不同记述细节略有差异，本文采用“亲自进入现场取回残骸”的表述。

6. The 1979 nuclear-test accident: an air-drop test failed, and he personally entered the site to collect the wreckage, receiving radiation. Confirmed historical fact (widely recorded). Note: accounts differ slightly in detail regarding the specific process of his "carrying the fragments back"; this essay uses the formulation "personally entered the site to retrieve the wreckage."

7. 去世：1985年确诊直肠癌，1986年7月29日因全身大出血去世。为确证史实。辨析：其直肠癌与1979年受辐射的因果关系，医学上难以做严格的直接归因，但一般认为辐射暴露是其健康受损的重要因素。

7. Death: diagnosed with rectal cancer in 1985, died of massive hemorrhaging on July 29, 1986. Confirmed historical fact. Note: medically it is difficult to establish a strict direct causal link between his rectal cancer and the 1979 radiation exposure, but radiation exposure is generally regarded as an important factor in the damage to his health.

8. 身份公开时间：其身份在1986年（去世前不久）被公开报道。为通行记载。

8. When his identity was disclosed: his identity was publicly reported in 1986, shortly before his death. A generally accepted account.

9. 荣誉：1986年被追授“全国劳动模范”；1999年被追授“两弹一星功勋奖章”。为确证史实。

9. Honors: posthumously named a National Model Worker in 1986; posthumously awarded the Medal of Merit for the Two Bombs and One Satellite in 1999. Confirmed historical fact.

10. 妻子许鹿希：为医学教育工作者，两人1953年结婚。其关于邓稼先的回忆，是研究其生平的重要一手材料。本文关于“他1958年对妻子说的话”的叙述，即源自此类回忆材料，属回忆性质。

10. His wife, Xu Luxi: a medical educator; the two married in 1953. Her recollections of Deng Jiaxian are an important first-hand source for the study of his life. This essay's account of "what he said to his wife in 1958" comes from such recollections and is therefore of a memorial nature.
