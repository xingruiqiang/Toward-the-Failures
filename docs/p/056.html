Chapter 056 · Part 3

任正非：43岁被骗200万、离婚、住棚屋的那个人

Ren Zhengfei: The Man Who, at Forty-Three, Was Swindled Out of Two Million, Divorced, and Living in a Shack

——“这次研发如果失败了，我只有从楼上跳下去”

—"If this research fails, my only choice is to jump from the top of the building"

2026年，“中年失业”是无数人心里最沉重的一个词。43岁，上有老下有小，背着房贷，突然被裁——很多人觉得，这个年纪再重新开始，几乎等于宣判。

In 2026, "midlife unemployment" is the heaviest phrase in countless people's hearts. At forty-three, with elderly parents above and young children below, carrying a mortgage, suddenly laid off—many feel that to start over at this age is almost a death sentence.

1987年，有一个人43岁：在国企被骗走200多万元货款，被除名，又离了婚；他上有年迈的父母要养，下有一儿一女要抚养，还要兼顾六个弟妹的生活。他带着一家人挤在一间十几平方米的小房子里。

In 1987, one man was forty-three: swindled out of more than two million yuan in payments at a state-owned enterprise, struck off the rolls, and divorced. He had aging parents to support above him, a son and a daughter to raise below, and six younger siblings whose lives he also had to look after. He squeezed his whole family into a small room of barely more than ten square meters.

那一年，他凑了2.1万元，创办了一家叫“华为”的公司。

That year he scraped together 21,000 yuan and founded a company called Huawei.

他叫任正非。他后来回忆那段日子说：“世界开始疯起来了，等不得我的不惑了。”

His name was Ren Zhengfei. Looking back on those days, he said: "The world began to go mad; it would not wait for me to reach the age of no doubts."

一、从军营到深圳

I. From the Barracks to Shenzhen

任正非1944年出生。他早年入伍，在部队里是技术骨干。1982年，国家大裁军，部队撤销，他选择转业，南下深圳，加入南油集团。

Ren Zhengfei was born in 1944. He joined the army at a young age and became a technical backbone in his unit. In 1982 the state carried out a large-scale demobilization, his unit was disbanded, and he chose to leave the military, heading south to Shenzhen to join the Nanyou Group.

在南油集团，他被任命为旗下一家电子公司的副总经理。军旅出身的他，做事认真，也很相信人。

At the Nanyou Group he was appointed deputy general manager of one of its electronics companies. Coming from the military, he was conscientious in his work and trusted people deeply.

但正是因为这一点，他栽了一个大跟头。一次经济往来中，他被一家贸易公司骗走了200多万元的货款。

But it was precisely this trait that brought him down hard. In one business transaction, a trading company swindled him out of more than two million yuan in payments.

要知道，当时内地普通人的月工资还不到100元，200多万元是一笔天文数字的巨款。作为军官，他的退伍金只有3800元。

Keep in mind that at the time an ordinary person on the mainland earned a monthly wage of less than 100 yuan; more than two million was an astronomical sum. As a military officer, his discharge pay amounted to only 3,800 yuan.

这笔账最终由他承担责任。他被公司除名，随后和妻子离了婚，家庭解体。43岁的他，遭遇了一场巨大的中年危机。

In the end the debt was laid at his door. He was struck off the company's rolls, then divorced his wife, and his family fell apart. At forty-three, he suffered a massive midlife crisis.

二、2.1万元，一间居民楼

II. 21,000 Yuan, One Room in a Residential Building

1987年，43岁的任正非凑了2.1万元，和几个合伙人一起，创立了“深圳华为技术有限公司”，寓意“心系中华，有所作为”。

In 1987, at forty-three, Ren Zhengfei scraped together 21,000 yuan and, with a few partners, founded Shenzhen Huawei Technology Co., Ltd.—a name meant to convey "having China at heart, and accomplishing something."

为了省钱，他把公司开在居民楼里，一个月房租三四百元，而当时写字楼一个月要好几千。

To save money he set up the company in a residential building, where rent was three or four hundred yuan a month, whereas office space at the time cost several thousand a month.

公司做什么？他其实也很迷茫。最初，他考虑过做墓碑——一块墓碑石料成本七八十元，刻上字能卖四五百元，雕琢精细的能卖上千元。但他觉得“墓碑不是长久之计”，放弃了。

What would the company do? He was in fact quite adrift. At first he considered making tombstones—a tombstone's raw stone cost seventy or eighty yuan, once carved with characters it could sell for four or five hundred, and a finely wrought one for over a thousand. But he felt that "tombstones are not a long-term plan," and gave up the idea.

后来他代销火灾警报器、气浮仪等厂矿所需的工业仪器，但订货量太少，不足以维持运转。再后来，他看中了香港鸿年公司的小型HAX程控交换机，靠代销赚到了第一桶金。

Later he resold industrial instruments needed by factories and mines, such as fire alarms and flotation meters, but the orders were too few to keep the business going. Still later he took a liking to the small HAX program-controlled switchboard made by Hong Kong's Hongnian Company, and by reselling it earned his first pot of gold.

但代销门槛低、资金需求小，很快一堆小公司涌进来抢生意，供货供不上，利润也被迅速摊薄。任正非做了一个决定：自己进口组件，自己组装。

But reselling had a low barrier to entry and required little capital; soon a swarm of small companies rushed in to grab the business, supply could not keep up, and profits were quickly thinned out. Ren Zhengfei made a decision: import the components himself, and assemble them himself.

他在深圳湾一个简易仓库里雇了工人，仓库的一头用砖头砌了堵墙，组成单间，就是工人的生活之所。1988年起，他决定华为要研发自己的程控交换机。

He hired workers in a simple warehouse on Shenzhen Bay; at one end of the warehouse a brick wall was built to make a separate room, which served as the workers' living quarters. From 1988 on, he decided that Huawei would develop its own program-controlled switchboard.

三、“从楼上跳下去”

III. "Jump from the Top of the Building"

真正让华为脱胎换骨的，是自主研发这条路，而这条路差点要了任正非的命。

What truly transformed Huawei was the path of independent research and development—and that path nearly cost Ren Zhengfei his life.

当时中国通信市场上是“七国八制”的局面：日本NEC和富士通、美国朗讯、加拿大北电、瑞典爱立信、德国西门子、比利时贝尔、法国阿尔卡特——八家外国巨头，七种制式，把中国市场瓜分干净。

At the time China's telecom market was in a state described as "seven nations, eight standards": Japan's NEC and Fujitsu, America's Lucent, Canada's Nortel, Sweden's Ericsson, Germany's Siemens, Belgium's Bell, and France's Alcatel—eight foreign giants with seven different standards had carved up the Chinese market clean.

华为是没有钱搞研发的。任正非向企业拆借，利息高达20%到30%，相当于孤注一掷。

Huawei had no money for research. Ren Zhengfei borrowed from enterprises at interest rates as high as 20 to 30 percent, staking everything on a single throw.

1992年的一天，48岁的任正非站在办公室窗边，一字一顿地对干部们说：

One day in 1992, at forty-eight, Ren Zhengfei stood by the office window and said to his managers, weighing every word:

“这次研发如果失败了，我只有从楼上跳下去，你们还可以另谋出路。”

"If this research fails, my only choice is to jump from the top of the building; you can still find another way out."

1993年，华为研发出自己的交换机C&C08。就在同一年，国内95%的交换机企业都死掉了。华为靠赊销和“农村包围城市”的推广，勉强活了下来。

In 1993 Huawei developed its own switchboard, the C&C08. In that same year, 95 percent of the country's switchboard makers died off. Huawei barely survived, relying on sales on credit and a promotion strategy of "encircling the cities from the countryside."

随着技术积累越来越多，华为销售收入迅速突破亿元、十亿元。

As its technological base grew, Huawei's sales revenue quickly broke through one hundred million and then one billion yuan.

四、差点崩溃的2001—2002

IV. 2001–2002: The Years It Nearly Collapsed

活下来不代表安全。华为很快迎来了“二次创业”期，而这段时间，也是它差点死掉的时期。

Surviving did not mean being safe. Huawei soon entered a period of "second startup," and this period was also when it nearly died.

2000年前后，华为鼓励员工内部创业。任正非一度视作“干儿子”的年轻天才李一男，带着一千多万的设备和一百多名核心骨干离开，创办了港湾网络，直接成为华为的竞争对手。一时间华为内忧外患，元气大伤，业绩一落千丈。

Around 2000, Huawei encouraged employees to start businesses from within. Li Yinan, a young genius whom Ren Zhengfei had once regarded almost as a "godson," left with more than ten million yuan's worth of equipment and over a hundred core staff, founding Harbor Networks, which became a direct competitor to Huawei. For a time Huawei was beset by troubles at home and abroad, its vitality badly sapped, its performance falling off a cliff.

更糟的是，2002年，华为历史上出现了第一次负增长。与此同时，任正非的母亲遭遇车祸去世，他连最后一面都没见上。他自己的健康也在恶化——他得了抑郁症，还动过两次癌症手术。

Worse still, in 2002 Huawei recorded its first negative growth in its history. At the same time, Ren Zhengfei's mother died in a car accident, and he did not even manage to see her one last time. His own health was deteriorating too—he developed depression and underwent cancer surgery twice.

他后来在一篇广为流传的内部文章《华为的冬天》以及多次回忆中说：

He later said this in a widely circulated internal essay, "Huawei's Winter," and in many reminiscences:

“2002年，公司差点崩溃了。IT泡沫的破灭，公司内外矛盾的交集，我却无能为力控制这个公司，有半年时间都是噩梦，梦醒时常常哭。”

"In 2002 the company nearly collapsed. The bursting of the IT bubble, the convergence of conflicts inside and outside the company—yet I was powerless to control the company. For half a year everything was a nightmare, and I often woke up crying."

一个把“活下去”作为公司最高纲领的人，说出的这些话不是表演，而是真实的状态。

For a man who made "survival" his company's highest principle, these words were not a performance but a true account of his state.

华为最终挺了过来，并在此后成长为全球通信设备的领先企业，也成为长期承受国际封锁与压力的中国公司。

Huawei pulled through in the end, and afterward grew into a leading global maker of communications equipment—and a Chinese company that has long borne international blockade and pressure.

五、致2026：关于“43岁”和“活下去”

V. To 2026: On "Forty-Three" and "Survival"

2026年，我们最怕的就是“被抛弃”——被公司抛弃、被行业抛弃、被时代抛弃。任正非的故事里，最刺眼的一个数字是43：他在43岁被除名、离婚、背上巨债、住进十几平方米的房子，然后才开始创业。

In 2026, what we fear most is being "abandoned"—abandoned by our company, abandoned by our industry, abandoned by the times. The most glaring number in Ren Zhengfei's story is forty-three: at forty-three he was struck off the rolls, divorced, buried under enormous debt, and moved into a room of barely more than ten square meters—and only then did he start his business.

这里有两点值得今天的人记住。

There are two things here worth remembering for people today.

第一，“活下去”本身就是一种战略。 华为后来那句“最重要的是活下去”，不是口号，而是从债务、从被骗、从濒临崩溃中长出来的本能。对我们来说，与其执着于“我要多成功”，不如先解决“我怎么活下来、怎么把下一步走稳”。

First, "survival" is itself a strategy. Huawei's later line that "the most important thing is to survive" was not a slogan but an instinct grown out of debt, out of being swindled, out of hovering on the brink of collapse. For us, rather than fixating on "how successful I want to be," it is better first to answer "how do I survive, how do I take the next step steadily."

第二，失败和年龄，都不是终点，但确实会留下伤口。 任正非没有把那次被骗美化成“幸运”——那是真实的灾难。他后来能走出来，靠的是一次次把小事做对的耐心，而不是什么惊天逆转。

Second, neither failure nor age is an endpoint, but they do leave wounds. Ren Zhengfei never prettified that swindle as "good fortune"—it was a real catastrophe. What enabled him to come through in the end was the patience to get small things right again and again, not some earth-shaking reversal.

一个人的中年，不是被裁掉的那一刻决定的，而是他决定第二天早上还起不起来干活的那一刻决定的。

A person's midlife is not decided by the moment he is laid off, but by the moment he decides whether to get up the next morning and go back to work.

值得一提的是，面对2002年前后的危机，任正非并没有选择“收缩保命”，而是一边压缩成本，一边继续往研发里砸钱。他后来解释过这种看起来“反常识”的做法：越是冬天，越要把钱花在能长出春天的那个地方。对一个43岁才起步的公司来说，“活下去”从来不是“守成”，而是“继续往前挪”。

It is worth noting that, facing the crisis around 2002, Ren Zhengfei did not choose to "retrench to stay alive"; he cut costs while continuing to pour money into research. He later explained this seemingly "counterintuitive" approach: the deeper the winter, the more you must spend money where spring can grow. For a company that only got started at forty-three, "survival" was never "holding the line" but "continuing to inch forward."

最后还有一句值得琢磨。华为后来把“以客户为中心，以奋斗者为本，长期坚持艰苦奋斗”写进了公司的核心价值观。这几句话不是口号，而是从任正非43岁那年、从那间十几平方米的出租屋里长出来的。他自己就是“长期艰苦奋斗”的第一个样本——一个被骗过、被除名过、住过棚屋的人，用三十年时间，把“活下去”三个字，做成了一家世界级公司。

One last line is worth pondering. Huawei later wrote into its core values the phrases "customer-centric, dedicated to strivers, and committed to long-term hard work." These are not slogans; they grew out of Ren Zhengfei's forty-third year, out of that rented room of barely more than ten square meters. He himself was the first sample of "long-term hard work"—a man who had been swindled, struck off the rolls, and who had lived in a shack, who over thirty years turned the word "survival" into a world-class company.

所以对今天的中年人来说，任正非的故事也许能带来一点实际的安慰：你不是“过期”了，你只是还没到你那家公司成立的那一年。

So for today's middle-aged people, Ren Zhengfei's story may offer a bit of practical comfort: you are not "past your expiration date"; you simply haven't yet reached the year your own company gets founded.
