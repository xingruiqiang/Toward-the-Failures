曼德拉：在牢里待了27年的“囚犯”

Mandela: The “Prisoner” Who Spent 27 Years Behind Bars

——他当总统时，已经76岁

— He was already seventy-six when he became president

← 返回主页 Home第二辑　屡败屡战的坚韧行者27 / 100约 2939 字 · 2939 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，如果你问一个人：人生被浪费27年，还剩下什么？很多人会觉得，那就是彻底完了。

In 2026, if you ask someone what is left after twenty-seven years of a life have been wasted, most people would feel that it is simply all over.

但有一个人，27年是在监狱里度过的。他出狱时已经71岁，四年后当选总统，成了整个南非的“国父”。更关键的是：这27年，并没有把他变成一个满心仇恨的人，反而把他变成了一个能说服仇人和解的人。

But there was one man who spent twenty-seven years in prison. He was seventy-one when he walked out, and four years later he was elected president, becoming the “father of the nation” for all of South Africa. More crucially: those twenty-seven years did not turn him into a man consumed by hatred; they turned him into a man who could persuade his enemies to reconcile.

他叫纳尔逊·曼德拉。他的故事说明：监狱可以关住一个人的身体，但关不住一个人要不要变成什么样子。

His name was Nelson Mandela. His story shows that a prison can lock up a person’s body, but it cannot lock up what that person chooses to become.

一、酋长家的孩子，走上了对抗制度的路

I. A Chief’s Son Who Took the Road Against the System

1918年7月18日，曼德拉出生在南非特兰斯凯的一个部落酋长家庭。按出身，他本可以过一种相对体面的生活。但他后来进了大学，读了法律，在金山大学（威特沃特斯兰德大学）接触到了不同种族的学生和各种争取平等的思想。

On July 18, 1918, Mandela was born into a tribal chief’s family in Transkei, South Africa. By birth, he could have lived a relatively respectable life. But he went on to university and studied law, and at the University of the Witwatersrand he came into contact with students of different races and with various ideas about the struggle for equality.

当时统治南非的是国民党，它通过一系列法律，把种族隔离系统化、制度化：按肤色决定一个人能住在哪、能上什么学、能不能投票，甚至能不能自由走动。黑人可以被任意搜捕、殴打。肤色，成了一道决定命运的墙。

At the time, South Africa was ruled by the National Party, which through a series of laws systematized and institutionalized apartheid: skin color decided where a person could live, what school he could attend, whether he could vote, even whether he could move about freely. Black people could be seized and beaten at will. Skin color became a wall that decided a person’s fate.

1944年，曼德拉加入非洲人国民大会（非国大），投身政治。

In 1944, Mandela joined the African National Congress (ANC) and threw himself into politics.

二、从“非暴力”到“民族之矛”

II. From “Nonviolence” to the “Spear of the Nation”

早年，非国大主张非暴力抗争。1952年，曼德拉参与领导“蔑视不公正法令运动”，因此遭到当局两次限制其公开活动的禁令。他越来越意识到，只靠非暴力，对方根本不会松手。

In its early years, the ANC advocated nonviolent resistance. In 1952, Mandela helped lead the “Campaign of Defiance against Unjust Laws,” and for this he was twice banned by the authorities from public activity. He came to realize more and more clearly that with nonviolence alone, the other side would never loosen its grip.

1961年，曼德拉参与创建非国大的武装组织“民族之矛”（Umkhonto we Sizwe），并任总司令。他秘密出国访问，出席反殖民运动大会，呼吁国际社会对南非实施经济制裁。

In 1961, Mandela helped found Umkhonto we Sizwe (“Spear of the Nation”), the armed wing of the ANC, and became its commander-in-chief. He traveled abroad in secret, attended anti-colonial congresses, and called on the international community to impose economic sanctions on South Africa.

在那个年代，他被一些西方政客视为“恐怖分子”——英国首相撒切尔曾称非国大是“恐怖组织”，美国总统里根也曾谴责曼德拉。这不是后世的说法，而是当时真实存在的主流态度。

In those years, he was regarded by some Western politicians as a “terrorist” — British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher once called the ANC a “terrorist organization,” and U.S. President Ronald Reagan also denounced Mandela. This is not a later-day characterization, but a mainstream attitude that genuinely existed at the time.

三、1962：逮捕，与27年

III. 1962: Arrest, and Twenty-seven Years

1962年8月，曼德拉被捕。当局以“煽动罪”和“非法越境罪”判他5年监禁。

In August 1962, Mandela was arrested. The authorities sentenced him to five years in prison for “incitement” and “illegal border crossing.”

1964年，在著名的“里沃尼亚审判”中，他又被以“企图以暴力推翻政府”的罪名，改判为终身监禁。他被押往南非戒备最森严的监狱——罗本岛。

In 1964, in the famous “Rivonia Trial,” he was re-sentenced to life imprisonment on the charge of “attempting to overthrow the government by violence.” He was taken to South Africa’s most heavily guarded prison — Robben Island.

在罗本岛上，他被关在狭小阴暗的牢房里；在比勒陀利亚的监狱里，他曾被单独关押，每天只有上午和下午各半小时的活动时间，房间里没有自然光，什么都不能写。他做苦役，采石、修路。此后的牢狱岁月，前后加起来整整27年。

On Robben Island he was held in a small, dark cell; at the Pretoria prison he was once held in solitary confinement, allowed only half an hour of exercise in the morning and another half hour in the afternoon each day, with no natural light in his room and nothing whatever he could write. He did hard labor, quarrying stone and building roads. The prison years that followed added up to a full twenty-seven years.

但奇怪的事发生了：当局把他关得越久，他在世界上的分量越重。

But something strange happened: the longer the authorities kept him locked up, the greater his weight in the world.

1981年，上万名法国人联名请愿，要求释放他；1982年，全球53个国家的2000名市长签名呼吁；1983年，英国78名议员发表联合声明，50多个城市的市长在伦敦游行。许多国家向南非施加压力。他的形象，随着牢门一天天关闭，反而一天天高大。

In 1981, tens of thousands of French people signed a petition demanding his release; in 1982, two thousand mayors from fifty-three countries around the world signed an appeal; in 1983, seventy-eight British members of Parliament issued a joint statement, and the mayors of more than fifty cities marched in London. Many countries brought pressure to bear on South Africa. As the prison door closed day after day, his image grew taller and taller instead.

四、一句“不”,和一次惊天和解

IV. One “No,” and a Stunning Reconciliation

1985年，南非当局提出条件：只要曼德拉放弃武装斗争，就可以获得自由。他拒绝了。

In 1985, the South African authorities set out their terms: if Mandela would give up armed struggle, he could go free. He refused.

他的理由后来被写进了他自传《漫漫自由路》的精神里：只有自由人，才能谈判。一个在牢里的人，没有资格代表被压迫的人去签协议。

His reasoning was later woven into the spirit of his autobiography, Long Walk to Freedom: only a free man can negotiate. A man in prison has no standing to sign an agreement on behalf of the oppressed.

1990年2月11日，迫于内外压力，南非当局宣布无条件释放曼德拉。他走出监狱大门时，71岁。

On February 11, 1990, under pressure from within and without, the South African authorities announced Mandela’s unconditional release. He was seventy-one when he walked out through the prison gates.

很多人以为，接下来会是一场清算、一场复仇。毕竟27年的青春、健康、自由都被夺走了。黑人们高呼着要拿回属于他们的一切，白人们则恐惧“多数黑人的报复”。

Many people assumed that what followed would be a reckoning, a revenge. After all, twenty-seven years of youth, health, and freedom had been taken from him. Black people were crying out to take back everything that was theirs, while white people feared “the revenge of the black majority.”

但曼德拉做了一件让全世界至今仍在谈论的事：他选择了宽恕与和解。

But Mandela did something the whole world is still talking about: he chose forgiveness and reconciliation.

他说过一句被无数人引用的话：

He said a line that countless people have quoted:

当我走出囚室、迈向通往自由的大门时，我已经清楚，自己若不能把痛苦与怨恨留在身后，那么其实我仍在狱中。

“As I walked out of the cell and toward the gate that leads to freedom, I already knew that if I could not leave my pain and resentment behind, then I would in truth still be in prison.”

1994年5月10日，曼德拉宣誓就任南非总统。他邀请了三位当年曾伤害过他的人到场：一位是狱警，一位是镇压黑人最残酷的前白人总统，一位是当年在审判中力主判他死刑的检察官。他就这样，当着全世界的面，向他们，也向所有南非人，做出了“和解”的承诺。

On May 10, 1994, Mandela was sworn in as President of South Africa. He invited to be present three of the people who had once harmed him: a prison guard, the former white president who had most brutally suppressed black people, and the prosecutor who had argued for his death sentence at his trial. And so, before the eyes of the whole world, he made a pledge of “reconciliation” to them, and to all South Africans.

他还邀请前白人总统德克勒克担任副总统；1995年，他穿上南非橄榄球队的球衣，出现在橄榄球世界杯决赛现场——那项运动，曾是白人种族隔离的象征。他要让“一支球队，一个国家”成为可能。

He also invited former white president F. W. de Klerk to serve as deputy president; in 1995 he put on the jersey of South Africa’s national rugby team and appeared at the final of the Rugby World Cup — a sport that had once been a symbol of white apartheid. He wanted to make “one team, one nation” possible.

1993年，他与德克勒克共同获得诺贝尔和平奖。1999年，他只任一届总统就卸任，把权力交给了更年轻的人。

In 1993, he and de Klerk jointly received the Nobel Peace Prize. In 1999, having served only a single term, he stepped down and handed power to a younger generation.

五、致2026年的我们

V. To Us in 2026

曼德拉的故事，不是“苦难终有回报”那么简单。它真正震撼人的地方，在于他给出了一个选择：

Mandela’s story is not as simple as “suffering is at last repaid.” What truly shakes people is that he offered a choice:

在拥有了足够的理由去恨的时刻，他选择了不恨。

At the moment when he had every reason to hate, he chose not to hate.

27年，对于一个政治人物来说，几乎意味着彻底作废：没有职位，没有影响力，和朋友、家人、时代隔绝。可正是这27年，让他从“一个激进的反抗者”，变成了“一个能带领全民族走向和解的人”。他在监狱里读书、思考、和不同的人谈判——牢房成了他最深的一所大学。

Twenty-seven years, for a political figure, almost means being rendered void: no office, no influence, cut off from friends, family, and his own times. Yet it was precisely these twenty-seven years that turned him from “a radical resister” into “a man who could lead a whole people toward reconciliation.” In prison he read, thought, and negotiated with all kinds of people — the cell became his deepest university.

对2026年的我们，他留下两句话。

To us in 2026, he left two statements.

第一，你现在的“被耽误”，不一定就是耽误。如果你此刻困在一个看不到前途的位置、一段停滞的时期、一种无能为力的处境里——请别急着把它定义为“白费”。有些东西，只能在没有选择的时候才长出来：耐心、定力、对人性的理解。

First: being “held back” as you are now does not necessarily mean being held back. If at this moment you are trapped in a position with no future, in a stalled period, in a situation of powerlessness — don’t be too quick to define it as “wasted.” Some things can only grow when you have no choices left: patience, composure, an understanding of human nature.

第二，决定你是谁的最大权力，永远在你手里——那就是选择怎么回应你遭遇的事。南非完全可以滑进内战，曼德拉出狱时，双方都准备好流血。他却选择了把痛苦“留在身后”。这不是软弱，这是一个人能拥有的、最难的一种强大。

Second: the greatest power to decide who you are is always in your own hands — the power to choose how you respond to what you meet. South Africa could easily have slid into civil war; when Mandela came out of prison, both sides were ready to shed blood. Yet he chose to leave his pain “behind.” This is not weakness; it is the hardest kind of strength a person can possess.

自由不只是砸掉锁链，而是用尊重别人自由的方式继续活下去。这是他留给所有后来者的定义。

Freedom is not only smashing one’s chains; it is continuing to live in a way that respects the freedom of others. That is the definition he left for all who came after.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

7. “曼德拉被关押27年”——准确。但需注意其监禁地点的变化：主要在罗本岛（1964—1982），后期转至波尔斯摩尔、维克多·韦斯特、康斯坦蒂亚等监狱，最后一段为软禁式看管。文中已作简要说明。

7. “Mandela was imprisoned for twenty-seven years” — accurate. But note the changes in his places of detention: mainly Robben Island (1964–1982), later transferred to Pollsmoor, Victor Verster, and Constantia prisons, the final period being a house-arrest-style custody. The essay has briefly explained this.

8. “曼德拉出狱时71岁、当总统时76岁”——按生年1918推算：1990年获释时71岁，1994年就任时76岁，无误。

8. “Mandela was 71 when he was released and 76 when he became president” — reckoned from his birth in 1918: he was 71 at his release in 1990 and 76 at his inauguration in 1994. No error.

9. “他一开始就主张非暴力”——需分层。非国大早期确以非暴力为主，但1961年后曼德拉转而参与创建武装组织“民族之矛”，此后长期兼有武装斗争。文中如实呈现这一转变。

9. “He advocated nonviolence from the very start” — this needs qualification. In its early years the ANC did rely mainly on nonviolence, but after 1961 Mandela helped found the armed organization Umkhonto we Sizwe, and thereafter long combined armed struggle with other means. The essay presents this shift faithfully.

10. “西方一直支持曼德拉”——不符史实。在其斗争年代，英美政要确曾将非国大/曼德拉视为“恐怖分子”或“恐怖组织”（如撒切尔、里根的相关表态）；美国直至2008年才将曼德拉从相关观察名单中移除。文中已注明，以免造成“西方一贯支持”的误读。

10. “The West always supported Mandela” — this does not match the historical record. During his years of struggle, British and American statesmen did regard the ANC and Mandela as “terrorists” or a “terrorist organization” (such as the relevant statements by Thatcher and Reagan); the United States did not remove Mandela from the relevant watch list until 2008. The essay notes this, to avoid the misreading that “the West consistently supported him.”

11. “就职典礼邀请的三位狱警/旧敌”——这一广为人知的情节，不同版本在人物身份细节上略有出入（如所邀狱警人数、“前总统博塔”“检察官珀西·犹他”等）。文中按“邀请了曾伤害他的人（含狱警等）”概括，未将每一位身份作绝对化表述。

11. “The three prison guards or old enemies invited to the inauguration” — in this widely known episode, different versions differ slightly in details of identity (such as the number of guards invited, references to “former president Botha” and “prosecutor Percy Yutar,” and so on). The essay summarizes it as “inviting those who had harmed him (including prison guards, and so on),” without stating any single identity in absolute terms.
