褚时健：71岁入狱，74岁上山种橙的“橙王”

Chu Shijian: Jailed at 71, the "Orange King" Who Went Up the Mountain at 74 to Grow Oranges

——衡量一个人成功的标志，不是看他登到顶峰的高度，而是看他跌到低谷的反弹力

— The measure of a person's success is not how high he climbs to the summit, but how forcefully he bounces back from the bottom

← 返回主页 Home第三辑　在废墟上重建的人48 / 100约 3154 字 · 3154 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，很多人正在经历自己的“30岁危机”：行业下行，裁员，35岁被优化，简历投出去石沉大海。我们习惯把“年龄”当成一道越来越窄的门——过了某个岁数，好像就再没有重新开始的权利。

In 2026, many people are going through their own "crisis at thirty": their industry is in decline, layoffs are everywhere, they get "optimized out" at thirty-five, and the résumés they send out vanish without a reply. We are used to treating "age" as a door that keeps narrowing—past a certain number, it seems, you no longer have the right to start over.

但有一个人，是在71岁被判无期徒刑、独生女儿在狱中自杀之后，74岁才重新上山的。他在荒山上种了十年橙子，85岁那年，让自己的橙子卖到了北京。人们管那橙子叫“褚橙”，也管它叫“励志橙”。

But there was a man who, at 71, was sentenced to life imprisonment, whose only daughter took her own life in prison—and who, at 74, went back up the mountain. He planted oranges on barren hillside for ten years, and at 85 he had his oranges selling in Beijing. People called those oranges "Chu Orange," and they also called them "inspiration oranges."

他叫褚时健。他这一生，前半段把一家快倒闭的烟厂做成了亚洲第一，被称为“亚洲烟草大王”；后半段，他从顶峰摔进牢房，又用一颗橙子，把自己从深渊里一点一点拉了回来。

His name was Chu Shijian. In the first half of his life, he turned a nearly bankrupt cigarette factory into the best in Asia, and was called the "king of Asian tobacco." In the second half, he fell from the summit into a prison cell—and then, with a single orange, pulled himself back up from the abyss, inch by inch.

他说过一句话：“衡量一个人成功的标志，不是看他登到顶峰的高度，而是看他跌到低谷的反弹力。”

He once said: "The measure of a person's success is not how high he climbs to the summit, but how forcefully he bounces back from the bottom."

一、酒坊里的少年

I. A Boy in the Distillery

1928年，褚时健出生在云南玉溪华宁县一个普通的农家。15岁那年，父亲在日军轰炸中受伤后去世，家里一下子塌了顶梁柱。

In 1928, Chu Shijian was born into an ordinary farming family in Huaning County, Yuxi, Yunnan. When he was 15, his father was wounded in a Japanese bombing raid and died soon after, and the family's main pillar collapsed at once.

作为长子，他辍了学，回家烤酒、种地，和母亲一起撑起一个家。烤酒是个苦活，要守着灶火，夜里几次起来看火候、翻料。这段日子里，他练出了两样东西：一是算账的本事——他会把每一斤苞谷、每一斤柴、每一分工钱都算得清清楚楚；二是“把事情做到极致”的脾气。同样一灶酒，别人出酒率低，他能多出两成。

As the eldest son, he dropped out of school, came home to distill liquor and work the fields, and helped his mother hold the household together. Distilling is hard labor: you have to watch the stove, getting up several times a night to check the heat and turn the mash. In those years he developed two things. One was a gift for accounts—he could reckon every catty of corn, every catty of firewood, every cent of wages down to the last detail. The other was a temper that had to "do a thing to the absolute limit." From the same batch of mash, where others got a low yield, he could get twenty percent more liquor.

这两样东西，后来跟着他走了一辈子。

Those two things stayed with him for the rest of his life.

青年时期，他扛过枪、打过仗，二十出头就当了地方上的小领导。新中国成立后，他在基层一路做事，从人事科长做起。如果没有后面的事，他大概会是一个普通的、还不错的地方干部。

In his youth he carried a rifle and went to war, and by his early twenties he was a minor local official. After the founding of New China, he worked his way up at the grassroots level, starting as a personnel section chief. Had nothing of what followed happened, he would probably have been an ordinary, fairly capable local cadre.

转折发生在1979年。那一年，他51岁，被派去接手玉溪卷烟厂。

The turning point came in 1979. That year, at 51, he was sent to take over the Yuxi Cigarette Factory.

二、“烟王”的诞生

II. The Birth of the "Tobacco King"

那时的玉溪卷烟厂，是一家濒临倒闭的小厂：设备老旧，产品滞销，工人没劲头，仓库里堆着卖不出去的烟。

The Yuxi Cigarette Factory at that time was a small plant on the verge of collapse: aging equipment, unsold products, demoralized workers, and a warehouse piled with cigarettes nobody would buy.

褚时健进去以后，做的第一件事不是生产，而是“算账”和“改规则”。他推行“包干制”——工资、奖金和产量、质量挂钩。这在当时是打破平均主义的做法，很多老国企不敢碰。他还抓技术、抓原料，亲自跑到烟田里去，研究烟叶怎么种才够香。

Once Chu Shijian came in, the first thing he did was not to produce, but to "do the accounts" and "change the rules." He introduced a "contract-and-quota" system (baogan)—wages and bonuses tied to output and quality. At the time this broke with egalitarianism, and many old state-owned enterprises dared not touch it. He also pushed on technology and raw materials, going out to the tobacco fields himself to study how the leaves should be grown to be fragrant enough.

效果很快显现：他把这家小厂带到了全国第一、世界第五。到上世纪90年代中期，“红塔山”成了中国名牌香烟，光这个品牌的无形资产就高达332亿元。在他效力红塔集团的十几年里，累计创造利税超过1400亿元，而他自己获得的报酬，加起来不到百万。

The results showed quickly: he took this small factory to number one in China and fifth in the world. By the mid-1990s, "Hongtashan" had become a famous Chinese cigarette brand, whose intangible assets alone were valued at 33.2 billion yuan. Over the dozen-plus years he worked for the Hongta Group, it generated cumulative taxes and profits of more than 140 billion yuan; his own compensation, all told, came to less than a million.

他后来成为“十大改革风云人物”，被称为“亚洲烟草大王”、“中国烟草大王”。这是他人生的顶点。

He later became one of the "Ten Figures of Reform," and was called the "king of Asian tobacco" and the "king of Chinese tobacco." This was the summit of his life.

三、从顶峰坠落

III. Falling from the Summit

1995年2月，一封匿名举报信，把褚时健从人生巅峰直接拽了下来。

In February 1995, an anonymous letter of accusation yanked Chu Shijian straight down from the peak of his life.

调查结果是：他主谋私分公款、贪污受贿，其中个人贪污约170多万美元。这个数字的确切构成，判决书讲得清楚，本文不重复展开细节，但有一句话可以作为背景：他后来一再表达的意思是，为国家贡献了一辈子，临近退休，想“为自己的将来想想”。

The investigation concluded that he had masterminded the private distribution of public funds and taken bribes, personally embezzling some 1.7 million US dollars. The exact composition of that figure is set out clearly in the judgment, and this essay will not rehearse the details. But one sentence can serve as background: what he repeatedly expressed afterward was the idea that he had given his whole life to the state, and, nearing retirement, wanted "to think about his own future."

1999年1月9日，云南省高级人民法院宣判：褚时健因巨额贪污和巨额财产来源不明罪，判处无期徒刑，剥夺政治权利终身。那一年，他71岁。

On January 9, 1999, the Yunnan Higher People's Court ruled: Chu Shijian was sentenced to life imprisonment for massive embezzlement and for possessing huge amounts of property whose source he could not explain, with deprivation of political rights for life. That year he was 71.

而在此之前，1995年12月1日，他唯一的女儿褚映群，在河南洛阳的狱中自杀身亡。妻子马静芬也被关押。一个家，在几个月里碎掉了。

And before that, on December 1, 1995, his only daughter, Chu Yingqun, had taken her own life in a prison in Luoyang, Henan. His wife, Ma Jingfen, was also imprisoned. A family shattered within a few months.

很多人说，褚时健这辈子算是完了。一个70多岁的老人，背着无期徒刑，家破人亡，还能有什么未来？

Many said Chu Shijian's life was over. An old man in his seventies, carrying a life sentence, his family destroyed—what future could he possibly have?

四、哀牢山上，74岁重新开始

IV. On the Ailao Mountains, Starting Over at 74

2001年，因为严重的糖尿病，褚时健获准保外就医，回到家中养病。活动范围被限制在老家一带。

In 2001, because of severe diabetes, Chu Shijian was granted medical parole and returned home to recover. His range of movement was restricted to the area around his hometown.

2002年，他74岁。按他的资历、人脉和年纪，本可以安安静静地过完余生。但他向老朋友借了钱，在云南哀牢山承包了一片荒山——先是2400亩——开始种橙子。

In 2002 he was 74. Given his credentials, his connections, and his age, he could have spent his remaining years in quiet retirement. Instead, he borrowed money from old friends, contracted a stretch of barren mountain in the Ailao range in Yunnan—2,400 mu to start, roughly 400 acres—and began growing oranges.

七十多岁的老人，住在山上，从种苗、施肥、剪枝到灌溉，一样一样地学。他不上网，但每天看报、听收音机，《新闻联播》一天不落。遇到难题，他的第一反应是翻书，经常一个人看到凌晨三四点。为了让橙子的甜酸比达到更适合中国人口味的18:1，他亲手用鸡粪、烟沫、甘蔗糖泥调配出一种特殊的肥料。

An old man in his seventies, living on the mountain, learning everything one item at a time, from seedlings and fertilizing to pruning and irrigation. He didn't use the internet, but he read the newspapers and listened to the radio every day, never missing the evening news. When he hit a problem, his first instinct was to open a book, and he would often read alone until three or four in the morning. To bring the orange's sugar-to-acid ratio to 18:1, the level better suited to Chinese tastes, he personally blended a special fertilizer out of chicken manure, tobacco dust, and sugarcane molasses.

有人问他为什么还要折腾，他的回答很朴素：“不勤快的人，在任何时候也不会有好结果。”

Someone asked him why he was still bothering at his age, and his answer was plain: "A person who isn't diligent will never get a good result, at any time."

橙子第一年不好吃，第二年也一般，销量不畅。前六七年，全靠过去的朋友、徒弟们帮衬。他用了六年时间，才把满山的冰糖橙“驯化”到理想的品质。

The oranges weren't tasty the first year, and only so-so the second; sales were slow. For the first six or seven years he depended entirely on the help of old friends and former apprentices. It took him six years to "tame" the rock sugar oranges all over the mountain to the quality he wanted.

五、“褚橙”为什么甜

V. Why "Chu Orange" Is Sweet

2012年，是褚时健种橙的第十个年头。这一年，褚橙与生鲜电商平台“本来生活”合作，第一次大规模进入北京市场。

2012 was the tenth year of Chu Shijian's orange growing. That year, Chu Orange partnered with the fresh-produce e-commerce platform Benlai Life and entered the Beijing market on a large scale for the first time.

互联网救了这颗橙子。它不只是橙子，它成了一个符号——一个关于“不服输”的符号。人们管它叫“励志橙”。原本预计只试销20吨，结果一个月卖了200吨。2014年，褚橙销售额过亿，纯利七千多万。当年那片2400亩的荒山，成了拥有35万株冰糖橙、固定资产8000万元的现代农业示范基地。

The internet saved this orange. It was more than an orange; it became a symbol—a symbol of refusing to give in. People called it the "inspiration orange." The plan had been to test-sell just 20 tons, but 200 tons sold in a month. In 2014, Chu Orange's sales passed 100 million yuan, with net profits of more than 70 million. That 2,400-mu stretch of barren mountain became a modern agricultural demonstration base with 350,000 rock sugar orange trees and 80 million yuan in fixed assets.

更难得的是他的态度。当越来越多的人把他当“神”来请教时，他一再泼冷水：“不要把我看成神。”

What is rarer still is his attitude. As more and more people came to him as if he were a god, he kept pouring cold water on it: "Don't see me as a god."

他说，自己能把橙子种好，靠的不是什么秘诀，就是把账算清楚、把活干到底。他讨厌被神化，因为他太清楚，一个人是怎么从顶峰摔下来的。

He said that what let him grow good oranges was no secret formula, just doing the accounts clearly and carrying the work through to the end. He hated being deified, because he knew all too well how a person falls from the summit.

2019年3月5日，褚时健在云南玉溪病逝，享年91岁。

On March 5, 2019, Chu Shijian died of illness in Yuxi, Yunnan, aged 91.

六、致2026：人生没有太晚的开始

VI. To 2026: It's Never Too Late to Begin

2026年，我们这代人特别怕两件事：一是“来不及了”，二是“跌倒了就起不来”。

In 2026, our generation is especially afraid of two things: first, that "it's too late," and second, that "once you fall, you can't get up."

35岁焦虑、40岁转行、被裁之后不敢告诉家人……我们太习惯把人生的容错率算得极小极小了。

Anxiety at 35, a career change at 40, not daring to tell your family after being laid off… We have grown far too used to figuring life's margin for error as small, as small as it can possibly be.

但褚时健提醒我们的是另一种算法。

But Chu Shijian reminds us of a different way of reckoning.

他51岁才当上烟厂厂长，71岁入狱，74岁上山，85岁让橙子卖到全国。他的“人生曲线”，不是一道平滑向上的斜线，而是一条从顶峰坠到底、又从底部慢慢爬起来的V字。

He didn't become a cigarette factory director until 51, was jailed at 71, went up the mountain at 74, and at 85 had his oranges selling across the country. His "life curve" is not a smooth upward slope but a V—falling from the summit to the bottom, then slowly climbing back up from the base.

有人说，衡量一个人的成功，不是看他站得多高，而是看他跌到底之后弹得多高。褚时健用一颗橙子，给出了他自己的答案。

Some say that to measure a person's success, you don't look at how high he stands, but at how high he bounces after hitting the bottom. With a single orange, Chu Shijian gave his own answer.

对我们这些还没有跌到谷底、却在焦虑中提前替自己“判了刑”的人来说，他这半生的意思也许很简单：

For those of us who haven't yet hit bottom but, in our anxiety, have already passed sentence on ourselves in advance, the meaning of his later half-life may be very simple:

年龄不是开关，失败也不是终点。真正的分水岭，是你还愿不愿意在明天早上，重新开始干活。

Age is not a switch, and failure is not the end. The real watershed is whether you are still willing to start working again tomorrow morning.

8. “不要把我看成神”——褚时健生前多次公开表述，有采访记录可查。

8. "Don't see me as a god"—stated publicly by Chu Shijian on numerous occasions during his lifetime, with interview records available.

9. 传说与演义：“王石田朴珺合影”一类细节属财经圈谈资，与本文主旨无关，未予采信。另有“褚时健出狱时一无所有”之类的戏剧化说法与事实不符——他保外就医后仍有旧部与朋友资助，这也是他能重新起步的重要条件之一。

9. Legend and embellishment: details such as the "photo with Wang Shi and Tian Pujun" belong to financial-circle gossip and are unrelated to the point of this essay; they have not been accepted here. Another dramatic claim—that "Chu Shijian had nothing when he left prison"—does not match the facts: after his medical parole he still had old subordinates and friends supporting him, and this was one of the important conditions that let him start again.

10. 本文对其早年烤酒、算账习惯的描写，依据公开传记与口述材料，属合理还原，不作史料定性。

10. The depiction in this essay of his early years distilling liquor and his habit of keeping accounts is based on published biographies and oral accounts; it is a reasonable reconstruction and is not offered as historical fact.
