Chapter 051 · Part 3

王兴：校内网卖了、饭否关了，第九次才做成美团

Wang Xing: He Sold Xiaonei, Lost Fanfou—and Only Got Meituan Right on His Ninth Try

——别人叫它“九败一胜”，他自己只说“屡败屡战”

—Others call it "nine defeats, one victory"; he says only "lose again and again, and fight on again and again."

2026年，很多年轻人在纠结一个词：“要不要坚持”。

In 2026, many young people are agonizing over a single phrase: "Should I keep at it?"

坚持得久了，看不到结果，会被说成死磕；换赛道，又会被说成三分钟热度。创业者更是如此，一次失败，可能就意味着资金、团队、信誉全部归零。

Stick with something long enough without seeing results, and people call it stubbornness; switch tracks, and people call it a three-minute passion. Founders are even harder hit: one failure can wipe out their capital, their team, and their reputation all at once.

有一个人，从25岁开始连续创业，做过十来个项目。他比Facebook还早做出中国版校园社交，也做出了中国最早的微博类产品，可这两次，一次被迫卖掉，一次被关停。媒体一度叫他“中国最倒霉的创业者”。

There is a man who began founding companies at twenty-five and has built a dozen or so projects. He created a Chinese version of campus social networking earlier than Facebook did, and he also built China's earliest Twitter-like product. But of those two attempts, one was forcibly sold and the other was shut down. For a time the media called him "China's unluckiest entrepreneur."

他叫王兴。到2010年，他创办了美团。那一年，全国有超过5000家团购网站同时开战。最后活下来并且赢了的，是他。

His name is Wang Xing. By 2010 he had founded Meituan. That year, more than five thousand group-buying sites across the country went to war at once. The one that survived and won was his.

一、一个“好学生的叛逆”

I. The Rebellion of a "Good Student"

1979年，王兴出生在福建龙岩。1997年，他被保送进清华大学电子工程系。2001年本科毕业后，他赴美留学，在特拉华大学读计算机工程。

Wang Xing was born in Longyan, Fujian, in 1979. In 1997 he was admitted to Tsinghua University's Department of Electronic Engineering without having to sit the entrance exam. After finishing his undergraduate degree in 2001, he went to the United States and studied computer engineering at the University of Delaware.

2004年，他做了一个让很多人不理解的决定：中断在美国的博士学业，回国创业。理由是——他看到了社交网络巨大的机会。

In 2004 he made a decision few people could understand: he broke off his doctoral studies in the United States and went home to start a company. His reason—he had seen the enormous opportunity in social networking.

他先做了两个项目：“多多友”（一个泛人群的社交网站）和“游子图”（面向海外用户），都无疾而终。连续失败之后，他把目光锁定在一个更细分的市场：大学校园。

He first built two projects: "Duoduoyou" (a social site for a broad general audience) and "Youzitu" (aimed at users overseas). Both came to nothing. After these repeated failures, he fixed his sights on a narrower market: the university campus.

二、校内网：第一次“活着卖掉”

II. Xiaonei: The First Time He "Sold While Still Alive"

2005年12月，王兴和清华的上下铺同学王慧文、赖斌强等人，一起创办了校内网。

In December 2005, Wang Xing founded Xiaonei together with his Tsinghua dormitory classmates Wang Huiwen, Lai Binqiang, and others.

这个项目做得很早——比Facebook进入中国还早。它迅速在校园里走红，2006年用户就突破了100万。

The project was made remarkably early—earlier than Facebook's arrival in China. It quickly became a campus phenomenon; by 2006 it had passed one million users.

但问题也随之而来：服务器和带宽成本飙升，融资又迟迟不到位。创始团队没有足够的钱去支撑快速增长。最终，他们只能把校内网卖掉——买家是千橡互动集团（陈一舟），作价约200万美元。

But problems came with it: server and bandwidth costs soared, and funding was slow in arriving. The founding team did not have enough money to sustain such rapid growth. In the end they had no choice but to sell Xiaonei—the buyer was Oak Pacific Interactive (Chen Yizhou), for about two million dollars.

多年以后，校内网成为人人网的根基之一，人人网赴美上市首日市值一度超过70亿美元。而这一切，和王兴已经没有关系了。

Years later, Xiaonei became one of the foundations of Renren, and on its first day of trading after listing in the U.S., Renren's market value briefly exceeded seven billion dollars. None of it had anything to do with Wang Xing anymore.

这是他第一次“为他人做嫁衣”。

That was the first time he made a wedding dress for someone else's bride.

三、饭否与海内：第二次、第三次归零

III. Fanfou and Hainei: The Second and Third Times Back to Zero

2007年，王兴同时推出了两个新项目：饭否和海内网。

In 2007 Wang Xing launched two new projects at once: Fanfou and Hainei.

饭否是中国最早的微博类产品，比新浪微博还早两年，聚集了当时中国最优质的一批用户。海内网则是更有用户黏性的社交网站。

Fanfou was China's earliest Twitter-like product, two years ahead of Sina Weibo, and it gathered the finest users in China at the time. Hainei was a social network with stronger user stickiness.

结果，两次都不顺。海内网面对的竞争对手开心网推出了“抢车位”“偷菜”等一系列小游戏，迅速抢走了大量用户；王兴团队并不擅长游戏，最终在2008年放弃了海内。饭否则在2009年因监管原因被关闭，此后长时间未能重启，等它再上线时，新浪微博已经崛起，先发优势彻底丧失。

Both, however, went badly. Hainei's competitor Kaixin001 launched a series of mini-games—"stealing parking spaces," "stealing vegetables"—and quickly pulled away a large share of users; Wang Xing's team was not good at games, and in 2008 they gave up on Hainei. Fanfou was shut down in 2009 for regulatory reasons and could not be revived for a long time; by the time it came back online, Sina Weibo had risen, and its first-mover advantage was gone for good.

三次创业，三次归零。 那时的王兴，已经接近“破产”边缘。

Three ventures, three times back to zero. By then Wang Xing was close to the edge of "bankruptcy."

但他没有停下来。他说过一句话：“传统行业创业好比登山，山就在那里，你总有机会接近；互联网创业犹如冲浪，浪潮一浪接一浪，错过了这一波就再也没有机会了。”

But he did not stop. He once said: "Building a business in a traditional industry is like climbing a mountain—the mountain is simply there, and you always have a chance to approach it; building an internet business is like surfing—one wave follows another, and once you miss this wave, you never get another chance."

四、千团大战：第九次，他终于赢了

IV. The War of a Thousand Group-Buying Sites: On the Ninth Try, He Finally Won

2010年3月4日，王兴创办了美团网。

On March 4, 2010, Wang Xing founded Meituan.

这个项目几乎完全照搬了美国Groupon的团购模式。当时几乎没有人看好他——团购赛道最疯狂的时候，全国有超过5000家团购网站同时开战，广告大战烧掉数十亿。拉手网、窝窝团、24券……每一个都比美团有钱、有势头。

The project copied almost wholesale the group-buying model of America's Groupon. Almost no one was bullish on him at the time—at the height of the frenzy, more than five thousand group-buying sites nationwide went to war at once, and the advertising battles burned through billions. Lashou, Wowo Tuan, 24Quan... every one of them had more money and more momentum than Meituan.

但王兴做对了一件事：他不烧钱打广告，而是死磕运营效率和用户体验。

But Wang Xing got one thing right: instead of burning money on advertising, he hammered away at operational efficiency and user experience.

当同行们疯狂补贴拉新时，美团在优化商家供应链；当同行们密集投放地铁公交广告时，美团在练内功。他为公司定下的价值观是：“消费者第一，商家第二。”

While his rivals frantically handed out subsidies to acquire users, Meituan was optimizing merchants' supply chains; while his rivals blanketed the subways and buses with ads, Meituan was building its internal strength. The value he set for the company was: "Consumers first, merchants second."

2011年，团购泡沫破裂，5000家网站死掉九成。美团不但活了下来，还在2012年实现了盈利。

In 2011 the group-buying bubble burst, and nine out of ten of those five thousand sites died. Meituan not only survived but turned a profit in 2012.

这是他创业将近十年后，第一次真正意义上的“胜仗”。

It was the first true "victory" of his career, nearly a decade after he began.

此后，美团提出“T型战略”，从团购横向扩展到电影、酒店、外卖等垂直行业。2015年10月，美团与大众点评宣布合并；2018年9月，美团点评在香港上市。

After that, Meituan proposed its "T-shaped strategy," expanding sideways from group-buying into verticals such as film, hotels, and food delivery. In October 2015, Meituan and Dianping announced their merger; in September 2018, Meituan Dianping listed in Hong Kong.

但王兴的“顺风”也并没有一直持续。外卖业务要面对阿里、饿了么的凶猛补贴战；出行、共享单车（收购摩拜）等新业务烧掉了巨额资金；与部分早期投资方之间的关系也几经波折。也就是说，即便在美团已经“赢了”之后，他依然要一次次处理新的危机。“九败一胜”不是终点，更像是一种长期的生存状态。

But Wang Xing's tailwind did not last forever. Food delivery had to face brutal subsidy wars with Alibaba and Ele.me; new ventures such as ride-hailing and bike-sharing (the acquisition of Mobike) burned through enormous sums; and his relationships with some early investors went through repeated ups and downs. In other words, even after Meituan had "won," he still had to deal with one new crisis after another. "Nine defeats, one victory" is not the finish line—it is more like a long-term condition of survival.

五、他到底“胜”在哪里

V. Where Exactly Did He "Win"?

外界喜欢用“九败一胜”来概括王兴。这个说法有力，但可能也简化了他。

The outside world likes to sum up Wang Xing with "nine defeats, one victory." The phrase is forceful, but it may also flatten him.

从校内网到饭否再到美团，王兴身上有一条连续的线：他一直在做同一件事——把人连接起来，然后解决一个真实的需求。 校内网连接同学，饭否连接表达，美团连接消费者和商家。前两次他没跑通，是因为时机、资金和监管；第三次他跑通了，是因为他学会了“不烧钱、练内功、尊重商业常识”。

From Xiaonei to Fanfou to Meituan, there is one continuous thread in Wang Xing: he has always been doing the same thing—connecting people, and then solving a genuine need. Xiaonei connected classmates, Fanfou connected expression, Meituan connects consumers and merchants. He failed to make the first two work because of timing, money, and regulation; he made it the third time because he had learned to "not burn cash, build internal strength, and respect commercial common sense."

他自己对“企业家精神”的定义是：

His own definition of "entrepreneurship" is this:

“常规想法是，你有多大资源做多大事。但真正的企业家是少数的，（企业家精神）是疯子一般对机会的追求。”

"The conventional idea is: the more resources you have, the bigger the thing you can do. But true entrepreneurs are rare; (entrepreneurship) is a madman's pursuit of opportunity."

有意思的是，他这话里既有“疯子般的追求”，也有前半程摔出来的教训——光有疯子般的追求是不够的，还得有一百次摔倒后仍然愿意再站起来的钝感。

What is interesting is that in his words there is both the "mad pursuit" and the lesson wrung from the falls of his earlier years—a mad pursuit alone is not enough; you also need the numbness that, after falling a hundred times, still leaves you willing to stand up again.

六、致2026：失败不是履历上的污点，而是必要的学费

VI. To 2026: Failure Is Not a Stain on Your Résumé but Necessary Tuition

2026年，我们这个时代对“连续性”极度不宽容。简历上有一段空窗、有一次创业失败、有一次被裁，很多人就觉得自己“脏”了。

In 2026, our age is extremely intolerant of "continuity." A gap on a résumé, one failed startup, one layoff—and many people feel they have been "dirtied."

王兴的经历提供了一个不同的视角：他前面那三次失败，其实是后面那次成功的必要条件。

Wang Xing's experience offers a different perspective: those first three failures were, in fact, the necessary condition for the one success that followed.

如果不是校内网因为缺钱被卖掉，他不会懂得现金流的重要；如果不是饭否被关停，他不会懂得边界和合规；如果不是海内输给游戏，他不会懂得什么叫“做自己擅长的事”。

If Xiaonei had not been sold for lack of money, he would never have understood the importance of cash flow; if Fanfou had not been shut down, he would never have understood boundaries and compliance; if Hainei had not lost to games, he would never have understood what it means to "do what you are good at."

对我们来说，重要的不是“我要几次成功”，而是“我能不能在失败之后，仍然保持下一次尝试的能力和勇气”。

For us, what matters is not "how many times will I succeed," but "can I, after failing, still keep the ability and the courage to try one more time."

还有一点值得说。王兴这个人不太像个“天生的创业者”——他内向、爱读书、喜欢思考，甚至有点学究气。但他有一点很特别：他把每一次失败都当成了调研。 校内网教会他现金流，饭否教会他边界，海内教会他专注，最后所有这些，都变成了美团的“常识”。

There is one more thing worth saying. Wang Xing does not much look like a "born entrepreneur"—he is introverted, loves reading, likes to think, and is even a little bookish. But he has one unusual quality: he treated every failure as research. Xiaonei taught him cash flow, Fanfou taught him boundaries, Hainei taught him focus, and in the end all of it became Meituan's "common sense."

所谓“九败一胜”，说到底不是运气，而是一种拒绝下牌桌的耐心。

The so-called "nine defeats, one victory," in the end, is not luck but a kind of patience—a refusal to leave the card table.

对2026年正在经历第一份工作失败、第一个项目失败、第一次被裁的年轻人来说，王兴的履历大概能提供一点松弛感：你现在的“失败”，很可能只是“第N次”失败，而后面还有第N+1次机会。真正的输，是你自己先不玩了这个牌局。

For the young people of 2026 who are going through their first job failure, their first project failure, their first layoff, Wang Xing's record can perhaps offer a little relief: your current "failure" may well be only the Nth failure, and an N+1st chance still lies ahead. The real loss is when you yourself are the first to quit the game.
