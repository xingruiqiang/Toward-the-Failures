Afterword

关于这100个人，和这本书

About These 100 People, and About This Book

← 返回主页 Home《致敬失败者》

写完之后，我想说三句话。

Now that it is finished, I want to say three things.

第一句：“失败”这个词，被我们用坏了。

The first: we have ruined the word "failure."

我们习惯用它来形容一个人“不行”、“完了”、“没出息”。可这本书里的100个人告诉我们，一个人是否失败，往往要等几十年甚至几百年，才能真的看清楚。哥白尼、孟德尔、梵高、特斯拉、图灵——在他们活着的时候，几乎所有人都认为他们输了；可时间给出的判决，和当时完全相反。

We are used to using it to describe someone as "no good," "finished," "a nobody." But the hundred people in this book tell us that whether a person has failed often cannot be seen clearly for decades, even centuries. Copernicus, Mendel, Van Gogh, Tesla, Turing—while they lived, almost everyone believed they had lost; yet the verdict handed down by time was the exact opposite.

第二句：历史上真正重要的东西，往往不是被成功者推动的，而是被不认输的人推动的。

The second: the things that truly matter in history are usually not carried forward by the successful, but by those who refuse to concede.

这些人里，有的穷死，有的自杀，有的被处死，有的至今下落不明。他们没能活着看到自己“成功”的那一天。可他们留下的东西——一首曲子、一个公式、一幅画、一句诗、一本书、一条航路——替他们活了下来。

Among them, some died poor, some took their own lives, some were executed, some vanished and were never found. They did not live to see their own "success." But the things they left behind—a song, a formula, a painting, a line of poetry, a book, a sea route—lived on in their place.

第三句：这本书，是一群人的故事，也是一个时代的提问。

The third: this book is the story of a group of people, and it is also a question asked by an age.

2026年，我们面对的是另一种“乱世”：经济放缓，行业内卷，房价波动，算法和KPI追着人跑。我们怕掉队，怕被淘汰，怕自己成为那个“失败者”。

In 2026 we face a different kind of "troubled time": a slowing economy, cutthroat competition in every trade, a housing market that swings, algorithms and KPIs forever chasing us down. We fear falling behind, fear being rendered obsolete, fear becoming that "failure."

那就看看他们吧。他们中没有一个人，是靠“不掉队”而被记住的。

Then look at them. Not one of them is remembered for having kept up.

关于成书：本书共收录100位人物的传记，按主题分为五辑，每辑前有导语，每篇文末附《史料来源与辨析》，力求把“史实”与“传说”分开——有趣的叙事，和可靠的材料，本可以并存。部分篇目在资料整理与文字打磨过程中使用了人工智能工具，所有史料均经逐条核对，凡属后世传说、孤证或尚有争议者，均在文末明确标注。

On the making of the book: it gathers the lives of one hundred figures, arranged by theme into five parts, each opening with a preface and each entry closing with a note on "Sources and Critical Notes," in an effort to separate historical fact from legend—for a good story and reliable material can well coexist. Some entries made use of artificial intelligence in the compiling of materials and the polishing of prose; every source was checked line by line, and anything that is later legend, isolated testimony, or still disputed is clearly marked at the end of the entry.

最后，感谢每一位读完这本书的人。

Finally, my thanks to everyone who reads this book to the end.

愿你我都不是真的失败者——只是还没被时代读懂而已。

May you and I never be true failures—only people the age has not yet learned to read.

邢瑞强

Raymond Xing
