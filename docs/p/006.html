Chapter 006 · Part 1

李书福：敢向时代求一次失败的机会

Li Shufu: Bold Enough to Ask the Age for One Chance to Fail

——写给即将谢幕的李书福，也送给困局中的80后90后

— Written for Li Shufu as he takes his final bow, and for the post-80s and post-90s caught in their own impasse

作为一名80后，亲历过时代高速增长，也正面对经济放缓、资产缩水、行业内卷的现实。我们很多人埋头打拼半生，投入时间、金钱、青春，却未必能拿到预期的回报。现实的挫折常常让人害怕失败，害怕试错，害怕一腔热血最后换来一场空。很多时候，我们不敢出发，不是看不到机会，而是输不起。

As a member of the post-80s generation, I lived through an era of rapid growth and now face the reality of a slowing economy, shrinking assets, and industries turning inward. Many of us have labored with our heads down for half a lifetime, pouring in time, money, and youth, and may still not get the return we expected. Setbacks in real life make people afraid of failing, afraid of trial and error, afraid that all their passion will come to nothing. Often we dare not set out—not because we cannot see opportunity, but because we cannot afford to lose.

回望中国实业四十年，李书福的一生，恰恰给我们提供了一种完全不一样的人生样本。一个从台州乡野走出来的草根创业者，没有名校光环，没有资本靠山，一路跌跌撞撞，屡战屡败，却敢当众说出那句振聋发聩的话：请给我一次失败的机会。如今岁月流转，他也即将步入退休，走完轰轰烈烈的创业大半生。他这一段草根逆袭、不惧失败的故事，尤其值得身处压力之下的80后、90后细细品读。

Looking back over forty years of Chinese industry, Li Shufu's life offers us a model of a wholly different kind. A grassroots entrepreneur who came out of the countryside of Taizhou, with no halo of an elite school and no powerful backer, he stumbled and fell again and again, losing battle after battle—yet he dared to say aloud, in words that jolt the ear: "Give me one chance to fail." Now time has moved on, and he is about to step into retirement, closing out a vigorous half-lifetime as a founder. His story of grassroots ascent and fearlessness in the face of failure deserves especially to be read closely by the post-80s and post-90s under pressure today.

李书福的起点，平凡得不能再平凡。少年时代家境普通，十几岁就出来谋生，靠着120元本钱开照相馆谋生，在改革开放的浪潮里摸爬滚打，一点点挣下第一桶金。他做过废旧金属回收，创办北极花冰箱厂，生意红火一时，却因为产业政策调整，工厂被迫关停，多年心血付诸东流。后来投身海南地产，又遇上泡沫破裂，几千万身家几乎亏光，一夜回到原点。

Li Shufu's starting point was as ordinary as it gets. From a modest family, he left home to make a living in his teens, opening a photo studio with 120 yuan in capital, scrambling through the waves of reform and opening and slowly earning his first pot of gold. He went into scrap metal collection, founded the Beijihua refrigerator plant, and it boomed for a time—until a change in industrial policy forced the plant to shut down and years of effort went down the drain. Later he threw himself into Hainan real estate, only to hit the bursting of a bubble; tens of millions in wealth nearly evaporated and he was back to square one overnight.

换做很多人，几番重创之后，多半会选择求稳，守着一份安稳日子不再冒险。但李书福没有就此躺平。摔过跟头，他反而更加坚定要扎根实业。转身杀入摩托车行业，冲破重重壁垒，把摩托车生意做了起来。可他心中，还有一个旁人看来近乎疯狂的梦想——造属于中国人自己的轿车。

Most people, after a few such blows, would choose safety—settle into a stable life and take no more risks. But Li Shufu did not lie down. If anything, each fall made him more determined to put down roots in industry. He turned and charged into motorcycles, broke through barrier after barrier, and built the business up. Yet in his heart there remained a dream that others saw as nearly insane—to build a sedan that belonged to the Chinese people themselves.

上世纪九十年代，轿车制造几乎是国企的专属领地，民营资本很难踏足。行业内普遍认为造车是高不可攀的高技术壁垒。李书福一句“汽车不就是四个轮子，几个沙发，加上一个铁壳吗”，引来满场嘲讽，大家把他当成不知天高地厚的“汽车疯子”，等着看他惨败收场。没有技术，他就拆解国外整车，反复钻研；没有成熟生产线，工人就拿着榔头钣金，一锤一锤敲出第一台吉利豪情轿车。

In the 1990s, sedan manufacturing was almost the exclusive territory of state-owned enterprises; private capital could hardly get a foothold. The industry generally regarded car-making as an insurmountably high technological barrier. Li Shufu's remark—"Isn't a car just four wheels, a few sofas, and a metal shell?"—drew a roomful of mockery. People took him for a "car lunatic" who didn't know his own limits and waited to watch him go down in flames. With no technology, he took apart foreign vehicles and studied them over and over; with no proper production line, workers pounded sheet metal with hammers, blow by blow, to turn out the first Geely Haoqing sedan.

最难的不是技术，是没有生产资质。投入巨额资金，造出了车子，却拿不到市场准入的“准生证”，生产屡屡被叫停，巨额投资悬于一线，进退维谷。四处奔走求助，到处碰壁，不被理解，不被看好。就在这样近乎绝境的时刻，李书福说出了中国商业史上那句掷地有声的请求：请国家允许民营企业家做轿车梦，如果失败，请给我一次失败的机会吧。

The hardest part was not the technology but the lack of a production license. He invested enormous sums, produced cars, and yet could not obtain the "birth permit" for market access; production was halted again and again, the huge investment hung by a thread, and he was caught between advance and retreat. He ran everywhere seeking help, hit walls wherever he went, was understood by no one and favored by no one. It was at such a moment, near the end of his rope, that Li Shufu made the request that rings down China's business history: "Let the state allow a private entrepreneur to dream of building sedans. And if I fail—give me one chance to fail."

他不求保证成功，只求允许试错。成功了，为中国汽车闯出一条民营道路；失败了，全部损失自己承担，不给国家增添负担，只留下一份教训与经验。这份坦荡，这份敢于直面失败的勇气，打动了很多人。

He did not ask for a guarantee of success, only for permission to try and fail. If he succeeded, he would carve out a private path for China's auto industry; if he failed, he would bear the entire loss himself, add no burden to the state, and leave behind only a lesson and some experience. That openness, that courage to face failure squarely, moved many people.

这条路依旧走得无比艰难。下线仪式发出几百份邀请函，业内几乎无人到场；第一批样车品质粗糙，他不惜把上百台车辆直接碾压销毁，绝不把劣质产品推向市场；资金链数次濒临断裂，银行不愿放贷，同行冷眼旁观，嘲讽与质疑如影随形。一直到2001年，中国加入WTO前夕，吉利终于拿到轿车生产资质，成为国内首家获得轿车生产许可的民营企业，草根造车的梦想才算真正落地。

The road remained desperately hard. Hundreds of invitations went out for the roll-off ceremony, and almost no one from the industry came; the first batch of sample cars was rough in quality, and he would rather crush and destroy more than a hundred of them outright than push shoddy products onto the market; the cash flow came near to breaking time and again, banks would not lend, peers watched coldly from the sidelines, and mockery and doubt followed at every step. It was not until 2001, on the eve of China's entry into the WTO, that Geely finally obtained a sedan production license, becoming the first private enterprise in the country to hold one—and only then did the grassroots dream of building cars truly land.

拿到资质，只是新征程的起点。之后的李书福继续上演了震惊全球的商业篇章。2010年，实力尚有限的吉利，完成“蛇吞象”，以18亿美元收购百年豪华品牌沃尔沃100%股权，当时全世界都在质疑，认为草根民企会毁掉这个经典品牌。他顶住舆论压力，尊重原有团队，保留沃尔沃独立运营，让亏损的豪华品牌重获新生，同时反哺吉利自身技术成长，改写了中国汽车工业的格局。往后又布局领克、路特斯，布局新能源，从低端代步车，一步步走向全球汽车产业的舞台中央。

Obtaining the license was only the starting point of a new journey. What followed was a business chapter that astonished the world. In 2010, Geely—still limited in strength—completed a "snake swallowing an elephant," acquiring 100 percent of the century-old luxury brand Volvo for 1.8 billion US dollars. At the time the whole world doubted it, believing a grassroots private company would ruin this classic brand. He withstood the pressure of public opinion, respected the existing team, and kept Volvo operating independently, bringing the loss-making luxury brand back to life while at the same time feeding its technology back into Geely's own growth and rewriting the landscape of China's auto industry. Later he built out Lynk & Co and Lotus, moved into new energy, and advanced step by step from a low-end runabout toward the center of the global auto industry.

回望李书福的半生，他不是一路开挂的天才，而是一个不断和失败相处的实践者。开照相馆遇到管制、冰箱厂关停、地产巨亏、造车没有资质、收购海外品牌饱受质疑，人生重大挫折一次接着一次。他最可贵的特质，不是永远能赢，而是不怕输，敢争取一个失败的机会。很多人害怕失败，总想确保万无一失才敢迈步；而李书福的逻辑是：就算最后失败，试过本身就有价值。

Looking back on Li Shufu's half-lifetime, he is no genius coasting on easy wins but a man of practice who has learned to live alongside failure. The photo studio ran into restrictions, the refrigerator plant was shut down, real estate brought huge losses, car-making lacked a license, the overseas acquisition drew heavy skepticism—major setbacks came one after another. His most valuable trait is not that he always wins but that he is not afraid to lose and dares to fight for a chance to fail. Many people fear failure and want to be sure of every step before they take it; Li Shufu's logic is simply this: even if you fail in the end, the trying itself has value.

这恰恰照见当下80后、90后的困境。我们成长于经济高速上行的时代，习惯了努力就有回报的环境。如今房产波动、行业增速放缓，职场竞争加剧，很多人变得极度惧怕失败。不敢转行，不敢创业，不敢尝试新赛道，害怕投入之后得不到结果，害怕资产缩水，害怕付出全部最后一场空。我们总想拿到一张成功的保证书，却忘记，人生本来就没有稳赢的剧本。

This throws light precisely on the predicament of today's post-80s and post-90s. We grew up in an era of rapid economic ascent, used to a world where effort brought reward. Now property swings, industries slow, workplace competition intensifies, and many people have become extremely afraid of failure. They dare not change careers, dare not start businesses, dare not try a new track—afraid that investment will yield no result, afraid that assets will shrink, afraid that giving everything will end in nothing. We always want a written guarantee of success, forgetting that life never came with a script that guarantees a win.

李书福用一生告诉我们：真正困住人的，往往不是现实的困难，而是内心对失败的恐惧。失败并不可耻，不敢尝试，才会彻底关上所有可能性。他出身草根，没有得天独厚的条件，一次次被现实打翻，却一次次重新站起来。他懂得，试错本身就是成长的一部分，就算结局不尽人意，那些经历、认知、能力，都不会白白消耗。

Li Shufu tells us with his whole life: what truly traps a person is usually not the difficulty of reality but the fear of failure within. Failure is no disgrace; refusing to try is what closes off every possibility for good. He came from nothing, had no exceptional advantages, was knocked down by reality again and again, and stood up again and again. He understands that trial and error are themselves part of growth—even if the outcome disappoints, the experience, the understanding, and the skills are never spent for nothing.

时代浪潮滚滚向前，如今李书福行将谢幕，属于他的轰轰烈烈创业时代渐渐翻篇。但那份“求一次失败的机会”的精神，依旧有现实重量。放到今天，未必人人都要去创业造车。但这份心态可以照亮我们普通人的生活：面对转型，不必追求绝对稳妥；面对变局，不必过度焦虑得失；资产可以缩水，事业可以起伏，人生允许试错。

The tide of the times rolls on, and Li Shufu is now nearing his final bow; the vigorous age of his founding career is slowly turning the page. But the spirit of "asking for one chance to fail" still carries real weight. Today not everyone needs to start a business or build cars, yet this mindset can light the way for ordinary lives: in the face of transformation, we need not demand absolute safety; in the face of upheaval, we need not be consumed by anxiety over gain and loss; assets may shrink, careers may rise and fall, and life allows room for trial and error.

时代不会永远高歌猛进，个人也不会永远一帆风顺。不是每一次努力都必须大获全胜，我们也可以争取属于自己的那一次“失败的机会”。敢于出发，敢于承受不完美的结果，在风浪中沉淀自己，跌倒之后拥有重新起身的底气。

An era does not advance at full song forever, and no individual sails smooth from end to end. Not every effort has to end in complete victory; we too can fight for our own "one chance to fail." Dare to set out, dare to bear an imperfect result, settle yourself amid the wind and waves, and you will have the strength to rise again after a fall.

愿身处压力之下的80后90后，读懂李书福留给时代的启示：不必畏惧低谷，不必惧怕试错。允许自己有输的可能，才有机会看见赢的曙光。纵然世事艰难，依旧保有敢闯敢拼的内心，在自己的人生道路上踏实前行。

May the post-80s and post-90s under pressure today read the lesson Li Shufu leaves to his era: do not fear the trough, do not dread trial and error. Only when you allow yourself the possibility of losing do you have a chance to glimpse the dawn of winning. Though the world be hard, keep within you the heart that dares to venture and strive, and walk steadily forward on the road that is your own.
