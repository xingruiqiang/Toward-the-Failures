第一辑　生不逢时的先行者

Part One · Visionaries Before Their Time

他们看得太远，远到同时代的人看不见他们的背影。

They saw too far ahead—so far that their own age could not even make out their backs.

在这一辑里，你会遇到那些被时代判为“疯子”“异端”“不务正业”的人：被赶出校门的爱迪生，一生贫困的特斯拉，在瘟疫里躲回乡下的牛顿，坐在专利局里的爱因斯坦，周游列国到处碰壁的孔子，转身出关、不知所终的老子，抛下六便士去追月亮的高更，被投票处死的苏格拉底，临终才敢出书的哥白尼，鲜花广场上的布鲁诺，跪着认罪的伽利略，种了八年豌豆的孟德尔，死在冰原上的魏格纳，写下第一段程序的洛夫莱斯，弄丢了记忆的香农……

In this part you will meet those their age judged mad, heretical, or idle: Edison, driven out of school; Tesla, poor all his life; Newton, who fled back to the countryside during the plague; Einstein, sitting in a patent office; Confucius, wandering from state to state and meeting rejection at every turn; Laozi, who turned and passed through the frontier, never to be seen again; Gauguin, who cast aside the sixpence to chase the moon; Socrates, put to death by a vote; Copernicus, who dared to publish only at the end of his life; Bruno, on the Campo de' Fiori; Galileo, kneeling to confess; Mendel, who tended his peas for eight years; Wegener, who died on the ice; Lovelace, who wrote the first program; Shannon, who lost his memory…

把话说在前面：他们被误解、被嘲笑、被排挤，甚至被烧死。

Let it be said plainly: they were misunderstood, mocked, pushed aside, and some of them burned.

但他们留在世上的东西，比那个误解他们的时代，活得久得多。

But what they left in the world has outlived, by far, the age that misunderstood them.
