孟德尔：在修道院后院种了八年豌豆的“失败修士”

Mendel: The “Failed Monk” Who Spent Eight Years Growing Peas in a Monastery Garden

——他摸到了遗传的密码，却等不到任何一个读者

— He touched the code of heredity, yet waited in vain for a single reader

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者18 / 100约 3313 字 · 3313 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，“基因”已经是一个连手机广告都要蹭的词。人们花几百块钱就能测出自己的祖先来自哪片草原，能在几分钟里看到自己有没有某种病的风险。基因编辑、靶向药、亲子鉴定，这些都建立在一套叫“孟德尔定律”的规则之上。

In 2026, “gene” is a word even phone advertisements try to cash in on. For a few hundred yuan, people can find out which grassland their ancestors came from and see within minutes whether they carry the risk of a certain disease. Gene editing, targeted drugs, paternity testing—all of these rest on a set of rules called “Mendel's laws.”

可发明这套规则的那个人，一辈子都没听过“基因”这个词。他把八年青春押在两万八千株豌豆上，得出了一串漂亮的数字，然后把这些数字交给世界。世界没有骂他，也没有夸他——世界只是，完全没看见他。

But the man who devised those rules never once heard the word “gene” his whole life. He staked eight years of his youth on twenty-eight thousand pea plants, arrived at a string of beautiful numbers, and handed those numbers to the world. The world did not curse him, nor praise him—the world simply, entirely, failed to notice him.

对一个研究者来说，这可能是比被嘲笑更深的失败：你不是错了，你是根本不存在。

For a researcher, this may be a failure deeper than being mocked: you are not wrong, you simply do not exist.

一、农家子弟，走进了修道院

I. A Farmer's Son Who Walked into a Monastery

1822年，格雷戈尔·孟德尔出生在奥匈帝国西里西亚的海因岑多夫，一个今天属于捷克的小村子。父亲是农民，也做点果树嫁接的活。家里穷，供不起他一直念书。为了不中断学业，年轻的孟德尔做了一个在旁人看来“认命”的选择——1843年，他进了布尔诺的圣托马斯修道院，成了一名修士。

In 1822, Gregor Mendel was born in Heinzendorf, in Silesia in the Austro-Hungarian Empire, a small village that belongs to the Czech Republic today. His father was a farmer who also did a little fruit-tree grafting. The family was poor and could not afford to keep him in school. To avoid interrupting his studies, the young Mendel made a choice that others saw as “resigning himself to his fate”—in 1843 he entered the monastery of St. Thomas in Brno and became a monk.

在那个年代，修道院对穷孩子来说，是少数几个能“吃饱饭还能读书”的地方。孟德尔在这里改名格雷戈尔，后来还被送去维也纳大学进修物理、数学和自然科学。回到布尔诺后，他在一所中学代课当老师。他两次去考正式的教师资格，两次都没考过——理由听起来像是笑话：历史考得还行，自然科学的答卷却被考官认为“缺乏必要的术语和条理”。

In those days, a monastery was one of the few places where a poor boy could “eat his fill and still study.” There Mendel took the name Gregor, and was later sent to the University of Vienna to study physics, mathematics, and natural science. After returning to Brno he taught as a substitute teacher at a secondary school. Twice he sat the examination for a formal teaching certificate, and twice he failed—for a reason that sounds like a joke: he did reasonably well in history, but the examiners judged his natural-science paper to “lack the necessary terminology and organization.”

一个后来颠覆了整个生物学的人，被判定“不会讲自然科学”。

A man who would later overturn the whole of biology was ruled “incapable of teaching natural science.”

二、八年，两万八千株豌豆

II. Eight Years, Twenty-Eight Thousand Pea Plants

1856年，孟德尔34岁。他在修道院后院划出一小块长约35米、宽约7米的菜地，开始种豌豆。这一种，就是八年。

In 1856, Mendel was thirty-four. In the monastery's back garden he marked off a small plot about 35 meters long and 7 meters wide and began to grow peas. And he kept at it for eight years.

在今天看来，他的方法简直是“笨”到极致：他先花两年筛选出纯种品系，然后亲手把一株的花粉刷到另一株的柱头上，再用布袋套住花朵，防止蜜蜂捣乱。七年里，他种下约两万八千株豌豆，观察了约四万朵花，亲手数过近四十万颗种子。

Seen from today, his method was almost comically “clumsy”: he first spent two years selecting pure-breeding lines, then with his own hands brushed the pollen of one plant onto the stigma of another, and covered the flowers with cloth bags to keep the bees from interfering. Over seven years he planted some twenty-eight thousand pea plants, observed about forty thousand flowers, and counted close to four hundred thousand seeds by hand.

但他做的事情里，藏着两个超越时代的关键：

But within what he did lay two keys that outstripped his age:

第一，他只挑界限分明的性状——圆粒还是皱粒，黄子叶还是绿子叶，高茎还是矮茎，一共七对。别人研究遗传，看的是“整体像不像爹妈”，模糊成一团；他把问题切成了非黑即白的开关。

First, he chose only sharply defined traits—round seeds or wrinkled, yellow cotyledons or green, tall stems or short, seven pairs in all. Others who studied heredity looked at whether the whole “resembled the parents,” a blur; he cut the problem into switches that were either black or white.

第二，他数数。他把每一代里各种性状各占多少，一株一株地数出来，再算比例。

Second, he counted. He counted out, plant by plant, how much of each trait appeared in each generation, and then calculated the ratios.

结果出现了那些漂亮的数字：一对性状杂交，第一代全显一种，第二代却是3∶1；两对性状一起看，更精细的分布浮出水面。

The result was those beautiful numbers: cross one pair of traits, and the first generation shows only one form, while the second gives 3:1; look at two pairs together, and a still more precise distribution surfaces.

孟德尔得出的结论是：性状不是“混合”的，而是由成对的“因子”决定，一个来自父本、一个来自母本，显性的盖住隐性的，但隐性的并没有消失，只是躲了起来，随时可以在下一代重新冒头。

Mendel's conclusion was this: traits are not “blended” but determined by paired “factors,” one from the father and one from the mother; the dominant one masks the recessive, but the recessive does not vanish—it merely hides, ready to reappear in the next generation at any time.

用一句话概括：他证明了遗传不是把两杯水倒在一起，而是把两副牌洗在一起。

To put it in one sentence: he proved that heredity is not pouring two cups of water together, but shuffling two decks of cards together.

三、他把结果念给全世界听，全世界打了个哈欠

III. He Read His Results to the Whole World, and the Whole World Yawned

1865年2月8日和3月8日，孟德尔两次站在布尔诺自然史协会的讲台上，宣读他的豌豆实验。台下坐了四十来个人，大多是本地的中学教师、神职人员和一些业余爱好者。他讲完，没有引发什么讨论。

On February 8 and March 8, 1865, Mendel twice stood at the podium of the Natural History Society of Brno and read out his pea experiments. Some forty people sat in the audience, mostly local secondary-school teachers, clergy, and a few amateurs. When he finished, it stirred no discussion.

1866年，论文正式发表在协会年报上，题为《植物杂交实验》，长达四十八页。他自费印了四十份，分寄给当时欧洲重要的生物学家，其中包括慕尼黑的名家内格利。

In 1866 the paper was formally published in the society's annual proceedings, under the title Experiments on Plant Hybridization (Versuche über Pflanzen-Hybriden), forty-eight pages long. He printed forty copies at his own expense and sent them out to important European biologists of the day, among them the renowned Nägeli of Munich.

内格利的回复客气而冷淡。他建议孟德尔“应该在别的物种上再验证一下”，然后继续去研究他自己坚信的“混合遗传”。

Nägeli's reply was polite and cold. He suggested that Mendel “should verify it again on other species,” and then went on to pursue his own firmly held “blending inheritance.”

这就是孟德尔一生最残酷的地方：他没有收到一封反驳信，因为没有人值得反驳他。

This is the cruelest thing about Mendel's life: he never received a single rebuttal, because no one thought him worth rebutting.

从1866年到1900年，整整三十四年，这篇论文几乎销声匿迹。后人的统计是：这三十多年里，它被引用的次数，大约只有四次。科学史家给它起了一个名字——“生物学史上最怪异的沉默事件之一”。

From 1866 to 1900, a full thirty-four years, the paper all but vanished. Later statisticians found that over those three-plus decades it was cited only about four times. Historians of science have given it a name—one of “the strangest silences in the history of biology.”

四、当上院长的“成功者”，和被遗忘的失败者

IV. The “Success” of Becoming Abbot, and the Forgotten Failure

如果只看世俗标准，孟德尔晚年并不算惨。1868年，他当选为修道院院长。在外人眼里，这是一个从农民儿子爬到“一把手”的成功故事。

Judged by worldly standards alone, Mendel's later years were hardly miserable. In 1868 he was elected abbot of the monastery. To outsiders, this was the success story of a farmer's son who climbed to the top post.

但恰恰是这个“成功”，掐死了他的科学。

But it was precisely this “success” that throttled his science.

当上院长之后，教务、财务、和政府的税赋纠纷，占满了他的时间。他还在坚持研究，继续做植物杂交、养蜜蜂、记气象，但他再也没有做出像豌豆那样干净漂亮的东西了。1884年1月6日，他因肾脏与心脏的疾病去世，享年六十一岁。

After becoming abbot, administrative affairs, finances, and tax disputes with the government filled his time. He kept trying to do research, continuing plant hybridization, keeping bees, recording the weather, but he never again produced anything as clean and beautiful as the peas. On January 6, 1884, he died of kidney and heart disease, at the age of sixty-one.

更让人唏嘘的是身后事。他去世后不久，修道院的新任院长烧掉了他留下的大批论文和实验记录——在他看来，那些都是和修道院无关的私人杂物。孟德尔用八年数出来的原始数据，就这样成了灰。

Still more poignant is what happened after his death. Not long after he died, the monastery's new abbot burned the large quantity of papers and experimental records he had left behind—in his view, they were private clutter unrelated to the monastery. The raw data Mendel had counted out over eight years thus became ash.

三十四年前他寄出的那些油印小册子，还静静地躺在几所大学的图书馆里，落着灰。

The mimeographed pamphlets he had sent out thirty-four years earlier still lay quietly in the libraries of a few universities, gathering dust.

五、1900年，三个人同时“重新发现”了他

V. In 1900, Three Men “Rediscovered” Him at Once

时间到了1900年。

The time then came to 1900.

荷兰的德弗里斯、德国的科伦斯、奥地利的切马克——三位植物学家，各自做杂交实验，各自独立地看到了那个似曾相识的3∶1。他们在准备发表论文时，先后去查文献，然后都撞上了同一件事：三十四年前，布尔诺有个神父，早就把这些写清楚了。

Holland's de Vries, Germany's Correns, and Austria's Tschermak—three botanists, each conducting hybridization experiments, each independently seeing that familiar 3:1. As they prepared to publish their papers, they each went to check the literature, and all three ran into the same thing: thirty-four years earlier, a priest in Brno had already set all of this down clearly.

1900年，他们各自发文，把“孟德尔定律”这个名字重新推到世界面前。这一年，被视为遗传学的诞生之年。

In 1900 they each published, pushing the name “Mendel's laws” back before the world. That year is regarded as the birth year of genetics.

而此时，孟德尔已经去世十六年。他从未在论文里宣称自己发现了什么“定律”，也从未知道“基因”这个词——这个词要等到1909年才由丹麦人约翰森提出。他只知道，自己种过很多豌豆，数过很多豆子，得出过一些他自己也未必完全明白其分量的结论。

By then, Mendel had been dead sixteen years. He never claimed in his paper to have discovered any “law,” and never knew the word “gene”—a word that would not be coined until 1909, by the Dane Johannsen. He knew only that he had grown a great many peas, counted a great many beans, and drawn conclusions whose full weight even he perhaps did not entirely grasp.

还有一个被反复讲述的细节要打个问号：很多人说孟德尔“生前是个失败者、被人嘲笑”。真实情况更微妙——他没有被嘲笑，他是被无视。而在修道院里，他其实是受人尊敬的院长。他的“失败”，不是世俗意义上的落魄，而是一个提前看到答案的人，遇上了一群还没准备好提问的人。

There is one much-repeated detail that needs a question mark attached: many people say Mendel was “a failure in his lifetime, mocked by others.” The truth is more subtle—he was not mocked, he was ignored. And within the monastery he was in fact a respected abbot. His “failure” was not worldly destitution, but that of a man who saw the answer early, meeting a group of people not yet ready to ask the question.

六、致2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

我们今天太习惯用“有没有反馈”来判断一件事值不值得做。

We are far too accustomed today to judging whether a thing is worth doing by “whether there is any feedback.”

发一条动态，十分钟没人点赞，就删了；写一篇文章，没有流量，就觉得白写；做一个项目，半年看不到回报，就急着换赛道。

Post an update, and if no one likes it in ten minutes, we delete it; write an article, and if there is no traffic, we feel it was written in vain; start a project, and if there is no return in half a year, we rush to switch tracks.

孟德尔会告诉你另一件事：你做的事情有没有价值，和你有没有收到掌声，是两回事。他用八年，把一个问题算到了当时没人能算的精度；剩下的三十四年里，他一个字都没等到。但他的那些豌豆，最终养活了整个现代生物学，养活了我们今天所有的基因检测、育种和药物。

Mendel would tell you something else: whether what you are doing has value and whether you receive applause are two entirely different matters. He used eight years to compute a problem to a precision no one could match at the time; over the remaining thirty-four years, he waited for not a single word. Yet his peas ultimately fed the whole of modern biology, and they feed all our gene testing, breeding, and drugs today.

如果你此刻正在做一件没人看得懂的事，请记住1865年2月8日的那个下午——一位中学代课老师站在四十个人面前，念完了自己八年的心血，掌声寥寥。他没有停下。

If you are right now doing something no one else can understand, remember that afternoon of February 8, 1865—a substitute schoolteacher stood before forty people, finished reading out eight years of his heart's work, and the applause was sparse. He did not stop.

有些工作的读者，不在你的时代。你得替他们把答案先写下来。

Some work has its readers outside your own time. You must write the answer down for them first.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

8. “孟德尔生前被嘲笑、被视为失败者”——不够准确。学界的主流描述是被忽视（neglect），而非被嘲弄；他在修道院内地位不低，且曾任院长。文中按“被无视”处理。

8. “Mendel was mocked and seen as a failure in his lifetime”—not quite accurate. The mainstream scholarly description is neglect, not mockery; he held no low position within the monastery, and he served as abbot. This chapter treats it as “being ignored.”

9. “gene（基因）一词由孟德尔提出”——错误。孟德尔用的是“因子/元素”（Elemente）；“基因”由丹麦植物学家约翰森（Wilhelm Johannsen）于1909年提出。

9. “The word gene was proposed by Mendel”—incorrect. Mendel used “factors/elements” (Elemente); “gene” was proposed by the Danish botanist Wilhelm Johannsen in 1909.

10. “孟德尔自己发现了'孟德尔定律'”——需谨慎。他并未宣称发现“定律”；“孟德尔定律”这一命名及其系统表述，来自1900年的三位再发现者（尤其科伦斯）与后续学者。

10. “Mendel himself discovered ‘Mendel's laws’”—this requires caution. He never claimed to have discovered a “law”; the naming and systematic formulation of “Mendel's laws” came from the three rediscoverers of 1900 (especially Correns) and later scholars.

11. 关于他去世后手稿被焚毁一事，流传甚广，细节（焚毁范围）因原始材料残缺难完全确证，文中按常见说法简述。

11. The burning of his manuscripts after his death is widely told, but the details (the extent of what was burned) are hard to confirm completely given the fragmentary nature of the original materials; this chapter follows the common account in brief.
