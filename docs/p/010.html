Chapter 010 · Part 1

高更：为月亮丢掉六便士的“落魄经纪人”

Gauguin: The “Down-and-Out Broker” Who Gave Up Sixpence for the Moon

——一个年薪百万的中年人，如何亲手毁掉自己的下半生

— How a middle-aged man earning a million a year destroyed the second half of his own life

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者10 / 100约 3027 字 · 3027 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

在2026年的今天，我们说起高更，是拍卖行里那串令人眩晕的数字，是《我们从哪里来？我们是谁？我们到哪里去？》那幅挂在博物馆里、让人久久挪不动步的巨作。可如果你穿越回1903年太平洋上的一座荒岛，你会看到一个完全不同的人：一个被梅毒和心脏病折磨、穷困潦倒、死在异乡的落魄画家。他死时，画作几乎无人问津；他连一块像样的墓碑都未必留得住。

When we speak of Gauguin in 2026, we mean the dizzying figures at auction, the monumental Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going? that hangs in a museum and leaves people rooted to the spot. But if you traveled back to a remote island in the Pacific in 1903, you would see a completely different person: a destitute painter ravaged by syphilis and heart disease, dying in a foreign land. When he died, his paintings drew almost no interest; he may not even have left behind a decent gravestone.

而在此之前的三十五岁，他是巴黎证券交易所里一个收入丰厚、西装革履的经纪人，收藏着印象派的名画，有妻子，有五个孩子，住体面的房子。

And before that, at thirty-five, he was a well-paid, well-tailored broker at the Paris stock exchange, collecting famous Impressionist paintings, with a wife, five children, and a respectable house.

他亲手把这一切，全部丢掉了。

With his own hands, he threw all of it away.

一、三十五岁前的“成功人士”

I. A “Success” Before Thirty-Five

高更1848年生于巴黎。他的身世颇有故事：父亲是个记者，母亲带着秘鲁贵族血统——她的外祖母弗洛拉·特里斯坦，是法国有名的早期社会主义与女权主义活动家。

Gauguin was born in Paris in 1848. His background was colorful: his father was a journalist, and his mother carried Peruvian noble blood—her maternal grandmother, Flora Tristan, was a well-known early French socialist and feminist activist.

他小时候随家人远渡秘鲁的利马，父亲在途中去世。他在异乡度过了童年，之后回到法国。十七岁，他当了水手，后来加入海军，漂洋过海五六年。

As a child he traveled with his family to Lima, Peru, and his father died on the way. He spent his childhood in a foreign land, then returned to France. At seventeen he became a sailor, later joined the navy, and spent five or six years at sea.

1873年，他回到巴黎，进入证券交易所，成了一名股票经纪人。他做得很成功，收入优渥。同年，他娶了丹麦女子梅特，之后陆续有了五个孩子。

In 1873 he returned to Paris, entered the stock exchange, and became a stockbroker. He did well, earning a comfortable income. That same year he married a Danish woman, Mette, and went on to have five children.

他业余学画，还出手大方地收藏印象派画家（莫奈、毕沙罗等）的作品。在朋友眼里，他是个有品位、有钱、生活体面的中产男人。

He studied painting in his spare time and lavishly collected works by Impressionists (Monet, Pissarro, and others). In the eyes of his friends, he was a middle-class man of taste, money, and respectability.

——如果故事停在这里，他就是那个时代标准的“人生赢家”。

— If the story had stopped here, he would have been the standard “winner in life” of his era.

二、股市崩盘，他做了那个“不理性”的选择

II. The Market Crashes, and He Makes the “Irrational” Choice

1882年，法国金融危机，巴黎股市崩盘，高更的收入一落千丈。

In 1882, a financial crisis struck France, the Paris stock market collapsed, and Gauguin's income plummeted.

多数人的选择会是：勒紧裤腰带，熬过去，保住工作。

Most people's choice would have been: tighten your belt, ride it out, hold on to your job.

高更的选择是：干脆不干了，全职画画。

Gauguin's choice was: quit outright and paint full-time.

1883年，他辞去证券经纪人的工作，投入全部精力作画。结果，全家迅速陷入贫困。

In 1883 he resigned from his job as a stockbroker and devoted all his energy to painting. As a result, the whole family quickly slid into poverty.

用任何一套理性标准看，这都是一次彻底的“失败”：他主动放弃了一份体面高薪的职业，去从事一个当时几乎养不活自己的行当。他后来画了半辈子，也穷了半辈子。

By any rational standard, this was a complete “failure”: he voluntarily gave up a respectable, well-paid profession to take up a trade that at the time could barely keep him alive. He would paint for half a lifetime, and be poor for half a lifetime.

三、众叛亲离：他把妻子和五个孩子留在丹麦

III. Abandoned by All: He Left His Wife and Five Children in Denmark

1885年，贫困压垮了这个家庭。高更随妻子回了她的故乡哥本哈根——可他在那儿也待不下去，不受待见。最后，他带着小儿子回到巴黎，其余几个孩子跟着母亲留在丹麦。

In 1885, poverty crushed the family. Gauguin went with his wife to her hometown, Copenhagen—but he could not settle there either, and was unwelcome. In the end he took his youngest son and returned to Paris, while the other children stayed in Denmark with their mother.

他抛下了妻子和孩子。

He abandoned his wife and children.

用今天的话说：一个中年男人，破产、失业、离乡、抛妻弃子。在主流社会的道德评价里，他几乎是“渣男”“失败者”的模板。后来所有关于他的传记、小说（比如毛姆的《月亮与六便士》），都无法绕过这层沉重的底色。

In today's terms: a middle-aged man—bankrupt, unemployed, uprooted, having abandoned his wife and children. By mainstream moral judgment, he was practically the template of the “deadbeat” and the “loser.” Every later biography and novel about him (such as Maugham's The Moon and Sixpence) has had to reckon with this heavy undercurrent.

他甚至一度去巴拿马、去马提尼克岛，想做点生意翻身，结果只落得大病一场。

He even went for a time to Panama and to Martinique, hoping to make some money and turn his fortunes around, only to fall seriously ill.

四、阿尔勒的九周：和梵高的那场决裂

IV. Nine Weeks in Arles: The Break with Van Gogh

1888年，他生命里出现了一段著名的插曲。

In 1888, a famous episode entered his life.

他应梵高之邀，去了法国南部的阿尔勒，住进了梵高为迎接他而布置的“黄房子”。

At Van Gogh's invitation, he went to Arles in the south of France and moved into the “Yellow House,” which Van Gogh had prepared to welcome him.

两个性格、理念都极其强烈的画家，在一起同住了九个星期。高更画向日葵的梵高，梵高敬重又嫉妒高更。两人从互相欣赏，走到激烈争吵。

Two painters of immensely strong character and conviction lived together for nine weeks. Gauguin painted Van Gogh painting sunflowers; Van Gogh both respected and envied Gauguin. The two moved from mutual admiration to fierce quarrels.

1888年12月23日，梵高精神崩溃，割下了自己左耳的一部分。高更当晚离开了阿尔勒，两人从此再未见面。（这段公案的具体起因，至今众说纷纭，但“高更的离去”与梵高的崩溃高度相关，是学界大体接受的判断。）

On December 23, 1888, Van Gogh suffered a breakdown and cut off part of his own left ear. That night Gauguin left Arles, and the two never met again. (The precise cause of this affair is still debated, but that “Gauguin's departure” was closely bound up with Van Gogh's breakdown is a judgment broadly accepted by scholars.)

高更不是施害者，也不是受害者。他是另一个同样在理想与现实之间被撕碎的人。

Gauguin was neither perpetrator nor victim. He was another man torn apart between ideal and reality.

五、塔希提：一个文明人逃向“想象中的天堂”

V. Tahiti: A Civilized Man Flees to an “Imagined Paradise”

1891年，高更做了一件更“疯狂”的事：他离开欧洲，远赴南太平洋的塔希提岛（大溪地）。

In 1891, Gauguin did something even “crazier”: he left Europe and sailed for the island of Tahiti in the South Pacific.

他想要什么？他厌倦了欧洲的“文明”，想去一个原始的、未被污染的地方，找回艺术最本真的东西。

What did he want? He was weary of European “civilization” and wanted to go to a primitive, unpolluted place to recover the truest essence of art.

可现实很快打了他的脸。塔希提早已是殖民地，他心中的“天堂”并不存在。他带来的钱花光了，画的画卖不掉，他本人又疾病缠身。

But reality soon slapped him in the face. Tahiti had long been a colony; the “paradise” in his mind did not exist. The money he brought ran out, the paintings he made would not sell, and he was plagued by illness.

1893年，他回过一次法国，靠继承了一笔叔父的遗产才勉强支撑，还办了一场失败的画展——几乎什么都没卖出去。1895年，他再次远赴塔希提，这一次，他基本没有再回来。

In 1893 he returned to France once, scraping by only on an inheritance from an uncle, and held a failed exhibition—almost nothing sold. In 1895 he set out for Tahiti again, and this time he essentially never came back.

在塔希提，他画出了他最著名的那些作品：被殖民阴影笼罩的、色彩浓烈得近乎野蛮的岛上妇女、土地和神话。他用颜料，给自己造了一个并不存在的人间乐园。

In Tahiti he painted his most famous works: island women, land, and myth, shadowed by colonial rule and rendered in colors so intense they verge on the savage. With paint, he built himself a paradise on earth that did not exist.

六、女儿死了，他也想死：那幅最大的画

VI. His Daughter Dies, and He Wants to Die Too: The Largest Painting

1897年，高更收到了一个消息：他最疼爱的女儿阿琳娜在丹麦死于肺炎。

In 1897, Gauguin received news: his most beloved daughter, Aline, had died of pneumonia in Denmark.

他自己的梅毒也到了晚期，身体全面崩溃。

His own syphilis had reached its late stage, and his body was collapsing on every front.

在极度的痛苦和绝望里，他画下了一生中尺幅最大的作品——宽近四米、高约一米四的《我们从哪里来？我们是谁？我们到哪里去？》。那是关于生命、关于生与死的终极追问，也是他为自己准备的“遗言”。

In extreme pain and despair, he painted the largest work of his life—Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going?, nearly four meters wide and about 1.4 meters tall. It was an ultimate inquiry into life, into being and death, and also the “last testament” he prepared for himself.

画完之后，他走进深山，服下毒药，想就此结束生命。

After finishing it, he walked into the mountains, swallowed poison, and tried to end his life then and there.

结果，他被人发现，救活了。

In the end, he was found and brought back to life.

一个万念俱灰、连死都没死成的人，又活了六年。

A man utterly without hope, who could not even succeed in dying, went on to live six more years.

七、死在荒岛，死后封神

VII. Dying on a Remote Island, Deified After Death

1901年，他迁往更偏远的马克萨斯群岛，在多米尼克岛上盖了一座木屋，取名“快乐之家”。他在那里与殖民当局不断冲突，为原住民打抱不平。

In 1901 he moved to the more remote Marquesas Islands and built a wooden house on the island of Hiva Oa (then called Dominica), naming it the “House of Pleasure.” There he clashed constantly with the colonial authorities, speaking out on behalf of the indigenous people.

1903年5月8日，高更在那间屋子里去世，年仅54岁。死时贫困潦倒。

On May 8, 1903, Gauguin died in that house, only fifty-four years old. He died in poverty.

而他死后，世界开始疯狂地重新认识他。

And after his death, the world began to rediscover him with a vengeance.

他的画作价格飞涨，他被奉为后印象派最伟大的画家之一，是现代艺术（尤其是野兽派、原始主义）的源头之一。他当年在塔希提画下、无人问津的那些画，如今被全世界最重要的博物馆争相收藏。

The prices of his paintings soared; he was hailed as one of the greatest Post-Impressionist painters and as one of the wellsprings of modern art (above all Fauvism and Primitivism). The Tahitian paintings that once drew no interest are now eagerly collected by the world's most important museums.

那个被家人抛弃、被同行嘲笑、被市场拒绝、被疾病和绝望反复碾过的人，成了艺术史上的巨人。

The man abandoned by his family, mocked by his peers, rejected by the market, and repeatedly crushed by illness and despair became a giant of art history.

八、结语：致2026年，每个想“重来一次”的人

VIII. Conclusion: To Everyone in 2026 Who Wants to “Start Over”

高更的一生，用世俗标准评价，几乎是彻底的失败：他抛弃了高薪的工作，落得穷困；他抛下了家庭，背负骂名；他远走荒岛追求理想，却只换来疾病、孤独和死亡。他不是什么道德楷模，他的选择里，有自私，有逃避，也有对身边人真实的伤害。

Judged by worldly standards, Gauguin's life was an almost total failure: he threw away a well-paid job and ended up in poverty; he abandoned his family and bore the blame for it; he fled to a remote island in pursuit of his ideals and got back only illness, loneliness, and death. He was no moral exemplar; his choices contained selfishness, escapism, and real harm to those close to him.

可是，他至少回答了一个很多人不敢面对的问题：

But he did at least answer a question many people dare not face:

当“月亮”（理想）和“六便士”（现实）摆在你面前时，你到底选哪个？

When the “moon” (your ideal) and the “sixpence” (reality) are set before you, which do you choose?

大多数人选六便士——这没错，这是生活。但总有人会选月亮，哪怕代价是全盘皆输。

Most people choose the sixpence—and there is nothing wrong with that; that is life. But there will always be someone who chooses the moon, even if the price is losing everything.

2026年，我们困在房贷、KPI、行业内卷里，很多人心里都藏着一个“想辞掉一切、重新来过”的念头，却不敢动。我们怕的，从来不是失败本身，而是“输掉全部”之后，别人怎么看你。

In 2026, we are trapped by mortgages, KPIs, and cutthroat competition within our industries, and many of us secretly harbor a wish to “quit everything and start over,” yet dare not act. What we fear was never failure itself, but how others will see us after we “lose it all.”

高更没有给你一个完美的答案。他的人生甚至可以说是反面教材。但他用一生证明了一件事：

Gauguin does not give you a perfect answer. His life could even be called a cautionary tale. But with his whole life he proved one thing:

人可以为了一件真正相信的事，把自己活成世俗眼里的“失败者”——而这，也是一种活法。

A person can, for the sake of one thing he truly believes in, live himself into a “failure” in the world's eyes—and that, too, is a way to live.

你不必学他抛妻弃子、远走荒岛。但你可以问自己一句：我这一生，到底有没有一件，值得我为之“不计后果”的事？

You need not imitate him by abandoning your wife and children and fleeing to a remote island. But you can ask yourself one question: in this life of mine, is there anything at all worth “risking everything” for?

如果连这个问题都不敢问，那才是真正的失败。

If you do not even dare to ask that question, that is the real failure.

Appendix: Sources and Notes

主要史料

Primary Sources

1. 高更书信集（尤其致妻子梅特、友人及1903年前后书信），是研究其生平最直接的一手材料。

1. The letters of Gauguin (especially those to his wife Mette and to friends, and those written around 1903)—the most direct primary material for studying his life.

2. 高更《诺阿·诺阿》（Noa Noa，塔希提手记）——注意：该书经诗人夏尔·莫里斯润色，行文带有文学加工，引作史实需谨慎。

2. Gauguin's Noa Noa (Tahitian journal)—note: the book was polished by the poet Charles Morice, and its prose contains literary embellishment; cite it as historical fact with caution.

3. 梵高与弟弟提奥的书信——记录了1888年阿尔勒“黄房子”同住九周及决裂的经过。

3. The letters of Van Gogh and his brother Theo—recording the nine weeks of shared life in the “Yellow House” in Arles in 1888 and the break between them.

4. 高更晚年书信及马克萨斯群岛时期记录——含其与殖民当局的冲突。

4. Gauguin's late letters and records from his Marquesas period—including his conflicts with the colonial authorities.

生卒与事实

Dates and Facts

5. 生于1848年6月7日巴黎，卒于1903年5月8日法属马克萨斯群岛希瓦瓦岛（多米尼克岛）。1883年辞去证券交易所工作全职作画；1885年家庭破裂；1888年10月—12月与梵高在阿尔勒同住约九周；1891年首次赴塔希提；1897年得知爱女阿琳娜死讯后完成《我们从哪里来？我们是谁？我们到哪里去？》并服毒自杀未遂；1901年迁居马克萨斯群岛。

5. Born June 7, 1848, in Paris; died May 8, 1903, on Hiva Oa (then Dominica) in the French Marquesas Islands. In 1883 he resigned from the stock exchange to paint full-time; in 1885 his family broke up; from October to December 1888 he lived with Van Gogh in Arles for about nine weeks; in 1891 he traveled to Tahiti for the first time; in 1897, after learning of the death of his beloved daughter Aline, he completed Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going? and attempted suicide by poison; in 1901 he moved to the Marquesas Islands.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

6. 梵高割耳事件——具体起因学界迄无定论，本文以“与高更离去高度相关”表述。

6. The Van Gogh ear-cutting incident—scholarship remains inconclusive about its precise cause; this chapter presents it as “closely bound up with Gauguin's departure.”

7. 毛姆《月亮与六便士》——以高更经历为原型之一的小说，情节为虚构，不可当史料采信。

7. Maugham's The Moon and Sixpence—a novel drawing in part on Gauguin's life; its plot is fictional and should not be taken as historical evidence.
