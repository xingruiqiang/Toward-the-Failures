马斯克：2008年，火箭炸了三次，钱也快没了

Musk: In 2008, the Rockets Blew Up Three Times, and the Money Was Almost Gone

——“乐观，悲观，滚蛋吧！我们会让这一切成为现实。”

—"Optimism, pessimism, fuck that; we're going to make it happen."

2026年，创业者的日子不好过。融资难、市场冷、账上现金撑不过几个月，很多人半夜醒来第一件事就是算自己的钱还能撑多久。

In 2026, life is hard for founders. Fundraising is difficult, the market is cold, the cash on the books will not last more than a few months, and the first thing many people do when they wake in the middle of the night is work out how much longer their money can hold out.

但有一个人，把这种绝境过成了教科书。那一年他37岁，火箭连续炸了三次，电动车公司濒临破产，他本人离破产只有一步，还要同时付自己和前妻的离婚律师费。他后来接受采访时说，那一年他“离精神崩溃只有一步之遥”。

But one man turned that kind of dead end into a textbook case. That year he was thirty-seven; his rockets blew up three times in a row, his electric-car company was on the brink of bankruptcy, he himself was one step from insolvency, and he still had to pay the divorce lawyers for both himself and his ex-wife. He later said in an interview that, that year, he was "one step away from a nervous breakdown."

他叫埃隆·马斯克。2008年，是他人生最黑暗的一年；但那一年发生的事，也决定了他后来的一切。

His name is Elon Musk. 2008 was the darkest year of his life; but what happened that year also determined everything that came after.

一、把全部身家押上赌桌

I. Putting His Entire Fortune on the Table

马斯克早年参与创办了PayPal。2002年，eBay收购PayPal，他从中拿到约1.8亿美元。

In his early years, Musk helped found PayPal. In 2002, eBay acquired PayPal, and he came away with about 180 million dollars.

换成大多数人，这笔钱足够舒舒服服过完一生。但马斯克几乎把这笔钱全部投进了两家当时看起来“注定要失败”的公司：一家是做火箭的SpaceX，一家是做电动车的特斯拉。

For most people, that money would be enough to live out a comfortable life. But Musk put nearly all of it into two companies that at the time looked "doomed to fail": SpaceX, which built rockets, and Tesla, which built electric cars.

这两件事，在当时都被认为是“疯子才会做的生意”——私人公司发射火箭？火箭是国家才能干的事；电动车？过去一百年里，只有福特一家美国车企真正实现过量产并盈利，其他尝试者早就破产、被世人遗忘了。

Both were considered "businesses only a madman would attempt." A private company launching rockets? Rockets were something only nations could do. Electric cars? In the past hundred years, only one American carmaker, Ford, had ever truly achieved mass production and profit; the other hopefuls had long since gone bankrupt and been forgotten.

而到2008年，两家公司同时走到了悬崖边。

And by 2008, both companies had reached the edge of the cliff at the same time.

二、猎鹰1号：连续三次爆炸

II. Falcon 1: Three Explosions in a Row

SpaceX的猎鹰1号火箭，前三次发射全部失败。

SpaceX's Falcon 1 rocket failed on all of its first three launches.

2008年8月3日，在太平洋上的夸贾林岛，第三次试射。这一次，马斯克押上了全部——火箭携带着一颗昂贵的美国空军卫星、两颗NASA的小卫星，还有《星际迷航》中扮演“斯科蒂”的演员詹姆斯·杜汉的骨灰。

On August 3, 2008, on Kwajalein Atoll in the Pacific, came the third attempt. This time Musk had bet everything—the rocket carried an expensive U.S. Air Force satellite, two small NASA satellites, and the ashes of James Doohan, the actor who played "Scotty" in Star Trek.

升空过程非常顺利，现场爆发出欢呼声。2分20秒后，第一级火箭按计划分离。有效载荷似乎正在朝预定轨道飞去。

The ascent went very smoothly, and cheers erupted on the ground. Two minutes and twenty seconds in, the first stage separated as planned. The payload seemed to be heading toward its intended orbit.

然后，意外发生了。助推器按预期开始下降，仅一秒后，它突然向前冲刺了一下，撞上了第二级火箭。视频画面变成一片空白。

Then the accident happened. The booster began its descent as expected, and just one second later it suddenly lunged forward and struck the second stage. The video feed went blank.

问题出在他们重新设计的默林发动机冷却系统上：这套系统在关闭后还保有一点推力，在太空的真空环境中，残余燃料的微小喷发就让助推器向上飘了一英尺——刚好撞上了上面一级。

The problem lay in the redesigned cooling system of their Merlin engine: the system retained a little thrust after shutdown, and in the vacuum of space, a tiny puff of residual fuel floated the booster up by a foot—just enough to collide with the stage above it.

马斯克的钱已经花完了。

Musk's money had run out.

他在发射失败几小时后对外宣布：

A few hours after the failed launch, he announced:

“SpaceX今后在执行过程中不会跳过任何一步。毫无疑问，SpaceX必将在抵达预定轨道方面取得成功。我永远不会放弃，我是说永远不会。”

"SpaceX will never skip a step in its execution going forward. There is no doubt that SpaceX will succeed in reaching orbit. I will never give up—I mean never."

第二天，团队做好了迎接他暴怒的准备——以往他总是会把责任推给某个人。但出乎所有人意料，他告诉大家：洛杉矶工厂里放着第四枚火箭的零部件，要尽快把它运到夸贾林岛，六周内发射。

The next day, the team braced for his fury—in the past he had always found someone to blame. But to everyone's surprise, he told them: the parts for a fourth rocket were sitting in the Los Angeles factory, and they were to ship it to Kwajalein as fast as possible and launch within six weeks.

人力资源总监后来回忆：“大楼里的气氛从绝望和失败的愁云惨淡，顷刻间变成了人声鼎沸和壮志雄心。”

The head of human resources later recalled: "The mood in the building went from the gloom of despair and failure to a din of voices and soaring ambition in an instant."

三、特斯拉：现金流大出血

III. Tesla: A Massive Hemorrhage of Cash Flow

与此同时，特斯拉也在生死线上挣扎。

Meanwhile, Tesla was also struggling on the line between life and death.

2008年2月，第一台量产版Roadster下线，马斯克开着车绕行庆祝。但那只是“阶段性小胜利”。真正的挑战是既要量产又要盈利，而次贷危机已经开始蔓延。

In February 2008, the first production Roadster rolled off the line, and Musk drove it around in celebration. But that was only a "small, staged victory." The real challenge was mass-producing and turning a profit at the same time—and the subprime crisis had already begun to spread.

上半年，特斯拉的资金快用完了。为了给公司输血，马斯克做了一件在法律边缘游走的事：他抽用了客户为尚未交付车辆支付的定金，来充当公司的运营费用。有高管和董事认为这些钱应该托管，他的回应是：“我们如果不这么做，就只剩死路一条了。”

In the first half of the year, Tesla's funds were nearly gone. To keep the company alive, Musk did something that skirted the edge of the law: he drew on customer deposits paid for cars that had not yet been delivered, using them for the company's operating costs. Some executives and board members believed the money should be held in escrow. His response was: "If we don't do this, there is nothing left for us but death."

那年秋天，情况更危急。他开始恳求朋友和家人出钱，帮特斯拉发工资。

That autumn, things grew more desperate. He began begging friends and family for money to help Tesla meet its payroll.

他的弟弟金博尔在经济衰退中损失了大部分资金，手里只坚持持有37.5万美元的苹果股票。马斯克说：“我需要你把这些钱投入特斯拉。”金博尔卖掉了股票，随即接到银行的愤怒警告电话，说他正在毁掉自己的信用记录。他的回答是：“对不起，但我必须这样做。”——几周后，那家银行自己倒闭了。

His brother Kimbal had lost most of his money in the recession and was holding on to only 375,000 dollars' worth of Apple stock. Musk said: "I need you to put that money into Tesla." Kimbal sold the stock and promptly received an angry warning call from his bank, saying he was destroying his own credit. His answer was: "I'm sorry, but I have to do this."—A few weeks later, that bank went under itself.

朋友比尔·李投了200万，谷歌的谢尔盖·布林投了50万，甚至特斯拉的普通员工也给他签了支票。马斯克以个人名义借款支付各种开销，其中每月还要同时支付自己和前妻的离婚律师费，合计17万美元。

His friend Bill Lee put in two million; Google's Sergey Brin put in 500,000; even ordinary Tesla employees wrote him checks. Musk borrowed money in his own name to cover various expenses, including the combined divorce-lawyer fees for himself and his ex-wife—170,000 dollars a month.

他的第二任妻子后来回忆，那些夜晚她惊恐地看着他在睡梦中喃喃自语、挥舞手臂大喊：“他有睡惊症，只在睡梦中尖叫……有时他冲进洗手间，开始呕吐。我会站在马桶边，扶着他的头。”

His second wife later recalled those nights when she watched in alarm as he muttered in his sleep and thrashed his arms, shouting: "He had night terrors, screaming only in his sleep... Sometimes he would rush into the bathroom and start vomiting. I would stand by the toilet, holding his head."

四、“你必须砍掉一家公司”

IV. "You Have to Cut One of the Companies"

随着2008年接近尾声，身边所有人都认为，马斯克必须做一个决定：把不断减少的资源集中在一家公司上，否则两边都会死。

As 2008 drew to a close, everyone around him believed Musk had to make a decision: concentrate his shrinking resources on one company, or both would die.

他最亲密的伙伴马克·容科萨走进他的办公室问：“伙计，你为什么还不肯放弃一家公司？如果SpaceX合你的心意，那就把特斯拉弃了吧。”

His closest partner, Mark Juncosa, walked into his office and asked: "Dude, why won't you give up on one of the companies? If SpaceX is your thing, then let Tesla go."

马斯克拒绝了。他说，放弃特斯拉等于再一次向世人宣告“电动车不靠谱”；而放弃SpaceX，“我们可能永远不会成为跨行星物种”。

Musk refused. He said that giving up Tesla would amount to telling the world once again that "electric cars are unreliable," and that giving up SpaceX meant "we may never become a multi-planet species."

他给出了一个很少有人会用的比喻：

He offered a metaphor few people would ever use:

“对我来说，这就像你有两个孩子，快没吃的了，你可以给每个孩子分一半，但这样他们可能都会饿死；或者你把所有的食物给一个孩子，增加至少一个孩子存活的概率。但由我自己来决定一生一死，这种事我干不出来，所以我决定必须付出我的全部来拯救两个孩子。”

"For me, it's like having two children and running out of food. You can give each child half, but then they may both starve; or you give all the food to one child and increase the odds that at least that one survives. But for me to decide who lives and who dies—that is something I can't bring myself to do. So I decided I had to give everything I had to save both children."

五、奇迹般的三天

V. Three Miraculous Days

2008年8月，就在第三次试射失败之后，一个不可思议的转机出现了。

In August 2008, right after the third failed launch, an incredible turn of events appeared.

赶来增援的，是当年把马斯克从PayPal CEO位置上赶下台的那群人——“PayPal黑帮”。他当年被赶下台时，本可以与他们势同水火，但他保持了体面的关系。彼得·蒂尔最终同意，通过创始人基金向SpaceX注资2000万美元。

The reinforcements came from the very group that had once ousted Musk from his position as PayPal's CEO—the "PayPal Mafia." When he was pushed out back then, he could have made bitter enemies of them, but he kept the relationship decent. Peter Thiel eventually agreed to invest 20 million dollars in SpaceX through Founders Fund.

借着这笔钱，马斯克跨过了生死线，宣布继续为第四次发射提供资金。

With that money, Musk crossed the line between life and death and announced that he would keep funding a fourth launch.

2008年9月28日，猎鹰1号第四次发射成功——成为第一枚进入地球轨道的私人研发的液体燃料火箭。

On September 28, 2008, Falcon 1 launched successfully on its fourth attempt—becoming the first privately developed liquid-fueled rocket to reach Earth orbit.

然后是圣诞节前的那几天。

Then came those few days before Christmas.

12月23日，NASA通知SpaceX，公司赢得了约16亿美元的国际空间站货运合同。马斯克后来说，他当时激动得几乎握不住电话，脱口而出：“我爱你们！”

On December 23, NASA informed SpaceX that the company had won a roughly 1.6-billion-dollar contract to resupply the International Space Station. Musk later said he was so overwhelmed that he could barely hold the phone, and blurted out: "I love you guys!"

12月24日，特斯拉的投资人同意注资，让公司撑过了那个冬天。

On December 24, Tesla's investors agreed to put in money, carrying the company through that winter.

三天之内，两家公司分别被人“从悬崖边拉了回来”。 这就是流传很广的“2008年圣诞节奇迹”。

Within three days, both companies were each "pulled back from the edge of the cliff." This is the widely circulated "2008 Christmas miracle."

六、致2026：绝境里最重要的东西

VI. To 2026: The One Thing That Matters Most in a Dead End

2026年，我们身边充满了“理性”的声音：这个赛道太卷，那个风口已经过了，普通人不该赌上身家。这些声音大多是对的。绝大多数孤注一掷的人，确实失败了，而且再没爬起来——我们只记得那个活下来的马斯克。

In 2026, we are surrounded by "rational" voices: this field is too crowded, that opportunity has already passed, ordinary people shouldn't gamble their whole fortune. Most of these voices are right. The overwhelming majority of people who go all in really do fail, and never get back up—we remember only the Musk who survived.

所以马斯克的故事不该被简化成“敢赌就会赢”。它真正值得琢磨的，是另外一点：

So Musk's story should not be reduced to "dare to gamble and you will win." What is truly worth pondering is another point:

在几乎没有胜算的那一年，他做对了一件极难的事——他既没有放弃，也没有把资源摊薄到两边都死。他保持了强度，同时赌上了自己全部的信誉。

In a year when he had almost no chance of winning, he got one extremely difficult thing right—he neither gave up nor spread his resources so thin that both sides died. He kept up the intensity while staking his entire reputation.

还有他那句近乎粗鲁的乐观主义。当记者问他，如何在那种处境下保持乐观时，他的回答很马斯克：

And then there was his almost rude optimism. When a reporter asked him how he stayed optimistic in that situation, his answer was pure Musk:

“乐观，悲观，滚蛋吧！我们会让这一切成为现实。上帝为我做证，我一心只想让它成功。”

"Optimism, pessimism, fuck that; we're going to make it happen. God is my witness, I'm going to make this work."

这不是心灵鸡汤，而是一种在绝望中仍然保持行动能力的本事。对2026年正在“钱快烧完、事快做不下去”的人来说，也许真正的问题不是“这事能不能成”，而是“我今天还能不能像没出事一样，把该做的那一步做完”。

This is not chicken soup for the soul; it is a kind of skill—the ability to keep acting even in despair. For those in 2026 who are "running out of money and finding it harder and harder to keep going," perhaps the real question is not "can this succeed," but "can I still, today, take the next step that needs taking, as if nothing were wrong."

7. 需要辨析的说法：网络流传的“马斯克2008年睡在工厂地板上”确有其事（他本人及多位员工回忆提到他常在工厂、办公室过夜），但与“圣诞奇迹”是两个不同时段的细节，不应混为一谈。

7. A point that requires clarification: the claim circulating online that "Musk slept on the factory floor in 2008" is true (he himself and several employees recall that he often spent the night at the factory and the office), but it belongs to a different period from the "Christmas miracle," and the two should not be conflated.

8. 另需注意：所谓“马斯克以1.8亿美元全部押注”是概括说法——他确实投入了几乎全部套现所得，但过程中也接受了外部投资人与朋友的资金支持，并非纯粹“一个人扛”。

8. Also worth noting: the claim that "Musk bet all 180 million dollars" is a summary—he did invest almost all of his cashed-out proceeds, but along the way he also accepted financial support from outside investors and friends, so it was not purely "one man carrying it all."

9. “他离精神崩溃只有一步”——出自马斯克本人2014年采访原话，属自述。

9. "He was one step from a nervous breakdown"—this comes from Musk's own words in a 2014 interview and is his own account.

10. 本文对其心理与情绪的描写，依据《马斯克传》中当事人与身边人的回忆，不作超出公开材料的推断。

10. The descriptions of his psychology and emotions in this article are based on the recollections of the subject and those around him in the Musk biography, and make no inferences beyond the public material.
