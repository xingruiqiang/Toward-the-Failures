华特·迪士尼：破产、被夺走角色的“没想象力的人”

Walt Disney: Bankrupt, Robbed of His Characters—the "Man with No Imagination"

——他被说成“没有好点子”，然后创造了米老鼠

—He was said to have "no good ideas," and then he created Mickey Mouse

← 返回主页 Home第三辑　在废墟上重建的人45 / 100约 2750 字 · 2750 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，迪士尼是全球最大的娱乐帝国之一。米老鼠、白雪公主、迪士尼乐园，几乎是几代人的共同记忆。

In 2026, Disney is one of the largest entertainment empires in the world. Mickey Mouse, Snow White, Disneyland—for generations these have been shared memories.

而这个帝国，是从一连串失败开始的：他的第一家动画公司破产了；他创造的红极一时的卡通角色，被人连版权带走，还顺手挖光了他的画师；他想拍一部动画长片，被整个好莱坞嘲笑为“迪士尼的愚蠢”。

And this empire began with a string of failures: his first animation company went bankrupt; the wildly popular cartoon character he created was taken from him, copyright and all, along with nearly his entire staff of animators; and when he wanted to make an animated feature film, all of Hollywood mocked it as "Disney's Folly."

他叫华特·迪士尼。他没有天生的资本，没有名校背景，只有一种东西：输了就换个地方，重新开始。

His name was Walt Disney. He had no inherited fortune, no elite-school pedigree—only one thing: whenever he lost, he moved somewhere new and started over.

一、从救护车司机到动画学徒

I. From Ambulance Driver to Animation Apprentice

1901年，华特·迪士尼出生在美国芝加哥。他十六岁就辍了学，同年（1918年）一战还在打，他因为年龄不够参军，就加入了红十字会，被派到法国当了一段时间的救护车司机。

Walt Disney was born in Chicago in 1901. He dropped out of school at sixteen, and that same year, 1918, with World War I still raging, he was too young to enlist in the army, so he joined the Red Cross and was sent to France, where he spent some time driving an ambulance.

回国后，他回到堪萨斯城，想靠画画吃饭。他进了一家广告/商业美术公司做绘图员。

After returning home, he went back to Kansas City determined to make a living by drawing. He took a job as a draftsman at an advertising and commercial art firm.

这里要说清一件事：后世流传最广的一个故事是——“迪士尼被一家报社解雇，编辑说他'缺乏想象力、没有好点子'”。这个故事很励志，但并没有可靠来源。没有可查的原始记录证明有哪位编辑说过这样的话；而且他做的是广告公司，不是报社。这个“被因缺乏想象力解雇”的细节，更像是一个越传越像真的城市传说。

One thing needs to be made clear here. The most widely repeated story about him is this: "Disney was fired by a newspaper, and the editor told him he 'lacked imagination and had no good ideas.'" It is an inspiring story, but it has no reliable source. There is no verifiable primary record showing that any editor ever said such a thing; moreover, the firm he worked for was an advertising company, not a newspaper. This detail—"fired for lacking imagination"—is more like an urban legend that grows truer with every retelling.

真实情况是：他在广告公司的这份工作没能做久（一说被裁），此后他开始给人画卡通、做短片。

The reality is that his job at the advertising firm did not last long (one account says he was laid off), and afterward he began drawing cartoons and making short films for others.

二、第一次破产：Laugh-O-Gram

II. The First Bankruptcy: Laugh-O-Gram

1921年前后，迪士尼在堪萨斯城和别人合伙，创办了一家动画公司——Laugh-O-Gram，制作卡通短片在本地影院放映。

Around 1921, in Kansas City, Disney went into partnership with others to found an animation company called Laugh-O-Gram, which produced cartoon shorts to be shown in local theaters.

这些片子在小范围里是受欢迎的。但问题出在经营上：迪士尼招募了一批不错的人才，付给他们的薪水远超公司的收入。钱花得比挣得快，公司很快就撑不住了。

These films were well received on a small scale. But the problem lay in the business side: Disney hired a group of talented people and paid them salaries far beyond the company's income. Money went out faster than it came in, and the company soon could not hold on.

1923年，Laugh-O-Gram破产。

In 1923, Laugh-O-Gram went bankrupt.

他连买一张去加州的车票都要精打细算。最后，他带着几件衣服和一部没完成的片子，坐火车去了好莱坞。

He had to count every penny just to afford a train ticket to California. In the end, he took a few clothes and one unfinished film and boarded a train to Hollywood.

三、第二次打击：角色被人拿走

III. The Second Blow: His Character Taken Away

到了好莱坞，他和哥哥罗伊一起创办了“迪士尼兄弟工作室”（后来改名华特迪士尼制片厂）。

Once in Hollywood, he and his brother Roy founded the Disney Brothers Studio (later renamed Walt Disney Productions).

1927年，他们做出了一个非常成功的卡通角色——幸运兔奥斯华（Oswald the Lucky Rabbit）。片子很火，周边也很赚钱。

In 1927 they created a hugely successful cartoon character—Oswald the Lucky Rabbit. The films were a hit, and the merchandise sold well too.

然后，灾难来了。

Then disaster struck.

他们的发行商（环球影业一方）在合同上做了手脚——奥斯华这个角色的版权归发行商所有。紧接着，发行商一边挖走了迪士尼几乎所有的动画师，一边通知他：以后这个角色由我们自己来做了。

Their distributor (on the Universal side) had worked a trick into the contract—the copyright to Oswald belonged to the distributor. Then, at almost the same moment, the distributor poached nearly all of Disney's animators and informed him that from then on they would be making the character themselves.

迪士尼一瞬间什么都没有了：角色没了，团队没了，只能从头再来。

In an instant Disney had nothing left: the character was gone, the team was gone, and he had to start all over again.

据他自己后来的叙述，在从纽约回好莱坞的火车上，他画出了一只老鼠。他的妻子莉莉安建议把“莫蒂默”改成一个更亲切的名字——米奇（Mickey Mouse）。

By his own later account, on the train back to Hollywood from New York he drew a mouse. His wife, Lillian, suggested changing the name from "Mortimer" to something friendlier—Mickey Mouse.

（注意：这个“火车上画出米老鼠”的故事，是迪士尼本人的说法，被视为公司起源的经典叙述，但它更像是被反复讲述过的“官方版本”，具体细节难以完全考证。）

(Note: this story of drawing Mickey Mouse on the train is Disney's own account, and it is regarded as the classic telling of the company's origin. But it is more like an "official version" that has been repeated many times over, and its specific details are hard to verify fully.)

四、第三次：从米老鼠到“迪士尼的愚蠢”

IV. The Third Time: From Mickey Mouse to "Disney's Folly"

1928年，米老鼠的第一部有声动画《威利号汽船》上映，轰动全美。这一次，迪士尼赢了——而且他从此牢牢抓住版权，不再让别人动他的角色。

In 1928, Mickey Mouse's first sound cartoon, Steamboat Willie, was released and caused a sensation across America. This time Disney won—and from then on he held tight to his copyrights and never let anyone else touch his characters again.

但更大的挑战在后面。

But a greater challenge lay ahead.

他想做一件在当时被认为“根本不可能”的事：拍一部长篇动画电影——一个多小时的、正片的动画片，而不只是几分钟的短片。他选的故事是《白雪公主》。

He wanted to do something then considered "utterly impossible": make an animated feature film—a full-length picture running more than an hour, not just a few minutes of short. The story he chose was Snow White.

整个好莱坞都在笑他。他们把这部片子叫作“迪士尼的愚蠢”（Disney's Folly），断定它会拖垮整个公司。资金链一度真的断了，迪士尼不得不拿还没完成的片段去给银行看，才换来继续制作的贷款。

All of Hollywood laughed at him. They called the film "Disney's Folly" and were certain it would drag the whole company under. At one point the money really did run out, and Disney had to show unfinished footage to the banks to get the loans that let production continue.

1937年，《白雪公主与七个小矮人》上映，成为当时最卖座的影片之一，也开创了“动画长片”这个类型。

In 1937, Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs was released, becoming one of the biggest box-office hits of its time and creating the genre of the animated feature.

后来的事大家都知道了：1955年，迪士尼乐园开业（他为了建乐园，抵押了自己的寿险、卖掉度假屋，还跟电视台合作换取投资）。

What came next is well known: in 1955 Disneyland opened (to build it, he mortgaged his life insurance, sold his vacation home, and partnered with a television network in exchange for investment).

1966年，华特·迪士尼因肺癌去世，享年65岁。他没有看到他设想的“未来世界”（EPCOT）建成。

In 1966, Walt Disney died of lung cancer at the age of sixty-five. He never saw the "future world" (EPCOT) he had envisioned built.

五、致2026年的我们

V. To Us in 2026

迪士尼的故事，几乎是“创业失败学”的标准案例。

Disney's story is almost a textbook case in the "study of entrepreneurial failure."

第一，注意那些失败的性质：他不是“做不出好东西”，而是“不懂经营”、“被合伙人/合作方坑”、“押注太大被人笑”。这些不是能力问题，是经验问题——而经验，只能从失败里长出来。他的第一次失败是财务失控，第二次失败是合同没看清，第三次他才学会怎么保护自己的东西。

First, pay attention to the nature of those failures: he did not fail at "making good things"—he failed at "understanding business," at being cheated by partners and collaborators, at betting too big and being laughed at. These are not problems of ability but of experience—and experience can only grow out of failure. His first failure was financial chaos, his second was not reading a contract carefully enough, and only with the third did he learn how to protect what was his.

第二，被人拿走成果，不是终点，是逼你换一种活法的起点。奥斯华被抢走之后，他失去了角色、团队和市场，但恰恰是这次惨败，逼出了米老鼠。如果你最珍视的东西被夺走了，那就用剩下的那点力气，重新画一个。

Second, having your work taken from you is not the end—it is the starting point that forces you to live a different way. After Oswald was snatched away, he lost the character, the team, and the market—but it was precisely this crushing defeat that forced Mickey Mouse into being. If the thing you treasure most is taken from you, then take whatever strength you have left and draw a new one.

第三，“所有人都说不行”的地方，可能正是价值所在。《白雪公主》被全行业嘲笑，结果它开创了一个时代。当所有人都说你疯了的时候，先别急着改主意——先问问自己，他们是不是只是没看懂。

Third, the place where "everyone says it can't be done" may be exactly where the value lies. Snow White was mocked by the entire industry, and it went on to open a new era. When everyone says you are mad, do not rush to change your mind—first ask yourself whether they simply failed to understand.

那个“被说没有好点子”的人，后来给全世界的孩子造了一个王国。

The man who was said to have "no good ideas" went on to build a kingdom for children all over the world.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

6. “迪士尼被报社解雇、编辑说他'缺乏想象力'”——此说法缺乏可靠来源，很可能是城市传说。 多篇考据文章指出：没有可追溯到原始出处的记录；且他当年就职的是广告/商业美术公司，而非报社。文中明确标注为“流传甚广但无可靠来源”，未作史实使用。

6. "Disney was fired by a newspaper, and the editor said he 'lacked imagination'": this claim lacks any reliable source and is very likely an urban legend. Several investigative articles point out that there is no record traceable to an original source, and that the firm he worked for at the time was an advertising and commercial art company, not a newspaper. The main text explicitly labels it "widely circulated but without a reliable source" and does not use it as historical fact.

7. “他在回好莱坞的火车上画出米老鼠”——这是迪士尼本人及公司长期叙述的版本，属“官方起源故事”，具体细节（车票、时间、画的经过）难以独立证实；但由他本人讲述、且被广泛采信，文中已注“据他自己后来的叙述”。

7. "He drew Mickey Mouse on the train back to Hollywood": this is the version long told by Disney himself and by the company, and belongs to the "official origin story"; the specific details (the ticket, the timing, how the drawing was made) are difficult to verify independently. But because he told it himself and it has been widely accepted, the main text notes it with the phrase "by his own later account."

8. “Laugh-O-Gram破产的原因是给画师薪水过高”——此为常见解释（他对人才慷慨、成本失控），有一定史料支持，但公司失败是多种因素综合的结果（市场、资金、经营经验），文中按“薪酬超过收入、撑不住”的概括处理。

8. "The cause of Laugh-O-Gram's bankruptcy was paying his animators too much": this is a common explanation (he was generous with talent, and costs ran out of control) and has some documentary support, but the company's failure was the combined result of many factors (the market, capital, business inexperience). The main text handles it with the summary "salaries exceeded income, and the company could not hold on."

9. “他本人破产了”——需注意区分：他个人从未宣布破产，破产的是他早年经营的公司（Laugh-O-Gram）。建乐园时他确实极度缺钱、抵押寿险、卖房，但并未“个人破产”。文中已作区分。

9. "He himself went bankrupt": a distinction is needed here. He personally never declared bankruptcy; what went bankrupt was the company he ran in his early years (Laugh-O-Gram). When building Disneyland he was indeed desperately short of money, mortgaged his life insurance, and sold his house, but he was never "personally bankrupt." The main text draws this distinction.

10. 文中未涉及但需知悉：迪士尼在1940年代曾与FBI、众议院非美活动调查委员会（HUAC）合作、指认“共产党人”，这段历史存在争议，此处不作展开。

10. Not covered in the main text but worth knowing: in the 1940s Disney cooperated with the FBI and the House Un-American Activities Committee (HUAC), naming alleged "Communists." This period of his history is controversial and is not explored here.
