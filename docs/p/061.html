董明珠：36岁丧夫，从“追债的业务员”做起

Dong Mingzhu: Widowed at 36, Starting Out as a Debt-Collecting Saleswoman

——“董明珠走过的路，寸草不生”

—"Where Dong Mingzhu has walked, not a blade of grass grows."

2026年，有一类人活得特别难：单亲父母。要挣钱，要带孩子，要在职场上不被边缘化，还要面对“你这个年纪还折腾什么”的目光。

In 2026, one group of people has it especially hard: single parents. They have to earn a living, raise children, avoid being pushed to the margins at work—and face the looks that say, "At your age, why are you still making trouble for yourself?"

有一个女人，1984年丈夫病逝时，儿子才两岁。她在南京的化工研究所做行政工作，一个人带孩子，过了六年。1990年，36岁的她做了一个决定：把8岁的儿子托付给母亲，独自南下，去珠海打工。

There was a woman whose husband died of illness in 1984, when their son was only two. She held an administrative job at a chemical research institute in Nanjing and raised the boy alone for six years. In 1990, at thirty-six, she made a decision: she left her eight-year-old son in her mother's care and headed south by herself to find work in Zhuhai.

她从一名最基层的销售业务员做起，22年后，坐上了格力电器董事长的位置。

She started as a salesperson at the very bottom of the ladder. Twenty-two years later, she took the seat of chairman of Gree Electric Appliances.

她叫董明珠。外界形容她：“董明珠走过的路，寸草不生。”

Her name is Dong Mingzhu. Outsiders describe her this way: "Where Dong Mingzhu has walked, not a blade of grass grows."

一、“这辈子最大的转折点，是丈夫去世”

I. "The biggest turning point of my life was my husband's death"

1954年，董明珠出生在南京一个普通人家。她后来的人生轨迹，本该和千万个普通女性一样：读书、工作、结婚、生子，过平淡而安稳的日子。

Dong Mingzhu was born in 1954 to an ordinary family in Nanjing. By the usual script, her life should have followed the same path as millions of ordinary women: study, work, marry, have children, and live out quiet, stable days.

1984年，丈夫突然因病去世。她30岁，儿子才两岁。

In 1984, her husband died suddenly of illness. She was thirty; her son was barely two.

她后来说过一句话，坦率得让人心口一紧：“我这辈子最大的转折点是丈夫去世。如果不是这件事，我根本就不会走现在这条路。如果他在，根本就不会同意我来珠海。”

She said something later that is so blunt it makes your chest tighten: "The biggest turning point in my life was my husband's death. If not for that, I would never have taken the road I'm on now. If he were still here, he would never have agreed to let me come to Zhuhai."

为了养孩子，她一个人撑了六年。1990年，36岁的她辞掉了南京那份“令人羡慕的”行政管理工作，把儿子留给奶奶照顾，南下广东。

To support her child, she held on alone for six years. In 1990, at thirty-six, she resigned from her "enviable" administrative post in Nanjing, left her son with his grandmother, and went south to Guangdong.

一次偶然的机会，她替朋友到珠海办事，被那里安静、开阔的环境吸引，决定留下找工作。她应聘进了当时还叫“珠海海利空调器厂”的格力（格力电器的前身），成了一名基层销售业务员。

On a chance errand for a friend, she traveled to Zhuhai and was drawn to its quiet, open surroundings; she decided to stay and look for work. She was hired by Gree—then still called the Zhuhai Haili Air Conditioner Factory, the forerunner of Gree Electric—as a front-line salesperson.

那一年，她36岁，从零开始。

That year she was thirty-six and starting from zero.

二、40天，追回42万

II. Forty Days, 420,000 Yuan Recovered

进入格力后，她接到的第一个任务，不是卖空调，而是追债。

The first assignment she received after joining Gree was not to sell air conditioners but to collect a debt.

前任业务员留下了一笔四十多万元的应收账款。追债是每个公司都头疼的事——钱挂在账上，随时可能变成死账、烂账。而且这笔债跟她个人毫无关系，同事们都避之不及。

A previous salesperson had left behind more than four hundred thousand yuan in accounts receivable. Chasing debts is a headache for any company—the money sits on the books and can turn into a dead or bad debt at any moment. And this debt had nothing to do with her personally; her colleagues all steered clear of it.

董明珠却一头扎了进去。她连续40天去对方老总的办公室“死缠烂打”，见不到人就动之以情、晓之以理地跟公司员工讲道理。最后，对方被缠得无可奈何，让她把库存的空调拉走抵债。

Dong Mingzhu plunged in anyway. For forty days straight she went to the other company's boss's office and "hung on like a burr." When she couldn't see him, she tried to reason with his employees, appealing to both their feelings and their sense of fairness. In the end the company was worn down beyond endurance and let her haul away air conditioners from its inventory to settle the debt.

她拉走了一车空调，还多搬了一些货物，直到能抵上那42万元为止。清点完，她把多余的货单独存放，又打电话让对方派人来拉走。她的解释很有意思：“把多余货物退还给他，是让他知道，我们不想占他们便宜；让他们派人来拉货，是对他们不守诚信的一次惩罚。”

She took a truckload of air conditioners and moved extra goods as well, until the value covered the 420,000 yuan. After taking inventory, she set the surplus aside, then called and asked the other side to send someone to collect it. Her explanation is telling: "Returning the extra goods was to let them know we don't want to take advantage of them. Making them send someone to pick them up was a punishment for their bad faith."

这件事，让当时的总经理朱江洪对她刮目相看，也为她打开了后面的路。

This episode made then-general manager Zhu Jianghong look at her with new eyes—and it opened the road ahead of her.

三、“先款后货，绝不赊账”

III. "Payment First, Goods After—Never on Credit"

追债的经历，让董明珠意识到一个行业问题：当时整个行业流行“先货后款”的代销制，厂家处于绝对弱势，经销商颐指气使。

The debt-collecting experience made Dong Mingzhu aware of an industry problem: at the time the whole sector favored consignment on "goods first, payment later" terms, leaving manufacturers at a severe disadvantage and dealers domineering.

她给自己定了一条规矩：“先款后货，绝不赊账。”

She set herself one rule: "Payment first, goods after—never sell on credit."

在当时的市场环境下，这个想法显得“可笑至极”——她被许多经销商果断拒绝。但她不肯改。终于，在安徽淮南，一位女经理被她的勤奋和诚恳打动，同意先进20万元的货试试。

In the market climate of the time, the idea seemed "utterly absurd"—many dealers turned her down flat. But she refused to change it. At last, in Huainan, Anhui, a woman manager was moved by her diligence and sincerity and agreed to take 200,000 yuan's worth of goods as a trial.

为了做成这第一笔生意，董明珠采取了“上门跟踪服务”：她站在店里做推销，给顾客讲解，还建议经理发动员工向亲友推荐。这种“签完合同还管到底”的做法，为她赢得了口碑。口碑就是最好的广告。

To close this first deal, Dong Mingzhu adopted a "follow-through in-store service": she stood in the shop doing sales pitches, explaining the products to customers, and even suggested the manager mobilize staff to recommend them to friends and relatives. This habit of staying with a deal all the way through after the contract was signed earned her word-of-mouth—and word-of-mouth is the best advertising there is.

结果：仅1992年，她在安徽的销售额就突破了1600万元，占公司总销售业绩的12.5%。调往南京后，她又签下一张200万元的订单，当年个人销售额攀升到3650万元。

The results: in 1992 alone, her Anhui sales topped 16 million yuan, 12.5 percent of the company's total. After she was transferred to Nanjing, she signed another 2-million-yuan order, and her personal sales that year climbed to 36.5 million yuan.

两年时间，一个人的销售额，占了整个公司的六分之一。

In two years, one person's sales accounted for a sixth of the entire company.

四、留下来的人

IV. The One Who Stayed

1994年，格力内部出现了一次严重的危机：部分骨干业务员突然“集体辞职”。

In 1994, a serious crisis erupted inside Gree: a group of key salespeople suddenly "resigned en masse."

那是格力最困难的时候，也是对董明珠的一次试探。以她当时的业绩和名气，完全有条件跳槽去拿更高的薪水和职位。她留了下来，并被全票推选为经营部部长。

It was Gree's hardest moment, and also a test of Dong Mingzhu. With her track record and reputation at the time, she was well placed to jump ship for higher pay and a better title. She stayed—and was elected unanimously as head of the sales department.

此后，她带领格力连续多年稳居中国空调产销量的第一位。2012年，她被正式任命为格力集团董事长。从一名基层业务员到董事长，她用了22年。

After that, she led Gree to hold first place in China's air-conditioner production and sales for many years running. In 2012 she was formally appointed chairman of Gree Group. From front-line salesperson to chairman, the journey took her twenty-two years.

而她付出的代价也很清楚：二十多年里，她一直没有再婚。她用一种近乎冷酷的方式安慰自己：“即使是两个人，到老了时，总也是有一个人要先走的，剩下的那个还是一个人。”

The price she paid was equally clear: for more than twenty years she never remarried. She consoled herself in a way that borders on coldness: "Even with two people, when you grow old, one of you will always go first, and the one left behind is still alone."

五、1996年那场“凉夏血战”

V. The "Cool-Summer Bloodbath" of 1996

1996年，天气反常，全国空调行业进入“凉夏血战”——销量骤降，各厂家纷纷降价抢市场。

In 1996 the weather turned strange, and the national air-conditioner industry entered a "cool-summer bloodbath"—sales plunged and manufacturers slashed prices to grab market share.

那时已经升为销售经理的董明珠，做了一个反其道而行之的决定：宁可让出市场，也不降价。

Dong Mingzhu, by then promoted to sales manager, made a decision that went against the grain: she would rather give up market share than cut prices.

她带领23名营销业务员，面对的是国内一些厂家成百上千人的营销队伍。她还宣布，拿出1亿元利润的2%，按销售额比例补贴给每一个经销商。

She led twenty-three sales staff against the marketing armies—hundreds or even thousands strong—of some domestic manufacturers. She also announced that 2 percent of 100 million yuan in profit would be paid out as a subsidy to every dealer in proportion to its sales.

结果出人意料：那一年格力销售增长17%，首次超越春兰，成为国内空调行业销量和销售额最大的企业。

The result was unexpected: that year Gree's sales grew 17 percent, overtaking Chunlan for the first time to become the largest company in China's air-conditioner industry by both units and revenue.

这个决定背后，是董明珠的一个核心判断：价格战打不出品牌，只会把整个行业拖进泥潭。 宁可短期让利，也要守住产品的价值。此后在她领导下，格力连续多年在空调产销量、销售收入、市场占有率上位居全国首位。

Behind the decision was a core conviction of Dong Mingzhu's: a price war builds no brand; it only drags the whole industry into the mud. Better to yield profits in the short term than to surrender the product's value. Under her leadership thereafter, Gree ranked first nationwide for years in air-conditioner output and sales, sales revenue, and market share.

六、致2026：关于“晚出发”和“没有退路”

VI. To 2026: On "Starting Late" and "Having No Retreat"

2026年，女性的职场处境依然不容易，30+、35+的“年龄门槛”、“生育惩罚”、“单亲歧视”，仍然真实存在。

In 2026, women's position in the workplace is still not easy. The "age thresholds" at thirty-plus or thirty-five-plus, the "motherhood penalty," and discrimination against single parents are all still very real.

董明珠的故事当然不能被简化成“努力就能成功”——它里面有时代的机遇、有贵人的赏识、也有她极端的工作强度。她不完美，她的强硬也常常引发争议。

Dong Mingzhu's story, of course, cannot be reduced to "effort brings success"—it contains the opportunities of an era, the recognition of people who helped her along, and her extreme intensity at work. She is not perfect, and her toughness has often stirred controversy.

但有一点是确定的，而且对今天的很多人有直接的力量：她是36岁才从零开始的。

But one thing is certain, and it speaks with direct force to many people today: she started from zero at thirty-six.

她没有年轻资本，没有资源背景，没有退路。她唯一的优势是——她没有退路，所以她比谁都拼。

She had no youth to trade on, no resources or connections, no fallback. Her only advantage was this—she had no fallback, so she fought harder than anyone.

对我们这些还有退路、却总是犹豫的人来说，董明珠最值得记住的也许不是“她有多强”，而是她那句朴素的话：她当时“也没什么别的雄心大志，就是想着多卖出台空调，多挣点钱，养我儿子”。

For those of us who still have a fallback and yet keep hesitating, the most memorable thing about Dong Mingzhu may not be "how strong she is" but a plain sentence of hers: at the time she had "no great ambitions—I just thought about selling one more air conditioner, earning a little more money, and raising my son."

很多了不起的事，一开始都只是“我得把日子过下去”。 区别只在于，有人在撑日子的过程里，把自己撑成了一个更硬的人。

Many remarkable things begin as nothing more than "I have to keep the days going." The only difference is that some people, in the very act of holding life together, hold themselves into someone harder.

董明珠身上大概有两种力量，一种是“能力”，一种是“退无可退”。前者可以学，后者学不来——但正因为学不来，它才显得珍贵。今天很多人的困境恰恰相反：不是没有退路，而是退路太多，所以总是在犹豫。当一个人真的没有退路的时候，他反而会爆发出自己都想不到的东西。

There are roughly two forces in Dong Mingzhu: one is "ability," the other is "having nowhere to retreat." The first can be learned; the second cannot—and precisely because it cannot be learned, it seems precious. The predicament of many people today is the opposite: it isn't that they have no fallback, but that they have too many, so they are forever hesitating. When a person truly has no fallback, he can surprise even himself.

还有一点值得说：董明珠的“狠”，并不是天生的。她在南京做了十几年行政工作，性格未必强势。是那场丧夫之痛、那份独自养家的压力，把一个温和的女人，一点点磨成了后来的样子。所以她故事里最动人的部分，不是“她赢了”，而是“她本来没有别的选择，只能往前”。

One more thing worth saying: Dong Mingzhu's "ruthlessness" was not innate. She spent over a decade in administrative work in Nanjing, and her temperament was not necessarily forceful. It was the pain of losing a husband and the pressure of supporting a family alone that slowly ground a gentle woman into who she later became. So the most moving part of her story is not "she won," but "she had no other choice and could only move forward."

10. This piece does not assess Gree's business controversies in recent years or Dong Mingzhu's own public controversies (livestreaming, investments, and so on); it confines itself to the theme of "starting from zero at thirty-six, from the ground up."
