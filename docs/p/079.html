Chapter 079 · Part 4

嵇康：刑场上的最后一支曲子

Ji Kang: The Last Song at the Execution Ground

——“《广陵散》于今绝矣。”

—"Guangling San is lost to the world today."

← 返回主页 Home第四辑　用痛苦喂养的灵魂79 / 100约 3061 字 · 3061 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，很多人已经不太知道嵇康是谁了。但中国文人心里一直有个位置是留给他的：一个明明可以飞黄腾达，却偏偏选择不合作，最后被推到刑场上的人。

In 2026, many people no longer really know who Ji Kang was. But Chinese men of letters have always kept a place for him in their hearts: a man who could clearly have risen to great wealth and honor, yet chose instead not to cooperate, and was in the end dragged to the execution ground.

他是曹操的曾孙女婿，是当时最出色的音乐家、玄学家和文学家，是“竹林七贤”里最耀眼的那一个。他本可以安享富贵。

He was the husband of Cao Cao's great-granddaughter, the finest musician, metaphysician, and writer of his day, and the most dazzling of the Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove. He could have lived out his life in comfort and luxury.

可他四十三岁左右被押上刑场。行刑前，他看了看日影，索来一张琴，弹了一曲《广陵散》，然后说了那句话：

Yet at around the age of forty-three he was taken to the execution ground. Before the sentence was carried out, he glanced at the shadow of the sun, called for a zither, played the piece Guangling San, and then spoke those words:

“《广陵散》于今绝矣。”

"Guangling San is lost to the world from this day on."

一、一个“本该”做权贵的人

I. A Man Who "Should" Have Been a Man of Power

嵇康，字叔夜，约223年生于谯国铚县（今安徽濉溪一带）。他早年丧父，由母亲和兄长抚养长大。

Ji Kang, courtesy name Shuye, was born around 223 in Zhi County of the state of Qiao (in present-day Suixi, Anhui). His father died when he was young, and he was raised by his mother and his elder brother.

他的才华是全方位的。史书上说他“身长七尺八寸，美词气，有风仪”——个子高，相貌好，气质出尘。他读书极多，尤其精通老庄；他弹琴、写诗、作赋，都是当时一流。他还喜欢打铁，常常赤着上身，在自家院子里锻铁，打出来的农具拿去换酒。

His talents were of every kind. The histories describe him as "seven feet eight inches tall, with fine speech and bearing, and a distinguished air"—tall, handsome, with an unworldly presence. He read enormously, and was especially versed in Laozi and Zhuangzi; his zither playing, poetry, and rhapsodies were all first-rate for his time. He also liked to forge iron, often bare-chested, hammering metal in his own courtyard, and he traded the farm tools he made for wine.

他娶了曹操的曾孙女（一说孙女）长乐亭主，因此与曹魏皇室有姻亲关系，做过“中散大夫”这样的官，后世称他“嵇中散”。

He married Changle Tingzhu, a great-granddaughter of Cao Cao (some say granddaughter), and so was linked by marriage to the Cao Wei imperial house. He held the honorary post of Grand Master of Palace Leisure (zhongsan dafu), and later ages called him "Ji the Palace Leisure Master."

按常理，他属于那个时代最顶层的圈子。可偏偏，他生在了一个最不适合做自己的时代。

By any ordinary reckoning, he belonged to the very top circle of his age. And yet, of all times, he was born into the one least suited to being oneself.

二、司马氏的天下

II. The World of the Sima Clan

嵇康成年的时候，曹魏的实权已经落到了司马氏手里。

By the time Ji Kang came of age, real power in Cao Wei had already fallen into the hands of the Sima clan.

司马懿、司马师、司马昭父子一步步架空曹家，杀戮异己。“高平陵之变”之后，大批曹魏的拥护者被清洗。那些年，士人最大的难题不是“如何做官”，而是“如何活着”。

Sima Yi and his sons Sima Shi and Sima Zhao step by step hollowed out the Cao family and killed off those who opposed them. After the Incident at the Gaoping Tombs, large numbers of Cao Wei's supporters were purged. In those years, the hardest question for a scholar-official was not "how to get an office" but "how to stay alive."

顺从，就等于替篡位者背书；不顺从，就可能掉脑袋。

To submit was to endorse a usurper; not to submit was to risk losing your head.

绝大多数人选择了顺从，或者至少保持沉默。嵇康选择的是第三条路：明着不给你面子。

The vast majority chose to submit, or at least to keep silent. Ji Kang chose a third path: openly refusing to give you face.

三、那封著名的绝交信

III. The Famous Letter of Breaking Off Relations

嵇康最著名的“不合作宣言”，是写给老朋友山涛（字巨源）的那封信。

Ji Kang's most famous "declaration of non-cooperation" was the letter he wrote to his old friend Shan Tao (courtesy name Juyuan).

山涛也是“竹林七贤”之一。后来山涛做了大官，在升迁时，推荐嵇康来接替自己原来的职位。这在当时是一种善意的举荐。

Shan Tao was also one of the Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove. Later, when Shan Tao had risen to high office and was promoted, he recommended that Ji Kang take over his former position. At the time this was a well-meant recommendation.

但嵇康的回应，是一封公开信——《与山巨源绝交书》。信里说得很直接：我不适合做官，我这个人散漫、不守礼法、不能忍受拘束。他还列举了自己的“七不堪、二不可”，用极其锋利的语气，把“做官”这件事骂了一顿。

But Ji Kang's answer was an open letter—the "Letter of Breaking Off Relations with Shan Juyuan." In it he spoke bluntly: I am unfit for office; I am undisciplined, I ignore ritual law, I cannot bear constraint. He even listed his "seven intolerances and two impossibles," and in an extremely sharp tone proceeded to curse the whole business of holding office.

这封信的杀伤力，不在于它拒绝了山涛，而在于它把整个官场的虚伪，当众撕开给了所有人看。

The force of the letter lay not in its refusal of Shan Tao but in the way it tore open the hypocrisy of the entire official world and showed it to everyone, in public.

山涛因此与他疏远，但并没有记恨他。后来嵇康被处死前，把自己的儿子嵇绍托付给了山涛——山涛也真的把嵇绍抚养成人，并让他做了官。一个人可以跟你不做朋友，但依然值得你把儿子交给他。 这种关系，今天的人恐怕很难理解。

Shan Tao drifted apart from him because of this, but bore him no grudge. Later, before Ji Kang was executed, he entrusted his son Ji Shao to Shan Tao—and Shan Tao truly raised Ji Shao to adulthood and helped him enter office. A man may refuse to be your friend and still be worth entrusting with your son. Such a relationship is probably hard for people today to understand.

四、钟会的那一拜

IV. Zhong Hui's Visit

嵇康得罪的人里，有一个特别危险——钟会。

Among the people Ji Kang had offended was one who was especially dangerous—Zhong Hui.

钟会是司马昭的心腹，才华极高，也极记仇。他年轻时仰慕嵇康，曾带着大批随从去拜访。据说当时嵇康正在打铁，向秀在拉风箱，嵇康头也不抬，把钟会晾在一边。

Zhong Hui was a trusted confidant of Sima Zhao, a man of exceptional talent and exceptionally long memory for grudges. In his youth he had admired Ji Kang and once came to visit with a large retinue. It is said that Ji Kang was forging iron at the time, with Xiang Xiu working the bellows, and that Ji Kang did not even raise his head, leaving Zhong Hui standing there ignored.

钟会站了很久，转身要走。嵇康这时才开口问：“何所闻而来？何所见而去？”（你听到什么来的？看到什么走的？）

Zhong Hui stood there a long while, then turned to leave. Only then did Ji Kang speak and ask, "What did you hear that brought you here? What did you see that makes you go?" (What did you come here for, and what did you see that sends you away?)

钟会答：“闻所闻而来，见所见而去。”

Zhong Hui answered, "I came because of what I heard; I go because of what I saw."

这段对话成了名句。但从那以后，钟会就记下了这笔账。

This exchange became a famous saying. But from that day on, Zhong Hui kept score.

五、吕安案

V. The Case of Lü An

把嵇康送进死地的，是一桩朋友的官司。

What sent Ji Kang to his death was a lawsuit involving a friend.

嵇康有一个朋友叫吕安。吕安的哥哥吕巽品行恶劣，诬告吕安不孝。在那个“以孝治天下”的时代，“不孝”是重罪。嵇康站出来为吕安作证，还写了信与吕巽绝交。

Ji Kang had a friend named Lü An. Lü An's elder brother Lü Xun was a man of bad character, and he falsely accused Lü An of being unfilial. In an age that "governed the empire by filial piety," unfiliality was a grave crime. Ji Kang came forward to testify for Lü An, and also wrote a letter breaking off relations with Lü Xun.

这件事本来只是一桩家事纠纷，但钟会看到了机会。他向司马昭进言，说嵇康是“卧龙”，不能留，留着就是后患。他还特别强调嵇康与曹魏的姻亲关系，以及那封《与山巨源绝交书》体现出的“不合作态度”。

At first this was merely a family dispute, but Zhong Hui saw an opportunity. He advised Sima Zhao that Ji Kang was a "crouching dragon" who could not be allowed to remain, that to keep him was to keep future trouble. He laid particular stress on Ji Kang's marriage ties to Cao Wei, and on the "uncooperative attitude" shown by that "Letter of Breaking Off Relations with Shan Juyuan."

于是，一桩朋友的官司，变成了一个政治处决。

And so a friend's lawsuit became a political execution.

约262年（一说263年），嵇康被处死，年仅三十九、四十岁上下。

Around 262 (some say 263), Ji Kang was executed, only about thirty-nine or forty years old.

六、刑场上的琴声

VI. The Sound of the Zither at the Execution Ground

关于嵇康之死，流传最广的是刑场上的那一幕。

Of the stories about Ji Kang's death, the most widely told is that scene at the execution ground.

据说他被押赴刑场时，神色不变。他看了看日影，知道离行刑还有一点时间，就向人要来一张琴，从容地弹了一曲《广陵散》。曲终，他叹息：“当年袁孝尼曾经请求我教他这支曲子，我没有答应。《广陵散》从今天起，就断绝了。”

It is said that as he was led to the execution ground his expression did not change. He glanced at the shadow of the sun, knew there was still a little time before the sentence, and asked for a zither. Calmly he played the piece Guangling San. When it ended, he sighed: "Long ago Yuan Xiaoni asked me to teach him this piece, and I refused. From today, Guangling San is at an end."

一个即将被杀死的人，最后关心的是“一支曲子从此失传”。 这一幕，成为中国文化里最有名的一个“死的姿态”。

A man about to be killed, and what he cared about in the end was that "a piece of music would be lost forever." This scene became one of the most famous "postures of death" in Chinese culture.

太学生三千人曾上书为他求情，没有用。

Three thousand students of the Imperial Academy once submitted a petition pleading for his life. It was no use.

七、他到底留下了什么

VII. What He Left Behind

嵇康的著作，主要收在《嵇康集》里。他写过《养生论》《声无哀乐论》，也写过大量诗与赋。

Ji Kang's writings are mainly collected in the Collected Works of Ji Kang. He wrote "On Nourishing Life" and "Music Has Neither Sorrow Nor Joy," as well as a great many poems and rhapsodies.

在《声无哀乐论》里，他提出一个非常超前的观点：音乐本身并没有哀乐，哀乐是人心里带进去的。 这个论点，把审美的主体性推到了极致，在中国美学史上是一个重大的贡献。

In "Music Has Neither Sorrow Nor Joy" he advanced a strikingly forward-looking idea: music in itself contains neither sorrow nor joy; sorrow and joy are brought to it from the human heart. This argument pushed the subjectivity of aesthetic experience to its limit, and it is a major contribution in the history of Chinese aesthetics.

在《大人先生传》（也有归于其友人的说法）一类的文章里，“越名教而任自然”的思潮被推向了高峰——不要让外加的规矩，去扭曲一个人本来的样子。

In essays such as "Biography of Master Great Man" (which some attribute to a friend of his), the current of thought that "transcends moral doctrine and follows nature" reached its peak—do not let rules imposed from outside distort what a person truly is.

他留下的，不只是几篇文章、一支曲子。他留下的是一种人格的样本：在一个必须表态的年代，一个人可不可以什么都不说，只做自己？

What he left behind was not merely a few essays and a piece of music. He left a sample of a kind of person: in an age when one had to declare a position, can a person say nothing at all and simply be himself?

嵇康用命回答了这个问题：可以，但代价是死。

Ji Kang answered that question with his life: yes, but the price was death.

八、写给2026年的我们

VIII. To Us in 2026

今天，“不合群”是个带点负面色彩的词。我们被教育要“高情商”、要“会来事”、要“识时务”。

Today "not fitting in" is a word with a slightly negative coloring. We are taught to have "high emotional intelligence," to know how to play the game, to be "wise to the times."

嵇康恰恰是“高情商”的反面。他不给权臣面子，不给老朋友台阶，不给自己留退路。他不是不懂人情世故，他是懂，但不愿用。

Ji Kang was precisely the opposite of "high emotional intelligence." He gave no face to the powerful minister, offered no graceful exit to an old friend, left himself no way back. It was not that he failed to understand the ways of the world; he understood them, but would not use them.

我们不必都成为嵇康。在一个普通人需要养家糊口的时代，要求每个人都“不为五斗米折腰”，是不现实的，也是一种廉价的道德绑架。

We need not all become Ji Kang. In an age when ordinary people have families to feed, to demand that everyone "refuse to bow for five pecks of rice" is unrealistic, and a cheap form of moral coercion.

但嵇康的价值恰恰在于：他用最极端的方式，给“人还可以有骨气”这件事，保留了一个证据。 当所有人都说“没办法、只能这样”的时候，总有人要证明——不是的，还有另一种活法。

But Ji Kang's value lies precisely in this: in the most extreme way, he preserved a piece of evidence for the proposition that "a person can still have backbone." When everyone says "there's no way, this is the only option," someone must prove that—no, there is another way to live.

《广陵散》真的绝了吗？没有。一千八百年来，它被后人一次次考证、复原、重新弹奏。嵇康没能保住那支曲子，但历史替他保住了。

Was Guangling San really lost? No. Over eighteen hundred years it has been repeatedly studied, reconstructed, and played again by later generations. Ji Kang could not keep that piece of music alive, but history kept it for him.

这或许就是“失败者”最深的慰藉：你当下失去的，未必真的失去；你守住的，也未必只是你自己。 你守的那个东西，会变成一种气，散到很多后来人的心里去。这，也许就是这本书记下他的全部理由。

This may be the deepest consolation of the "failure": what you lose in the present is not necessarily truly lost; and what you hold fast to may not be yours alone. The thing you hold fast to becomes a kind of vital spirit, dispersed into the hearts of many who come after. This is perhaps the whole reason this book records him.

他死后，他的朋友们四散零落。但“竹林七贤”这个名字，从此成了一种人格的象征——在那个必须站队的年代，还有人愿意什么都不站，只做自己。嵇康没有留下政权，没有留下财富，没有留下基业。他留下的，是一首曲子、几篇文章，以及一个“人还可以有骨气”的证据。

After his death his friends scattered. But the name "Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove" became from then on a symbol of a kind of character—that in an age when one had to take sides, there was still someone willing to take no side at all and simply be himself. Ji Kang left no regime, no wealth, no estate. What he left was a piece of music, a few essays, and a piece of evidence that "a person can still have backbone."

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：嵇康（字叔夜），约223年生，约262年（一说263年）被杀，年约39—40岁。其生卒年学界有不同考订，本文采用“约”的表述。谯国铚县（今安徽濉溪一带）人。为通行记载。

1. Basic biographical facts: Ji Kang (courtesy name Shuye), born around 223, killed around 262 (some say 263), aged about 39–40. Scholars differ on his dates of birth and death; this essay uses "around." A native of Zhi County in Qiao (present-day Suixi, Anhui). This is the standard account.

2. 与曹魏皇室的关系：娶曹操曾孙女（一说孙女）长乐亭主，官中散大夫。辨析：其妻的具体辈分（曾孙女或孙女），不同史料记载有出入，本文并列。

2. His relation to the Cao Wei imperial house: he married Changle Tingzhu, a great-granddaughter (some say granddaughter) of Cao Cao, and held the office of Grand Master of Palace Leisure. Note: sources differ on his wife's exact generation (great-granddaughter or granddaughter); this essay gives both.

3. 打铁：《晋书·嵇康传》载其“性绝巧而好锻”，常与向秀在树下锻铁。为确证史实。

3. Forging iron: "The Biography of Ji Kang" in the Jinshu (Book of Jin) records that "by nature he was extremely ingenious and fond of forging," and that he often forged iron beneath a tree with Xiang Xiu. This is a well-attested fact.

4. 《与山巨源绝交书》：为确证史实。山涛荐其代己任官，嵇康作此书。注意：“绝交”更接近“公开表达不合作立场”，而非私人恩怨；嵇康临终托孤于山涛，说明两人并未真正反目。

4. "Letter of Breaking Off Relations with Shan Juyuan": a well-attested fact. Shan Tao recommended that he take over his own office, and Ji Kang wrote this letter. Note: "breaking off relations" here comes closer to "publicly declaring a stance of non-cooperation" than to a personal quarrel; that Ji Kang on his deathbed entrusted his orphan son to Shan Tao shows the two had not truly fallen out.

5. 钟会访嵇康的对话：“何所闻而来？何所见而去？”出自《世说新语》等，为流传极广的记载，属笔记性质，细节可能有加工，但钟会与嵇康不睦、钟会后进谗言，为多方印证的基本事实。

5. The dialogue when Zhong Hui visited Ji Kang: "What did you hear that brought you here? What did you see that makes you go?" comes from the Shishuo Xinyu (A New Account of Tales of the World) and other sources. It is an extremely widely circulated account of the anecdote ("notes") type, and the details may be embellished; but that Zhong Hui and Ji Kang were on bad terms, and that Zhong Hui later slandered him, are basic facts corroborated from several directions.

6. 吕安案与被害：吕安被兄长吕巽诬告，嵇康出面作证，钟会趁机进谗，司马昭下令处死。为确证史实。其被杀的深层原因是政治性的“不合作”，而非单纯的案件本身。

6. The Lü An case and his death: Lü An was falsely accused by his elder brother Lü Xun, Ji Kang came forward to testify, Zhong Hui seized the chance to slander him, and Sima Zhao ordered his execution. This is a well-attested fact. The deeper cause of his death was political "non-cooperation," not the case itself.

7. 刑场弹《广陵散》：出自《世说新语》等记载，流传极广，属笔记性材料。此事被历代反复书写，已成为文化符号；但其具体细节（如是否真的弹完整曲）无法严格确证，本文以“流传最广的记载”表述。

7. Playing Guangling San at the execution ground: this comes from the Shishuo Xinyu and other records; it is very widely circulated and belongs to "notes"-type material. The episode has been retold again and again down the ages and has become a cultural symbol; but its specific details (such as whether he truly played the piece through to the end) cannot be strictly verified, so this essay presents it as "the most widely told account."

8. 太学生请愿：史载有太学生为其请愿，为通行记载。

8. The petition of the Imperial Academy students: the histories record that students of the Imperial Academy petitioned on his behalf; this is the standard account.

9. 著作：《嵇康集》辑本为其主要文集；《声无哀乐论》的美学观点，历来被高度重视。其哲学地位与“越名教而任自然”的思潮，是魏晋玄学的核心内容之一。

9. His writings: the reconstructed Collected Works of Ji Kang is his main collection; the aesthetic views of "Music Has Neither Sorrow Nor Joy" have always been highly regarded. His philosophical standing and the current of thought that "transcends moral doctrine and follows nature" are among the core elements of Wei–Jin metaphysics (xuanxue).
