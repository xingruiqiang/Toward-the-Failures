Chapter 084 · Part 5

斯科特：输掉了比赛，却让全世界记住了他

Scott: He Lost the Race, Yet Made the Whole World Remember Him

——“我们坚持不住了……请照顾好我们的人。”

—"We are getting weaker… For God's sake, look after our people."

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人84 / 100约 3072 字 · 3072 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，我们太熟悉“赢”这件事了。比赛要赢，项目要赢，人生要赢。第二名往往没人记得。

In 2026, we are all too familiar with winning. Races must be won, projects must be won, life must be won. The runner-up is usually forgotten.

但历史上有一场比赛，赢家几乎被遗忘，输家却成了不朽的名字。

But history holds one race in which the winner was almost forgotten and the loser became an immortal name.

1911年，两个人都想成为第一个到达南极点的人。挪威人阿蒙森赢了，英国人斯科特输了——他晚了三十四天到南极点。更惨的是，在返程路上，斯科特和他的四名队员全部冻死在离补给站只有十几公里的地方。

In 1911, two men each wanted to be the first to reach the South Pole. The Norwegian, Amundsen, won; the Englishman, Scott, lost—arriving at the Pole thirty-four days late. Worse still, on the return journey Scott and his four companions all froze to death barely more than ten kilometers from a supply depot.

按理说，这是一个彻底的失败故事。可一百多年过去了，人们记住斯科特的，甚至比记住阿蒙森更多。

By any reckoning this is a story of total failure. Yet more than a century later, people remember Scott even more than they remember Amundsen.

原因，藏在他留下的日记里。

The reason lies in the diary he left behind.

一、一个不年轻的探险家

I. An Explorer No Longer Young

1868年，罗伯特·福尔肯·斯科特出生在英国。

Robert Falcon Scott was born in England in 1868.

他年轻时加入了英国皇家海军，一步步做到军官。1901年到1904年，他带领过一次南极探险（“发现号”探险），虽然没有到达南极点，但积累了名声和经验。

He joined the Royal Navy as a young man and rose step by step to officer rank. From 1901 to 1904 he led an Antarctic expedition (the Discovery expedition); though it did not reach the South Pole, it earned him fame and experience.

1910年，四十二岁的斯科特决定再来一次。他要带领“特拉诺瓦号”探险队，去完成那个当时无数人梦想的目标：成为第一个踏上南极点的人。

In 1910, at forty-two, Scott decided to try again. He would lead the Terra Nova expedition to achieve the goal countless people dreamed of at the time: to be the first to set foot on the South Pole.

那是一个“极地竞赛”的年代。就在斯科特筹备的同时，挪威探险家阿蒙森也在准备。两人都盯上了那同一个点——南纬九十度。

It was the age of the polar race. Even as Scott was making his preparations, the Norwegian explorer Amundsen was preparing too. Both men had their eyes on the same point—ninety degrees south.

二、两种完全不同的准备

II. Two Completely Different Preparations

这场竞赛的胜负，很大程度上在出发前就决定了。

The outcome of the race was, to a large extent, decided before either man set out.

阿蒙森是做足了功课的人。他长期生活在北极地区，学会了因纽特人的生存方法：穿兽皮衣服，用狗拉雪橇，储备大量新鲜肉类来预防坏血病。他的队伍靠的是“经验”，是一整套在极地活下来的智慧。

Amundsen had done his homework. He had long lived in the Arctic and learned the survival methods of the Inuit: wearing animal-skin clothing, using dog sleds, and laying in large quantities of fresh meat to prevent scurvy. His team relied on "experience"—a whole body of knowledge for staying alive in the polar regions.

斯科特的方式完全不同。他带的是马匹、摩托雪橇和少量狗。他手下的队员，很多是没有极地经验的英国海军军官和科学家。他们靠的是“纪律”和“意志”。

Scott's approach was entirely different. He brought ponies, motor sledges, and only a few dogs. Many of his men were Royal Navy officers and scientists with no polar experience. They relied on "discipline" and "willpower."

当然，斯科特此行还有一个重要的科学目标：沿途做地质、气象、生物研究。 他的队伍里有科学家，一路上采集了大量标本。这一点很重要——他不是纯粹为了“赢”，他还想“了解”。

Of course, Scott's journey had another important purpose: scientific research. Along the way they would study geology, meteorology, and biology. His team included scientists, and they collected a great many specimens en route. This matters—he was not aiming purely to "win"; he also wanted to "understand."

但在那种极端环境里，“再多带一点研究器材”，往往就意味着“少带一点燃料和食物”。他背负的东西，比阿蒙森更多。

But in that kind of extreme environment, "a little more research equipment" usually meant "a little less fuel and food." He carried more than Amundsen did.

三、1912年1月17日

III. January 17, 1912

斯科特一行人在1911年11月1日出发，向南极点冲刺。途中，他们遇到了极其恶劣的天气，还损失了好几匹马。

Scott's party set out on November 1, 1911, on their final push to the Pole. Along the way they ran into brutally bad weather and lost several ponies.

1912年1月17日，他们终于到达了南极点附近的区域。

On January 17, 1912, they finally reached the region of the South Pole.

然后，他们看到了阿蒙森留下的帐篷和挪威国旗。

Then they saw the tent and the Norwegian flag that Amundsen had left behind.

阿蒙森在1911年12月14日就已经到达了南极点。

Amundsen had reached the South Pole back on December 14, 1911.

斯科特在日记里写下的那句话，是这段历史里最沉重的一页之一。他说：“上帝啊，这是个多么可怕的地方！我们已竭尽全力，但结局就是这样……”

The line Scott wrote in his diary is one of the heaviest pages in this history. He wrote: "Great God! This is an awful place, and terrible enough for us to have laboured to it without the reward of priority…"

他们输了。晚了三十四天。

They had lost. By thirty-four days.

四、返程

IV. The Return

真正的死亡，是从返程开始的。

The real dying began on the way back.

五个人拖着极度疲惫的身体往回走。寒冷、饥饿、雪盲、坏血病，一样一样压上来。天气比去程更糟，路面变成了犁开的雪沟，走一天比不上平时走一小时。

The five men dragged their utterly exhausted bodies homeward. Cold, hunger, snow blindness, scurvy—each pressed down on them in turn. The weather was worse than on the way out, and the surface had turned into ploughed snow-ruts; a whole day's march covered less ground than an ordinary hour.

1912年2月17日，队员埃文斯（Edgar Evans）因伤病和体力崩溃去世。

On February 17, 1912, the team member Edgar Evans died from injuries and physical collapse.

1912年3月17日，另一个队员奥茨（Lawrence Oates）的情况急剧恶化。他的脚严重冻伤，拖累了所有人的速度。他在日记里知道，自己再走下去就是让全队陪葬。

On March 17, 1912, another member, Lawrence Oates, took a sharp turn for the worse. His feet were severely frostbitten, and he was slowing everyone down. He knew that if he went on, he would drag the whole party to its death.

那天早上，他走出帐篷，对同伴们说了一句：“我出去一下，可能要一会儿。”然后走进了暴风雪中，再也没有回来。

That morning he walked out of the tent and said to his companions, "I am just going outside and may be some time." Then he stepped into the blizzard and never came back.

他用自己的消失，给同伴腾出了几公斤的负重和一点时间。

By vanishing, he freed his companions of a few kilograms of weight—and of a little time.

但这点时间，还是不够。

But even that little time was not enough.

五、最后的帐篷

V. The Last Tent

1912年3月19日，剩下的斯科特、威尔逊、鲍尔斯三个人被一场连续几天的暴风雪困住了。他们距离下一个补给站——“一吨营”——只剩下十几公里。

On March 19, 1912, the three survivors—Scott, Wilson, and Bowers—were trapped by a blizzard that lasted for days. Barely more than ten kilometers lay between them and the next depot, One Ton Camp.

十几公里。在正常情况下，就是半天不到的路。 但在零下四十度的暴风雪里，这三个人已经再也挪不动一步了。

Ten-odd kilometers. Under normal conditions, less than half a day's walk. But in a blizzard at minus forty, these three men could no longer move a single step.

他们在帐篷里挨过了最后几天。1912年3月29日，斯科特写下了他日记的最后一句话（大意）：

They endured their last few days in the tent. On March 29, 1912, Scott wrote the final entry in his diary (in substance):

“我们坚持不住了……我们已竭尽全力。请照顾好我们的家人。”

"We shall stick it out to the end, but we are getting weaker, of course, and the end cannot be far. We have done our best. For God's sake, look after our people."

他们身边的补给只剩很少。他们采集的那些珍贵的地质标本，一直带着——最后被发现时，还在他们的雪橇上。

The supplies beside them were nearly gone. The precious geological specimens they had collected stayed with them to the end—when they were finally found, the specimens were still on their sledges.

1912年11月12日，搜救队找到了那座帐篷。三具遗体，和他们的日记、信件、标本。

On November 12, 1912, a search party found the tent. Three bodies, along with their diaries, letters, and specimens.

六、他为什么被记住

VI. Why He Is Remembered

阿蒙森赢了。他成了“第一个到达南极点的人”，这个头衔永远不会被改变。

Amundsen won. He became "the first man to reach the South Pole," and that title will never be changed.

但斯科特得到了另一种东西：他的日记被完整地带了回来。

But Scott gained something else: his diary was brought back intact.

那些日记是他在极端痛苦中，一笔一笔写下的。里面没有豪言壮语，只有冷静的记录、对队友的关心、对情形的判断，以及在最绝望时仍然保持的体面。他在自己快死的时候，还在担心别人的家人。

Those diaries were written stroke by stroke in extreme agony. There are no grand speeches in them, only calm records, concern for his companions, judgments about their situation, and a dignity he kept even in his most desperate moments. Dying himself, he was still worrying about other men's families.

一个人的日记，把一个“失败者”变成了一个“人”。

One man's diary turned a "loser" into a "human being."

英国为他举行了隆重的纪念。他被塑造成坚韧、牺牲、团队精神的象征。当然，后来也有研究者批评他：批评他的准备不充分，批评他固执地不用狗、非要骑马，批评他的决策失误。

Britain gave him an elaborate memorial. He was cast as a symbol of endurance, sacrifice, and team spirit. Of course, later researchers criticized him too: his inadequate preparation, his stubborn insistence on ponies instead of dogs, his errors of judgment.

这些批评都是对的。但恰恰是这些“错误”，让这个悲剧更有分量——他不是超人，他是一个有判断、有失误、并且最终承担了后果的普通人。

These criticisms are all fair. But it is precisely these "mistakes" that give the tragedy its weight—he was not a superman but an ordinary man who made judgments, made errors, and in the end bore the consequences.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

这个时代，我们太容易被“结果”绑架。没赢就是失败，没成就是白干。

In this age, we are all too easily held hostage by "results." Not winning means failure; not succeeding means wasted effort.

斯科特提醒我们：在结果之外，还有过程里的东西，是结果夺不走的。

Scott reminds us: beyond the result, there is something in the process that no result can take away.

他输了比赛，但他没有在绝望里变成一个刻薄的人。他的队员奥茨，为了不拖累同伴，选择走进暴风雪。斯科特本人，在快死的时候还在写“请照顾好我们的人”。

He lost the race, but he did not turn bitter in despair. His companion Oates, to spare the others, chose to walk into the blizzard. Scott himself, on the edge of death, was still writing "look after our people."

这些人输了，但他们配得上“体面”这两个字。

These men lost, but they were worthy of the word "dignity."

2026年，我们可能赢不了那场竞赛，可能拿不到那个第一。但我们可以选择，在失败的时候，还做一个像样的人。斯科特把这件事，在零下四十度的帐篷里，做到最后一天。

In 2026, we may not be able to win that race; we may not get that first place. But we can choose to still be decent people when we fail. Scott did exactly that, in a tent at minus forty, right up to his last day.

还有一层值得我们记住：斯科特一行出发时，带的是科学研究的目标。他们采集的岩样，后来被证明对理解南极大陆的地质历史有重要价值。他们没能把命带回来，却把几十公斤重的岩石标本带回来了。 这说明他们在那样的绝境里，仍然没有放弃自己认为重要的事。这种“输了也要把该做的事做完”的劲头，才是“失败者”这三个字真正的分量。输赢是一时的，而一个人在小事上不肯松手的样子，是会被历史记很久的。斯科特输了那场比赛，却赢下了另一种更长久的记住。

There is one more layer worth remembering: when Scott's party set out, they carried a scientific purpose. The rock samples they collected were later shown to be of real value for understanding the geological history of the Antarctic continent. They could not bring their lives back, but they did bring back tens of kilograms of rock specimens. This shows that even in such extremity they never abandoned what they believed mattered. That determination—"even in losing, finish what must be done"—is the true weight of the word "loser." Winning and losing are momentary, but the way a person refuses to let go of small things will be remembered by history for a long time. Scott lost that race, yet he won another, longer-lasting kind of remembrance.

今天，南极点上立着纪念阿蒙森的标记，也有纪念斯科特一行的地方。两个人都被记住了。但如果你去读当年的日记，会发现最打动人的，从来不是“我赢了”，而是“我们坚持不住了，请照顾好我们的人”。人类真正愿意反复回望的，往往不是胜利者，而是在绝境里还想着别人的那些人。

Today, markers commemorating Amundsen stand at the South Pole, along with a place honoring Scott's party. Both men are remembered. But if you read the diaries of that time, you will find that what moves people most was never "I won," but "we are getting weaker—look after our people." What humanity truly returns to again and again is often not the victors, but those who, in extremity, still thought of others.

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：罗伯特·福尔肯·斯科特（Robert Falcon Scott），1868年6月6日生于英国，1912年3月29日（或前后）卒于南极，年43岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Robert Falcon Scott, born in England on June 6, 1868; died in Antarctica on or around March 29, 1912, aged 43. Established fact.

2. 第一次探险：1901—1904年“发现号”探险。为确证史实。

2. First expedition: the Discovery expedition, 1901–1904. Established fact.

3. 1910年“特拉诺瓦号”探险出发。为确证史实。

3. Departure of the Terra Nova expedition in 1910. Established fact.

4. 阿蒙森先到南极点：1911年12月14日阿蒙森抵达南极点；斯科特一行于1912年1月17日抵达。为确证史实。

4. Amundsen reached the Pole first: Amundsen arrived at the South Pole on December 14, 1911; Scott's party arrived on January 17, 1912. Established fact.

5. 队员死亡：埃文斯于1912年2月17日去世；奥茨于1912年3月17日走出帐篷后死亡；斯科特、威尔逊、鲍尔斯于1912年3月29日前后在帐篷内死亡。为确证史实。

5. Deaths of the team members: Evans died on February 17, 1912; Oates died after walking out of the tent on March 17, 1912; Scott, Wilson, and Bowers died in the tent around March 29, 1912. Established fact.

6. 距离补给站：最后营地距离“一吨营”补给站约11英里（约18公里）。为通行记载。

6. Distance to the depot: the final camp lay about 11 miles (roughly 18 kilometers) from the One Ton Depot. Standard account.

7. 日记与遗体被发现：1912年11月12日搜救队发现帐篷及遗体、日记、标本。为确证史实。

7. Discovery of the diaries and bodies: on November 12, 1912, a search party found the tent along with the bodies, diaries, and specimens. Established fact.

8. 斯科特日记最后的话：因译本差异，中文表述各有不同。本文只保留其大意，不逐字引用，以免因转译而失真。

8. Scott's final diary lines: Chinese renderings vary with the translation. This book keeps only their substance rather than quoting word for word, so as to avoid distortion through retranslation.

9. 关于评价：斯科特究竟是“英雄”还是“失败的指挥官”，历史上长期存在争论。有研究者批评其准备不足、决策失误（如坚持用马和人力拖拽、未充分利用雪橇犬）；也有研究者为他的科学目标和团队表现辩护。本文的态度是：两者都对——他有决策失误，也有真实的勇气与体面，不必二选一。

9. On his reputation: whether Scott was a "hero" or a "failed commander" has long been debated. Some researchers criticize his insufficient preparation and errors of judgment (such as insisting on ponies and man-hauling rather than making full use of sled dogs); others defend his scientific aims and his team's conduct. This book's position: both are right—he made mistakes in judgment, and he also showed real courage and dignity; there is no need to choose between the two.
