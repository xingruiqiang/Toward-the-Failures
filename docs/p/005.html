Chapter 005 · Part 1

朱镕基：于低谷破局，向时代突围

Zhu Rongji: Breaking Through in the Trough, Charging Out Against the Times

——致困局中前行的80后90后

— To the post-80s and post-90s pressing forward through hard times

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者5 / 100约 2775 字 · 2775 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

作为一名80后，人近半百，我深知我们这代人和90后正在经历的人生变局。我们曾亲历中国经济狂飙突进的黄金二十年，见证房价稳步上涨、行业遍地机遇、努力就有回报的时代红利。可如今，潮水渐退，经济增速放缓，各行各业告别高速扩张进入存量博弈，房产价值波动、个人资产缩水、职场竞争内卷、发展空间收窄，压力层层叠加，很多人陷入迷茫、焦虑与自我怀疑，甚至渐渐丧失破局的勇气。

As a member of the post-80s generation, now close to fifty, I know well the profound shifts in life that my generation and the post-90s are living through. We once witnessed China's twenty golden years of breakneck economic growth, when housing prices rose steadily, opportunities were everywhere, and effort was always rewarded. But now the tide is receding. Growth has slowed, every industry has moved from rapid expansion into a zero-sum contest over what already exists; property values swing, personal assets shrink, workplace competition turns inward, room to advance narrows, and the pressures pile up layer upon layer. Many people sink into confusion, anxiety, and self-doubt, and gradually lose the courage to break out at all.

身处时代转型的阵痛之中，回望历史，朱镕基同志的人生轨迹与奋斗征程，恰好为身处困局的我们，提供了最珍贵的精神答案。他的人生从不是年少成名、一帆风顺的传奇，而是前半生沉淀蛰伏、近五十岁才迎转机、于时代最难关口迎难而上、以改革破局突围的硬核人生。这份历经低谷、笃行不怠、逆势攻坚的定力与魄力，正是当下80后、90后最需要的人生底气。

Amid the labor pains of this generational transition, looking back at history, the life and struggles of Comrade Zhu Rongji offer us, caught as we are in our own impasse, precisely the spiritual answer we most need. His life was never the legend of early fame and smooth sailing; it was a hard-won life—decades of settling and biding his time in the first half, a turning point only as he neared fifty, and then, at the era's most difficult threshold, stepping forward to meet hardship and breaking through with reform. That steadiness and daring—forged in the trough, sustained without slackening, hardened by pushing against the current—is exactly the inner ground the post-80s and post-90s need most today.

很多人只熟知他主政时期的雷霆改革、铁血担当，却不知他人生的前大半程，都是在沉淀、等待与坚守中度过的。朱镕基同志1928年出生于湖南长沙，年少勤学笃行，心怀报国之志，考入清华大学电机系深造，深耕专业、锤炼本领，早早立下为国为民的初心。走出校园后，他长期扎根工业、经济一线，深耕计划、生产、管理等基层岗位，数十年如一日深耕实干，积累了扎实的经济治理经验，看透了行业发展的规律与民生发展的痛点。

Many people know only the thunderous reforms and iron resolve of his years in power; few know that the greater part of his life was spent in settling, waiting, and holding fast. Zhu Rongji was born in Changsha, Hunan, in 1928. A diligent and earnest student with a heart set on serving his country, he entered Tsinghua University to study electrical engineering, deepening his expertise and tempering his skills, resolving early on to serve the nation and its people. After leaving campus, he put down roots for the long term on the front lines of industry and the economy, working his way through grassroots posts in planning, production, and management. Decade after decade he worked in earnest, accumulating solid experience in economic governance and seeing through to the underlying patterns of industry and the sore points of people's livelihoods.

时代从不会辜负默默沉淀的人，但时代的馈赠，往往迟到且历经磨砺。在漫长的岁月里，他几经沉浮，褪去浮躁、沉淀心性，在平凡的岗位上坚守初心、打磨能力，从未因境遇平淡而懈怠，从未因前路未知而迷茫。直至1987年，年近五十的他，才真正迎来人生事业的关键转机。这一年，他奔赴改革开放的前沿上海，先后担任上海市委副书记、市长、市委书记等重要职务。

An era never fails those who quietly build themselves up—but its gifts often come late and only after much grinding. Through long years of rise and fall, he shed his restlessness and steadied his character, holding to his original purpose and honing his abilities in ordinary posts, never slacking because his circumstances were plain, never losing his way because the road ahead was unknown. It was not until 1987, when he was nearly fifty, that a real turning point in his career finally arrived. That year he went to Shanghai, the frontier of reform and opening, where he successively served as deputy secretary of the Shanghai Municipal Party Committee, mayor, and party secretary.

彼时的中国，正处在改革开放的关键十字路口，机遇与风险并存，改革进入深水区，诸多矛盾交织叠加。计划经济的思维桎梏尚未完全打破，市场经济发展壁垒重重，国企改革滞后、市场活力不足、对外开放步伐迟缓，经济转型阻力巨大。无数人畏惧改革风险、固守固有格局，不敢突破、不愿创新，整个国家的经济发展，亟需一场敢为人先的突破与重构。

China at that moment stood at a critical crossroads of reform and opening, where opportunity and risk coexisted and reform had entered deep waters, with contradictions piling up and intertwining. The mental shackles of the planned economy had not yet been fully broken; a market economy faced barrier after barrier; state-owned enterprise reform lagged, market vitality was weak, and the pace of opening to the outside world was slow—resistance to economic transformation was immense. Countless people feared the risks of reform and clung to the existing order, daring not to break through and unwilling to innovate. The nation's economy urgently needed a bold, precedent-setting breakthrough and restructuring.

年近五十，在多数人选择求稳守成、安度余生的年纪，朱镕基却选择逆势而上、勇挑重担。扎根上海的数年里，他大刀阔斧推进地方改革，破除体制机制弊端，激活市场内生动力，整治作风顽疾、优化发展环境，推动上海突破发展瓶颈、实现跨越式发展，为全国改革开放积累了宝贵的地方经验，也让世人看到了中国改革破局的坚定决心。

At nearly fifty—an age when most choose to play it safe, hold what they have, and ease into the rest of their lives—Zhu Rongji chose instead to go against the current and shoulder a heavy burden. In his years rooted in Shanghai, he drove local reform with bold strokes, dismantling institutional and systemic defects, awakening the market's internal drive, tackling chronic problems in official conduct, and improving the environment for development. He pushed Shanghai past its bottlenecks and into a leapfrog advance, accumulating valuable local experience for reform and opening nationwide and showing the world China's firm resolve to break through.

1991年之后，他进入中央工作，正式站上了国家经济改革的核心舞台，扛起了中国经济转型、对外开放的千钧重担。整个九十年代，是中国近代经济发展最艰难、最关键的转型攻坚期，也是争议最多、阻力最大、考验最严峻的时期。彼时，国内经济结构失衡、通胀压力凸显、国企改革举步维艰、财税体制弊端凸显，国际上全球化浪潮席卷，外部竞争与封锁压力并存，中国经济前路迷雾重重，稍有不慎就可能错失时代机遇、陷入发展困局。

After 1991 he moved to work at the central level and formally stepped onto the core stage of national economic reform, taking on the crushing weight of China's economic transformation and opening. The whole of the 1990s was the hardest and most crucial period of that transformation in China's modern economic history—and the period of the most controversy, the greatest resistance, and the severest tests. At home the economic structure was out of balance, inflationary pressure was acute, state-owned enterprise reform was mired in difficulty, and the flaws of the fiscal and tax systems stood out. Abroad, the wave of globalization was sweeping the world, and pressure from external competition and blockade came at once. The road ahead for China's economy was thick with fog; one misstep and the era's opportunities might be lost, the economy trapped.

面对内忧外患的复杂局面，面对层层叠叠的改革阻力，面对无数质疑与争议，朱镕基始终秉持“不管前面是地雷阵还是万丈深渊，我都将一往无前、义无反顾”的担当，以雷霆手段推进全方位改革，开启了中国经济脱胎换骨的转型之路。他力推分税制改革，理顺中央与地方财政关系，筑牢国家经济发展的制度根基；大刀阔斧推进国务院机构改革，将国务院组成部门从40个精简至29个，大幅压缩行政编制，破除行政冗余、提升治理效能，为市场松绑减负；他攻坚国企改革、整治金融乱象、调控经济过热，成功带领中国抵御亚洲金融风暴冲击，实现经济软着陆，稳住了中国经济基本盘。

Facing a complex situation of troubles at home and abroad, facing layer upon layer of resistance to reform, facing doubts and controversy without number, Zhu Rongji held throughout to the conviction that "whether the road ahead is a minefield or a bottomless abyss, I will press on without turning back." With thunderous methods he drove all-round reform and set China's economy on a path of thoroughgoing transformation. He pushed hard for tax-sharing reform, straightening out fiscal relations between the central and local governments and laying a firm institutional foundation for national economic development; he cut the number of ministries under the State Council from forty to twenty-nine, sharply reducing administrative staffing, clearing out bureaucratic redundancy and improving governance, loosening the bonds on the market and lightening its load; he took on state-owned enterprise reform, cleaned up disorder in finance, and reined in an overheating economy, successfully leading China to withstand the Asian financial crisis, achieve a soft landing, and steady the fundamentals of the Chinese economy.

在所有改革壮举中，最具远见、最影响深远的，莫过于矢志推动中国加入世界贸易组织。九十年代，入世谈判是一场漫长且艰难的拉锯战，国内争议激烈、思想分歧巨大，国外层层设卡、百般刁难，37个成员国的双边谈判错综复杂，中美谈判更是历经25轮艰苦博弈，多次陷入僵局。很多人畏惧开放风险、担忧冲击本土产业，主张保守观望；也有人急于求成、妥协退让。

Of all these reform feats, none was more farsighted or more far-reaching than his determined push for China to join the World Trade Organization. In the 1990s, the accession talks were a long and arduous tug-of-war: at home the controversy was fierce and opinion sharply divided; abroad, hurdle after hurdle and obstruction after obstruction were set in the way. Bilateral negotiations with thirty-seven member states were a tangled web, and the talks between China and the United States alone went through twenty-five grueling rounds, time and again deadlocking. Many people dreaded the risks of opening and feared the blow to domestic industry, arguing for caution and watching from the sidelines; others were eager for a quick result and willing to concede.

关键时刻，朱镕基立足国家长远发展大局，顶住所有压力、摒弃短视思维，亲自坐镇谈判一线，坚守底线、灵活博弈，据理力争、寸土不让，既捍卫国家核心利益，又展现大国开放格局，最终推动中国在2001年成功入世。这一历史性突破，彻底打通了中国与世界经济的连接通道，让中国全面融入全球产业链、供应链、价值链，为21世纪中国二十年的经济高速增长、产业迭代升级、综合国力腾飞，奠定了决定性的制度基础与开放格局。

At the critical moment, Zhu Rongji, keeping the larger picture of the nation's long-term development in view, withstood all pressure and set aside shortsighted thinking. He personally took his place on the front line of the negotiations, holding the line and maneuvering with flexibility, arguing his case and ceding not an inch—defending the nation's core interests while showing the openness of a great power. In the end he brought China's accession to success in 2001. That historic breakthrough opened a channel between China's economy and the world's, fully integrating China into global industrial, supply, and value chains and laying the decisive institutional foundation and open posture for two decades of rapid growth, upgrading of industry, and the rise of China's overall national strength in the twenty-first century.

回望这段峥嵘岁月，我们更能读懂何为“逆势破局，向难而行”。朱镕基的半生奋斗，从来没有顺势而为的安逸，只有迎难而上的坚守；从来没有一蹴而就的成功，只有厚积薄发的沉淀。五十岁遇转机，六十岁扛大任，于国家经济最低迷、改革最难行的时代关口，以一己之担当，托举一国之未来。

Looking back on those eventful years, we can better understand what it means to "break through against the current and walk toward hardship." Zhu Rongji's half-lifetime of struggle knew no comfort of going with the flow—only the steadfastness of meeting hardship head-on; it knew no overnight success—only the long accumulation that finally bursts forth. A turning point at fifty, a great burden at sixty: at the threshold of a time when the nation's economy was at its lowest and reform hardest to carry forward, he lifted a nation's future with his own share of responsibility.

反观当下的我们，80后、90后正处在人生与时代的双重转型期。我们抱怨资产缩水、机遇变少、压力倍增，感叹时代红利消退、努力得不到回报，却常常在困境中焦虑内耗、轻言放弃、畏缩不前。我们习惯了高速增长的舒适，却忘了真正的成长与突破，从来都诞生于低谷与变局之中。

Turn now to ourselves: the post-80s and post-90s stand at a double transition, in our lives and in our times. We complain that assets are shrinking, opportunities fewer, pressure multiplying; we lament that the era's dividends have faded and effort goes unrewarded—yet in hardship we often turn anxious and inward, give up too easily, and shrink from moving forward. We have grown used to the comfort of rapid growth, forgetting that real growth and breakthrough are born in the trough and in upheaval.

朱镕基的人生，给所有困局中的年轻人上了最深刻的一课：人生从没有太晚的转机，也没有一成不变的困境。低谷不是终点，而是沉淀蓄力、破局重生的起点。五十岁尚且可以从零出发、勇担时代重任，我们正值壮年，又何惧一时的风雨、短暂的失意？

Zhu Rongji's life teaches every young person stuck in an impasse the deepest lesson of all: there is no turning point that comes too late, and no predicament that never changes. The trough is not the end—it is the starting point for gathering strength, breaking through, and being reborn. If a man of fifty could set out from zero and take up the heavy tasks of his age, then we, in the prime of our strength, need hardly fear a spell of wind and rain or a season of disappointment.

当下的经济放缓、行业洗牌、资产调整，不是时代的落幕，而是新一轮转型的序幕。就像九十年代的改革开放，历经阵痛才换来涅槃重生，如今的我们，正在经历时代迭代的必经阵痛。房产波动、行业内卷、收入调整，本质上是粗放式增长落幕、高质量发展开启的转型常态。淘汰浮躁、沉淀初心、深耕价值、坚守笃行，才能在新的时代浪潮中站稳脚跟、突围成长。

Today's slowing economy, the reshuffling of industries, the adjustment of assets—these are not the closing of an era but the prelude to a new round of transformation. Just as reform and opening in the 1990s passed through labor pains before rebirth, so we now are living through the pain that must come with an era changing over. Property swings, industries turning inward, incomes readjusting—at bottom these are the normal shape of a transition as crude growth ends and high-quality development begins. Only by casting off impatience, returning to first principles, cultivating real value, and holding to steady effort can we find our footing in the new tide and break out and grow.

他半生蛰伏、厚积薄发，教会我们耐得住寂寞，才能守得住繁华；他顶住万难、锐意改革，教会我们越是低谷，越要敢于破局；他立足长远、坚守初心，教会我们摒弃短视焦虑，笃行方能致远。没有谁的人生一帆风顺，没有哪个时代永远高歌猛进，个人如此，国家亦然。

His half-lifetime of lying low and then bursting forth teaches us that only those who can endure quiet solitude can hold on to prosperity; his standing firm against every difficulty and reforming with resolve teaches us that the lower the trough, the more we must dare to break through; his taking the long view and holding to his original purpose teaches us to cast aside shortsighted anxiety, for only steadfast action carries us far. No one's life runs smooth from end to end, and no era advances at full song forever—true of individuals, and true of nations.

作为身处转型时代的80后、90后，我们不必困于当下的得失，不必焦虑眼前的困境。资产缩水可以慢慢积累，行业放缓可以深耕精进，人生低谷可以蓄力翻盘。真正拉开人与人差距的，从来不是时代的红利，而是困境中不放弃、逆境中敢担当、变局中敢突破的坚韧品格。

As post-80s and post-90s living in an age of transition, we need not be trapped by present gains and losses, nor anxious over the difficulties before us. Shrunken assets can be slowly rebuilt, a slowing industry can be mastered through deeper work, and a low point in life can be used to gather strength and turn things around. What truly sets people apart is never the dividends of an era but the resilient character that refuses to give up in hardship, dares to take responsibility in adversity, and dares to break through in upheaval.

时代大浪淘沙，风雨淬炼初心。朱镕基先生迎难而上、逆势攻坚、厚积薄发的精神，穿越岁月、照亮当下。愿所有身处压力中的80后、90后，都能褪去浮躁、沉淀自我，不惧低谷、不畏变局，以深耕沉淀底气，以勇气突破困局，在时代转型的浪潮中，稳住初心、踏实前行，在自己的人生赛道上，破局重生、向阳生长，在平凡的岁月里，活出属于这一代人的担当与荣光。

The great waves of the times wash away the sand; wind and rain temper first principles. The spirit of Mr. Zhu Rongji—meeting hardship head-on, pressing against the current, and bursting forth from long accumulation—crosses the years and lights up the present. May all the post-80s and post-90s bearing pressure today shed their restlessness and settle into themselves, unafraid of the trough and undaunted by upheaval. May they ground their confidence in deep work and break through their impasse with courage, holding their original purpose steady and moving forward steadily amid the tide of transition; on their own track in life, breaking through and being reborn, growing toward the light—living out, in ordinary years, the responsibility and glory that belong to this generation.
