顾城：写下“黑夜给了我黑色的眼睛”的人，最后闭上了自己的眼睛

Gu Cheng: The Man Who Wrote "The Night Gave Me Black Eyes" Closed His Own Eyes in the End

——“我是一个任性的孩子，我想涂去一切不幸。”

—"I am a willful child; I want to paint away all misfortune."

2026年，我们在短视频里刷到“童话诗人顾城”的时候，多半只看到那句被反复引用的诗。人们说他纯粹、说他天真、说他是“一个被命运谋杀的诗人”。

In 2026, when "Gu Cheng, the fairy-tale poet" comes up in our short-video feeds, most of us see only that endlessly quoted line. People call him pure, call him innocent, call him "a poet murdered by fate."

但真实的顾城，比任何一个标签都复杂，也比任何一个标签都沉重。他是一个写出了中国当代最著名诗句之一的天才，也是一个在异国孤岛上，亲手毁灭了自己家庭的悲剧人物。

But the real Gu Cheng is more complex than any label, and heavier than any label. He was a genius who wrote one of the most famous lines in contemporary Chinese poetry, and also a tragic figure who, on a lonely island in a foreign country, destroyed his own family with his own hands.

他的诗属于星空，他的人生属于深渊。这两者，一直是同一个顾城。

His poetry belongs to the starry sky; his life belongs to the abyss. The two were always the same Gu Cheng.

一、诗人父亲身边的早熟少年

I. A Precocious Boy Beside a Poet Father

1956年9月24日，顾城出生在北京，祖籍上海。他的父亲顾工，本身就是一位有名气的诗人。

On September 24, 1956, Gu Cheng was born in Beijing; his family's ancestral home was Shanghai. His father, Gu Gong, was himself a poet of some renown.

这个家庭给了顾城两样东西：一种是文字的天赋，一种是漂泊的命运。1960年代末，顾工被下放，顾城跟着父亲去了山东的一片荒凉的盐碱地。他在那里放过猪、干过农活，也读了很多书。

This family gave Gu Cheng two things: a gift for words, and a fate of wandering. In the late 1960s, Gu Gong was sent down to the countryside, and Gu Cheng went with his father to a desolate stretch of saline-alkali land in Shandong. There he tended pigs, did farm work, and read a great many books.

1969年前后，少年顾城开始写诗。他没有上过什么像样的学，几乎全靠自学。他的诗里有一种极其罕见的清澈——他像个孩子一样看世界，用最简单的词，写最轻盈的意象。

Around 1969, the young Gu Cheng began to write poetry. He had hardly had any proper schooling and was almost entirely self-taught. His poems had an extremely rare clarity—he looked at the world like a child, using the simplest words to write the lightest images.

1977年前后，他的诗开始零星发表。很快，他被归入“朦胧诗”一派，与北岛、舒婷等人一起，成为那个时代最受关注的年轻诗人。

Around 1977, his poems began to appear in print here and there. Before long he was grouped with the "Misty Poetry" school, and alongside Bei Dao, Shu Ting, and others, he became one of the most watched young poets of that era.

二、“童话诗人”

II. The "Fairy-Tale Poet"

1979年，顾城写下了那首让他名垂诗史的作品《一代人》：

In 1979, Gu Cheng wrote the work that secured his place in poetic history, "A Generation":

黑夜给了我黑色的眼睛，

The night gave me black eyes,

我却用它寻找光明。

Yet I use them to seek the light.

只有两行，却几乎成了整整一代中国青年共同的精神坐标。它既是一个时代的伤痛总结，也是一个人不肯放弃的姿态。

Only two lines, and yet they became the shared spiritual coordinates of an entire generation of Chinese youth. It is both a summing-up of an era's wounds and one person's posture of refusal to give up.

此后，他被人们称为“童话诗人”。他的诗干净、纤细、带着一点不谙世事的倔强。1980年代初，他是朦胧诗群体里最耀眼的面孔之一，走到哪里都有人追随。

After that he was called the "fairy-tale poet." His poems were clean and delicate, carrying a touch of unworldly stubbornness. In the early 1980s he was one of the most dazzling faces of the Misty Poetry group, followed by admirers wherever he went.

但在这份光彩背后，是一个极其自我、极其不合群的人。他不擅长处理复杂的人际关系，也不愿意适应任何体制和规则。他把自己活成了一个“被现实追赶的孩子”。

But behind this brilliance was an extremely self-absorbed and unsociable person. He was not good at handling complex human relationships and was unwilling to adapt to any system or rule. He lived himself into "a child chased by reality."

三、谢烨，与那座叫“激流岛”的孤岛

III. Xie Ye, and the Lonely Island Called Waiheke

1979年，在从上海开往北京的火车上，顾城遇到了谢烨。两人一见倾心。这段爱情，被顾城写进了无数诗里。

In 1979, on a train from Shanghai to Beijing, Gu Cheng met Xie Ye. The two fell in love at first sight. This love was written by Gu Cheng into countless poems.

1983年，他们结婚。谢烨不仅是他生活里的伴侣，更是他创作上的助手——她替他抄诗、整理、处理外部世界的一切。可以说，顾城之所以能够像“孩子”一样活在诗里，是因为谢烨替他挡掉了整个现实世界。

In 1983, they married. Xie Ye was not only his partner in life but also his assistant in creation—she copied out his poems, organized them, and handled everything in the outside world. It could be said that Gu Cheng was able to live inside poetry "like a child" because Xie Ye blocked off the entire real world on his behalf.

1987年，顾城受邀出访欧洲。1988年，他们决定留在新西兰，最后定居在奥克兰附近的激流岛（Waiheke Island）。他们在岛上买了房子，过起了近乎与世隔绝的生活。

In 1987, Gu Cheng was invited to visit Europe. In 1988, they decided to stay in New Zealand, and eventually settled on Waiheke Island near Auckland. They bought a house on the island and began a life of near-total isolation.

岛上没有电，没有自来水，顾城自己养鸡、砍柴、种地。他越来越把自己封闭起来，越来越沉浸在一个由他一个人主宰的世界里。在岛上，他几乎不与外界往来，把全部生活都交由谢烨打理——买菜、寄信、见人、处理一切琐事。谢烨一边照顾他，一边还要照看他们的孩子。

The island had no electricity and no running water; Gu Cheng raised chickens, chopped firewood, and worked the land himself. He shut himself off more and more, sinking ever deeper into a world ruled by him alone. On the island he had almost no dealings with the outside world and handed his entire life over to Xie Ye to manage—buying groceries, mailing letters, meeting people, handling every trivial matter. Xie Ye looked after him while also caring for their child.

后来，一位年轻女性李英（诗中的“英儿”）也来到岛上，与他们同住。这段三人共处的畸形关系，最终成为一切崩溃的引线。1993年，李英离开，跟随他人去了欧洲。对顾城而言，这不是一段感情的结束，而是他苦心搭建的那个“小世界”的彻底破产。

Later a young woman, Li Ying (the "Ying'er" of the poems), also came to the island to live with them. This deformed three-way relationship eventually became the fuse for the collapse of everything. In 1993, Li Ying left, going to Europe with another man. For Gu Cheng this was not the end of a romance but the total bankruptcy of the "little world" he had painstakingly built.

从那之后，他陷入一种近乎疯狂的写作状态。他不眠不休地写那部后来被命名为《英儿》的小说，把这段私人经历几乎不加遮挡地摊开在纸上。他一生都在用文字建造一个只有他懂的世界，而这一次，他建造的是一座坟墓。

After that he fell into an almost mad state of writing. He wrote without sleep or rest the novel later titled Ying'er, spreading this private experience across the page almost without concealment. All his life he had used words to build a world only he understood, and this time what he built was a tomb.

四、1993年10月8日

IV. October 8, 1993

1993年，李英离开了激流岛，跟随他人去了欧洲。顾城的世界随之坍塌。他开始疯狂地写作，把这段经历写成了小说《英儿》。

In 1993, Li Ying left Waiheke Island, going to Europe with another man. Gu Cheng's world collapsed with her. He began writing frantically, turning this experience into the novel Ying'er.

据当时岛上的熟人回忆，那几天顾城的状态已经极度异常。谢烨则已经心力交瘁——她曾在给友人的信中流露过想要离开的念头：她想带着孩子，去过一种正常人的生活。这个念头，后来被很多人认为是那场冲突的直接背景。

According to the recollections of acquaintances on the island at the time, Gu Cheng's condition in those days was already extremely abnormal. Xie Ye, for her part, was utterly worn out—in letters to friends she had let slip a wish to leave: she wanted to take the child and live a normal life. That wish was later regarded by many as the direct background to the conflict.

1993年10月8日，在激流岛的住处附近，顾城与谢烨发生了激烈冲突。谢烨头部受到严重伤害。顾城随后在附近的一棵树上自缢身亡。谢烨被送医抢救，但因伤势过重，不幸离世。

On October 8, 1993, near their home on Waiheke Island, Gu Cheng and Xie Ye had a violent confrontation. Xie Ye suffered severe head injuries. Gu Cheng then hanged himself from a nearby tree. Xie Ye was rushed to hospital, but the injuries were too grave, and she died.

关于当天的细节，长期存在争议。需要注意的是：一些报道称顾城“用斧头砍死了妻子”，而警方在勘验后的结论中，认为现场发现的斧子与致命伤并无直接关系。 这一点被大量媒体忽略或简化了。

The details of that day have long been disputed. Note this: some reports said Gu Cheng "killed his wife with an axe," yet the police, after their examination, concluded that the axe found at the scene had no direct connection to the fatal wound. This point has been ignored or simplified by a great many media outlets.

换句话说，那天到底发生了什么，至今没有一个能让所有人信服的完整答案。可以确定的是：这是一场双向的毁灭，一对曾经相爱的人，同时在那个秋天的孤岛上失去了生命。

In other words, what exactly happened that day still has no complete answer that convinces everyone. What can be certain is this: it was a mutual destruction—two people who had once loved each other lost their lives at the same time, on that autumn island.

顾城死时37岁。

Gu Cheng was thirty-seven when he died.

五、天才与罪人的同一具身体

V. Genius and Sinner in the Same Body

顾城去世后，关于他的评价就再也没有统一过。

After Gu Cheng's death, opinions of him were never unified again.

一种声音把他说成“被世俗谋杀的纯粹诗人”，认为他只是太天真，不懂得如何在这个世界活下去。另一种声音则指出，他并不是一个无害的孩子——他在婚姻里的控制欲、他对身边人的支配、他对谢烨生命安全的漠视，都是真实的。

One voice called him "a pure poet murdered by the worldly," holding that he was merely too innocent and did not know how to survive in this world. Another voice pointed out that he was not a harmless child—his desire to control within the marriage, his domination of those around him, his indifference to Xie Ye's safety—all of this was real.

这两种说法都抓住了真相的一半。天才不等于善良，纯粹也不等于无辜。

Both views grasp half the truth. Genius does not equal goodness, and purity does not equal innocence.

顾城的诗是真诚的。他在《我是一个任性的孩子》里写：“我想在大地上画满窗子，让所有习惯黑暗的眼睛都习惯光明。”这句话至今读来仍然动人。

Gu Cheng's poetry is sincere. In "I Am a Willful Child" he wrote: "I want to draw windows all over the earth, so that all eyes accustomed to darkness may grow accustomed to light." Reading this even now, it still moves people.

但他也是一个把“任性”贯彻到底的人。他拒绝长大，拒绝承担成年人应有的责任，最终，这份拒绝变成了对最亲近之人的伤害。

But he was also a man who carried "willfulness" all the way to the end. He refused to grow up, refused to bear the responsibilities an adult should bear, and in the end that refusal turned into harm to those closest to him.

更值得警醒的是：在这段关系里，谢烨并不是一个没有声音的人。她是一个有才华、有判断、也曾表达过自己想法的人。可在外界的叙述里，她长期只是一个“顾城的妻子”，一个被写进诗人传记里的注脚。当一段关系里的一个人，被彻底简化为另一个人的附属品时，悲剧往往就已经埋下了。

More worth warning of is this: in this relationship, Xie Ye was not a person without a voice. She was someone with talent, with judgment, and with ideas she had expressed. Yet in outside accounts she was for a long time merely "Gu Cheng's wife," a footnote written into a poet's biography. When one person in a relationship is thoroughly reduced to an appendage of another, tragedy has usually already been planted.

六、写给2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

今天，“做自己”成了一句流行口号。很多年轻人会说：我不要被世俗绑架，我要活成我想要的样子。

Today, "being yourself" has become a popular slogan. Many young people say: I don't want to be held hostage by the worldly; I want to live as I wish.

顾城的故事，是这句话最沉重的注脚。

Gu Cheng's story is the heaviest footnote to that sentence.

一个人当然可以有自己的世界，但一个有他人存在的世界，就必然有边界。 越过那条边界的“纯粹”，不是自由，而是灾难。

A person can of course have a world of his own, but a world in which others exist necessarily has boundaries. A "purity" that crosses that boundary is not freedom but disaster.

我们纪念顾城，不是要美化他的结局，也不是要抹掉他的诗。而是要诚实地说：他的诗照亮过很多人，但他的人生，是一个关于“拒绝长大”的、代价极其惨痛的教训。

We remember Gu Cheng not to beautify his ending, nor to erase his poems, but to say honestly: his poetry lit the way for many people, but his life is a lesson about "refusing to grow up" whose cost was extremely bitter.

那两行“黑夜给了我黑色的眼睛，我却用它寻找光明”，至今还在无数人的朋友圈里流传。可写下它的人，最终没能为自己找到那一点点光。

Those two lines, "the night gave me black eyes, yet I use them to seek the light," still circulate today in countless people's social-media feeds. But the man who wrote them never managed, in the end, to find that small bit of light for himself.

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：顾城，1956年9月24日生于北京，原籍上海；1993年10月8日在新西兰激流岛身亡，年37岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Gu Cheng was born in Beijing on September 24, 1956, with ancestral home Shanghai, and died on Waiheke Island, New Zealand, on October 8, 1993, aged thirty-seven—a confirmed historical fact.

2. 家世与早年：父亲顾工为诗人；“文革”期间随父下放山东，有放猪等经历，出自顾工《顾城诗全编》代序等自述材料，可信度较高。

2. Family background and early years: his father Gu Gong was a poet; during the Cultural Revolution he was sent down to Shandong with his father, where he tended pigs and so on—drawn from Gu Gong's own accounts, including his preface to The Complete Poems of Gu Cheng, and of relatively high credibility.

3. 《一代人》（1979年）、《我是一个任性的孩子》等代表作：为确证史实。

3. Representative works such as "A Generation" (1979) and "I Am a Willful Child": confirmed historical facts.

4. 与谢烨相识、结婚：1979年在京沪列车上相识，1983年结婚，为通行记载（谢烨本人及顾城作品中均有记述）。

4. Meeting and marrying Xie Ye: they met in 1979 on the Beijing–Shanghai train and married in 1983—a common record (described both by Xie Ye herself and in Gu Cheng's works).

5. 激流岛（Waiheke Island）生活：1988年前后定居新西兰奥克兰附近的激流岛，生活极其闭塞，为确证史实。

5. Life on Waiheke Island: around 1988 they settled on Waiheke Island near Auckland, New Zealand, living in extreme isolation—a confirmed historical fact.

6. 李英（“英儿”）与三人共处：李英曾与顾城、谢烨在岛上共同生活，后于1993年离开。此为确证事实，但三人关系的具体内情，主要来自顾城《英儿》及李英本人的回忆《魂断激流岛》，双方叙述有出入，需谨慎对待。

6. Li Ying ("Ying'er") and the three living together: Li Ying once lived on the island with Gu Cheng and Xie Ye, and left in 1993. This is a confirmed fact, but the specific inner workings of the three-way relationship come mainly from Gu Cheng's Ying'er and Li Ying's own memoir Soul Broken on Waiheke Island; the two accounts differ and should be treated with caution.

7. 1993年10月8日事件：谢烨头部受重创后死亡，顾城自缢身亡。关键辨析：关于凶器，媒体广泛流传“顾城用斧头砍死妻子”的说法；但据警方勘验记录，现场发现的斧子与造成致命伤的凶器之间缺乏直接关联，此说法存在争议。本文如实指出这一点，不采信未经勘验证实的单一版本。另需说明：事发地点的具体位置（住处内外）在不同记述中说法不一。

7. The events of October 8, 1993: Xie Ye died after suffering severe head injuries, and Gu Cheng hanged himself. A key clarification: regarding the weapon, the claim that "Gu Cheng hacked his wife to death with an axe" circulated widely in the media; but according to police examination records, the axe found at the scene lacked any direct connection to the weapon that caused the fatal wound, and this claim is disputed. This piece points this out honestly and does not credit a single version unverified by examination. It should also be noted that the exact location of the incident (inside or outside the residence) is described differently in different accounts.

8. 关于评价：本文不采用“纯粹的童话诗人被世俗杀害”这类单一叙事，因为这种说法会掩盖其行为对他人的伤害。同时也不否定其诗歌成就。历史人物的评价，允许同时承认其才华与过失。

8. On appraisal: this piece does not adopt a single narrative such as "a pure fairy-tale poet murdered by the worldly," because that would conceal the harm his actions did to others. Nor does it deny his poetic achievement. In appraising historical figures, it is permissible to acknowledge both their talent and their faults.
