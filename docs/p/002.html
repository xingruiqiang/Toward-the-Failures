Chapter 002 · Part 1

罗阳：“失败”到极致，才见光芒

Luo Yang: Only When "Failure" Reaches Its Extreme Does the Light Appear

Have you ever had a moment like this?

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者2 / 100约 940 字 · 940 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

你有没有过这样的时刻？

Have you ever had a moment like this?

拼尽全力，却一次次落空。

You give it everything you have, and yet you come up empty again and again.

觉得自己，就是个彻头彻尾的失败者。

You feel that you are nothing but a total, thorough failure.

今天，我想给你讲一个“失败者”的故事。

Today I want to tell you the story of one "failure."

他叫罗阳，歼-15舰载机研制现场总指挥。

His name was Luo Yang, the chief on-site commander of the J-15 carrier-based fighter development program.

听起来很厉害，对不对？

Sounds impressive, doesn't it?

可很少有人知道，他的路上，全是失败。

But few people know that his road was paved with failures.

2002年，“瓦良格”号航母来到中国。

In 2002, the aircraft carrier Varyag came to China.

我们要造自己的舰载机，却连一张完整的图纸都没有。

We were going to build our own carrier-based fighter, and we did not even have a single complete set of blueprints.

外国封锁技术，嘲讽我们“不可能做到”。

Foreign countries blockaded the technology and mocked us: "Impossible."

罗阳接下这个烂摊子，没人觉得他能成。

Luo Yang took on this mess, and no one thought he could pull it off.

他不是天才，只是个埋头苦干的普通人。

He was not a genius, just an ordinary man who worked doggedly.

温和内敛，一笑眼睛眯成缝，不张扬，不炫耀。

Gentle and reserved, his eyes narrowing to slits when he smiled, never showing off, never boasting.

可干起活来，他跟自己死磕。

But when it came to work, he fought himself to the death.

第一次设计折叠机翼，失败。

The first attempt at folding wings: failure.

改了又改，还是达不到要求。

Revised again and again, still short of the requirement.

拦阻钩调试，一次次卡壳，一次次重来。

The arresting hook jammed in test after test, and test after test he started over.

试飞时，飞行员说油门杆不舒服，全盘推翻整改。

During a test flight, a pilot said the throttle stick did not feel right, and the whole design was scrapped and reworked.

那几年，他每天工作20个小时。

In those years he worked 20 hours a day.

熬得头发发白，熬得身体透支。

Staying up until his hair turned white, until his body was running on empty.

身边有人劝他：算了吧，太难了，别硬扛。

People around him urged him: Give it up. It's too hard. Don't force it.

他只是摇摇头，继续泡在研制现场。

He only shook his head and went on burying himself at the development site.

他也曾焦虑，也曾迷茫。

He too had felt anxious, had felt lost.

就像现在的你——

Just like you are now—

学生党熬夜刷题，成绩还是上不去；

Students burning the midnight oil over practice problems, and the grades still won't rise;

中年人面临失业，房贷压得喘不过气；

Middle-aged people facing layoffs, crushed by their mortgages until they can't breathe;

创业者一腔热血，最后却落得一身债务；

Entrepreneurs full of passion, ending up buried in debt;

老年人退休后空虚迷茫，被病痛缠身。

Older people adrift and bewildered after retirement, plagued by illness.

我们都曾是“失败者”，都曾在深夜里崩溃。

We have all been "failures." We have all broken down in the dead of night.

罗阳也一样，但他没放弃。

Luo Yang was the same, but he did not give up.

2012年11月18日，他刚从航展回来，没回家。

On November 18, 2012, he had just returned from an airshow and did not go home.

裹件棉衣，直奔辽宁舰。

He wrapped a cotton coat around himself and headed straight for the Liaoning.

这是歼-15的最后考验，也是他与“失败”的终极对决。

This was the J-15's final test, and his ultimate showdown with "failure."

5天，他寸步不离甲板。

For five days he did not leave the deck for a moment.

眼睛死死盯着每一次起降，心提到了嗓子眼。

His eyes were fixed on every takeoff and landing, his heart in his throat.

11月23日，历史性的一刻。

On November 23, a historic moment.

歼-15成功着舰，完美上演“刀尖上的舞蹈”。

The J-15 landed successfully, a flawless performance of "a dance on the tip of a knife."

他赢了，赢过了所有的质疑和失败。

He had won—won over all the doubt and all the failures.

可谁也没想到，两天后，他倒下了。

But no one could have imagined that two days later, he collapsed.

2012年11月25日，辽宁舰凯旋。

On November 25, 2012, the Liaoning returned in triumph.

他走下舷梯，满脸疲惫，连拥抱的力气都没有。

He walked down the gangway, his face hollowed by exhaustion, without even the strength for an embrace.

突发心梗，3个多小时抢救，还是没能留住他。

A sudden heart attack. More than three hours of resuscitation, and still they could not keep him.

51岁，他打赢了“失败”，却没能留住自己。

At 51, he had beaten "failure" but could not hold on to himself.

他没来得及喝庆功酒，没来得及陪母亲安享晚年。

He never got to drink the celebratory toast, never got to spend his mother's old age at her side.

可他用一生告诉我们：

But with his life he told us:

所谓失败，不过是成功的铺垫；

So-called failure is nothing but the groundwork of success;

所谓坚守，就是哪怕身处绝境，也不放弃前行。

So-called perseverance means that even in a hopeless place, you do not give up moving forward.

你现在经历的压力、迷茫、挫败，罗阳都曾扛过。

The pressure, the confusion, the setbacks you are going through now—Luo Yang bore them all.

他没能走完的路，该我们替他走下去。

The road he could not finish, we should walk it for him.

学生党别放弃，每一次刷题，都是在靠近梦想；

Students, don't give up—every problem you work through brings you closer to your dream;

中年人别认输，每一次坚持，都是在为家人托底；

Middle-aged people, don't surrender—every time you hold on, you are holding up your family;

老年人别迷茫，每一次热爱，都是生命的新生。

Older people, don't lose your way—every passion is a rebirth of life.

没有天生的赢家，只有不肯认输的勇者。替罗阳走完剩下的路，你走过的每一步，都算数！

There are no born winners, only brave souls who refuse to surrender. Walk the rest of the road for Luo Yang—every step you take counts!
