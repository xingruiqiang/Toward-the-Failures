路遥：用生命写完《平凡的世界》，没等到它变成经典

Lu Yao: He Finished Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World) with His Life, and Did Not Live to See It Become a Classic

——“像牛一样劳动，像土地一样奉献。”

——"Toil like an ox, give like the earth."

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人96 / 100约 2997 字 · 2997 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，《平凡的世界》是无数年轻人的“人生必读书”之一。它被一代代读者反复阅读，被改编成电视剧，被列为各种书单的第一名。

In 2026, Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World) is one of the "must-reads for life" of countless young people. It has been read again and again by generation after generation of readers, adapted into a television series, and placed at the top of all kinds of reading lists.

但写下它的人，没有看到这一天。

But the man who wrote it did not live to see this day.

他在四十二岁那年去世，死因是肝硬化和消化道出血。在写《平凡的世界》最后的阶段，他的身体已经垮到“像个垂危的病人半躺在桌面上，斜着身子勉强用笔在写”。他知道自己活不长，但他还是把这部书写完了。

He died at forty-two, of cirrhosis and gastrointestinal bleeding. In the final stage of writing Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World), his body had already collapsed to the point that he was "half-lying on the desk like a dying patient, leaning sideways, barely able to write with his pen." He knew he did not have long to live, but he finished the book all the same.

他叫路遥。

His name was Lu Yao.

一、被父亲“送人”的七岁孩子

I. A Seven-Year-Old Boy "Given Away" by His Father

1949年12月3日，路遥出生在陕北绥德专区清涧县的一个农民家庭。他的小名叫“卫儿”。

On December 3, 1949, Lu Yao was born to a peasant family in Qingjian County, Suide Prefecture, in northern Shaanxi. His childhood name was "Wei'er."

家里太穷了，孩子又多。1957年深秋，七岁的路遥被父亲带着，从清涧一路走到了延川——不是去走亲戚，是去过继给伯父。

The family was too poor, and there were too many children. In the late autumn of 1957, seven-year-old Lu Yao was taken by his father on foot all the way from Qingjian to Yanchuan — not to visit relatives, but to be given in adoption to his uncle.

路遥后来回忆过那一天。他说，他躲在村里一棵老树的背后，眼看着父亲走了。他特别伤心，觉得父亲把他“出卖”了。但他咬着牙忍住了，没有哭，也没有追上去。

Lu Yao later recalled that day. He said he hid behind an old tree in the village and watched his father leave. He was terribly sad, feeling that his father had "sold him out." But he gritted his teeth and held it in; he did not cry, and he did not run after him.

一个七岁的孩子，用一次沉默的告别，开始了他“独立做人”的一生。

With a single silent farewell, a seven-year-old boy began a life of "standing on his own as a person."

伯父家也很穷。到了上学的年纪，家里拿不出学费。有一天早晨，养母把他从炕上叫起来，在他脖子上挂了一个书包，轻声说：“上学去吧。”

His uncle's family was poor too. When he reached school age, the family could not afford the tuition. One morning his adoptive mother roused him from the kang, hung a schoolbag around his neck, and said softly, "Go to school."

这一句话，改变了路遥的一生。

That one sentence changed Lu Yao's whole life.

二、在县城读书的农村孩子

II. A Country Boy Schooled in the County Town

在延川县城上学的时候，路遥第一次尝到了“城乡差距”的滋味。

While attending school in the county town of Yanchuan, Lu Yao tasted the "gap between city and countryside" for the first time.

当时县城放电影，一张票一毛钱。对路遥来说，这是一笔掏不出来的钱。他受不了自己被排斥在“流行文化”之外——于是他想了个办法：拼命读书、读报，把县城唯一的新华书店和阅览室变成了自己的“精神食堂”。

At the time, the county town showed films, a ticket costing one jiao. For Lu Yao this was money he simply could not produce. He could not bear to be shut out of "popular culture" — so he came up with a solution: he read books and newspapers with all his might, turning the county's only Xinhua Bookstore and reading room into his own "spiritual canteen."

他把从书里看到的世界，讲给同学们听。很快，他成了同学眼中的“故事大王”，请看电影的人多到数不清。他用读书，跨过了那张一毛钱的电影票。

He told his classmates about the worlds he saw in books. Soon he became the "king of storytellers" in their eyes, with more people offering to treat him to films than he could count. Through reading, he crossed over that one-jiao movie ticket.

他学习极好。在千余名考生中，他考上了延川中学。但他吃的是同学帮衬的“丙等伙食”，吃不上的时候，就去野外找野果子。

He studied extremely well. Among more than a thousand candidates, he won admission to Yanchuan Middle School. But he ate the "Grade C" meals that classmates helped him with, and when he could get no meal at all, he went out to the fields to look for wild fruit.

1966年，他考上了西安石油化工学校——然后“文革”开始了，学校停止招生。

In 1966, he was admitted to the Xi'an School of Petroleum and Chemical Engineering — and then the Cultural Revolution began, and the school stopped admitting students.

三、从《人生》到《平凡的世界》

III. From Rensheng (Life) to Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World)

1973年，在当时的延川县委书记的推荐下，路遥进入了延安大学中文系。这是他命运的转折点。

In 1973, on the recommendation of the then-secretary of the Yanchuan County Party Committee, Lu Yao entered the Department of Chinese at Yan'an University. It was the turning point of his fate.

1976年，他成为《延河》杂志的编辑。1980年，他的中篇小说《惊心动魄的一幕》获得全国优秀中篇小说奖。他去北京领奖，钱是借的。

In 1976 he became an editor at the magazine Yan He (Yan River). In 1980 his novella Jingxin Dongpo de Yimu (A Thrilling Scene) won the National Award for Outstanding Novella. He went to Beijing to accept the prize on borrowed money.

1982年，他写出了《人生》。这部中篇小说发表在《收获》上，立刻引起轰动，并获得了全国优秀中篇小说奖。

In 1982 he wrote Rensheng (Life). Published in Shouhuo (Harvest), the novella caused an immediate sensation and won the National Award for Outstanding Novella.

《人生》让路遥成了当时最受瞩目的作家之一。

Rensheng (Life) made Lu Yao one of the most watched writers of the day.

但他没有停在那里。他要写一部更大的书。1983年，他开始动笔写《平凡的世界》——一部要以陕北一个农民家庭的十年为线索，全景式展现中国1975年到1985年城乡社会变化的百万字长篇小说。

But he did not stop there. He wanted to write a bigger book. In 1983 he began writing Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World) — a million-character novel that, following a peasant family in northern Shaanxi across ten years, would present a panoramic view of the changes in Chinese urban and rural society from 1975 to 1985.

四、六年，用命去换

IV. Six Years, Bought with His Life

写《平凡的世界》，路遥用的是“燃烧自己”的方式。

In writing Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World), Lu Yao's method was to "burn himself."

他用了六年。他前后读了几十部中外长篇，翻阅了大量报纸，去煤矿、去农村实地体验生活。他的写作状态，被他自己的文字记录了下来：

It took him six years. He read dozens of Chinese and foreign novels along the way, pored over a great many newspapers, and went to coal mines and villages to experience life firsthand. His state of mind while writing was recorded in his own words:

“写作整个地进入狂热状态。身体几乎不存在，生命似乎就是一种纯粹的精神形式。日常生活变为机器人性质。”

"Writing entered an entirely feverish state. The body almost ceased to exist; life seemed to be a purely spiritual form. Daily life became robotic in nature."

写完第二部的时候，他的身体出了问题。他描述自己“身体软弱得像一摊泥”，“最痛苦的是吸进一口气就特别艰难”。但他隐瞒了病情，稍作治疗，就继续开始第三部的创作。

When he finished the second volume, his body gave out. He described himself as "weak as a puddle of mud," and said "the most painful thing is that drawing a single breath is extraordinarily hard." But he concealed his illness, took a little treatment, and went on to begin the third volume.

1988年5月25日，他终于写完了最后一行。写完最后一个标点，他从桌前站起来，做的第一件事，是把手里那支圆珠笔从窗户扔了出去。

On May 25, 1988, he finally finished the last line. After writing the final punctuation mark, he stood up from his desk, and the first thing he did was throw the ballpoint pen in his hand out the window.

六年，一部百万字的书，换掉了他身体里最后的一点储备。

Six years, and a million-character book, used up the last reserve left in his body.

五、迟到的认可

V. Recognition, Long Delayed

《平凡的世界》刚出来时，并没有立刻得到文学界的一致好评。

When Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World) first appeared, it did not immediately win unanimous praise from the literary world.

当时的文坛，正流行各种新的写法、新的观念。路遥这种“现实主义”“宏大叙事”“讴歌奋斗”的写法，被认为“过时”。第一部出来时，评论界反应冷淡。

At the time, the literary scene was taken with all kinds of new styles and new ideas. Lu Yao's kind of writing — "realism," "grand narrative," "celebrating struggle" — was considered "outdated." When the first volume appeared, the critics responded coldly.

但读者的反应完全相反。中央人民广播电台在作品还没写完的时候，就打破常规开始连播，无数听众每天守着收音机。这本书不是靠评论界捧起来的，是靠千千万万个普通人喜欢出来的。

But readers reacted in exactly the opposite way. China National Radio, before the work was even finished, broke with convention and began serializing it; countless listeners sat by their radios every day. This book was not lifted up by the critics — it was loved into being by millions of ordinary people.

1991年，《平凡的世界》获得了第三届茅盾文学奖。

In 1991, Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World) won the third Mao Dun Literature Prize.

但那时候，路遥的身体已经不行了。他去领奖，还要借钱。一个得了中国最高文学奖的作家，还在为路费发愁。

But by then Lu Yao's health was already failing. He went to accept the prize on borrowed money. A writer who had won China's highest literary award was still worrying about his travel fare.

这份“迟到”，其实贯穿了路遥的一生。他写的是最普通的人，讲的是最朴素的道理，但在一个追逐“新潮”和“技巧”的文坛里，他显得“不够时髦”。真正给他颁奖的，不是评委，是读者。

This "lateness" in fact runs through Lu Yao's whole life. He wrote about the most ordinary people and told the plainest truths, yet in a literary world chasing "novelty" and "technique," he seemed "not fashionable enough." The ones who truly gave him his award were not the judges, but the readers.

六、1992年11月17日

VI. November 17, 1992

1992年8月，路遥因肝病住进医院。11月17日上午8时20分，他在西安去世，年仅四十二岁。

In August 1992, Lu Yao was hospitalized for liver disease. At 8:20 a.m. on November 17, he died in Xi'an, aged only forty-two.

他去世前一个月，妻子与他办理了离婚。他的女儿当时才五岁。

A month before he died, his wife divorced him. His daughter was only five years old at the time.

他留下的最后一部署名作品，是一篇随笔——《早晨从中午开始》。这个名字，几乎是他一生的写照：他的人生，总是从中午才开始，因为上午的时间，全都用来睡觉了——他常年写作到凌晨。

The last signed work he left behind was an essay — Zaochen cong Zhongwu Kaishi (Morning Begins at Noon). The title was almost a portrait of his life: his days always began at noon, because the whole morning was spent asleep — he habitually wrote until dawn.

他把自己的命，兑换成了一部书。

He traded his life for a book.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

路遥的一生，是“把自己烧掉去照亮一件事”的一生。

Lu Yao's life was one of "burning himself up to light a single thing."

今天，我们不太提倡这种活法。我们说“身体是革命的本钱”，说要“work-life balance”，说要“长期主义”。这些都是对的。

Today we do not much advocate this way of living. We say "your health is the capital of the revolution," we say "work-life balance," we say "long-termism." All of that is right.

但路遥提醒我们另一件事：有些东西，是必须用命去换的。 他明知道自己的身体在垮，还是选择把书写完。他不是不知道代价，他是算过账，然后决定认这个账。

But Lu Yao reminds us of something else: some things can only be bought with your life. He knew his body was collapsing, and still chose to finish the book. It was not that he did not know the cost — he had done the accounting, and then decided to accept the bill.

《平凡的世界》里，孙少平在高中的时候，只能吃最差的黑面馍，躲在角落里等别人打完了饭再去拿。那个躲在角落里的少年，就是路遥自己。

In Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World), Sun Shaoping, in high school, could eat only the worst black-flour buns, hiding in a corner to wait until others had finished getting their food before he went to take his. That boy hiding in the corner is Lu Yao himself.

他写这本书，不是为了让谁感动，而是为了告诉那些和他一样的、从底层挣扎出来的人：“其实我们每个人的生活都是一个世界，即使最平凡的人，也要为他生活的那个世界而奋斗。”

He wrote this book not to move anyone, but to tell those who, like him, had struggled up from the bottom: "In fact, each of our lives is a world, and even the most ordinary person must struggle for the world in which he lives."

2026年，我们依然需要这句话。因为大多数人的人生，本来就是平凡的。而路遥用一生证明了：平凡不是失败，放弃才是。

In 2026 we still need this sentence. Because most people's lives are, by nature, ordinary. And Lu Yao proved with his whole life that ordinariness is not failure — giving up is.

路遥的一生，几乎没有“享过福”。他七岁被送人，年轻时饿过肚子，中年时为写作透支了身体，得奖时还在为路费发愁，四十二岁就离开了。可他把这些苦，全都熬成了一部能让无数普通人从中获得力量的书。他自己活得那么难，写出来的却是“要奋斗”这三个字。 这就是他为什么值得被记在这本书里——他不是用成功证明了什么，他是用“不放弃”证明了什么。

Lu Yao's life was one of almost no "comfort." Given away at seven, hungry in his youth, overdrawn in body for the sake of writing in middle age, worrying about travel fare when he went to receive his prize, gone at forty-two. Yet he boiled all this hardship down into a book from which countless ordinary people could draw strength. He lived so hard, and what he wrote was three words: "Keep struggling." This is why he deserves to be remembered in this book — not because he proved something through success, but because he proved something through "not giving up."

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：路遥，1949年12月3日生于陕西省清涧县，1992年11月17日在西安去世，年42岁。为确证史实。

1. Basic biographical facts: Lu Yao, born December 3, 1949, in Qingjian County, Shaanxi Province; died in Xi'an on November 17, 1992, aged 42. Verified historical fact.

2. 过继：1957年前后过继给延川的伯父。为其本人回忆与多方记载所证实。其“躲在树后看父亲离开”的情节，出自他本人的叙述，属自述材料。

2. Adoption: given in adoption to his uncle in Yanchuan around 1957. Confirmed by his own recollections and by multiple records. The episode of "hiding behind a tree and watching his father leave" comes from his own account and is autobiographical material.

3. 教育经历：1973年入延安大学中文系；1976年到《延河》编辑部工作。为确证史实。

3. Education: entered the Department of Chinese at Yan'an University in 1973; joined the editorial office of Yan He (Yan River) in 1976. Verified historical fact.

4. 《惊心动魄的一幕》《人生》：分别获第一、第二届全国优秀中篇小说奖。为确证史实。

4. Jingxin Dongpo de Yimu (A Thrilling Scene) and Rensheng (Life): won the first and second National Awards for Outstanding Novella, respectively. Verified historical fact.

5. 《平凡的世界》创作：1983年前后开始，1988年5月25日完成，历时约六年。为确证史实。

5. Composition of Pingfan de Shijie (Ordinary World): begun around 1983, completed on May 25, 1988, a span of about six years. Verified historical fact.

6. 中央人民广播电台连播：在作品尚未全部完成时即开始连播。为确证史实。

6. Serialization by China National Radio: began before the work was fully completed. Verified historical fact.

7. 茅盾文学奖：1991年获第三届茅盾文学奖。为确证史实。

7. Mao Dun Literature Prize: won the third Mao Dun Literature Prize in 1991. Verified historical fact.

8. 去世：1992年8月入住医院，11月17日因肝硬化、消化道出血去世。为确证史实。（另有说法指其患有乙肝并发展为肝硬化腹水，各资料表述略有差异。）

8. Death: hospitalized in August 1992; died on November 17 of cirrhosis and gastrointestinal bleeding. Verified historical fact. (Other accounts hold that he had hepatitis B that progressed to cirrhotic ascites; the various sources differ slightly in their wording.)

9. 引文：“像牛一样劳动，像土地一样奉献”——为路遥的名言，广泛引用。“其实我们每个人的生活都是一个世界……”一句，出自其本人表述，被广泛引用于其作品的传播材料中。

9. Quotations: "Toil like an ox, give like the earth" — a well-known saying of Lu Yao's, widely quoted. The line "In fact, each of our lives is a world..." comes from his own words and is widely quoted in promotional materials for his work.

10. 关于“领奖借钱”：这是路遥生平中流传很广的一个细节，见于多种回忆材料，本文如实引用，但因属回忆性质，不作绝对化表述。

10. On "borrowing money to accept the prize": this is a widely circulated detail from Lu Yao's life, found in various recollections; this essay cites it faithfully, but because it is a reminiscence, it is not stated in absolute terms.
