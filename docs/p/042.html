孙膑：被挖去膝盖骨的“废人”

Sun Bin: The “Cripple” Who Had His Kneecaps Cut Out

——他被同门残害成残废，装疯求生，最后在马陵道赢回了一切

—Maimed by his fellow disciple, he feigned madness to survive, and in the end won everything back at Maling

2026年，“被同事捅刀子”“被同门背刺”，是职场里最让人心寒的一种伤害。

In 2026, “getting knifed in the back by a colleague,” “being stabbed in the back by one of your own”—these are among the most chilling injuries of the workplace.

两千三百年前，有一个人经历的，比“背刺”要狠得多。他和一个人一起学兵法，是同门师兄弟。那个师兄弟先一步做了官，却嫉妒他的才能，把他骗到自己手下，然后挖掉了他的膝盖骨、在他脸上刺了字——让他成了一个站不起来、还带着耻辱标记的“废人”。

Twenty-three hundred years ago, one man went through something far crueler than a “stab in the back.” He studied the art of war together with another man; they were fellow disciples. That fellow disciple became an official first, but envied his talent, lured him to serve under him, and then had his kneecaps cut out and his face tattooed—making him into a “wreck” who could not stand and bore a mark of disgrace.

他叫孙膑。他没有死在那一年。他装疯活了下来，逃到齐国，最后在战场上一举击败了那个害他的人。

His name was Sun Bin. He did not die that year. He feigned madness, survived, fled to the state of Qi, and in the end defeated the man who had ruined him on the field of battle.

他一生最了不起的地方不是“报仇”，而是：他在失去身体之后，没有失去大脑，也没有失去耐心。

The most remarkable thing about his life is not the “revenge” but this: after losing his body, he did not lose his mind, nor his patience.

一、一场同门之间的算计

I. A Plot Between Fellow Disciples

孙膑是战国时期的齐国人，据说他是《孙子兵法》作者孙武的后代（“孙膑”这个“膑”字，后来反倒成了他的代号——因为他被施了膑刑）。

Sun Bin was a man of the state of Qi during the Warring States period, and is said to have been a descendant of Sun Wu, the author of the Sunzi Bingfa (The Art of War). (The “bin” in “Sun Bin” ended up serving as his byname—because he had been subjected to the punishment called bin, the removal of the kneecaps.)

他年轻时和一个叫庞涓的人一起学习兵法。两人是同门，关系一度很近。

When he was young, he studied the art of war together with a man named Pang Juan. They were fellow disciples, and for a time they were close.

后来，庞涓先到魏国做了将军。他自认才能不如孙膑，心里不安，于是暗中派人把孙膑请到魏国来。

Later, Pang Juan went to the state of Wei first and became a general. He reckoned his own talent inferior to Sun Bin’s, felt uneasy, and so secretly sent men to invite Sun Bin to Wei.

孙膑到了魏国，等待他的不是共事，而是陷害。庞涓捏造罪名，对他施以重刑：膑刑（挖去膝盖骨，使他无法行走）和黥刑（在脸上刺字）。

When Sun Bin arrived in Wei, what awaited him was not partnership but a frame-up. Pang Juan fabricated a charge and had him subjected to severe punishment: the bin punishment (removal of the kneecaps, so that he could not walk) and the qing punishment (tattooing the face).

一个被挖去膝盖的人，一个脸上带着囚徒印记的人，在当时的世道里，已经没有资格再谈什么功业了。

A man whose kneecaps had been cut out, a man branded on the face with a convict’s mark—in the world of that time, he no longer had any standing to speak of achievement.

二、装疯：为了活下来

II. Feigning Madness: In Order to Survive

被打成“罪人”之后，孙膑面前只剩下两条路：死，或者以一个废人的身份苟活。

Once he had been branded a “criminal,” only two roads lay before Sun Bin: death, or clinging to life as a cripple.

他选择了第三条：装疯。

He chose a third: feigning madness.

据记载，他装疯卖傻，甚至以吃猪食、卧于猪圈的方式来让人相信他真的疯了。庞涓一度派人试探，见他疯癫，才放松了警惕。

According to the records, he played the fool, going so far as to eat pig slop and lie down in a pigsty to convince people he had truly gone mad. Pang Juan sent men to test him, and only when they saw him raving did he relax his guard.

后来，齐国的使者出使魏国，孙膑设法见到他，用自己的才学打动了对方。齐国使者把他藏在车里，偷偷接回了齐国。

Later, an envoy of Qi came to Wei on a mission. Sun Bin managed to meet him and won him over with his learning. The Qi envoy hid him in his carriage and smuggled him back to Qi.

他活了下来。这本身就是最大的胜利——因为只要他还活着，庞涓就永远不可能安心。

He survived. That in itself was the greatest victory—for as long as he lived, Pang Juan could never rest easy.

三、在齐国，他从“工具”变成“军师”

III. In Qi, from “Tool” to “Military Adviser”

到了齐国，孙膑得到了齐国将军田忌的礼遇，做了他的门客。著名的“田忌赛马”故事，就出自这一时期。

Arriving in Qi, Sun Bin was received with courtesy by the Qi general Tian Ji and became his retainer. The famous story of “Tian Ji’s horse race” comes from this period.

按照记载，田忌和齐王赛马，马分三等。孙膑出的主意是：用你的下等马对齐王的上等马（输），用你的上等马对齐王的中等马（赢），用你的中等马对齐王的下等马（赢）——两胜一负，总比分赢了。

According to the account, Tian Ji raced horses against the King of Qi, with the horses divided into three grades. Sun Bin’s plan was this: match your inferior horses against the king’s superior horses (lose), your superior horses against the king’s middling horses (win), and your middling horses against the king’s inferior horses (win)—two wins to one loss, winning on the overall score.

这个故事表面上是“小聪明”，实则是一个极好的策略示范：在总体实力相当的情况下，胜负取决于资源配置的方式。孙膑的价值，不在于力气，而在于“怎么排兵布阵”。

On the surface the story is a bit of “cleverness,” but in fact it is an excellent demonstration of strategy: when overall strength is about equal, victory depends on how resources are allocated. Sun Bin’s value lay not in brute strength but in “how to array the troops and set the formations.”

一个站不起来的人，恰恰最擅长“排兵布阵”——因为他的战场从来不在马背上，而在脑子里。

A man who could not stand was precisely the one best at “arraying the troops”—because his battlefield was never on horseback but in his head.

四、桂陵与马陵：两次致命的反击

IV. Guiling and Maling: Two Deadly Counterstrikes

孙膑真正的高光，是两场战役。

Sun Bin’s real glory came in two battles.

第一场是桂陵之战（约公元前354年）。魏国围攻赵国都城邯郸，赵国向齐国求救。孙膑没有直接去邯郸硬碰硬，而是主张“围魏救赵”——直接打魏国的都城大梁，逼魏军回援；再在魏军回师的必经之路上，等他们。邯郸之围解了，魏军也吃了亏。

The first was the Battle of Guiling (c. 354 BCE). Wei laid siege to Handan, the capital of Zhao, and Zhao appealed to Qi for help. Rather than march straight to Handan and clash head-on, Sun Bin advocated “besieging Wei to rescue Zhao”—striking directly at Daliang, the capital of Wei, to force the Wei army to turn back, and then waiting for them on the road they would have to take. The siege of Handan was lifted, and the Wei army took a beating.

第二场是马陵之战（约公元前341年）。这一次，魏国攻韩国，韩国向齐求救。孙膑再次用“围魏”的办法把魏军调回来。这一次，他用了一个更精妙的心理战：

The second was the Battle of Maling (c. 341 BCE). This time Wei attacked Han, and Han appealed to Qi. Sun Bin again used the “besiege Wei” stratagem to draw the Wei army back. This time he employed an even more ingenious piece of psychological warfare:

他命令齐军进入魏地后，第一天挖十万人做饭的灶，第二天挖五万人的灶，第三天挖三万人的灶——制造出“齐军在大量逃亡、人数锐减”的假象。庞涓一路追来，看到灶数一天比一天少，大喜，认为齐军果然怯战溃散，于是丢下步兵，只率轻兵兼程追击。

He ordered the Qi army, after entering Wei territory, to dig cooking stoves for a hundred thousand men on the first day, for fifty thousand on the second, and for thirty thousand on the third—creating the illusion that the Qi army was deserting en masse and dwindling fast. Chasing them all the way, Pang Juan saw the number of stoves shrink day by day and was overjoyed, concluding that the Qi troops had indeed lost their nerve and scattered; so he left his infantry behind and pursued at full speed with only light troops.

孙膑在马陵道这个狭窄的地方，设下了埋伏。他命人把路边一棵大树的树皮削去，在上面写下字（一说“庞涓死于此树之下”），并约定：看见树下有火光，就万箭齐发。

At the narrow place known as the Maling road, Sun Bin laid an ambush. He had the bark stripped from a large tree by the roadside and words written on it (one version reads, “Pang Juan dies beneath this tree”), and gave the order: when firelight was seen under the tree, ten thousand crossbows were to loose at once.

夜里，庞涓追到树下，举火照看树上的字——就在那一刻，齐军万弩齐发。魏军大乱。庞涓自知败局已定，自刎而死。

At night, Pang Juan came up to the tree and raised a torch to read the writing on it—and at that very moment the Qi army loosed ten thousand crossbows. The Wei army fell into chaos. Knowing the battle was lost, Pang Juan cut his own throat and died.

十三年后，那个被挖去膝盖骨的人，赢回了全部。

Thirteen years later, the man whose kneecaps had been cut out won everything back.

五、他还写下了一部兵书

V. He Also Wrote a Book on War

孙膑不只是会打仗。他还著有兵书。

Sun Bin was not only good at fighting. He also wrote a book on war.

1972年，山东临沂银雀山的一座汉墓中出土了大量竹简，其中就有《孙膑兵法》。这批竹简解决了一个大问题：此前，学界长期对“孙膑是否另有兵书、还是《孙子兵法》被误记在他名下”存在争论。竹简的出土，证实了孙武的《孙子兵法》与孙膑的兵书，确实是两部不同的著作。

In 1972, a large number of bamboo slips were unearthed from a Han dynasty tomb at Yinqueshan in Linyi, Shandong, among them the Sun Bin Bingfa (Sun Bin’s Art of War). These slips settled a major question: previously, scholars had long debated whether Sun Bin had a separate book on war, or whether the Sunzi Bingfa had simply been misattributed to him. The discovery of the slips confirmed that Sun Wu’s Sunzi Bingfa and Sun Bin’s book on war were indeed two different works.

这意味着：那个被人挖掉膝盖骨的人，不仅活下来了、报了仇，还留下了自己的军事思想，一直传到今天。

This means that the man whose kneecaps were cut out not only survived and took his revenge, but also left behind his own military thought, which has come down to the present day.

关于他的结局，《史记》没有明确交代，只说他后来“名显天下”。有一说他最后选择了归隐。

As for his end, the Shiji does not say clearly, noting only that he later “made his name known throughout the world.” One account holds that he chose in the end to retire from the world.

六、致2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

孙膑的故事，是“被摧毁”与“不认命”之间的一场漫长角力。

Sun Bin’s story is a long wrestling match between “being destroyed” and “refusing to accept one’s fate.”

第一，身体被毁了，脑子还在；脑子还在，就还有仗可打。庞涓以为挖掉膝盖骨就能把他变成废人——他算了身体，却没算人心和智力。孙膑无法久站、无法骑马，但他能设计整个战场。你的某个“部位”被拿走了，不等于你整个人被拿走了。

First, the body may be ruined, but the mind remains; and while the mind remains, there are still battles to be fought. Pang Juan thought that cutting out his kneecaps would turn him into a wreck—he had reckoned the body but not the heart and the intelligence. Sun Bin could not stand for long or ride a horse, but he could design an entire battlefield. Because some “part” of you has been taken does not mean the whole of you has been taken.

第二，“装疯”不是懦弱，是活下去的策略。在绝对劣势下，硬碰硬是匹夫之勇；暂时示弱、保存自己，才等得到反击的那一天。真正的强者，懂得什么时候该忍。

Second, “feigning madness” is not cowardice; it is a strategy for survival. Facing overwhelming disadvantage, hitting back head-on is the courage of a fool; only by showing weakness for a time and preserving yourself can you live to see the day of counterstrike. The truly strong know when they must endure.

第三，报仇最好的方式，是活得更好、赢得更彻底。孙膑没有去暗杀庞涓，他是在战场上、光明正大地把对方击败。他没有把自己变成庞涓那样的人——他用对方的规则赢不了的方式，赢了对方。

Third, the best way to take revenge is to live better and win more completely. Sun Bin did not go assassinate Pang Juan; he defeated him openly, on the battlefield. He did not turn himself into the kind of man Pang Juan was—he beat his opponent by means that his opponent’s own rules could not defeat.

一个站不起来的人，最后让整个魏国的军队，倒在了马陵道上。

A man who could not stand brought the whole army of Wei down on the Maling road.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

7. “孙膑与庞涓是同学、同从鬼谷子学习”——此为流传的通行说法，但史料依据较弱：《史记》仅言两人有过交往（庞涓“自以为能不及孙膑”，召之至魏），并未明确记载他们是同门或师从鬼谷子；“同门”情节多见于后世传说与文学演绎。文中已用“据说”“一同学习兵法”的谨慎表述。

7. “That Sun Bin and Pang Juan were classmates who both studied under Guiguzi”—this is the widely circulated common account, but its source basis is weak: the Shiji says only that the two men had dealings (Pang Juan, “considering his own ability inferior to Sun Bin,” summoned him to Wei), and does not state clearly that they were fellow disciples or that they studied under Guiguzi; the “fellow disciples” episode appears mostly in later legend and literary elaboration. The text uses the cautious phrasing “is said to have” and “studied the art of war together.”

8. “他装疯吃猪食”——见于《史记》等早期记载（孙膑“佯狂”以避祸），但具体细节（吃猪食、卧猪圈等）在不同文献与后世叙述中有所不同，文中按“装疯卖傻”的概括处理，未把细节当作确凿事实。

8. “That he feigned madness and ate pig slop”—this appears in the Shiji and other early records (Sun Bin “feigned madness” to avoid disaster), but the specific details (eating pig slop, lying in a pigsty, and so on) differ among different documents and later narratives; the text handles it with the summary phrasing “played the fool” and does not treat the details as established fact.

9. “马陵之战树上刻'庞涓死于此树之下'”——此事见于《史记》等记载（一说为“庞涓死此树下”），但具体字句在不同版本中有差异，文中已注“一说”。

9. “That at the Battle of Maling the words ‘Pang Juan dies beneath this tree’ were carved on a tree”—this appears in the Shiji and other records (one version reads “Pang Juan dies under this tree”), but the exact wording varies among different versions; the text notes “one version reads.”

10. “孙膑最后归隐/结局如何”——史料没有明确交代其结局，所谓“归隐”属后人推测或传说，文中已如实说明“《史记》没有明确交代”。

10. “That Sun Bin retired from the world in the end, and how he ended his days”—the historical sources do not clearly state his end; the so-called “retirement” is later conjecture or legend, and the text states truthfully that “the Shiji does not say clearly.”

11. “围魏救赵”与“田忌赛马”——前者为《史记》所载的真实战略；后者见于《史记》等记载，但带有明显的“故事”色彩（细节完整、对话生动），宜视为体现其谋略思想的故事，而非逐字可考的事件纪实。

11. “Besieging Wei to rescue Zhao” and “Tian Ji’s horse race”—the former is a genuine strategy recorded in the Shiji; the latter appears in the Shiji and other records but has a clear “story” quality (complete details, vivid dialogue) and is best regarded as a story illustrating his strategic thinking rather than a word-for-word verifiable record of events.
