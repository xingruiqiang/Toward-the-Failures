曹雪芹：举家食粥的“败落公子”

Cao Xueqin: The "Fallen Noble" Whose Whole Household Ate Gruel

——他从锦衣玉食跌到“举家食粥”，用十年写下一部没写完的书

From silken robes and fine fare down to a family that ate only gruel, he spent ten years writing a book he would never finish

← 返回主页 Home第二辑　屡败屡战的坚韧行者37 / 100约 2542 字 · 2542 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，“阶层下滑”是很多人心里的隐忧。父辈挣下的家底，能不能托住自己？如果不能，人生是不是就完了？

In 2026, "downward mobility" is a quiet dread in many people's minds. Can the fortune our parents built still hold us up? And if it cannot—is life over?

三百年前，有一个人，经历了中国历史上最著名的一次“家道中落”。他从小在富贵乡里长大，却在少年时亲眼看着家族被抄、房屋被没收、一切归零。他后半生住在北京西郊，穷到“举家食粥酒常赊”——一家人喝粥度日，喝酒常常要赊账。

Three hundred years ago, one man lived through the most famous "decline of a great house" in Chinese history. Raised amid wealth and luxury, he watched as a boy of his family's property being seized, its houses confiscated, everything reduced to nothing. He spent the second half of his life in the western suburbs of Beijing, so poor that "the whole household ate gruel, and the wine was always bought on credit"—the family lived on congee, and the drinking money was routinely owed.

可就是在这最落魄的十年里，他写下了《红楼梦》。而且，他到死都没能写完。

And yet it was in those most wretched ten years that he wrote Honglou Meng (Dream of the Red Chamber). And to the end, he never finished it.

他的失败是真实的、彻底的；他从失败里长出来的东西，是中国文学的最高峰。

His failure was real and complete; what he grew out of that failure is the supreme peak of Chinese literature.

一、从“锦衣纨袴”到“举家食粥”

I. From "Silken Robes and Fine Silks" to "the Whole Household Ate Gruel"

曹雪芹的生年，学界至今有争议，通常定在1715年前后。他出身于一个极其显赫的家族：他的曾祖、祖父、父辈，几代人担任“江宁织造”——这是一个直接为皇室管理江南丝绸采办的肥缺，也是皇帝安插在江南的心腹要职。

The year of Cao Xueqin's birth remains disputed among scholars; it is usually placed around 1715. He came from an extraordinarily prominent family: his great-grandfather, grandfather, and father—generations of them—held the post of "Commissioner of the Jiangning Imperial Silk Manufactory," a lucrative office that managed the court's silk procurement in the Jiangnan region and served as a key position through which the emperor kept trusted eyes on the south.

康熙皇帝六次南巡，其中四次住在曹家。可以想象那种场面：接驾、张灯、花银子如流水。曹雪芹童年过的，就是这样的日子。

The Kangxi Emperor made six tours south, and on four of them he stayed with the Cao family. One can imagine the spectacle: receiving the imperial procession, lanterns lit everywhere, silver spent like water. This was the daily life of Cao Xueqin's childhood.

然后在雍正六年（1728年），一切结束了。曹家被革职、抄家，家产充公。一个十来岁的孩子，从云端跌到了地面。

Then, in the sixth year of the Yongzheng reign (1728), it all ended. The family was stripped of its office, its property seized, its assets confiscated. A boy of ten or so fell from the clouds to the ground.

此后，曹家迁回北京，境况一天不如一天。到曹雪芹成年之后，他已经是一个名副其实的破落户。他晚年住在北京西郊，靠卖画、朋友接济过活。

Afterward the Caos moved back to Beijing, their circumstances growing worse by the day. By the time Cao Xueqin reached adulthood, he was a ruined scion in the fullest sense. In his later years he lived in the western suburbs of Beijing, scraping by on selling paintings and the help of friends.

他的朋友敦诚写过一句诗，形容他的生活：“举家食粥酒常赊”。另一个朋友敦敏的诗里也提到，他常到酒肆里赊账喝酒。这就是他后半生的日常。

His friend Duncheng wrote a line of verse describing his life: "The whole household ate gruel, and the wine was always bought on credit." Another friend, Dunmin, mentioned in a poem that he often drank on credit at the tavern. Such was the daily reality of his later years.

二、“披阅十载，增删五次”

II. "Ten Years of Reading, Five Revisions"

关于《红楼梦》的创作，最可靠的一句话，就写在书里：

The most reliable statement about the writing of Honglou Meng is written into the book itself:

后因曹雪芹于悼红轩中披阅十载，增删五次。

Later, Cao Xueqin, in the Mourning-the-Red Studio, read it over for ten years and revised it five times.

十年，五次大改。他从富贵写到贫寒，把整个家族的记忆、他看到过的人、他经历过的繁华与崩塌，都熔进了这部书里。

Ten years, five major revisions. He wrote from wealth down to poverty, fusing into this one book the memory of an entire family, the people he had seen, the splendor and the collapse he had lived through.

书里写的是什么？表面看，是一个贵族大家庭的兴衰：大观园里的少男少女，诗酒风流、儿女情长；底下看，是这个家族内部一点一点烂掉的过程——贪腐、倾轧、虚伪、以及最终无可挽回的败落。

What is the book about? On the surface, the rise and fall of an aristocratic clan: the young men and women of the Grand View Garden, their poetry, wine, and romances. Beneath the surface, it is the slow rot within the family—greed, intrigue, hypocrisy, and at last an irreversible decline.

非常耐人寻味的一点是：他把“败落”写得那么好，正因为他亲历过它。一个从没富过的人，写不出大观园里的排场；一个从没穷过的人，写不出那种“树倒猢狲散”之后的凉。曹雪芹两样都尝过。

What is most telling is this: he wrote "decline" so well precisely because he had lived it. A man who had never been rich could not have written the grandeur of the Grand View Garden; a man who had never been poor could not have written the chill that follows when "the tree falls and the monkeys scatter." Cao Xueqin had tasted both.

三、一部没写完的书

III. A Book Left Unfinished

《红楼梦》最让人揪心的地方在于：它没写完。

The most heartbreaking thing about Honglou Meng is that it was never finished.

今天通行的版本是120回，前80回一般认为是曹雪芹所作，后40回一般认为由高鹗等人整理续补。也就是说，我们读到的那个“结局”，很可能不是他本人想给出的结局。

The standard edition today runs to 120 chapters. The first 80 are generally attributed to Cao Xueqin; the last 40 are generally thought to have been compiled and completed by Gao E and others. In other words, the "ending" we read is very likely not the ending he himself intended to give.

为什么没写完？

Why was it left unfinished?

根据《红楼梦》早期抄本“脂砚斋”的批语，有一条写得极沉痛：

Among the annotations in "Zhiyanzhai," the early manuscript commentary on Honglou Meng, one note is written with profound grief:

壬午除夕，书未成，芹为泪尽而逝。

On the eve of the renwu New Year, the book unfinished, Qin died with his tears exhausted.

意思是：（某年）除夕夜，书还没完成，曹雪芹就“泪尽”而死了。也就是说，他是抱着没写完的书稿走的。他连书里那个他亲手创造的世界的结局，都没能亲手交出来。

It means that on the eve of the New Year, the book still incomplete, Cao Xueqin died "his tears run dry." He departed with the unfinished manuscript in his arms. He could not hand over, with his own hands, even the fate of the world he had created.

关于他的卒年，红学界一直有三说：壬午说（1763年2月12日除夕）、癸未说（1764年2月1日除夕）、甲申说（1764年春）。无论哪一说，他都死在四十多岁——新婚不久的遗孀，就这样留了下来。

As to the year of his death, the field of Redology has long held three theories: the renwu theory (the New Year's eve of February 12, 1763), the guiwei theory (the New Year's eve of February 1, 1764), and the jiashen theory (the spring of 1764). By any account, he died in his forties—leaving behind a widow married only a short while.

四、为什么他可以被写进“失败者”

IV. Why He Belongs Among "The Failures"

按照最世俗的标准，曹雪芹的一生是一场彻底的失败：家业败了、身份没了、中年潦倒、无官无职、书没写完、儿子（据部分记载）早夭、自己四十几岁就死了。

By the most worldly standards, Cao Xueqin's life was an utter failure: the family fortune gone, his status lost, destitute in middle age, holding no office, the book unfinished, a son (according to some accounts) dead young, and himself dead in his forties.

但他做的事，恰恰是：把这场失败，变成了人类共有的财富。

But what he did was precisely this: he turned that failure into a treasure shared by all humanity.

《红楼梦》后来成为中国古典小说的巅峰，被翻译成几十种语言，在世界范围内被反复研究。而它最动人的底色，不是爱情，是那种“眼看它起高楼、眼看它楼塌了”的荒凉感——那份荒凉，就是曹雪芹自己的人生。

Honglou Meng went on to become the summit of the classical Chinese novel, translated into dozens of languages and studied again and again around the world. And its most moving undertone is not love but that sense of desolation—"watching them raise the tall mansion, watching the mansion fall"—and that desolation was Cao Xueqin's own life.

他没有从废墟里爬出来重建一个像样的生活，他只是坐在废墟上，把它写了下来。而这一写，就写成了不朽。

He did not climb out of the ruins to rebuild a decent life. He simply sat down among the ruins and wrote them down. And in the writing, they became immortal.

五、致2026年的我们

V. To Us in 2026

曹雪芹的故事，是给所有“失去过”的人的一份慰藉。

Cao Xueqin's story is a consolation for everyone who has ever lost something.

第一，失去的东西，未必只能成为伤口，它也可以成为理解力。如果曹家没有倒，曹雪芹大概会是一个风雅的公子，写些应酬的诗，过完平凡的一生。恰恰是那场抄家，让他看懂了“繁华”和“幻灭”这两个词。他失去的是家底，得到的是眼光。

First, what is lost does not have to become only a wound; it can also become understanding. Had the Cao family not fallen, Cao Xueqin would likely have been an elegant young gentleman, writing the occasional verse and living out an ordinary life. It was precisely that confiscation that let him truly understand the two words "splendor" and "disillusionment." What he lost was his fortune; what he gained was his vision.

第二，不是所有重要的东西，都要在你活着的时候完成。他没写完《红楼梦》，可他留下的80回，已经足够撑起一个学科（红学）、足够被读三百年。有时候，完成不是你的任务；留下一个足够好的开头，是。

Second, not everything important has to be finished within your lifetime. He never finished Honglou Meng, yet the 80 chapters he left behind were enough to sustain an entire discipline (Redology) and enough to be read for three hundred years. Sometimes finishing is not your task; leaving behind a beginning good enough is.

所以，如果你此刻正经历某种失去——失去工作、失去位置、失去曾经拥有的一切——请记住北京西郊那间喝粥的房子。那个“败落公子”，在人生最冷的十年里，写下了中国最温暖也最悲凉的一部书。

So if you are going through some kind of loss right now—of a job, of a position, of everything you once had—remember that gruel-eating house in the western suburbs of Beijing. That "fallen noble," in the coldest ten years of his life, wrote the warmest and most sorrowful book in China.

他的一生在往下掉，他的笔在往上走。

His life was falling; his pen was rising.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

8. “曹雪芹的卒年”——红学界至今无定论，壬午、癸未、甲申三说并存。文中如实并列三说，未作单一判断。

8. "The year of Cao Xueqin's death"—the field of Redology has reached no consensus to this day; the three theories of renwu, guiwei, and jiashen all stand. The text sets them side by side honestly, without making a single judgment.

9. “曹雪芹就是贾宝玉”——需谨慎。《红楼梦》具有强烈的自传性色彩（家族经历、生活方式多有对应），但不能简单等同于作者本人的自传；书中人物是艺术创造。文中仅说明其“亲历过繁华与败落”，未将其与书中人物画等号。

9. "Cao Xueqin is Jia Baoyu"—caution is needed here. Honglou Meng carries a strong autobiographical coloring (with many correspondences in family experience and way of life), but it cannot be simply equated with the author's own autobiography; the characters in the book are artistic creations. The text states only that he "lived through splendor and decline," and does not equate him with any character in the book.

10. “后40回一定不是曹雪芹写的”——这是主流意见（一般认为前80回为曹作、后40回为高鹗等续补），但关于后40回的作者与来源，学界仍有不同讨论。文中采用“一般认为”的表述。

10. "The last 40 chapters were certainly not written by Cao Xueqin"—this is the mainstream view (the first 80 chapters are generally taken as Cao's, the last 40 as completed by Gao E and others), but scholars still disagree about the authorship and origin of the last 40 chapters. The text uses the phrasing "generally thought."

11. “他家被抄家是因为政治斗争”——曹家被抄的背景复杂（亏空、政治环境变化、与皇室的微妙关系等），不宜简化为单一原因；文中仅记述“被革职抄家”这一结果。

11. "His family was confiscated because of political struggle"—the background to the Cao family's confiscation was complex (financial deficits, shifts in the political climate, delicate relations with the imperial house, and so on), and it should not be reduced to a single cause; the text records only the outcome, that the family "was stripped of office and confiscated."

12. “敦诚诗中的'举家食粥酒常赊'”——为友人诗句，是了解其晚年生活状况的重要旁证，但诗歌本身带有文学性，不宜逐字当作精确的账目记录；文中按“形容”处理。

12. "'The whole household ate gruel, and the wine was always bought on credit' in Duncheng's poem"—this is a line of verse by a friend and an important piece of corroborating evidence for understanding his later life, but poetry is literary in nature and should not be taken word for word as a precise account book; the text treats it as a "description."
