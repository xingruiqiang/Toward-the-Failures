王传福：一个“造电池的”要去造车

Wang Chuanfu: A "Battery Maker" Who Decided to Build Cars

——被人笑“找死”的那一年，他29岁刚辞职、揣着250万

—The Year People Mocked Him for "Courting Death," He Was 29, Just Resigned, and Carrying 2.5 Million Yuan

← 返回主页 Home第三辑　在废墟上重建的人60 / 100约 3094 字 · 3094 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，很多人的职业困境是“跨界”：在一个行业干了多年，想换个方向，却总有人告诉你“你不专业”“你不行”“这行水太深”。

In 2026, the career dilemma for many people is "crossing over": after years in one industry, they want to change direction, but someone always tells them "you're not a professional," "you can't do it," "this field runs too deep."

有一个人，本来是中国最顶尖的电池专家，是体制内最年轻的处级干部之一。他偏偏辞了职，借了250万去创业做电池；做了几年，把电池做到了世界前列，又突然宣布要去造汽车。

There was a man who was already one of China's top battery experts, one of the youngest section-level officials in the state system. He went and resigned anyway, borrowed 2.5 million yuan to start a battery business; after a few years he had brought his batteries to the front ranks of the world, and then suddenly announced he was going to build automobiles.

当时市场上的反应是：一个造电池的去造车，这是找死。

The market's reaction at the time: a battery maker going off to build cars—that's courting death.

他叫王传福。后来，他造的电动车，让巴菲特都掏了钱。

His name was Wang Chuanfu. Later, the electric cars he built got even Warren Buffett to open his wallet.

一、一个“没有退路”的少年

I. A Boy with "No Way Back"

1966年，王传福出生在安徽无为的一个农家。家里兄弟姐妹多，日子过得紧。

Wang Chuanfu was born in 1966 to a farming family in Wuwei, Anhui. There were many brothers and sisters, and the family lived tight.

13岁那年，父亲去世；15岁那年，母亲也走了。少年王传福几乎是在没有父母的境况下长大的。他很早就明白了一件事：没有人可以依靠，只能靠读书和做事改变命运。

When he was thirteen his father died; at fifteen his mother passed away too. The young Wang Chuanfu grew up almost entirely without parents. He understood one thing very early: there was no one to lean on—only study and work could change his fate.

1987年，他从中南工业大学（今中南大学）冶金物理化学专业毕业；1990年，又在北京有色金属研究总院拿到硕士学位。毕业后他留院工作，因为业务能力突出，26岁就被破格提拔为研究院里最年轻的处级干部之一。

In 1987 he graduated from Central South University of Technology (today's Central South University) in metallurgical physical chemistry; in 1990 he earned a master's degree at the Beijing General Research Institute for Nonferrous Metals. After graduating he stayed on there, and because his professional ability stood out, at twenty-six he was unusually promoted to be one of the youngest section-level officials at the institute.

这是一条标准的“体制内上升通道”：稳定、体面、前途可见。

This was a standard "advancement track within the system": stable, respectable, with a visible future.

1995年，29岁的王传福做了一个让所有人意外的决定：辞职，创业。

In 1995, at twenty-nine, Wang Chuanfu made a decision that surprised everyone: he resigned and started his own business.

二、250万和一条“不体面”的路

II. 2.5 Million Yuan and an "Undignified" Path

他看中的是充电电池。

What he set his sights on was rechargeable batteries.

当时，日本企业垄断了全球的电池市场，靠的是昂贵的自动化生产线。中国想进入这个行业，缺资金、缺设备，几乎没有胜算。

At the time, Japanese companies monopolized the global battery market, relying on expensive automated production lines. For China to enter the industry meant lacking capital, lacking equipment, with almost no chance of winning.

王传福的做法很“土”，但很有效：他用人工替代机器。 把一条本该由自动化设备完成的产线，拆解成可以由工人手工操作的工序，用大量廉价而熟练的劳动力，去替代昂贵的设备投资。

Wang Chuanfu's approach was decidedly "backward," but effective: he substituted people for machines. He broke down a production line that should have been handled by automated equipment into steps that workers could perform by hand, using a large force of cheap, skilled labor to replace expensive investment in equipment.

1995年，他借了250万元，在深圳创立了比亚迪。

In 1995 he borrowed 2.5 million yuan and founded BYD in Shenzhen.

这个模式在当时被很多人看不起——“靠人海战术做高科技”。但它跑通了：比亚迪的电池成本远低于日本对手，很快抢下了市场。2002年，比亚迪在香港上市。到2000年代中期，比亚迪已经成为全球主要的充电电池制造商之一，被称作“电池大王”。

Many people looked down on this model at the time—"doing high tech by sheer mass of manpower." But it worked: BYD's batteries cost far less than its Japanese rivals', and it quickly seized the market. In 2002 BYD listed in Hong Kong. By the mid-2000s BYD had become one of the world's major makers of rechargeable batteries, known as the "Battery King."

三、2003年：所有人都说“找死”

III. 2003: Everyone Said "Courting Death"

2003年1月，王传福宣布了一件震惊市场的事：比亚迪收购西安秦川汽车77%的股权，正式进军汽车行业。

In January 2003, Wang Chuanfu announced something that shocked the market: BYD was acquiring a 77 percent stake in Xi'an Qinchuan Automobile, formally entering the auto industry.

消息一出，市场用脚投票——比亚迪的股价大跌，很多投资者公开表示反对。理由很简单：电池和汽车是两个完全不同的行业。 汽车是资金、技术、供应链极度密集的行业，一家做电池的公司凭什么去造车？

The moment the news broke, the market voted with its feet—BYD's share price plunged, and many investors publicly objected. The reason was simple: batteries and cars are two completely different industries. Cars are an industry of extreme capital, technology, and supply-chain intensity—what business did a battery company have building cars?

当时的舆论，几乎一边倒地认为这是“不自量力”。

Public opinion at the time was almost unanimously that this was "overreaching."

王传福没有回头。他的逻辑是：比亚迪的核心能力是电池和制造，而未来汽车的方向，一定是电动化。他要做的不是“再做一个传统车厂”，而是把电池优势带进汽车——这是一次以技术为原点的跨界，而不是一次盲目扩张。

Wang Chuanfu did not turn back. His logic was this: BYD's core strengths were batteries and manufacturing, and the future of cars was certain to be electrification. What he wanted to do was not "build another traditional carmaker" but bring battery advantages into cars—a crossover that started from technology, not a blind expansion.

后来的事情证明了这条路的价值。2008年，巴菲特旗下的公司入股比亚迪，成为当时资本市场最受关注的投资之一。此后，比亚迪在新能源汽车领域一路成长为全球领先的企业。

What followed proved the value of this path. In 2008, a company under Warren Buffett took a stake in BYD, becoming one of the most closely watched investments in the capital markets at the time. After that, BYD grew into a global leader in new-energy vehicles.

四、“垂直整合”和“技术为王”

IV. "Vertical Integration" and "Technology Above All"

王传福身上有一个很鲜明的特点：他极度相信“自己做”。

One striking trait of Wang Chuanfu: he believes fiercely in "doing it ourselves."

比亚迪是业内著名的“垂直整合”企业——电池自己做，电机自己做，芯片自己做，甚至连生产设备也自己做。这种模式在管理学教科书里常被视为“不够专业”，但在供应链随时可能被卡脖子的年代，它救过比亚迪的命。

BYD is famous in the industry as a "vertically integrated" company—it makes its own batteries, its own motors, its own chips, even its own production equipment. This model is often considered "not specialized enough" in management textbooks, but in an era when the supply chain could be choked off at any moment, it saved BYD's life.

他还有一句广为人知的话，大意是：一个企业的核心竞争力，就是技术。 他不爱讲概念、不爱讲生态、不爱讲风口，而是一次次强调“把技术做出来”。

He has another widely known line, to the effect that: a company's core competitiveness is technology. He doesn't like talking about concepts, or ecosystems, or the next big wave—again and again he stresses "actually making the technology work."

这也是他这个人最“不讨喜”的地方：他不擅长讲故事，也不太会和舆论打交道。他像一个工程师，只对“能不能做出来”这件事感兴趣。

This is also the least "likable" thing about him: he is not good at telling stories, and not much good at dealing with public opinion. He is like an engineer, interested only in whether something can actually be made.

五、2008年，一个外国老人的押注

V. 2008: An Old Foreign Man's Bet

2008年，巴菲特旗下的公司入股比亚迪。这笔投资在当时颇为引人注目——一个以价值投资著称的美国老人，把目光投向了一家中国的电池和汽车企业。

In 2008, a company under Warren Buffett took a stake in BYD. The investment drew considerable attention at the time—an old American man famous for value investing had turned his gaze toward a Chinese battery and automobile company.

巴菲特的老搭档芒格，曾多次公开表达对比亚迪和王传福的欣赏。市场普遍认为，这笔投资是对“电池+电动车”这条技术路径的一次重要背书。

Buffett's old partner Charlie Munger repeatedly expressed his admiration for BYD and Wang Chuanfu in public. The market widely regarded the investment as an important endorsement of the "battery plus electric vehicle" technological path.

而在那前后，比亚迪也确实开始把重心向新能源汽车倾斜。当年为了“活下去”而练出来的电池功夫，终于在电动车上变成了真正的优势——不是因为风口来了他才转，而是他早就在那儿等着风口。

And around that time, BYD did indeed begin tilting its focus toward new-energy vehicles. The battery skills honed back then just to "stay alive" finally became a real advantage in electric cars—not because he turned when the wave came, but because he had long been standing there waiting for it.

六、“技术”到底值不值得信

VI. Is "Technology" Really Worth Believing In

王传福有一句话被反复引用，大意是：一个企业的核心竞争力，就是技术。

One line of Wang Chuanfu's gets quoted again and again, to the effect that: a company's core competitiveness is technology.

他也因此常常被批评“不够互联网”。在一个讲用户、讲体验、讲流量的时代，他坚持讲专利、产线、材料、工艺，显得有点“土”。

Because of this he is often criticized as "not internet enough." In an era that talks about users, experience, and traffic, he insists on talking about patents, production lines, materials, and processes—which makes him seem a bit "backward."

但这种“土”，其实是一个赌注：他赌中国制造的底层能力，终有一天会比任何模式创新都更结实。 这个赌注对不对，市场已经给出了部分答案。至少有一点是清楚的——在他被笑“找死”的那一年，他做的选择不是冲动，而是基于专业判断的长期主义。

But this "backwardness" is really a bet: he is betting that the underlying capability of Chinese manufacturing will one day prove more solid than any business-model innovation. Whether the bet is right—the market has already given a partial answer. At least one thing is clear: in the year he was mocked for "courting death," his choice was not impulse but long-termism grounded in professional judgment.

七、致2026：关于“跨界”这件事

VII. To 2026: On the Matter of "Crossing Over"

2026年，很多人想转行、想跨界，却被“你不专业”四个字劝退。

In 2026, many people want to change careers, want to cross over, only to be talked out of it by four words: "you're not a professional."

王传福的故事提供了一个重要的区分：

Wang Chuanfu's story offers an important distinction:

真正危险的跨界，是“抛下自己的一切，去追一个热点”；而真正有效的跨界，是“带着自己最强的能力，去一个新领域重新组合”。

The truly dangerous crossover is "throwing away everything you have to chase a hot trend"; the truly effective crossover is "carrying your strongest capability into a new field and recombining it there."

王传福敢去造车，不是因为他不懂汽车，而是因为他比谁都懂电池。他不是“放弃专业”，而是把自己的专业，搬到了一个更大的战场上。

Wang Chuanfu dared to build cars not because he didn't understand cars, but because he understood batteries better than anyone. He did not "abandon his specialty"; he moved his specialty onto a larger battlefield.

所以对今天想跨界的人来说，最该问自己的问题不是“我能不能做”，而是：我在原来那件事上积累的、别人替代不了的能力是什么？它能不能在我想去的那个新领域里，变成优势？

So for anyone today thinking about crossing over, the question to ask is not "can I do it" but: what capability have I built in my old work that no one else can replace? Can it become an advantage in the new field I want to enter?

如果你说不出这个答案，那可能不是跨界，是逃离。

If you can't answer that, then it may not be a crossover—it may be an escape.

最后还有一点：王传福的成功里，有很大一部分来自“不解释”。面对“找死”的嘲笑，他没有写长文反驳，也没有开新闻发布会辩解，他只是继续做——做电池、做汽车、做技术。在一个“凡事都要先自证”的时代，这种“我先做出来再说”的钝感，反而是一种稀缺的能力。

One last point: a large part of Wang Chuanfu's success comes from "not explaining." Faced with the mockery of "courting death," he did not write long rebuttals or hold press conferences to defend himself; he just kept working—batteries, cars, technology. In an era when you must prove yourself first in everything, this dullness of "I'll just make it first and talk later" is, ironically, a scarce ability.

顺便说一句，他后来做电动车，其实是把当年“用人工替代机器”的那股狠劲，换成了“用技术替代进口”的狠劲。路径变了，那股“不信邪”的劲儿没变。

By the way, when he later made electric cars, he essentially took that same fierce drive from "replacing machines with people" and turned it into "replacing imports with technology." The path changed; the stubborn refusal to believe anything was impossible did not.

所以，“跨界”这个词真正的意思，也许不是“我从头去学一个新行当”，而是“我把旧本事拿到了一个新地方用”。王传福不是一个跑去造车的电池工程师，他更像一个把电池装进汽车的人。这也解释了为什么他敢在所有人反对时下注——因为他赌的不是运气，而是自己手里那点别人拿不走的东西。

So the real meaning of "crossing over" may not be "I go learn a new trade from scratch" but "I take an old skill and use it somewhere new." Wang Chuanfu is not a battery engineer who ran off to build cars; he is more like a man who put batteries into cars. That also explains why he dared to place his bet when everyone opposed him—because what he wagered was not luck, but that small thing in his hands that no one could take away.
