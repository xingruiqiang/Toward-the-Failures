李时珍：三次落榜的“江湖郎中”

Li Shizhen: The "Itinerant Doctor" Who Failed the Examinations Three Times

——他写了一部近两百万字的巨著，却没能活着看到它印出来

He wrote a monumental work of nearly two million characters, yet did not live to see it in print

← 返回主页 Home第二辑　屡败屡战的坚韧行者38 / 100约 2792 字 · 2792 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，中医药已经走进一百多个国家，“青蒿素”这样的成果让世界重新审视传统医学。而这一切的源头之一，是一部叫《本草纲目》的书。

In 2026, traditional Chinese medicine has reached more than a hundred countries, and discoveries like artemisinin have led the world to look again at traditional medicine. One of the sources of all this is a book called Bencao Gangmu (Compendium of Materia Medica).

写这部书的人，是一个考了三次乡试、次次落榜的“失败秀才”。他放弃了功名，去当了一个在当时被人看不起的“民间医生”——按他自己的说法，那是一条“逆流的船”。他用二十七年，写了一部近两百万字的书。

The man who wrote it was a "failed licentiate" who sat the provincial examinations three times and failed every time. He gave up on office and rank to become a "folk doctor," a figure looked down upon in his day—by his own account, a "boat sailing against the current." He spent twenty-seven years writing a book of nearly two million characters.

然后，他在书即将印成的那一年死了。他一生都没能亲眼看见《本草纲目》印出来。

Then, in the very year the book was about to be printed, he died. Not once in his life did he see Bencao Gangmu come off the press.

一、一个被科举挡在门外的人

I. A Man Shut Out by the Imperial Examinations

1518年，李时珍出生在湖北蕲州（今蕲春）的一个医学世家。他的祖父是草药医生，父亲李言闻是当地名医，还曾在太医院任过职。

Li Shizhen was born in 1518 in Qizhou, Hubei (present-day Qichun), into a family of physicians. His grandfather was an herbal doctor, and his father, Li Yanwen, was a renowned local physician who had once served in the Imperial Medical Academy.

但在明代，医生的社会地位很低，收入也不稳定。父亲不想让儿子再吃这碗饭，希望他走科举这条路——考秀才、中举人、中进士，做官，光宗耀祖。

But in the Ming dynasty, doctors held low social standing and had uncertain incomes. His father did not want his son to make a living this way, and hoped he would take the examination path: become a licentiate, pass the provincial exam, pass the metropolitan exam, take office, and bring glory to the family name.

李时珍很争气：十四岁就考中了秀才。

Li Shizhen did well for himself: at fourteen he passed the examination and became a licentiate.

可接下来，他卡住了。十七岁、二十岁、二十三岁，他三次赴武昌参加乡试（考举人），三次全部落榜。

But then he got stuck. At seventeen, at twenty, and at twenty-three he went to Wuchang three times to sit the provincial examination (for the degree of juren), and all three times he failed.

在那个年代，“三次不中”基本就意味着这条路走到了尽头。李时珍做了一个决定：不考了，跟着父亲学医。

In that era, failing three times essentially meant that this road had come to an end. Li Shizhen made a decision: no more examinations—he would study medicine under his father.

据记载，他曾向父亲表达过这样的心志，大意是：这就像逆水行舟，但我的心比铁石还坚；请您成全我的心愿，我至死不怕艰难。

According to the records, he once expressed his resolve to his father in words to this effect: It is like a boat sailing against the current, but my heart is firmer than iron or stone; I beg you to grant me my wish, for I shall not fear hardship even unto death.

二、从王府到太医院，又主动离开

II. From a Prince's Mansion to the Imperial Medical Academy—and a Deliberate Departure

李时珍的医术进步极快。不到三十岁，他已经是当地有名的医生，病人不远千里来找他。

Li Shizhen's medical skill advanced with remarkable speed. Before he was thirty he was already a well-known local doctor, and patients traveled great distances to seek him out.

1551年前后，因为他治好了富顺王孙子的怪病，被武昌的楚王聘为“奉祠正”，兼管良医所的事务。后来又经推荐，进了京城的太医院。

Around 1551, having cured the strange illness of the Prince of Fushun's grandson, he was engaged by the Prince of Chu in Wuchang as "Fengsizheng," with concurrent charge of the affairs of the Hall of Good Physicians. Later, on recommendation, he entered the Imperial Medical Academy in the capital.

这是很多医生一辈子够不到的位置。但李时珍在太医院待的时间并不长——据研究，大约一年多。因为在太医院，他一次次向上面提出要重修本草（药学著作），却没人理他：当时的嘉靖皇帝醉心于炼制长生不老的丹药，对整理医书毫无兴趣。

This was a position most doctors could never reach in a lifetime. Yet Li Shizhen did not stay long at the Academy—by scholarly estimate, about a year or more. For there he repeatedly proposed to his superiors that the materia medica (pharmacological works) be revised, and no one paid him any heed: the Jiajing Emperor of the time was absorbed in concocting elixirs of immortality and had no interest whatever in compiling medical texts.

于是，李时珍辞职回乡了。

And so Li Shizhen resigned and went home.

他回乡后，以自己的字“东璧”为堂号，开了一家医馆（东璧堂），一边行医，一边开始做那件大事。

After returning home, he took his courtesy name, "Dongbi," as the name of his hall and opened a medical clinic (the Dongbi Hall), practicing medicine on one hand while beginning that great undertaking on the other.

三、二十七年，三易其稿

III. Twenty-Seven Years, Three Complete Drafts

为什么要重修本草？

Why revise the materia medica?

因为在李时珍看来，当时流传的本草书错误太多：药名混乱、同一味药被分成好几种、不同药又混为一谈，甚至有明显的以讹传讹。医生照着这些书开方，是会出人命的。

Because in Li Shizhen's eyes the materia medica in circulation were riddled with errors: the names of medicines were in confusion, a single drug was split into several, different drugs were lumped together, and there was even obvious transmission of falsehood upon falsehood. Doctors prescribing from these books could cost people their lives.

他决定自己干。方法很朴素，也很硬：

He decided to do it himself. His method was plain and rigorous:

- 读书：他参考的书籍文献，多达一千一百多种；

- Reading: the books and documents he consulted numbered more than eleven hundred;

- 走路：他的足迹遍及湖北、河北、河南、江西、安徽、江苏等地，实地考察药物；

- Walking: his footprints covered Hubei, Hebei, Henan, Jiangxi, Anhui, Jiangsu, and beyond, as he investigated medicinal substances on the ground;

- 拜师：他拜农民、渔夫、车夫、樵夫、捕蛇人为师，收集标本和民间单方。

- Learning from others: he took farmers, fishermen, carters, woodcutters, and snake-catchers as his teachers, collecting specimens and folk remedies.

关于他考察“蕲蛇”的故事很有名：当时市面上卖的多是假的蕲蛇，他为了看到真蛇，请捕蛇人带他上龙峰山，亲眼观察捕蛇、制蛇的全过程。为了核实穿山甲到底怎么吃蚂蚁，他亲自解剖了一只，发现古书上“诱蚁入甲下水吞食”的说法是错的——穿山甲是直接舐食的。

The story of his investigation of the "Qizhou snake" is well known: most of what was sold in the markets at the time was fake, and in order to see the real thing he asked snake-catchers to take him up Longfeng Mountain, where he observed the entire process of catching and preparing the snakes with his own eyes. To verify how a pangolin actually eats ants, he dissected one himself and found that the ancient texts' account—"it lures ants into its scales, then goes into the water and swallows them"—was wrong: the pangolin simply licks them up.

就这样，从1552年动笔，到1578年完成初稿，前后二十七年，三易其稿。全书52卷、约190万字，收录药物1892种，附方11096个，附图1109幅。

And so, from taking up the brush in 1552 to completing the first draft in 1578, twenty-seven years passed, with three complete rewrites. The finished work ran to 52 volumes and about 1.9 million characters, listing 1,892 medicinal substances, 11,096 appended prescriptions, and 1,109 illustrations.

四、书成之日，人已不在

IV. The Book Complete, the Man Already Gone

书写完了，但出版成了新难题。

The book was finished, but publishing became a new difficulty.

这是一部近两百万字的巨著，刻印成本极高。李时珍带着书稿四处奔走求人作序、找书商。他两次去拜访当时文坛领袖王世贞，请他为书作序。王世贞读完，大为惊叹，写下“性理之精蕴，格物之通典”这样的评语。

This was a monumental work of nearly two million characters, and the cost of woodblock printing was extremely high. Li Shizhen carried his manuscript everywhere, seeking prefaces and looking for publishers. Twice he called on Wang Shizhen, the leading literary figure of the day, asking him to write a preface. After reading it, Wang Shizhen was deeply astonished and wrote such praise as "the essence of natural philosophy, a comprehensive canon of the investigation of things."

1590年，《本草纲目》终于在南京开始刊刻。可就在即将刻成的时候，1593年，七十六岁的李时珍突然病逝。

In 1590, Bencao Gangmu finally went to the engravers in Nanjing. But just as it was nearing completion, in 1593, Li Shizhen, seventy-six years old, suddenly fell ill and died.

1596年，也就是他去世后的第三年，金陵版《本草纲目》正式问世。

In 1596, the third year after his death, the Jinling edition of Bencao Gangmu was formally published.

他一生最想看到的那一件事，他没能看到。临终前，他留下遗表，托付长子把书献给朝廷，希望它能广为流传。结果皇帝只批了九个字：“书留览，礼部知道，钦此。”

The one thing he most wanted to see in his life, he never saw. On his deathbed he left a memorial, entrusting his eldest son to present the book to the court, hoping it would circulate widely. In the end the emperor wrote only nine characters in reply: "Let the book be kept for perusal. The Ministry of Rites is informed. Respect this."

五、死后，世界来找他

V. After His Death, the World Came to Him

李时珍没能等到他的书出版，但世界等到了。

Li Shizhen did not live to see his book published, but the world lived to see it.

《本草纲目》问世后，在国内辗转翻刻近两百次，很快传入日本、朝鲜，又被译成拉丁、法、德、英、俄等多种文字。达尔文在著作中多次引用它，称它为中国古代的百科全书；英国科学史家李约瑟评价它是“明代最伟大的科学成就”。2011年，金陵版《本草纲目》入选联合国教科文组织《世界记忆名录》。

After Bencao Gangmu appeared, it was reprinted nearly two hundred times in China, quickly spread to Japan and Korea, and was translated into Latin, French, German, English, Russian, and other languages. Darwin cited it several times in his works, calling it an encyclopedia of ancient China; the British historian of science Joseph Needham judged it "the greatest scientific achievement of the Ming dynasty." In 2011, the Jinling edition of Bencao Gangmu was inscribed on UNESCO's Memory of the World Register.

而那个三次落榜的“江湖郎中”，如今与达尔文、哥白尼、牛顿、居里夫人的肖像一起，挂在莫斯科大学的长廊上。

And that "itinerant doctor" who failed the examinations three times now hangs, alongside the portraits of Darwin, Copernicus, Newton, and Marie Curie, in the gallery of Moscow State University.

六、致2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

李时珍的故事，讲的是“考场之外，还有另一条路”。

Li Shizhen's story is about how "beyond the examination hall, there is another road."

第一，他那三次落榜，也许恰恰是成全了他。如果李时珍顺利考上举人、进士，去做官，中国历史上就多了一个平庸的地方官，却会少掉一部《本草纲目》。他的“失败”，把他从一条不属于他的路上推了下来，推到了他真正该站的位置上。有时候，“走不通”不是惩罚，是引导。

First, those three failures may have been exactly what made him. Had Li Shizhen smoothly passed the provincial and metropolitan examinations and gone into office, Chinese history would have gained a mediocre local official and lost a Bencao Gangmu. His "failure" pushed him off a road that was not his and onto the place where he truly belonged. Sometimes "no way forward" is not a punishment but a guidance.

第二，真正的价值，是熬出来的，不是抢出来的。二十七年写一部书，三易其稿，走遍大半个中国，亲自上蛇山、解剖穿山甲——这份笨功夫，是任何“速成”替代不了的。

Second, true value is simmered, not seized. Twenty-seven years to write one book, three complete drafts, travels across most of China, climbing Snake Mountain in person, dissecting a pangolin himself—no "quick fix" can ever replace that kind of plodding effort.

第三，你的成果，可能比你自己活得更久。他没看到书印出来，但今天这本书活了四百多年，还在被引用、被研究、被翻译。有些人做的是“当期任务”，有些人做的是“百年工程”。

Third, your work may outlive you. He never saw his book printed, yet today it has lived for more than four hundred years and is still being cited, studied, and translated. Some people take on "the task of the moment"; others take on "a project of a hundred years."

他被科举刷了下来，最后却成了悬挂在人类文明长廊上的那批人之一。

Cast aside by the imperial examinations, he ended up among those whose portraits hang in the gallery of human civilization.

Appendix: Sources and Notes

主要史料

Primary Sources

生卒与事实

Dates and Facts

4. 生于1518年（湖北蕲州），卒于1593年，享年76岁。

4. Born in 1518 (Qizhou, Hubei), died in 1593 at the age of 76.

5. 14岁中秀才；此后三次乡试不第；约1552年动笔编《本草纲目》，1578年成书；1590年始刻，1596年（死后三年）金陵版问世。

5. Became a licentiate at 14; thereafter failed the provincial examination three times; began compiling Bencao Gangmu around 1552 and completed it in 1578; engraving began in 1590, and the Jinling edition appeared in 1596 (three years after his death).

6. 《本草纲目》凡52卷，约190万字，收药1892种，附方11096则，附图1100余幅。

6. Bencao Gangmu comprises 52 volumes, about 1.9 million characters, listing 1,892 medicinal substances, 11,096 appended prescriptions, and more than 1,100 illustrations.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

7. “李时珍曾任太医院院判（正六品）”——此点史学界有争议。一说他曾任太医院院判，一说他仅为御医（正八品），也有研究对其在太医院的具体职位提出不同看法。文中采用“进了太医院、任职约一年后辞职”的概括，未坐实具体品级。

7. "Li Shizhen once served as a court physician of the Imperial Medical Academy (rank 6a)"—this point is disputed among historians. One view holds that he served as Academy court physician, another that he was merely an imperial physician (rank 8a), and some studies raise differing views on his specific post at the Academy. The text uses the general formulation that he "entered the Imperial Medical Academy and resigned after about a year," without pinning down a specific rank.

8. “二十七年写成”与“前后共四十年”——不同资料表述不一：常见说法是“1552年动笔至1578年成书，历时27年”，也有材料（如部分官方介绍）称此后又经过约十年修订、前后共约四十年。文中采用“27年成书”的主流通行说法，并说明“三易其稿”。

8. "Written in twenty-seven years" versus "about forty years in all"—sources differ: the common account is that it took 27 years, from taking up the brush in 1552 to completion in 1578, while some materials (such as certain official introductions) say about ten more years of revision followed, making roughly forty years in total. The text adopts the mainstream, widely used "27 years to completion" formulation and notes the "three complete drafts."

9. “他去世时《本草纲目》即将刻成”——据记载，1593年李时珍去世时，金陵本尚在刊刻过程中，1596年正式出版。具体“临终时间点与刻书进度的关系”在不同材料中略有出入，文中按“即将刻成之际病逝”的通行叙述处理。

9. "Bencao Gangmu was about to be completed when he died"—records indicate that when Li Shizhen died in 1593, the Jinling edition was still in the process of being engraved, and it was formally published in 1596. The precise "relationship between the timing of his death and the progress of the printing" varies slightly among materials; the text follows the common narrative that he "died just as it was nearing completion."

10. “达尔文直接引用并称其为'中国古代百科全书'”——达尔文在著作中确曾引用过与中国古代博物学相关的材料（被研究者认为与《本草纲目》等文献有关），“中国古代百科全书”是后人对其意义的概括性评价；文中已作“称它为中国古代的百科全书”的表述，并注明出处为“评价/称誉”而非其原话直引。

10. "Darwin directly cited it and called it an 'encyclopedia of ancient China'"—Darwin did indeed cite materials related to ancient Chinese natural history in his works (which researchers connect with Bencao Gangmu and similar texts), but "encyclopedia of ancient China" is a later summary evaluation of its significance; the text already phrases it as "calling it an encyclopedia of ancient China" and notes the source as "evaluation/praise" rather than a direct quotation of his words.
