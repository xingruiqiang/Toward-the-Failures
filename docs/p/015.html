梵高：生前只卖出一幅画的“疯子”

Van Gogh: The “Madman” Who Sold Only One Painting in His Lifetime

——他把一生的痛，都调成了最亮的黄

— He mixed all the pain of his life into the brightest yellow

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者15 / 100约 2353 字 · 2353 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

在2026年的今天，梵高是全世界最有名的画家之一。他的《星月夜》印在帆布袋上，《向日葵》被挂在最大的博物馆里，他的画一次次拍出天价。可如果你走进1890年的法国奥维尔，你会看到一个完全不同的人：一个被当成疯子、进过精神病院、靠弟弟养着、37岁就自杀的失败者。他生前，几乎没有人买他的画。

In 2026, Van Gogh is one of the most famous painters in the world. His The Starry Night is printed on canvas tote bags, his Sunflowers hangs in the greatest museums, and his paintings have sold again and again for astronomical sums. But if you walked into Auvers, France, in 1890, you would see a completely different man: a failure who was taken for a madman, committed to an asylum, kept alive by his brother, and dead by his own hand at thirty-seven. In his lifetime, almost no one bought his paintings.

他把一生的灰暗，都调成了最亮的黄。

He mixed all the darkness of his life into the brightest yellow.

一、一个“干什么都不成”的人

I. A Man Who “Couldn't Do Anything”

1853年3月30日，梵高出生在荷兰津德尔特，父亲是个牧师。

On March 30, 1853, Van Gogh was born in Zundert, the Netherlands, to a father who was a pastor.

他年轻时，试过很多行当，几乎每一样都失败了：

In his youth he tried many trades, and nearly every one of them failed:

他在海牙、伦敦、巴黎的画廊当职员——最后被辞退；

He worked as a clerk in art galleries in The Hague, London, and Paris—and was finally dismissed;

他做过教师；

He worked as a teacher;

他跑到比利时博里纳日的煤矿区，当见习传教士——他把自己的一切都给了那些穷矿工，下到矿井里给他们讲道，结果因为“太狂热、不符合教会的体面”，被教会解聘。

He went to the coal-mining district of the Borinage, in Belgium, to serve as an apprentice preacher—he gave everything he had to those poor miners, descended into the pits to preach to them, and was dismissed by the church for being “too fanatical, unbecoming the dignity of the church.”

直到1880年，大概二十七岁，他才终于下定决心：我要当画家。

It was not until 1880, at about twenty-seven, that he finally made up his mind: I want to be a painter.

用任何标准看，他都是个大器晚成到不像话的失败者：二十多岁一无所成，全靠弟弟提奥养活。

By any standard, he was a late bloomer to an absurd degree, a failure: in his twenties he had accomplished nothing and depended entirely on his brother Theo.

而这个弟弟，是理解梵高的钥匙。提奥终身资助他——给他钱，给他买颜料，给他写信，也给他活下去的理由。兄弟俩留下的八百多封通信，是后人读懂梵高最重要的材料。

And that brother is the key to understanding Van Gogh. Theo supported him for life—giving him money, buying him paint, writing him letters, and giving him a reason to go on living. The more than eight hundred letters the brothers left behind are the most important material for later generations seeking to understand Van Gogh.

二、他的画，当时为什么卖不出去

II. Why His Paintings Would Not Sell at the Time

他早期的画，是灰暗的。那幅著名的《吃土豆的人》，色调沉，题材土——一群农民在昏暗的灯下吃土豆。市场不认这种东西。

His early paintings were dark. That famous work The Potato Eaters has a somber palette and a rustic subject—a group of peasants eating potatoes under a dim lamp. The market would have none of it.

后来在巴黎，他接触了印象派和日本浮世绘，色彩一下子炸开了。

Later, in Paris, he encountered Impressionism and Japanese ukiyo-e, and his color suddenly exploded.

可他的画法太“野”了：笔触粗，颜料厚，颜色堆到极致。当时的画商觉得，这根本是“没画完的草稿”。

But his way of painting was too “wild”: coarse brushstrokes, thick paint, color piled on to the extreme. Art dealers of the time felt this was simply an “unfinished sketch.”

有明确记载、能叫得出名字的、他生前卖出的画，只有一幅——《红色葡萄园》。

The only clearly documented painting, one whose title can be named, that he sold in his lifetime was a single work—The Red Vineyard.

1890年3月，在布鲁塞尔“二十人画会”的年度展览上，比利时画家安娜·博赫以400法郎买下了它。

In March 1890, at the annual exhibition of the “Les XX” society in Brussels, the Belgian painter Anna Boch bought it for 400 francs.

（顺便说清一个流传太广的说法：“梵高生前只卖出一幅画”。严格讲，有明确名称和记录的，是这一幅；但“仅此一幅”更像传说，实际成交可能不止一次。只能说——他生前几乎卖不出画。）

(Let us also clear up, by the way, a claim that has spread far too widely: “Van Gogh sold only one painting in his lifetime.” Strictly speaking, the one with a definite title and record is this one; but “only this one” is closer to legend, and there may in fact have been more than one sale. All one can say is—he could barely sell paintings in his lifetime.)

三、阿尔勒：黄房子和那个“割耳之夜”

III. Arles: The Yellow House and That “Night of the Cut Ear”

1888年2月，梵高去了法国南部的阿尔勒，租下了一栋黄房子。他心里有个梦想：建一个“南方画室”，让画家们住在一起。

In February 1888, Van Gogh went to Arles in the south of France and rented a yellow house. He had a dream: to establish a “Studio of the South” where painters could live together.

他画向日葵，画麦田，画星空，画隆河上的星夜。他要用最亮的黄，去对抗他人生里的灰。

He painted sunflowers, wheat fields, starry skies, and the starry night over the Rhône. With the brightest yellow he meant to fight back against the gray of his life.

他邀请了高更来同住。高更来了，住了大约九个星期。

He invited Gauguin to live with him. Gauguin came and stayed about nine weeks.

两个性格刚烈、理念强烈的人，从互相欣赏，走到激烈争吵。

Two men of fierce temperament and strong convictions moved from mutual admiration to furious quarrels.

1888年12月23日，梵高精神崩溃，割下了自己左耳的一部分，把它送给当地一名女子。当晚，高更离开了阿尔勒，两人此后再未见面。

On December 23, 1888, Van Gogh suffered a breakdown and cut off part of his own left ear, giving it to a local woman. That night Gauguin left Arles, and the two never met again.

（这件事的具体起因，学界至今没有定论。但“高更的离去”与梵高的崩溃高度相关，是大体被接受的判断。）

(The precise cause of this affair remains undecided in scholarship to this day. But that “Gauguin's departure” was closely bound up with Van Gogh's breakdown is a broadly accepted judgment.)

四、精神病院与最后的麦田

IV. The Asylum and the Last Wheat Field

1889年5月，梵高自愿住进了圣雷米的精神病院。在那里，他画出了《星月夜》《柏树》——那些最著名、最汹涌的作品。

In May 1889, Van Gogh voluntarily entered the asylum at Saint-Rémy. There he painted The Starry Night and Cypresses—those most famous, most turbulent works.

1890年5月，他搬到巴黎近郊的奥维尔，由加歇医生照看。

In May 1890, he moved to Auvers, on the outskirts of Paris, to be looked after by Dr. Gachet.

1890年7月27日，他在麦田里中枪（自伤），两天后，7月29日去世，年仅37岁。弟弟提奥守在他床边。

On July 27, 1890, he was shot in a wheat field (self-inflicted), and two days later, on July 29, he died, only thirty-seven years old. His brother Theo was at his bedside.

更让人心碎的在后头：六个月后，1891年1月25日，提奥也去世了。1914年，提奥的遗骨被迁葬到奥维尔，与哥哥并排长眠。

What is more heartbreaking came afterward: six months later, on January 25, 1891, Theo also died. In 1914 Theo's remains were moved to Auvers, to lie side by side with his brother.

五、他的“失败”，和他真正的胜利

V. His “Failure,” and His True Triumph

生前：几乎没人买他的画，他被当成疯子，进过精神病院，37岁自杀。

In his lifetime: almost no one bought his paintings, he was taken for a madman, committed to an asylum, and took his own life at thirty-seven.

死后：他被追认为后印象派最伟大的画家之一，深刻影响了表现主义、野兽派乃至整个二十世纪艺术。《加歇医生肖像》《没胡子的自画像》《鸢尾花》《向日葵》，一次次把他的名字送上天价拍卖榜。

After his death: he was recognized as one of the greatest Post-Impressionist painters, exerting a profound influence on Expressionism, Fauvism, and indeed the whole of twentieth-century art. Portrait of Dr. Gachet, Self-Portrait Without Beard, Irises, and Sunflowers sent his name again and again to the top of record auction lists.

而最该被记住的，是这个对比：他真正画画的时间只有大约十年；这十年里，他完成了约两千幅作品（含素描）。

And the comparison that most deserves remembering is this: he truly painted for only about ten years; in those ten years he completed roughly two thousand works (drawings included).

算一算：那个被称作“疯子”的人，平均每一天半，就要画完一幅画。

Do the math: the man called a “madman” finished, on average, one painting every day and a half.

六、结语：致2026年，每一个“努力了也没结果”的人

VI. Conclusion: To Everyone in 2026 Who “Tried Hard and Got Nowhere”

2026年，最让人绝望的，往往不是努力本身，而是“努力了却没有回报”。我们太熟悉那种感觉：加班到深夜，项目还是黄了；一心扑在某件事上，还是没人认可。

In 2026, what most often drives us to despair is not effort itself, but “effort that brings no return.” We know that feeling too well: working late into the night, only to watch the project fall through; pouring yourself into one thing, only to win no recognition.

梵高最狠的地方，不是他“坚持到了成功”——他根本没等到成功。他是在完全没有回报的情况下，画了十年。

What is most relentless about Van Gogh is not that he “persisted until success”—he never lived to see success. He painted for ten years with no return whatever.

他画向日葵的时候，没有人在买；他把最亮的黄涂满画布，是因为他的人生里，太缺那样一种颜色。他不是为了“成功”而画，他是为了活下去而画。

When he painted sunflowers, no one was buying; he covered the canvas with the brightest yellow because his life was so lacking in that color. He did not paint for the sake of “success”; he painted in order to go on living.

所以，如果你此刻正做着一件没人看见、没人买单、没人理解的事——请记住梵高：

So if you are right now doing something that no one sees, no one pays for, no one understands—remember Van Gogh:

他生前只卖出一幅画，而他画了一辈子，画了两千幅。

He sold only one painting in his lifetime, and he painted for a lifetime, and he painted two thousand.

有些事，值得做，不是因为它一定会成功，而是因为它让你成为你。

Some things are worth doing not because they are sure to succeed, but because they make you who you are.

生卒与事实

Dates and Facts

4. 生于1853年3月30日荷兰津德尔特，卒于1890年7月29日法国奥维尔。1880年前后开始绘画；1886—1888年在巴黎；1888年2月赴阿尔勒；1888年12月23日割耳；1889年5月入圣雷米精神病院；1890年5月迁奥维尔；1890年7月27日受枪伤，29日去世。弟弟提奥于1891年1月25日去世，1914年迁葬奥维尔与兄长并排。

4. Born March 30, 1853, in Zundert, the Netherlands; died July 29, 1890, in Auvers, France. Began painting around 1880; in Paris 1886–1888; went to Arles in February 1888; cut his ear on December 23, 1888; entered the asylum at Saint-Rémy in May 1889; moved to Auvers in May 1890; was shot on July 27, 1890, and died on the 29th. Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

5. “生前只卖出一幅画”——有明确名称与记录的仅《红色葡萄园》一幅；但“仅此一幅”为流行说法，1998年有研究者指出实际至少售出两幅，本文按“可确证者仅一幅、生前几乎卖不出画”表述。

6. 割耳事件的具体动机——学界无定论。

6. The precise motive for the ear-cutting incident—scholarship remains undecided.

7. 自伤说——主流说法为“麦田中自伤”，但现场无目击者，细节仍有争议，本文以“中枪（自伤）”表述。

7. The self-inflicted-wound account—the mainstream view is “self-inflicted wound in a wheat field,” but there were no witnesses at the scene and the details remain disputed; this chapter states it as “shot (self-inflicted).”
