罗永浩：欠了6个亿，被人叫“行业冥灯”的那个人

Luo Yonghao: Six Hundred Million in Debt and "The Industry's Death Omen"

——他把还债的过程，自己起名叫《真还传》

—He gave his debt-repayment process a name of his own: The Real Repayment Story

← 返回主页 Home第三辑　在废墟上重建的人50 / 100约 3046 字 · 3046 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，“债务”是很多年轻人心里的一根刺。有人欠着网贷，有人背着房贷，有人创业失败留下一个烂摊子。我们常常觉得，一旦欠了钱，人就矮了一截，抬不起头。

In 2026, "debt" is a thorn in the heart of many young people. Some owe money to online lenders, some carry mortgages, some are left with a mess after a failed venture. We often feel that once we owe money, we're somehow diminished, unable to hold our heads up.

但有一个人，欠了6个亿。他没有跑，没有躲，没有“下周回国”，而是亲自上直播带货，一件一件地把钱还了回去。他还给这段经历起了个调侃自己的名字，叫《真还传》。

But one man owed 600 million yuan. He didn't run, didn't hide, didn't say "I'll be back in the country next week"—instead he went on livestreams to sell products himself and paid the money back, one item at a time. And he gave this experience a self-mocking name: The Real Repayment Story.

他叫罗永浩。他的人生几乎是一部“连续失败史”：办网站、做英语培训、造手机、做电子烟、搞AR……几乎每一次都踩在了风口上，又几乎每一次都摔了下来。网友给他取了个外号，叫“行业冥灯”——干一行，垮一行。

His name is Luo Yonghao. His life is almost a "history of serial failure": starting websites, running English training, making phones, making e-cigarettes, working on AR... almost every time he stepped onto a rising trend, and almost every time he fell. Netizens gave him a nickname: the "industry death omen"—take up a trade, kill a trade.

一、从“网红老师”到“造手机的人”

I. From "Internet Celebrity Teacher" to "Phone Maker"

罗永浩1972年出生，早年是新东方的英语老师，因为讲课风格犀利幽默，被学生录下来传到网上，成了中国互联网最早的一批“网红”之一。后来他离开新东方，创办了牛博网，又办了老罗英语培训。

Luo Yonghao was born in 1972. In his early years he was an English teacher at New Oriental; because his lecturing style was sharp and humorous, students recorded him and posted the clips online, and he became one of the earliest "internet celebrities" on the Chinese internet. Later he left New Oriental, founded the blog platform Bullog, and then started Lao Luo's English Training.

2012年，他宣布要做手机，创办了锤子科技。

In 2012, he announced that he was going to make phones, and founded Smartisan.

那个时候，中国智能手机正在爆发。一个英语老师要做手机，外界几乎是一片嘲笑。但罗永浩坚持自己的“理想主义”路线：他要做“东半球最好用的手机”，要讲设计、讲情怀、讲工匠精神。发布会开成脱口秀，粉丝买账，锤子手机也确实做出了一些让人记住的产品。

At that time, China's smartphone market was exploding. An English teacher wanting to make phones drew almost universal ridicule. But Luo Yonghao stuck to his "idealist" path: he wanted to make "the best phone in the Eastern Hemisphere," to talk about design, about sentiment, about the craftsman's spirit. His launch events turned into stand-up comedy shows, his fans bought in, and Smartisan phones did produce some products people remember.

然而，手机是个极其残酷的行业——供应链、资金、渠道、规模，每一样都需要巨额的资本和成熟的管理。锤子科技始终没有真正跑通商业模式。

Yet the phone business is a brutally harsh industry—supply chains, capital, distribution channels, scale; each one demands enormous sums of money and mature management. Smartisan never truly made its business model work.

二、其实更早，他就“垮”过两次

II. In Fact, He Had Already "Collapsed" Twice, and Much Earlier

很多人只知道锤子科技，其实罗永浩“连续失败”的历史要长得多。

Many people know only about Smartisan; in fact, Luo Yonghao's history of "serial failure" goes back much further.

2006年，他离开新东方后创办了牛博网，一个以高质量作者和犀利观点著称的博客平台。它一度聚集了不少知名学者、媒体人和写作者，在当时的舆论场很有影响力。但它始终没有找到能养活自己的商业模式——不愿接自己不认可的广告，也不肯放下身段去做流量生意。后来牛博网在国内关闭了。

In 2006, after leaving New Oriental, he founded Bullog, a blogging platform known for its high-quality writers and sharp opinions. It once gathered many well-known scholars, journalists, and writers, and wielded considerable influence in the public discourse of the time. But it never found a business model that could support itself—unwilling to take on advertising he didn't personally endorse, and unwilling to lower himself to chase traffic. In the end, Bullog shut down in China.

紧接着，他又创办了老罗英语培训，靠个人的号召力招生，口碑不错，规模却始终有限，最终也没能做成一家真正立得住的公司。

Right after that, he founded Lao Luo's English Training, recruiting students through his personal appeal. Its reputation was good, but its scale remained limited, and in the end it never became a company that could truly stand on its own.

所以到2012年做锤子手机时，这已经是他的第三、第四个创业项目。锤子的失败，并不是一个人的“第一次失手”，而是一个连续创业者又一次的重击。也正因为前面已经摔过，他在欠下6个亿之后，才有那种异常平静的口气——“欠债还钱，我去还就是了。”

So by the time he started Smartisan in 2012, this was already his third or fourth entrepreneurial project. Smartisan's failure was not a man's "first misstep" but another heavy blow for a serial entrepreneur. And precisely because he had already fallen before, after taking on 600 million yuan of debt he spoke with that unusually calm tone—"You owe money, you pay it back. I'll go pay it."

三、6个亿是怎么来的

III. How the 600 Million Came About

2018年下半年，锤子科技的危机全面爆发。

In the second half of 2018, the crisis at Smartisan erupted in full.

据罗永浩自己的说法，公司在最困难时欠了银行、合作伙伴和供应商约6个亿。为了挽救公司，他签了个人无责任担保的1个多亿。

By Luo Yonghao's own account, at its most difficult point the company owed banks, partners, and suppliers about 600 million yuan. To save the company, he signed personal no-recourse guarantees for more than 100 million yuan of it.

按法律，这些大部分是公司债务，公司破产清算就可以不用还了。但他决定，把这笔债全部还完。他的理由很实在：一是想帮那些中小供应商；二是做人要厚道；三是为了以后还能谈合作。

Legally, most of these were company debts; a bankruptcy liquidation would have discharged them. But he decided to repay the entire sum. His reasons were practical: first, he wanted to help the small and medium-sized suppliers; second, a person should be decent; third, so that he could still negotiate partnerships in the future.

2019年11月，他因为债务问题被法院下发限制消费令，被冠上了“老赖”的称号。那一年，他还尝试了几个新项目——聊天宝、小野电子烟、“鲨鱼皮”（一种抗菌材料）——结果都不理想，“行业冥灯”这个外号就是那时候叫响的。

In November 2019, over the debt issue, a court issued a consumption restriction order against him, and he was branded a "deadbeat." That same year he tried several new projects—Liaotianbao (a messaging app), XIAOYE e-cigarettes, and "Shark Skin" (an antibacterial material)—none of which went well, and it was then that the nickname "industry death omen" caught on.

四、直播间里的“真还传”

IV. "The Real Repayment Story" in the Livestream Studio

2020年4月1日，罗永浩在抖音完成了直播带货首秀。

On April 1, 2020, Luo Yonghao made his livestream selling debut on Douyin.

那场直播其实很尴尬：他不会讲品，不会带气氛，连优惠机制都搞不明白，旁边的专业助播都救不回来。但凭借“自带流量”的个人品牌，那一晚的成交额超过了1.1亿元，累计观看人数超过4800万。

That livestream was actually quite awkward: he didn't know how to present products, couldn't work up an atmosphere, and couldn't even figure out the discount mechanics, so much so that even the professional co-host beside him couldn't save it. But thanks to his "traffic-magnet" personal brand, that night's sales exceeded 110 million yuan, with cumulative viewership of over 48 million.

当晚之后，他从科技创业者变成了头部带货主播，也有了后来的“交个朋友直播间”。

After that night, he went from a tech entrepreneur to a top-tier livestream host, and later came the "Jiao Ge Pengyou" (Make Friends) livestream studio.

2020年9月，他在综艺节目《脱口秀大会》上透露，那6亿债务已经还了快4个亿，剩下的预计一年内还清。他还自嘲说，还完钱要拍个纪录片，就叫《真还传》。

In September 2020, on the variety show Rock & Roast, he revealed that of the 600 million yuan of debt he had already repaid nearly 400 million, and expected to finish the rest within a year. He also joked that once he'd repaid it all, he'd make a documentary, to be called The Real Repayment Story.

后来他专门澄清过：“直播虽然是风口，但也没那么夸张。”所谓的4个亿，其实还了将近两年，其中还包括卖掉手机团队和相关知识产权的1.8亿元，加上做电子烟和直播电商赚的钱。

He later went out of his way to clarify: "Livestreaming may be a rising trend, but it's not that exaggerated either." The so-called 400 million had in fact taken nearly two years to repay, and it included the 180 million yuan from selling the phone team and related intellectual property, plus money earned from the e-cigarette business and livestream e-commerce.

面对外界的称赞，他的态度很有意思：他认为这是一个“远远超出契约精神的正面案例”，但也不是“欠债还钱天经地义”那么简单——他要还的，是一份自己给自己立的规矩。

Facing praise from the outside world, his attitude was interesting: he considered this "a positive case that far exceeds the spirit of contract," but it wasn't as simple as "paying what you owe is only right"—what he was repaying was a rule he had set for himself.

五、债没还完，但人没垮

V. The Debt Isn't Paid Off, but the Man Didn't Break

2022年6月，罗永浩宣布退出“交个朋友”管理层，“再次埋头创业”，这一次他选了AR（增强现实）。

In June 2022, Luo Yonghao announced that he was stepping back from the management of "Make Friends" to "bury himself in starting up again," this time choosing AR (augmented reality).

但故事没有结束。2024年8月，他在一篇《“真还传”第二季——从6亿到13亿》里透露：因为还债过程中产生的官司赔偿、滞纳金和罚款，债务总数又多了近1个亿；加上2022年底出现的新情况，债务从6亿变成了13亿。到那时为止，“真还传”一共还了8.24亿元。

But the story wasn't over. In August 2024, in a piece titled "The Real Repayment Story, Season Two—From 600 Million to 1.3 Billion," he revealed that due to lawsuit damages, late fees, and fines generated during the repayment process, the total debt had grown by nearly another 100 million; combined with new circumstances that arose at the end of 2022, the debt went from 600 million to 1.3 billion. By then, "The Real Repayment Story" had repaid a total of 824 million yuan.

也就是说，这场“还债连续剧”，到今天还没播完。

In other words, this "debt-repayment drama" still hasn't finished airing.

有网友问他：“为什么非要还？破产不就完了？”他的回答，其实道出了他这个人最矛盾也最动人的地方：他既是一个满嘴理想主义、常常高估自己的人，也是一个在契约面前不肯赖账的人。

A netizen asked him, "Why insist on repaying? Why not just go bankrupt and be done with it?" His answer in fact reveals the most contradictory—and most moving—thing about him: he is both a man who talks idealism at every turn and often overestimates himself, and a man who, in the face of a contract, refuses to welch on his debts.

六、致2026：关于“负责任地失败”

VI. To 2026: On "Failing Responsibly"

2026年，很多人开始讨论一个词：“体面地失败”。

In 2026, many people began discussing a phrase: "failing with dignity."

我们从小被教育要成功，却几乎没被教过怎么面对失败。怎么向投资人交代？怎么跟供应商结账？怎么跟家人解释？失败了之后，是躲起来，还是站出来把该负的责任负完？

We were taught from childhood to succeed, but almost never taught how to face failure. How do you account to your investors? How do you settle with your suppliers? How do you explain it to your family? After failing, do you hide away, or do you stand up and finish bearing the responsibility you ought to bear?

罗永浩提供的答案并不完美——他确实判断失误、确实把公司带进了坑里、确实说过很多过头话、也确实有过“过度承诺”的毛病。这些都是真实的代价，不该被“励志”两个字盖过去。但他至少做对了一件最关键的事：他没有把“失败”变成“逃债”的同义词。

The answer Luo Yonghao offers is not perfect—he did misjudge, he did lead the company into a ditch, he did make plenty of over-the-top remarks, and he did have a habit of "over-promising." These are real costs, and they shouldn't be papered over with the word "inspirational." But he got at least one crucial thing right: he did not let "failure" become a synonym for "dodging debt."

这里面有一层很朴素的东西。他在还债的过程中说过，那些债务里，他自己签了个人无限担保的不到15%，剩下的都是公司债务；按法律，公司破产清算就可以不还。但他还是决定把全部还完，理由里既有“想帮中小供应商”的情义，也有“以后还要谈合作”的现实考量——不是纯粹的圣人，也不肯做纯粹的逃兵。

There is something very plain and simple here. During the repayment process, he said that of those debts, the ones he had personally signed unlimited guarantees for amounted to less than 15 percent, and the rest were company debts; by law, a bankruptcy liquidation would have excused them. But he still decided to repay everything, his reasons combining the loyalty of "wanting to help the small and medium suppliers" with the practical calculation of "still needing to negotiate partnerships later"—not a pure saint, yet unwilling to be a pure deserter.

对我们这代人来说，罗永浩的故事里最值得记住的也许不是“直播还债”的戏剧性，而是那种“该我扛的，我扛”的姿态。

For our generation, the most memorable thing in Luo Yonghao's story may not be the drama of "repaying debt through livestreaming," but that stance—"what's mine to carry, I carry."

一个人可以做一个失败的创业者，但不必做一个失信的人。

A person can be a failed entrepreneur without having to be a person who breaks faith.

当一个人愿意为自己的选择承担后果，哪怕背负十几个亿，他也依然站得住。这大概就是这个时代里，“失败者”最体面的一种活法。

When a person is willing to bear the consequences of his own choices, even carrying more than a billion in debt, he still stands firm. This is, perhaps, the most dignified way for a "failure" to live in this era.
