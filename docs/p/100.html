Chapter 100 · Part 5

黄大年：放弃了英国的一切，回国拼到生命的最后一刻

Huang Danian: He Gave Up Everything in Britain to Come Home and Fight to His Last Breath

——“振兴中华，乃我辈之责。”

— “To rejuvenate China is the duty of our generation.”

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人100 / 100约 3068 字 · 3068 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，“归国潮”已经是一种常态。海外学成的人回国工作、创业，是很自然的选择。

In 2026, the “wave of returning home” has become an ordinary thing. For people who have studied abroad, coming back to work or start a business is a perfectly natural choice.

但在2009年，一个在国际学术界已经站到顶尖位置、在英国有房有车有诊所的科学家，选择放弃那一切，回到中国东北的一所大学，从头开始带团队攻关——而且回来之后，几乎把自己的命搭了进去。

But in 2009, a scientist who had already reached the top ranks of international academia—who had a house, a car, and a clinic in Britain—chose to give all of it up, return to a university in northeast China, and start from scratch leading a team to crack a hard problem. And after coming back, he nearly gave his life for it.

他叫黄大年。他回国七年，五十八岁去世。

His name was Huang Danian. He was back in China for seven years, and he died at fifty-eight.

一、从地质队走出来的科学家

I. A Scientist Who Came Out of a Geological Survey Team

1958年8月，黄大年出生在广西南宁的一个知识分子家庭。

Huang Danian was born in Nanning, Guangxi, in August 1958, into a family of intellectuals.

1975年，十七岁的他进入广西第六地质队，成了一名航空物探操作员。在这份工作里，他第一次接触到航空地球物理，也从此爱上了这个专业。

In 1975, at seventeen, he joined the Sixth Guangxi Geological Survey Team as an airborne geophysical survey operator. In that job he came into contact with airborne geophysics for the first time, and from then on he loved the field.

1977年，中国恢复高考。他考进了长春地质学院（后并入吉林大学）应用地球物理系。他在那里读完了本科和硕士，毕业后留校任教。

In 1977, China restored the college entrance examination. He was admitted to the Department of Applied Geophysics at Changchun College of Geology (later merged into Jilin University). There he completed his undergraduate and master's studies, and after graduating he stayed on to teach.

1988年，他加入了中国共产党。他的入党志愿书里有一段话：“若能做一朵小小的浪花，奔腾着加入献身者的滚滚洪流中，推动历史向前发展，才是一生中最值得骄傲和自豪的事情。”

In 1988 he joined the Communist Party of China. In his application to join the Party there was a passage: “If I can be a small wave, surging forward to join the mighty torrent of those who give themselves over, and push history forward, that would be the proudest and most glorious thing in a life.”

这句话，他后来用了整整一生去兑现。

He would spend his whole life making good on that sentence.

二、在英国，他做到了顶尖

II. In Britain, He Reached the Top

1992年，黄大年拿到了全国仅有的三十个公派出国名额中的一个，被选送到英国利兹大学攻读博士学位。

In 1992, Huang Danian won one of only thirty state-sponsored places to study abroad in the whole country, and was sent to the University of Leeds in Britain to pursue a doctorate.

1996年，他以排名第一的成绩，获得了利兹大学地球物理学博士学位。此后，他被派往英国，从事针对水下目标和深水油气的高精度探测技术研究。

In 1996 he earned his doctorate in geophysics from the University of Leeds with the top rank in his cohort. After that he was sent to Britain to work on high-precision detection technology for underwater targets and deepwater oil and gas.

他在英国剑桥的一家航空地球物理公司任高级研究员，一待就是十二年，还担任过研发部主任、博士生导师。他成了世界航空地球物理研究领域里，站在最前沿的那批人之一。

He worked as a senior researcher at an airborne geophysics company in Cambridge, England, staying for twelve years, and also served as head of research and development and as a doctoral supervisor. He became one of the people standing at the very front of airborne geophysics research in the world.

他在英国的生活，是安稳而优渥的：有房，妻子有自己的诊所，女儿学业有成。

His life in Britain was secure and comfortable: he had a house, his wife had her own clinic, and his daughter was doing well in her studies.

这正是他最难放弃的东西。

This was exactly the hardest thing for him to give up.

在英国的那些年，他一直惦记着母校。他经常回国交流，帮助师生们拓展国际视野。一个人在国外越成功，心里那根“要回去”的弦就绷得越紧。 他不是不知道回国意味着什么，他只是更清楚，自己学的这些东西，最后要用在什么地方。

During his years in Britain, he never stopped thinking about his alma mater. He often returned to China to exchange ideas and help teachers and students broaden their international horizons. The more successful a person becomes abroad, the tighter the string in his heart that says “I must go back” is drawn. He was not unaware of what returning home would mean; he simply knew more clearly where the things he had learned should ultimately be used.

三、2009年12月

III. December 2009

2009年12月24日，五十一岁的黄大年，从英国剑桥飞回中国。他悄然走进吉林大学。

On December 24, 2009, Huang Danian, fifty-one years old, flew back to China from Cambridge. He quietly walked into Jilin University.

他是第一位落地中国东北的“国家千人计划”专家。

He was the first expert of the national “Thousand Talents Plan” to land in northeast China.

有外国媒体报道称，他的回国，让某国的航母演习舰队后撤了一百海里。这个说法无法核实，但它从侧面说明了一件事：他掌握的技术，是极其重要的。

Some foreign media reported that his return to China had caused a certain country's aircraft carrier exercise fleet to pull back a hundred nautical miles. This claim cannot be verified, but it shows one thing from the side: the technology he possessed was extremely important.

他担任了吉林大学地球探测科学与技术学院教授，并被选为国家“深部探测关键仪器装备研制与实验”项目的首席科学家。

He became a professor at Jilin University's College of Geo-Exploration Science and Technology, and was chosen as chief scientist of the national project “Development and Experiment of Key Instruments and Equipment for Deep Exploration.”

四、他到底在做什么

IV. What Exactly He Was Doing

黄大年研究的，是“给地球做CT”的技术。

What Huang Danian researched was the technology of “doing a CT scan on the earth.”

他要攻关的，是一种叫“航空重力梯度仪”的装备。这套东西装在飞机上，可以探测地下的结构——就像是给地球做一次透视，能“看穿”地下每一个角落。

What he was working to crack was a device called the airborne gravity gradiometer. Mounted on an aircraft, the system can detect underground structures—as if giving the earth an X-ray, able to “see through” every corner beneath the surface.

这项技术，在资源勘探、深海探测等领域有极其重要的价值。而这项技术，长期被少数国家封锁。

This technology has extremely important value in fields such as resource exploration and deep-sea survey. And for a long time it was blockaded by a small number of countries.

黄大年回国后，以吉林大学为中心，组织了全国数百位优秀的科研人员，联合攻关。这是一项“十年磨一剑”的工作——他的团队经过六年刻苦攻关，取得的成果入选了国家科技创新成就展，与屠呦呦等著名科学家的成果并列上榜。

After returning to China, Huang Danian organized several hundred outstanding researchers from across the country, centered on Jilin University, to tackle the problem together. This was the work of “grinding a sword for ten years”—after six years of hard effort, his team's results were selected for the National Exhibition of Scientific and Technological Innovation Achievements, appearing on the list alongside the work of renowned scientists such as Tu Youyou.

他做到了。中国在这个方向上，突破了国外的技术封锁。

He did it. In this direction, China broke through the technology blockade imposed from abroad.

五、“像陀螺一样”的七年

V. Seven Years “Like a Spinning Top”

成果的背后，是极其惊人的付出。

Behind the results lay an astonishing amount of sacrifice.

从回国到去世，七年多里，他担任了国家多个技术攻关项目的首席专家。他几乎不休寒暑假，不休节假日。他常常工作到凌晨，忘了睡觉、忘了吃饭。

In the seven-plus years from his return to his death, he served as chief expert on several national key technology projects. He almost never took winter or summer vacations, and never took holidays. He often worked until the small hours, forgetting to sleep and forgetting to eat.

吉林大学地质宫507室是他的办公室。只要他不去出差，那间屋子的灯，每天都要亮到凌晨。

Room 507 in the Geology Building at Jilin University was his office. As long as he was not away on a trip, the light in that room stayed on until the small hours every day.

师生们说，他“像陀螺一样不知疲倦地旋转”。

Teachers and students said he “spun tirelessly like a top.”

2016年11月29日凌晨，他晕倒在了出差途中。回到长春，单位强制安排他做检查。检查结果还没出来，他又跑去北京出差了。

In the early hours of November 29, 2016, he collapsed while on a business trip. Back in Changchun, his institution ordered him to undergo a medical checkup. Before the results even came back, he rushed off to Beijing on business again.

等他回到长春，还没来得及喘口气，就拿到了住院通知：胆管癌。

When he got back to Changchun, before he even had time to catch his breath, he was handed a hospital admission notice: bile duct cancer.

六、2017年1月8日

VI. January 8, 2017

2017年1月8日，黄大年在长春去世，年仅五十八岁。

On January 8, 2017, Huang Danian died in Changchun, aged only fifty-eight.

他回国，只有七年多一点。

He had been back in China for only a little more than seven years.

他去世后，习近平对他的先进事迹作出重要指示，号召向他学习。他先后被追授“时代楷模”“全国优秀共产党员”“全国五一劳动奖章”“全国优秀教师”等称号。2019年，在中华人民共和国成立七十五周年前夕，国家主席习近平签署主席令，授予他“人民教育家”国家荣誉称号。

After his death, Xi Jinping issued important instructions on his exemplary deeds and called on people to learn from him. He was posthumously awarded titles including “Model of the Times,” “National Outstanding Communist Party Member,” the “National May 1st Labor Medal,” and “National Outstanding Teacher.” In 2019, on the eve of the seventy-fifth anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, President Xi Jinping signed a presidential order conferring on him the national honorary title of “People's Educator.”

一个从广西地质队走出来的年轻人，最后成了这个国家用最高规格纪念的人。

A young man who had come out of a Guangxi geological survey team ended up honored by this country in its highest form.

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

黄大年的故事里，最容易让人停下来想一想的，是他为什么回来。

In Huang Danian's story, the thing most likely to make a person stop and think is why he came back.

从世俗的角度看，他回国是“不划算”的：他在英国有地位、有收入、有安稳的生活；回国之后，是大量的会议、奔波、压力，以及一个需要从零开始攻克的难题。

By worldly standards, his return home was “not a good deal”: in Britain he had status, income, and a settled life; after returning, there were endless meetings, constant travel, pressure, and a hard problem that had to be cracked from scratch.

但他还是回来了。而且回来之后，是用“透支自己”的方式在干。

But he came back anyway. And once back, he worked in a way that “overdrew” himself.

他的入党志愿书里那句话，解释了一切：“若能做一朵小小的浪花，奔腾着加入献身者的滚滚洪流中。”——他并不觉得自己是“大人物”，他觉得自己只是洪流里的一朵浪花。

The sentence in his Party application explains everything: “If I can be a small wave, surging forward to join the mighty torrent of those who give themselves over.” He did not think of himself as a “great man”; he thought of himself as just one wave in that torrent.

他在给学生的毕业赠言里，写过一句话：“振兴中华，乃我辈之责。”这句话听起来很大，但落在他身上，是一间办公室每天亮到凌晨的灯，是一次次晕倒在出差途中的身体，是七年时间里的每一次选择。“我辈之责”这四个字，他用最短的七年，写了最长的答案。

In a farewell message to his students, he wrote a sentence: “To rejuvenate China is the duty of our generation.” The sentence sounds grand, but in his own life it became a light left on until the small hours in an office, a body that collapsed again and again on business trips, every choice made across seven years. With those few words—“the duty of our generation”—he wrote the longest of answers in the shortest of seven years.

2026年，我们谈“人生选择”的时候，常常用的是“性价比”。这没有错。但黄大年提醒我们，还有一种选择逻辑：我这一生的能力，能为一件更大的事，做点什么？

In 2026, when we talk about “life choices,” we often use the language of “value for money.” There is nothing wrong with that. But Huang Danian reminds us that there is another logic of choice: what can the abilities of my one life do for something larger?

他用五十八年，和自己在最前沿拼杀的这七年，回答了这个问题。

He answered that question with his fifty-eight years, and with the seven years he spent fighting at the very front.

书里写他的时候，我总想起他那本毕业纪念册复印件上的字——“振兴中华，乃我辈之责”。那年他还是个孩子。一个人小时候写下的理想，很少有人真的能践行一辈子。 而黄大年做到了，一直到他在出差途中最后一次晕倒。他的人生是不是“划算”，也许永远算不清；但它是不是“值得”，答案很清楚。

When I wrote about him in this book, I kept thinking of the words on the photocopy of his graduation album—“To rejuvenate China is the duty of our generation.” Back then he was still a boy. Few people really live out, for a whole lifetime, the ideal they wrote down in childhood. And Huang Danian did, all the way to the last time he collapsed on a business trip. Whether his life was a “good deal” may never be fully calculated; but whether it was “worth it,” the answer is clear.

他去世后，吉林大学地质宫507室的灯，终于不再亮到凌晨了。但那间办公室里留下的东西——那些图纸、那些方案、那些他带出来的学生——都还在继续往前走。他把自己的七年，换成了一个团队、一项技术、一批人的成长。这笔账，从任何角度看，都是他留给这个世界的东西。

After his death, the light in Room 507 of the Geology Building at Jilin University finally stopped burning until the small hours. But what was left in that office—the drawings, the plans, the students he had trained—all of it kept moving forward. He traded his seven years for a team, a technology, and the growth of a group of people. By any measure, that is what he left to this world.
