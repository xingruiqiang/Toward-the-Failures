卡夫卡：想烧掉自己全部手稿的“保险职员”

Kafka: The “Insurance Clerk” Who Wanted to Burn All His Manuscripts

——白天处理工伤索赔，夜里写下《变形记》

— By day he processed workers' injury claims; by night he wrote The Metamorphosis

← 返回主页 Home第一辑　生不逢时的先行者16 / 100约 2027 字 · 2027 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

在2026年的今天，卡夫卡是一个形容词。我们说一件事荒诞、窒息、莫名其妙、被一个看不见的机器碾过，就说它“很卡夫卡”。可如果你走进1908年的布拉格，你会看到一个几乎称不上“作家”的人：一个在保险公司上班的法律职员，白天处理工人的工伤索赔、写公文、打官腔；晚上回到家，才偷偷写那些后来震动世界的小说。

In 2026, Kafka is an adjective. When we call something absurd, suffocating, inexplicable, crushed by an invisible machine, we say it is “very Kafkaesque.” But if you walked into Prague in 1908, you would see a man who could hardly be called a “writer”: a legal clerk working at an insurance company, processing workers' injury claims by day, drafting official documents, spouting bureaucratic jargon; only when he went home at night did he secretly write the novels that would later shake the world.

他生前几乎没被当成作家。他甚至留下遗言：把我写的一切，全部烧掉。

In his lifetime he was almost never regarded as a writer. He even left a last instruction: burn everything I have written.

一、一个“一辈子没辞掉工作”的作家

I. A Writer Who “Never Once Quit His Job”

1883年7月3日，卡夫卡出生在布拉格的一个德语犹太家庭。父亲赫尔曼是白手起家的商人，强势、看重实际、对儿子既爱又轻视。

On July 3, 1883, Kafka was born in Prague into a German-speaking Jewish family. His father Hermann was a self-made businessman, forceful, practical, and at once loving and dismissive toward his son.

卡夫卡大学学的是法律，1906年拿到法学博士。1907年，他进了一家私人保险公司；1908年起，转到“波希米亚王国工人事故保险公司”——一家半官方的工伤事故保险机构。

Kafka studied law at university and received his doctorate in law in 1906. In 1907 he joined a private insurance company; from 1908 he moved to the “Workers' Accident Insurance Institute for the Kingdom of Bohemia”—a semi-official institution for work-injury insurance.

他在那里一直干到1922年因病退休。

He worked there until he retired on grounds of illness in 1922.

这意味着什么？意味着他写《变形记》《审判》《城堡》的那些年，白天都在同一种生活里：处理工人的工伤索赔，跟企业扯皮，写一份又一份官僚文书。

What does this mean? It means that during the years he wrote The Metamorphosis, The Trial, and The Castle, his days were spent in the same kind of life: handling workers' injury claims, wrangling with companies, writing one bureaucratic document after another.

他从来没想过靠写作活。他也从来不敢，辞掉那份工作。

He never thought of living by his writing. Nor did he ever dare to quit that job.

二、父子之间那道永远跨不过去的坎

II. The Barrier Between Father and Son That Could Never Be Crossed

父亲赫尔曼强壮、强势、精明，对儿子的“不成器”（瘦弱、敏感、不擅经营）始终不以为然。卡夫卡终身活在这道阴影下。

His father Hermann was strong, forceful, and shrewd, and he never thought much of his son's “uselessness” (frail, sensitive, poor at business). Kafka lived his whole life under this shadow.

1919年，他写了一封长信——《致父亲的信》，几十页，是控诉，也是自剖。他把一辈子说不出口的话，全写在了里面。

In 1919 he wrote a long letter—Letter to His Father, dozens of pages long, at once an indictment and a self-examination. He put into it all the things he had never been able to say in his whole life.

但这封信，他最终没有寄出去。

But in the end, he never sent this letter.

他也终身未婚。他和费莉丝两次订婚、两次解除；和尤丽叶订婚又解除；和米莲娜、多拉之间都有深情。

He also never married. He and Felice were engaged twice and broke it off twice; he and Julie were engaged and then broke it off; and he shared deep feelings with both Milena and Dora.

他一次次走到婚姻的门口，又一次次退了回来。

Again and again he walked to the threshold of marriage, and again and again he drew back.

三、1912年那个“判决之夜”

III. That “Night of Judgment” in 1912

1912年9月22日的夜里，卡夫卡通宵一口气写完了短篇小说《判决》。他后来说，那一夜，是他写作生涯真正的开端。

On the night of September 22, 1912, Kafka sat up all night and in one stretch wrote the short story “The Judgment.” He later said that that night was the true beginning of his writing life.

紧接着，是《变形记》（1915年出版）——一个人早上醒来，发现自己变成了一只甲虫。

Close on its heels came The Metamorphosis (published in 1915)—a man wakes one morning to find he has turned into a beetle.

他的小说世界里，全是荒诞：莫名其妙的审判，永远走不进去的城堡，变成虫子的人，被机器处死的军官。

His fictional world is all absurdity: an inexplicable trial, a castle one can never enter, a man turned into an insect, an officer executed by a machine.

而这一切，都相当程度上来自他的日常。那个庞大、冷漠、逻辑森严的官僚机器——他天天在保险公司里，跟它打交道。

And all of it came, to a considerable degree, from his daily life. That vast, cold, ruthlessly logical bureaucratic machine—he dealt with it every day at the insurance company.

四、他在世时的“失败”：几乎没被当成作家

IV. His “Failure” While Alive: Almost Never Regarded as a Writer

生前，他只出版过几本薄薄的小册子。三部长篇小说《审判》《城堡》《美国》，全都没有写完，也都没能出版。

In his lifetime he published only a few thin booklets. All three of his novels—The Trial, The Castle, and Amerika—were left unfinished, and none of them was published.

他极度自卑，对自己的作品评价极低，总觉得写砸了。

He was extremely self-deprecating, rated his own work very low, and always felt he had botched it.

1917年，他被诊断出肺结核。此后辗转疗养，身体一路垮下去。1919年前后，他已经基本无法正常工作。

In 1917 he was diagnosed with tuberculosis. After that he moved from one sanatorium to another, his health collapsing all the way down. By around 1919 he was essentially unable to work normally.

五、1924年：他留下了一个“烧掉一切”的遗言

V. 1924: He Left a Last Instruction to “Burn Everything”

1924年6月3日，卡夫卡在维也纳附近的一所疗养院去世，年仅40岁。因为喉结核，他最后连进食都变得困难。

On June 3, 1924, Kafka died at a sanatorium near Vienna, only forty years old. Because of tuberculosis of the larynx, he had in the end found it hard even to eat.

临终前，他给挚友马克斯·布罗德留下遗言：把我写的一切——已发表的、未发表的、书信、日记——全部烧掉。

On his deathbed, he left his close friend Max Brod a last instruction: burn everything I have written—published and unpublished, letters, diaries—all of it.

布罗德没有照办。

Brod did not obey.

他违背了朋友的遗愿，把卡夫卡的小说、手稿、书信一一整理、出版。

He went against his friend's last wish and arranged and published Kafka's novels, manuscripts, and letters one by one.

于是，一个本该被烧掉的名字，成了二十世纪最伟大的作家之一。也正因为布罗德的“背叛”，世界才得到了一个词：

And so a name that should have been burned became one of the greatest writers of the twentieth century. And it was precisely because of Brod's “betrayal” that the world gained a word:

“卡夫卡式”——形容那种莫名其妙的、令人窒息的官僚荒诞。

“Kafkaesque”—describing that inexplicable, suffocating bureaucratic absurdity.

六、结语：致2026年，每一个“白天上班、夜里做梦”的人

VI. Conclusion: To Everyone in 2026 Who “Works by Day and Dreams by Night”

我们总以为，创作、艺术、理想，需要“辞掉工作去追梦”。卡夫卡偏偏相反——他一辈子没辞掉那份保险公司的工作。

We always assume that creation, art, and ideals require “quitting your job to chase your dream.” Kafka is exactly the opposite—he never quit that insurance company job his whole life.

他白天面对文件、官司、工伤索赔、官僚嘴脸；晚上回到书桌前，把这些全部碾成文字。

By day he faced documents, lawsuits, injury claims, and officialdom's faces; at night he returned to his desk and ground all of it into words.

他没等到名声，没等到幸福，也没等到那封写给父亲的信被拆开。他甚至希望自己写的一切被烧掉。

He did not live to see fame, or happiness, or even that letter to his father being opened. He even wished that everything he wrote would be burned.

但他证明了一件事：一个被生活困住的人，依然可以在困住他的那个牢笼里，写出炸弹一样的作品。

But he proved one thing: a person trapped by life can still, inside the very cage that traps him, write works that are like bombs.

2026年，如果你也是那样的人——白天上班养家，只有夜里才属于自己：

In 2026, if you too are that kind of person—working by day to support a family, belonging to yourself only at night:

请记住卡夫卡。他没有把生活过成诗，他只是把生活过成了文学。

Remember Kafka. He did not turn his life into poetry; he simply turned his life into literature.

你不需要辞职去追梦。你只需要——今晚回到家，打开那盏灯，写下你真正想写的东西。

You do not need to quit your job to chase your dream. You need only—go home tonight, turn on that lamp, and write what you truly want to write.

Appendix: Sources and Notes

主要史料

Primary Sources

1. 卡夫卡书信、日记——尤其《致父亲的信》（1919年写，未寄出），是理解其父子关系与自我认知的核心文献。

1. Kafka's letters and diaries—especially Letter to His Father (written in 1919, never sent), the core document for understanding his relationship with his father and his sense of self.

2. 马克斯·布罗德《卡夫卡传》及其违背遗愿、整理出版卡夫卡遗稿的经过。

2. Max Brod's Kafka biography and the story of how he defied the last wish and arranged the publication of Kafka's surviving manuscripts.

3. 卡夫卡在“波希米亚王国工人事故保险公司”任职记录（1908—1922年），可与其作品中官僚体制的书写相互印证。

3. Kafka's employment records at the “Workers' Accident Insurance Institute for the Kingdom of Bohemia” (1908–1922), which can be set against the writing of bureaucracy in his works.

生卒与事实

Dates and Facts

4. 生于1883年7月3日布拉格，卒于1924年6月3日维也纳附近疗养院。1906年获法学博士；1912年9月22日夜写成《判决》；1915年《变形记》出版；1917年确诊肺结核；临终嘱托布罗德烧毁一切未刊稿。

4. Born July 3, 1883, in Prague; died June 3, 1924, at a sanatorium near Vienna. Received his doctorate in law in 1906; wrote “The Judgment” on the night of September 22, 1912; The Metamorphosis published in 1915; diagnosed with tuberculosis in 1917; on his deathbed entrusted Brod with burning all his unpublished manuscripts.

需辨析者（已在正文中说明）

Points Requiring Clarification (Addressed in the Main Text)

5. 《致父亲的信》确系未寄出，不可当作“已提交给父亲的控诉”采信。

5. Letter to His Father was indeed never sent, and should not be taken as “an indictment actually delivered to his father.”

6. “烧掉手稿”的嘱托对象是布罗德；布罗德违背遗愿出版，是卡夫卡“死后成名”的关键——这既是文学史上的幸事，也是对朋友遗愿的“背叛”。

6. The instruction to “burn the manuscripts” was given to Brod; that Brod defied the last wish and published them is the key to Kafka's “posthumous fame”—at once a stroke of good fortune for literary history and a “betrayal” of a friend's last wish.

7. 卡夫卡终身未婚，但并非没有情感经历：他与费莉丝两次订婚又解除，与尤丽叶订婚又解除，与米莲娜、多拉各有深情。

7. Kafka never married, but this does not mean he had no emotional life: he was engaged to Felice twice and broke it off twice, engaged to Julie and broke it off, and shared deep feelings with both Milena and Dora.
