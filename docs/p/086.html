阿梅莉亚·埃尔哈特：飞到了世界尽头，然后不见了

Amelia Earhart: She Flew to the End of the World, and Then Vanished

——“女人必须像男人那样勇于尝试。”

—“Women must try to do things as men have tried.”

← 返回主页 Home第五辑　走向未知的人86 / 100约 3052 字 · 3052 chars中英对照 ✓ Bilingual

2026年，女性飞行员早已不稀奇，女机长、女航天员都成了日常。但在将近一百年前，一个女人想开飞机，本身就是一件需要勇气的事。

In 2026, women pilots are nothing unusual—women captains and women astronauts are everyday. But nearly a hundred years ago, for a woman to want to fly a plane was itself a thing that took courage.

她做到了。她是第一个飞越大西洋的女性，第一个单人飞越大西洋的女性，第一个单人飞越美国本土的女性。她被美国人叫作“空中女王”。

She did it. She was the first woman to fly across the Atlantic, the first woman to fly it solo, and the first woman to fly solo across the continental United States. Americans called her the “Queen of the Air.”

1937年7月2日，她在太平洋上空失踪。美国出动了大批军舰、飞机，花了四百万美元，搜了三十九万平方公里的海域，什么也没找到。

On 2 July 1937, she disappeared over the Pacific. The United States sent out a great many ships and aircraft, spent four million dollars, and searched 390,000 square kilometres of ocean—and found nothing.

她的名字叫阿梅莉亚·埃尔哈特。她的失踪，成了二十世纪最著名的谜团之一。

Her name was Amelia Earhart. Her disappearance became one of the most famous mysteries of the twentieth century.

一、那个改变一生的十分钟

I. The Ten Minutes That Changed a Life

1897年7月24日，埃尔哈特出生在美国堪萨斯州。她的家境不错，父亲是律师。她小时候喜欢滑冰、爬树、打猎，是个不太“像女孩子”的女孩。

Earhart was born on 24 July 1897 in Kansas, in the United States. Hers was a comfortable family; her father was a lawyer. As a girl she loved to skate, climb trees, and hunt—she was not much of a “girlish” girl.

1920年，二十三岁的她在加州长滩参观一个机场。一位飞行员让她坐上了自己的飞机，飞了十分钟。她后来说：当飞机升到两三百英尺高的时候，“我就知道自己和飞行结下了不解之缘”。

In 1920, at the age of twenty-three, she visited an airfield at Long Beach, California. A pilot let her sit in his plane, and they flew for ten minutes. She later said that when the plane rose to two or three hundred feet, “I knew I had to fly.”

这十分钟，花了她父亲十美元。

Those ten minutes cost her father ten dollars.

为了凑够学飞的学费（大约一千美元，在当时是一笔巨款），她去做过卡车司机，在电话公司打过工。1921年，她正式开始学飞行。她的教练是一位女性飞行员先驱，妮塔·斯诺克。

To scrape together the fee for flying lessons (about a thousand dollars, a huge sum at the time), she worked as a truck driver and took a job at a telephone company. In 1921 she began to train as a pilot in earnest. Her instructor was a pioneering woman aviator, Neta Snook.

1922年，她靠母亲资助买了一架二手的黄色双翼机，她给它取名叫“金丝雀”。

In 1922, with her mother’s help, she bought a secondhand yellow biplane and named it “the Canary.”

二、“第一位飞越大西洋的女性”——但有个但书

II. “The First Woman to Fly the Atlantic”—With a Caveat

1928年6月，埃尔哈特出名了。她成为了第一位飞越大西洋的女性。

In June 1928, Earhart became famous. She became the first woman to fly across the Atlantic.

但这里需要说清楚一件事：她不是独自飞的。 那次航程中，她只是乘客——飞机由两名男性飞行员驾驶。她之所以被选中，很大程度上是因为她“长得像这个角色”。她自己也对此非常不满。她后来坦言，那次飞行让她觉得自己像个“行李”。

But one thing needs to be made clear here: she did not fly it alone. On that crossing she was only a passenger—the plane was flown by two male pilots. She was chosen in large part because she “looked the part.” She herself was deeply unhappy about it. She later admitted that the flight made her feel like “baggage.”

所以，她给自己定了一个新的目标：独自飞一次大西洋。

So she set herself a new goal: to fly the Atlantic alone.

1932年5月20日，她驾驶那架亮红色的洛克希德Vega飞机（她叫它“小红巴士”），从加拿大起飞，飞了将近十五个小时，在爱尔兰降落，成为第一位单人飞越大西洋的女性。

On 20 May 1932, flying a bright red Lockheed Vega (she called it “the little red bus”), she took off from Canada, flew for nearly fifteen hours, and landed in Ireland, becoming the first woman to fly the Atlantic solo.

这一次，是她自己握着操纵杆。同年8月，她又成为第一位单人飞越美国本土的女性。

This time it was her own hands on the controls. In August of the same year she became the first woman to fly solo across the continental United States.

美国为她举行了盛大的庆祝，纽约几十万人上街欢迎她。国会授予她“杰出飞行十字勋章”，总统亲自为她颁奖。她成了那个时代最有名的女性之一。

America threw her a grand celebration; hundreds of thousands of people lined the streets of New York to welcome her. Congress awarded her the Distinguished Flying Cross, and the president presented it to her in person. She became one of the most famous women of her era.

三、那个“最难的”航段

III. The “Hardest” Leg

埃尔哈特不断刷新纪录：从夏威夷飞加州，从洛杉矶飞墨西哥城，从墨西哥城飞纽瓦克。她是第一个完成很多航线的人。

Earhart kept setting records: Hawaii to California, Los Angeles to Mexico City, Mexico City to Newark. She was the first to complete many of these routes.

1937年，她决定做一件更大的事：环球飞行。

In 1937 she decided to do something bigger: fly around the world.

第一次尝试是在3月。她从加州向夏威夷起飞，但在起飞时飞机失控，起落架和两个螺旋桨都损坏了。只能放弃。

The first attempt came in March. She took off from California bound for Hawaii, but the plane went out of control during takeoff, damaging the landing gear and both propellers. She had to give it up.

第二次尝试在6月1日开始。这次她换了方向：从迈阿密出发，由西向东，经南美、非洲、印度、东南亚，绕地球一圈回到加州。全程大约四万六千公里。

The second attempt began on 1 June. This time she reversed her direction: starting from Miami, flying west to east by way of South America, Africa, India, and Southeast Asia, all the way around the globe and back to California. The route was about 46,000 kilometres in all.

陪她飞的，只有一位领航员——弗雷德·努南。

Her only companion on the flight was a navigator—Fred Noonan.

前面的航程都很顺利。到6月29日，他们飞抵新几内亚的莱城，已经飞了三万五千公里，完成旅程的三分之二。

The earlier legs went smoothly. By 29 June they had reached Lae in New Guinea, having flown 35,000 kilometres and completed two-thirds of the journey.

剩下的，是全程最危险的一段：从莱城飞到太平洋中的豪兰岛。

What remained was the most dangerous leg of the whole trip: the flight from Lae to Howland Island in the Pacific.

豪兰岛是一座极小的珊瑚岛——只有两三公里长、不到一公里宽。在茫茫太平洋上，它就是一个针尖。飞过去需要跨过四千多公里的海面，中间没有任何备降点。

Howland Island is a tiny coral island—just two or three kilometres long and less than a kilometre wide. In the vast Pacific, it is a pinpoint. Reaching it meant crossing more than 4,000 kilometres of open water, with no alternative landing place anywhere in between.

四、最后一次通话

IV. The Last Radio Call

1937年7月2日，埃尔哈特和努南从莱城起飞，飞向豪兰岛。

On 2 July 1937, Earhart and Noonan took off from Lae, bound for Howland Island.

美国海岸警卫队的“伊塔斯卡号”军舰停在豪兰岛外海，负责用无线电引导他们。但那天的通讯出了大问题：埃尔哈特的飞机能收到军舰的信号，军舰却很难把信号发回给她。而且，两人都不擅长使用摩尔斯电码。

The U.S. Coast Guard ship Itasca lay off Howland Island, tasked with guiding them by radio. But communications that day went badly wrong: Earhart’s plane could receive the ship’s signals, yet the ship could barely get its signal back to her. And neither of the two was skilled with Morse code.

清晨，埃尔哈特最后一次被确认的通话传了过来。大意是：我们一定在你上方，但看不见你……油快没了……一直联系不上你们……

In the early morning, Earhart’s last confirmed transmission came through. It went roughly: We must be on you but cannot see you… gas is running low… unable to reach you by radio…

这是人类最后一次听到她的声音。

It was the last time anyone would hear her voice.

此后，飞机和她的名字，都消失在了太平洋上。罗斯福总统下令展开当时最大规模的搜救，出动了大批军舰和飞机，最终一无所获。

After that, the plane—and her name—vanished into the Pacific. President Roosevelt ordered what was then the largest search-and-rescue operation ever mounted, sending out a great many ships and aircraft, and in the end it found nothing.

1937年7月19日，美国宣布埃尔哈特和努南失踪于海上。1939年1月5日，法院宣告她法律上死亡。

On 19 July 1937, the United States declared Earhart and Noonan lost at sea. On 5 January 1939, a court declared her legally dead.

五、她到底去了哪里

V. Where Did She Go?

八十多年来，关于她的失踪，出现了无数种说法。

For more than eighty years, countless theories about her disappearance have sprung up.

最被广泛接受的一种是：他们找不到豪兰岛，燃油耗尽，迫降在太平洋上，沉没了。 这符合已知的事实，也是最简单的解释。

The most widely accepted one is this: they failed to find Howland Island, ran out of fuel, ditched in the Pacific, and sank. It fits the known facts and is the simplest explanation.

另一种说法来自一个叫TIGHAR（国际历史飞机修复组织）的民间机构：他们认为飞机飞向了另一个方向，降落在了一座叫尼库马罗罗的荒岛上。他们在那座岛上找到了一些疑似营地的痕迹和物品。但这个结论始终没有得到决定性的证实。

Another comes from a private organization called TIGHAR (The International Group for Historic Aircraft Recovery): they believe the plane flew in a different direction and landed on an uninhabited island called Nikumaroro. They found traces of what may have been a camp, along with some objects, on that island. But the conclusion has never been conclusively confirmed.

还有更离奇的说法：她其实是被日本人俘虏了，或者她其实是去执行间谍任务，或者她改名换姓隐居了。这些说法大多缺乏可靠证据，属于推测。

There are even stranger theories: that she was in fact captured by the Japanese, or that she was actually on a spy mission, or that she changed her name and went into hiding. Most of these lack reliable evidence and are speculation.

2025年，美国政府表示将解密与她的失踪相关的档案。这也说明，这个谜团至今仍然牵动着很多人的心。

In 2025, the U.S. government said it would declassify files related to her disappearance. That too shows how this mystery still grips many people to this day.

六、她的另一面

VI. Another Side of Her

埃尔哈特不仅是一个飞行员，她也是一个推动女性权利的人。

Earhart was not only a pilot; she was also someone who advanced the cause of women’s rights.

她写书，做演讲，参与创建女飞行员的组织。她鼓励女性去学飞行、去做那些“被认为不该做的事”。她说过一句很有名的话：“我想这么做，只是因为我想这么做。女人必须像男人那样勇于尝试。”

She wrote books, gave lectures, and helped found an organization for women pilots. She encouraged women to learn to fly and to do the things “they were not supposed to do.” She said one very famous thing: “I want to do it because I want to do it. Women must try to do things as men have tried.”

她还和当时的美国第一夫人埃莉诺·罗斯福成了朋友，甚至带着第一夫人体验过飞行。

She also became friends with Eleanor Roosevelt, then the First Lady of the United States, and even took the First Lady up for a flight.

她把“飞行”这件事，变成了一种关于自由的宣言。 对一个1920年代的女性来说，能开飞机本身就是一个巨大的“不服从”。

She turned “flight” itself into a declaration about freedom. For a woman of the 1920s, being able to fly a plane was itself a grand act of “disobedience.”

七、写给2026年的我们

VII. To Us in 2026

埃尔哈特的故事，最动人的地方，不是“她成功了”，而是“她知道有多危险，还是去了”。

What moves us most in Earhart’s story is not “she succeeded,” but “she knew how dangerous it was and went anyway.”

她在出发前给丈夫写过信，说自己知道这趟旅程的风险。她不是不清楚，她是清楚，但选择了要做。

Before setting out she wrote a letter to her husband, saying she knew the risks of the journey. It was not that she failed to understand—she understood, and chose to do it anyway.

在一个女性连裤子都不太方便穿的年代，她选择去做一件随时可能死的事。

In an era when it was not even easy for a woman to wear trousers, she chose to do something that could kill her at any moment.

今天，我们很少面临那种“生死”级别的冒险。但我们依然会遇到属于自己的“豪兰岛”——那个看起来很小、在大海里几乎找不到的、但我们认定了要抵达的目标。

Today we rarely face adventures of that life-and-death scale. But we still meet our own “Howland Island”—the goal that looks so small, that can hardly be found in the vast ocean, and that we have resolved to reach.

埃尔哈特的结局是：她可能永远没能抵达。但她飞出去了，飞到了她能飞的极限，然后在那个方向上消失了。

Earhart’s ending is this: she may never have arrived. But she flew out—flew as far as she could—and then vanished in that direction.

有时候，“消失在追寻的路上”，比“安全地留在原地”，更接近一个人真正想要的人生。

Sometimes “vanishing on the way toward something” comes closer to the life a person truly wants than “safely staying where you are.”

史料来源与辨析 · Sources & Notes

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平基础事实：阿梅莉亚·埃尔哈特（Amelia Mary Earhart），1897年7月24日生于美国堪萨斯州，1937年7月2日在太平洋上空失踪。为确证史实。其“法律死亡”宣告日期为1939年1月5日。

1. Basic biographical facts: Amelia Mary Earhart, born on 24 July 1897 in Kansas, in the United States, disappeared over the Pacific on 2 July 1937. Established fact. The date of her declaration of legal death was 5 January 1939.

2. 1920年的十分钟飞行：为确证史实（她本人多次叙述，为这段经历的权威来源）。

2. The ten-minute flight of 1920: established fact (she described it many times herself, which makes her an authoritative source for this experience).

3. 1928年首次飞越大西洋：她是第一位飞越大西洋的女性，但并非独自驾驶，而是作为乘客，飞机由两名男性飞行员操纵。这一点常被简化或误传，本文特别标注。

3. The first Atlantic crossing of 1928: she was the first woman to fly across the Atlantic, but she did not pilot the plane herself—she was a passenger, and the plane was flown by two male pilots. This point is often simplified or misreported, so this article flags it in particular.

4. 1932年单人飞越大西洋：1932年5月20日从加拿大起飞，在爱尔兰降落，成为第一位单人飞越大西洋的女性。为确证史实。同年成为第一位单人飞越美国本土的女性。

4. The solo Atlantic crossing of 1932: she took off from Canada on 20 May 1932 and landed in Ireland, becoming the first woman to fly the Atlantic solo. Established fact. That same year she became the first woman to fly solo across the continental United States.

5. 1937年两次环球飞行尝试：3月第一次在夏威夷起飞失败；6月1日第二次从迈阿密出发。为确证史实。

5. The two round-the-world attempts of 1937: the first ended in failure on takeoff for Hawaii in March; the second began from Miami on 1 June. Established fact.

6. 失踪：1937年7月2日从莱城起飞前往豪兰岛，途中失去联系。为确证史实。领航员为弗雷德·努南（Fred Noonan）。

6. The disappearance: she took off from Lae for Howland Island on 2 July 1937, and contact was lost en route. Established fact. Her navigator was Fred Noonan.

7. 搜救：罗斯福下令展开大规模搜救，耗资约400万美元，未果。为通行记载。

7. The search: Roosevelt ordered a large-scale search that cost about four million dollars and ended in failure. Commonly accepted account.

8. 失踪原因的各种说法：官方立场为“未能找到豪兰岛、燃油耗尽、坠海”。辨析——TIGHAR的“尼库马罗罗岛”假说、日本俘虏说、间谍说、隐居说均未获决定性证实，本文明确将后几种归为“推测”。本文不采信任何未经证实的单一结论。

8. The various theories about the cause of the disappearance: the official position is “she failed to find Howland Island, ran out of fuel, and crashed into the sea.” Note—TIGHAR’s “Nikumaroro Island” hypothesis, the Japanese-captivity theory, the spy theory, and the hiding theory have all never been conclusively confirmed; this article explicitly classifies the latter several as “speculation.” This article does not accept any single unverified conclusion.

9. 关于“第一夫人埃莉诺·罗斯福曾想学飞行”：为一则流传甚广的说法，罗斯福夫人确实曾乘坐埃尔哈特驾驶的飞机，但“想拜师学飞”的细节属于传闻，本文以较谨慎的表述处理。

9. About “First Lady Eleanor Roosevelt wanted to learn to fly”: this is a widely circulated claim. Mrs. Roosevelt did indeed ride in a plane flown by Earhart, but the detail that she “wanted to take lessons” is hearsay; this article handles it with more cautious wording.
