左丘明：一个连“他是否存在”都成了谜的盲史家

Zuo Qiuming: The Blind Historian Whose Very Existence Became a Mystery

——两千年来，中国人用一个可能并不存在的人，安慰了所有在黑暗里写作的人

—For two thousand years, the Chinese have consoled everyone who wrote in the dark with a man who may never have existed

这一篇，要和前面的写法有点不同。

This essay has to be written a little differently from those before it.

因为前面每一位传记的主角，我们至少能确认：他真人真事，什么年代出生，做过什么。而这一位——左丘明——我们甚至无法确认他到底是谁，长什么样，活在哪一年，是否真的双目失明，甚至《左传》和《国语》究竟是不是他写的，都吵了两千年还没有定论。

Because for every earlier subject we could at least be sure of the basics: that he was a real person, when he was born, what he did. But this one—Zuo Qiuming—we cannot even confirm who he really was, what he looked like, what year he lived, whether he was truly blind, or even whether the Zuozhuan and the Guoyu were written by him at all. Two thousand years of argument have settled nothing.

可就是这样一个人，被司马迁写进了《报任安书》，和文王、孔子、屈原、孙膑并列，成为中国“受难者著书”传统里最悲壮的一个符号：一个盲人，在黑暗里，写下了照亮后世的史书。

And yet this very man was written into Sima Qian’s “Letter in Reply to Ren An,” set alongside King Wen, Confucius, Qu Yuan, and Sun Bin, and became the most tragic symbol in China’s tradition of “the suffering who write books”: a blind man who, in the darkness, wrote histories that would light the ages after him.

他的“失败”，不是某一件事没做成。他的“失败”是：他连一个确切的“自己”，都没能留给历史。

His “failure” was not that he failed to finish some particular thing. His “failure” was this: he could not leave history even a definite “self.”

一、史书上唯一确凿的那句话

I. The One Certain Sentence in the Histories

关于左丘明，最著名的记载只有一句，出自司马迁：

About Zuo Qiuming, the most famous record is a single sentence, and it comes from Sima Qian:

左丘失明，厥有《国语》。

“Zuo Qiu lost his sight, and so the Guoyu came to be.”

在《史记·太史公自序》里，司马迁也说过类似的话，并提到“鲁君子左丘明”担心弟子们对孔子的《春秋》各执己见、失去本意，于是“因孔子史记，具论其语，成《左氏春秋》”。

In the “Postface of the Grand Historian” in the Shiji, Sima Qian says something similar, and mentions that “the nobleman of Lu, Zuo Qiuming” worried that the disciples would each cling to their own reading of Confucius’s Chunqiu (Spring and Autumn Annals) and lose its true meaning, and so, “building on the records of Confucius, he fully set out their words and completed the Chunqiu of Master Zuo (Zuo Shi Chunqiu).”

也就是说，在司马迁的记录里，左丘明是：

In other words, in Sima Qian’s account, Zuo Qiuming was:

- 鲁国的“君子”（有学者据班固说法称他是鲁国太史）；

- a “nobleman” of the state of Lu (some scholars, following Ban Gu, call him the Grand Scribe of Lu);

- 双目失明；

- blind in both eyes;

- 《国语》以及被后世称为《春秋左氏传》（《左传》）的作者。

- the author of the Guoyu (Discourses of the States) and of what later generations called the Chunqiu Zuoshi Zhuan (Zuo’s Commentary on the Spring and Autumn), or Zuozhuan (The Commentary of Zuo).

此外，《论语·公冶长》里，孔子说过一句“左丘明耻之，丘亦耻之”——这说明，孔子同时代或稍早，可能确实有一个叫左丘明的人。

Besides this, in the “Gongye Chang” chapter of the Analects (Lunyu), Confucius says, “Zuo Qiuming would be ashamed of this; Qiu too is ashamed of it.” This suggests that in Confucius’s own time, or a little before, there may indeed have been a man named Zuo Qiuming.

这几乎就是全部了。

That is very nearly all of it.

二、两千年的“作者之争”

II. Two Thousand Years of the “Authorship Dispute”

从汉代开始，关于“左丘明到底写了什么”的争论就没停过。

From the Han dynasty onward, the argument over “what exactly Zuo Qiuming wrote” has never stopped.

支持者以司马迁、班固为代表，认为左丘明为《春秋》作传（即《左传》），后来又编了《国语》（被称为《春秋外传》）。

The supporters, represented by Sima Qian and Ban Gu, held that Zuo Qiuming wrote a commentary on the Chunqiu (that is, the Zuozhuan) and later compiled the Guoyu (known as the “Outer Commentary on the Spring and Autumn”).

怀疑者则一代比一代多：

The doubters, meanwhile, grew more numerous with every generation:

- 晋代傅玄就提出，《国语》非左丘明所作；

- In the Jin dynasty, Fu Xuan argued that the Guoyu was not written by Zuo Qiuming;

- 唐代刘知几把《国语》归入史书、而非“经”；

- In the Tang, Liu Zhiji classed the Guoyu among historical works rather than among the “classics”;

- 柳宗元专门写了《非国语》，逐条批驳；

- Liu Zongyuan wrote a whole work, the Fei Guoyu (“Against the Guoyu”), refuting it point by point;

- 到宋代，朱熹、郑樵等人都对作者问题存疑；

- By the Song dynasty, Zhu Xi, Zheng Qiao, and others all had doubts about the question of authorship;

- 近代以来，包括康有为在内的一批学者，甚至怀疑《国语》是西汉刘歆的托名之作。

- In modern times a group of scholars, Kang Youwei among them, even suspected that the Guoyu was a work falsely attributed to Zuo Qiuming but actually by Liu Xin of the Western Han.

现代学界的主流看法大致是：

The mainstream view in modern scholarship is roughly this:

- 《国语》不是某一个人的著作，而是一部成于战国初期、由熟悉各国历史的人根据史料整理汇编而成的著作，是“史料汇编”性质；

- The Guoyu is not the work of any one person but a compilation assembled in the early Warring States period by men familiar with the histories of the various states, working from historical materials; its nature is that of a “compilation of sources”;

- 《左传》风格与气象更统一，一般认为出于一人（或一系）之手，但其成书年代与作者是谁，同样没有定论，普遍认为成书于战国，非“与孔子同时的左丘明”亲笔。

- The Zuozhuan is more uniform in style and spirit and is generally thought to come from one hand (or one line of transmission), but its date of composition and its author are equally unsettled; it is widely held to have been completed in the Warring States period and not to be the autograph work of “a Zuo Qiuming contemporary with Confucius.”

换句话说：那个“双目失明、独自写下两部大书”的形象，很可能是后世（尤其是司马迁的孤证）塑造出来的。

In other words: that image—the blind man who single-handedly wrote two great books—was very likely shaped by later ages, above all by Sima Qian’s solitary testimony.

三、那么，为什么还要写他？

III. So Why Write About Him at All?

你可能会问：既然他这么不确定，为什么还要把他放进这本书？

You may well ask: if he is this uncertain, why put him in this book?

因为恰恰是这种“不确定”，让他成了一个极其典型的精神符号。

Because it is precisely this “uncertainty” that makes him such an archetypal spiritual symbol.

想一想司马迁写下“左丘失明，厥有《国语》”时的处境：他刚刚遭受了宫刑，正在用一群“受难者”来给自己打气。文王被囚、孔子受困、屈原放逐、孙膑被膑……而现在，他需要一个“身体有残缺、却写成了史书”的例子。左丘明，就是他找到的那个例子。

Think of the situation in which Sima Qian wrote “Zuo Qiu lost his sight, and so the Guoyu came to be”: he had just suffered castration, and he was rallying himself with a company of “sufferers.” King Wen imprisoned, Confucius in straits, Qu Yuan exiled, Sun Bin mutilated... and now he needed an example of a man “whose body was maimed, yet who still completed a history.” Zuo Qiuming was the example he found.

于是，“一个盲人在黑暗里写史”这个形象，被永久地刻在了中国文化的记忆里。它后来被无数身处困境的人反复引用，成了“在最不利的条件下依然有所作为”的象征。

And so the image of “a blind man writing history in the dark” was carved forever into the memory of Chinese culture. It was later invoked again and again by countless people in difficulty, and became a symbol of “still achieving something under the most unfavorable conditions.”

这个符号的力量，早就超过了对“左丘明到底是谁”这个问题的考证。它告诉我们：一个民族愿意把什么样的人，供进自己的精神祠堂。

The power of this symbol long ago outstripped any scholarly inquiry into “who Zuo Qiuming really was.” It tells us what kind of person a people is willing to enshrine in its own hall of spirits.

四、一个更深的“失败”

IV. A Deeper Kind of “Failure”

如果说前面几位是“努力了但被时代忽视”，那左丘明是另一种“失败”：他的存在本身，就被时间抹得只剩下一个轮廓。

If the earlier figures were “people who strove but were overlooked by their age,” then Zuo Qiuming represents another kind of “failure”: his very existence has been rubbed away by time until only an outline is left.

- 他没有留下自己的生平；

- He left no account of his own life;

- 他的著作归属被争了两千年，至今没有定论；

- The attribution of his works has been contested for two thousand years and remains unsettled today;

- 连“他是否真的失明”，也只有司马迁一句话作为依据，没有任何旁证。

- Even the question of “whether he was truly blind” rests on a single sentence of Sima Qian, with no corroborating evidence.

他就像一个站在历史入口处的人：我们看得见他的影子被拉得很长，长到两千年后还在，可我们始终看不清他的脸。

He is like a man standing at the entrance to history: we can see his shadow stretched very long—so long that it is still there two thousand years later—yet we can never quite make out his face.

而恰恰是这个“看不清”，成了一个提醒：我们今天以为的“确定的历史”，很多时候，只是几个人的一句话、一次转述、一次相信。

And it is precisely this “not being able to see clearly” that serves as a reminder: what we today take for “settled history” is very often just one sentence from someone, one retelling, one act of belief.

五、致2026年的我们

V. To Us in 2026

左丘明的故事，对生活在“一切都可考据、一切都可查证”的2026年的我们，有一种特别的意味。

For us in 2026, living in an age when “everything can be researched, everything can be verified,” the story of Zuo Qiuming carries a particular meaning.

第一，“被记住”和“真实存在过”是两回事。很多人一生兢兢业业、做了实事，却最终只是史书上的一行字，甚至连名字都留不下。左丘明的教训是：不要指望历史会公平地记住每一个人。真正能留下的，是你做出来的东西，而不是你的名字。

First, “being remembered” and “having really existed” are two different things. Many people work diligently their whole lives and do real things, yet in the end they are only a line in the histories—or not even a name. Zuo Qiuming’s lesson is this: do not expect history to remember everyone fairly. What can truly endure is the work you produce, not your name.

第二，一个符号，可以比一个真人活得更久。左丘明本人可能模糊不清，但“左丘失明，厥有《国语》”这句话，激励了两千年来无数在困境中写作、思考、坚持的人。有时候，一个人真正的贡献，不是他做了什么，而是他让后人相信了什么。

Second, a symbol can outlive a real person. Zuo Qiuming himself may be indistinct, but the sentence “Zuo Qiu lost his sight, and so the Guoyu came to be” has inspired countless people over two thousand years who wrote, thought, and persevered in adversity. Sometimes a person’s true contribution is not what he did, but what he made later generations believe.

第三，接受“不完美地被记住”。你不必要求自己的一生被精确、完整地记载。你只要确保：当人们提起那个“在最黑暗处仍不肯停下的人”时，你做的那些事，配得上被算进去。

Third, accept “being imperfectly remembered.” You need not demand that your life be recorded with precision and completeness. You need only make sure that when people speak of “the one who would not stop even in the darkest place,” the things you did are worthy of being counted in.

他可能从未被人真正看清过，但他成了所有“在黑暗里写字的人”的祖先。

He may never have been truly seen clearly by anyone, but he became the ancestor of everyone who “writes in the dark.”

Appendix: Sources and Notes

主要史料

Primary Sources

1. 司马迁《报任安书》：“左丘失明，厥有《国语》。”

1. Sima Qian, “Letter in Reply to Ren An”: “Zuo Qiu lost his sight, and so the Guoyu (Discourses of the States) came to be.”

2. 司马迁《史记·太史公自序》与《史记·十二诸侯年表》中关于“鲁君子左丘明”的记载。

2. Sima Qian, the Shiji (Records of the Grand Historian): the account of “the nobleman of Lu, Zuo Qiuming” in the “Postface of the Grand Historian” and the “Chronological Table of the Twelve Feudal Lords.”

3. 班固《汉书·艺文志》“《国语》二十一篇，左丘明著”之说；《论语·公冶长》孔子论左丘明之语。

3. Ban Gu, the Han Shu (History of the Han), “Monograph on Bibliography”: the statement that “the Guoyu, in twenty-one chapters, was written by Zuo Qiuming”; and Confucius’s remarks on Zuo Qiuming in the “Gongye Chang” chapter of the Analects (Lunyu).

4. 柳宗元《非国语》；傅玄、刘知几、朱熹、郑樵、康有为等历代质疑之说。

4. Liu Zongyuan, Fei Guoyu (“Against the Guoyu”); and the doubts raised over the centuries by Fu Xuan, Liu Zhiji, Zhu Xi, Zheng Qiao, Kang Youwei, and others.

需辨析者（全部为需辨析项——本人生平史料极度单薄）

Points Requiring Caution (all of them do—the sources on his life are extremely thin)

5. “左丘明其人是谁”——无定论。 除《论语》中孔子提及“左丘明”这一旁证外，其生平（生于何时、任何职、是否鲁太史）皆无可靠记载，班固称其为鲁太史亦属汉代之说，缺乏更早佐证。

5. “Who Zuo Qiuming actually was”—no settled answer. Apart from the incidental evidence of Confucius’s mention of “Zuo Qiuming” in the Analects, there is no reliable record of his life (when he was born, what office he held, whether he was Grand Scribe of Lu); Ban Gu’s calling him Grand Scribe of Lu is itself a Han dynasty claim lacking earlier corroboration.

6. “左丘明双目失明”——仅有司马迁一处记载（《报任安书》《史记·太史公自序》），无其他旁证，是否属实难以确认。

6. “Zuo Qiuming was blind in both eyes”—recorded only once, by Sima Qian (in the “Letter in Reply to Ren An” and the “Postface of the Grand Historian” in the Shiji), with no other corroboration; whether it is true is hard to confirm.

7. “左丘明著《左传》”——争议极大，无定论。 传统（司马迁、班固）归于左丘明；但自唐代起即受质疑，现代学界通行看法是《左传》成书于战国，作者不明，非“与孔子同时的左丘明”所著。

7. “Zuo Qiuming wrote the Zuozhuan”—hotly disputed, with no settled answer. Tradition (Sima Qian, Ban Gu) attributes it to Zuo Qiuming; but it has been questioned since the Tang, and the prevailing modern view is that the Zuozhuan was completed in the Warring States period by an unknown author, not by “a Zuo Qiuming contemporary with Confucius.”

8. “左丘明著《国语》”——现代学界普遍否认。 主流看法是《国语》为战国初期由多人依据各国史料汇编而成的著作，非一人所撰，更非左丘明个人之功。

8. “Zuo Qiuming wrote the Guoyu”—generally denied by modern scholarship. The mainstream view is that the Guoyu is a work compiled in the early Warring States period by several hands from the historical records of the various states; it is not the work of a single author, still less the achievement of Zuo Qiuming alone.

9. 因此，本文并非一篇“生平传记”，而是一篇“符号考”：正文已明确说明，左丘明作为“盲史家”的形象，很可能由司马迁（在自身受难处境下引用）及后世叙述所塑造，其真实面貌不可考。这一点与其他各篇的写法有本质区别，特此声明，以免读者误将其当作确凿史实。

9. This essay, therefore, is not a “biography” but a “study of a symbol”: the main text makes clear that the image of Zuo Qiuming as “the blind historian” was very likely shaped by Sima Qian (who cited him out of his own condition of suffering) and by later narrative, and that his real features cannot be known. This is fundamentally different from how the other essays are written, and it is stated here so that readers will not mistake it for established fact.
