三毛：把梦种在撒哈拉，把自己留给了孤独

Sanmao: She Planted Her Dreams in the Sahara and Left Herself to Loneliness

——“如果有来生，要做一棵树，站成永恒，没有悲欢的姿势。”

—“If there is a next life, I want to be a tree, standing for eternity, in a posture free of joy and sorrow.”

2026年，很多人在朋友圈里晒远方。机票、海岛、荒漠、极光，一张张照片被修得干净漂亮，然后配一句“人生就是要说走就走”。可真正的远方，从来不是滤镜里的风景，而是一个人敢于把熟悉的一切全部扔掉，去一个连地图上都找不到的荒地里，重新学会活着。

In 2026, many people post their faraway places on social media. Plane tickets, islands, deserts, the northern lights—each photo polished clean and pretty, captioned with a line like “Life is about just taking off.” But the true faraway is never the scenery behind a filter. It is a person daring to throw away everything familiar and go to a wilderness that cannot even be found on a map, to learn how to live all over again.

有一个人，真的这么做了。她把家安在了撒哈拉沙漠里，用装棺材的木箱做家具，用指甲油补牙，用破轮胎做坐垫。她把这段日子写成了书，让整整一代人第一次知道：原来远方是这样一种粗粝、贫瘠、又滚烫的生活。

One person actually did this. She made her home in the Sahara Desert, built furniture from wooden crates that had held coffins, patched her teeth with nail polish, and made cushions from worn-out tires. She turned those days into a book, and for a whole generation it was the first time anyone understood: so this is what the faraway is—a life so coarse, so bare, and so burning hot.

她的书卖了千万册，她的名字成了一个时代关于“流浪”的符号。可她本人，在48岁那年，用一条丝袜结束了自己的生命，死在台北一家医院的病房里，现场没有留下任何遗书。

Her books sold tens of millions of copies, and her name became a symbol of “wandering” for an era. But she herself, at forty-eight, ended her life with a silk stocking, dying in a hospital room in Taipei, leaving no note at the scene.

她叫三毛。

Her name was Sanmao.

一、那个被老师在脸上画圈的女孩

I. The Girl Whose Teacher Drew Circles on Her Face

1943年3月26日，三毛出生在重庆，原名陈懋平。她嫌“懋”字难写，自己把名字改成了陈平。祖籍浙江定海，1949年随家人去了台湾。

On March 26, 1943, Sanmao was born in Chongqing; her original name was Chen Maoping. Finding the character 懋 (mao) too hard to write, she changed her own name to Chen Ping. Her family roots were in Dinghai, Zhejiang, and in 1949 she went with her family to Taiwan.

她的童年，本可以安稳。父亲是律师，家境不错。但三毛从小就不是一个“正常”的孩子。她不爱跟同龄人玩，喜欢一个人跑到坟场去读书——她说那里安静。

Her childhood could have been secure. Her father was a lawyer, and the family was comfortably off. But Sanmao was never a “normal” child. She did not like playing with children her own age; she preferred to slip off alone to the graveyard to read—she said it was quiet there.

真正把她推向悬崖的，是初中时的一件事。她的数学成绩很差，有一次偶然发现老师出题都来自课本习题，就死记硬背，一连考了几次满分。老师怀疑她作弊，单独出了一份难度很高的卷子。三毛当然考砸了。

What truly pushed her to the edge was an incident in middle school. Her math grades were poor, and one time she noticed by chance that the teacher's test questions all came from the textbook exercises, so she memorized the answers by rote and scored full marks several times in a row. The teacher suspected her of cheating and set a separate, very difficult exam. Naturally, Sanmao failed it.

于是，那位老师当着全班同学的面，用毛笔蘸满墨汁，在三毛的眼睛周围画了两个大大的黑圈，像熊猫一样，然后让她到走廊上去站一圈。

So the teacher, in front of the whole class, dipped a brush full of ink and drew two big black circles around Sanmao's eyes, like a panda's, and then made her stand out in the corridor for a while.

对一个十几岁的孩子来说，这是毁灭性的羞辱。三毛从此开始逃学，躲进图书馆，最后彻底休学。她被诊断为严重的心理问题，一度把自己关在房间里，连吃饭都不愿出门见人。她后来回忆，那几年，她甚至想过死去。

For a child in her early teens, this was devastating humiliation. From then on Sanmao began skipping school, hiding away in libraries, and in the end she dropped out entirely. She was diagnosed with a serious psychological condition, and for a time shut herself in her room, unwilling even to come out to eat or see anyone. She later recalled that during those years she even thought about dying.

改变她的是一个人。父母请了画家顾福生来家里教她画画。顾福生没有把这当成一份“家教工作”，他看到了这个女孩身上那种极其敏感的东西。他告诉三毛的父母：她不是学不好，她只是需要另一个出口。

One person changed her. Her parents brought the painter Gu Fusheng to the house to teach her to paint. Gu Fusheng did not treat it as a mere tutoring job; he saw the extreme sensitivity in this girl. He told Sanmao's parents: it is not that she cannot learn—she simply needs another outlet.

从这个出口开始，三毛慢慢走了回来。她开始画画，开始写作，开始重新跟世界接触。她在《现代文学》上发表了第一篇作品《惑》。

Through that outlet, Sanmao slowly found her way back. She began to paint, to write, to make contact with the world again. She published her first work, “Bewilderment” (Huo), in the journal Modern Literature (Xiandai Wenxue).

一个被羞辱到想要消失的女孩，最后是靠一支画笔、一支笔杆，把自己从深渊边拉了回来。

A girl humiliated to the point of wanting to disappear was pulled back from the edge of the abyss by a paintbrush and a pen.

二、逃到西班牙，逃到撒哈拉

II. Fleeing to Spain, Fleeing to the Sahara

二十岁上下，三毛去中国文化大学旁听哲学课。在那里，她遇到了一位让她心碎的恋人，对方拒绝了她。她做了一个在当时极少见的选择：去西班牙。她进了马德里大学。然后是德国，是美国。

In her early twenties, Sanmao audited philosophy courses at the Chinese Culture University. There she met a lover who broke her heart, and he rejected her. She made a choice that was rare at the time: she went to Spain. She entered the University of Madrid. Then came Germany, and then the United States.

她像一个没有根的旅人，一直在路上。她说自己不是去欣赏风景，而是在逃——逃离那个让她窒息的自己。

She was a traveler without roots, always on the road. She said she was not going to admire the scenery but to flee—to escape the self that was suffocating her.

在西班牙，她遇到了荷西。那时荷西还是个高中生，比三毛小八岁，他对她说：等我六年，读完大学，服完兵役，我就娶你。三毛当时没当真，只当是小男孩的玩笑。

In Spain she met José. At the time he was still a high-school student, eight years younger than Sanmao, and he told her: wait for me six years—once I finish university and complete my military service, I will marry you. Sanmao did not take it seriously then, treating it as a boy's joke.

六年后，两人在西班牙重逢。荷西真的等了她六年。

Six years later, the two met again in Spain. José truly had waited six years for her.

1973年，他们决定结婚，并选择了西属撒哈拉。三毛说，她想去撒哈拉，“那是前世记忆里的乡愁”。荷西没有多问，提前在沙漠里找了份工作，先过去等她。

In 1973 they decided to marry, and chose the Spanish Sahara. Sanmao said she wanted to go to the Sahara, that it was “a homesickness remembered from a past life.” José did not ask many questions; he found a job in the desert ahead of time and went on before her to wait.

三、用棺材板做的家具

III. Furniture Made from Coffin Boards

撒哈拉的生活，绝对不是浪漫的。那里的房子是水泥地，家徒四壁，风一起，满屋子都是沙。三毛刚到时，家里只有一张床垫。

Life in the Sahara was absolutely not romantic. The house had a concrete floor and bare walls; when the wind rose, sand filled the whole room. When Sanmao first arrived, there was only a mattress in the house.

但三毛有本事把荒漠过成家。她捡来废弃的棺材木板做桌子，用旧轮胎做成坐垫，把捡来的石头上画上画。她去镇上买水、买菜，跟阿拉伯邻居打交道，把日常生活的琐碎，写成了惊心动魄的故事。

But Sanmao had a gift for turning a wasteland into a home. She salvaged discarded coffin boards and made a table from them, turned old tires into cushions, and painted pictures on the stones she picked up. She went into town to buy water and groceries, dealt with her Arab neighbors, and turned the small trivialities of daily life into thrilling stories.

《撒哈拉的故事》在1976年出版，一下子火了。三毛用一种极轻的、带点孩子气的语气，讲着极重的事——沙漠里的生与死、贫与病、爱与痛。读者第一次发现，原来“远方”不是明信片，而是一个人愿意在辛苦里认真生活的样子。

Stories of the Sahara (Sahala de Gushi) was published in 1976 and became an instant hit. In an extremely light, slightly childlike tone, Sanmao told of the heaviest things—life and death, poverty and illness, love and pain in the desert. For the first time, readers realized that the “faraway” is not a postcard, but the way a person is willing to live earnestly amid hardship.

她也为撒哈拉的当地人奔走。她写过那里的奴隶制，写过被压迫的女孩子，写过战争阴影下的小镇。她不是只写风花雪月，她看见了那片土地上真实的血和泪。

She also spoke up for the local people of the Sahara. She wrote about slavery there, about oppressed girls, about a small town under the shadow of war. She did not write only of moonlight and flowers; she saw the real blood and tears of that land.

四、荷西死了，她的世界也塌了一半

IV. José Died, and Half Her World Collapsed

1979年9月30日，荷西在拉帕尔马岛潜水时发生意外身亡。那是他们结婚的第六年。

On September 30, 1979, José died in a diving accident off the island of La Palma. It was the sixth year of their marriage.

三毛接到消息时，几乎崩溃。她守着荷西的遗体，不肯让人下葬。据说她曾在墓前几近癫狂。后来，是父母连夜从台湾飞去，才把她从那个岛上带回来。

When Sanmao received the news, she all but broke down. She kept watch over José's body and refused to let anyone bury him. It is said she was nearly deranged before the grave. In the end it was her parents, flying overnight from Taiwan, who brought her back from that island.

她在《梦里花落知多少》里写荷西，字字都是血。她一遍遍地写自己去墓园，写自己跟墓碑说话，写自己如何假装他还活着。那种丧偶之痛，被她写成了中文散文里最沉重的一段。

In her book How Many Flowers Have Fallen in My Dream (Mengli Hua Luo Zhi Duoshao) she wrote about José, every word steeped in blood. Again and again she wrote of going to the cemetery, of talking to his gravestone, of pretending he was still alive. She turned the grief of widowhood into some of the heaviest passages in Chinese prose.

从那以后，那个在沙漠里哈哈大笑的三毛，其实再也没有真正回来过。她回到了台湾，在文化大学任教，继续写作、演讲、旅行，看起来还是那个潇洒的三毛，但熟悉她的人都知道，她身上有一块地方，永远空了。

From then on, the Sanmao who had laughed out loud in the desert never truly came back. She returned to Taiwan, taught at Chinese Culture University, and kept writing, lecturing, and traveling. She still looked like the same free-spirited Sanmao, but those who knew her well knew that some part of her would always be empty.

五、她给世界讲了那么多故事，却没讲完自己的

V. She Told the World So Many Stories, but Never Finished Her Own

1990年，三毛写出了电影剧本《滚滚红尘》，由林青霞、秦汉主演，讲的是乱世中的爱情。这部电影当年拿下了多项金马奖，是三毛创作生涯里最后的高光。

In 1990 Sanmao wrote the screenplay Red Dust (Gungun Hongchen), starring Brigitte Lin and Chin Han, a story of love amid turbulent times. The film won several Golden Horse Awards that year, and it was the last high point of Sanmao's creative life.

1991年1月4日，三毛在台北荣民总医院去世。她此前因妇科手术住院。凌晨，护士发现她在浴室里，用一条丝袜自缢，年仅48岁。

On January 4, 1991, Sanmao died at Taipei Veterans General Hospital. She had been hospitalized for gynecological surgery. In the early hours of the morning a nurse found her in the bathroom, having hanged herself with a silk stocking. She was only forty-eight.

她的死因，至今仍有两种说法。一种认为是自杀——她长期抑郁，对荷西的思念从未停止。另一种认为，她当时服了药，意识不清，那次动作可能并非本意。现场没有留下遗书，这让一切成谜。

To this day there are two accounts of her death. One holds that it was suicide—she had long been depressed, and her longing for José never ceased. The other holds that she had taken medication at the time and was not fully conscious, so the act may not have been intentional. No note was left at the scene, and this has kept everything a mystery.

无论真相如何，结论都指向同一件事：这个写了无数远方的人，最终还是没能翻过自己心里的那座山。

Whatever the truth, the conclusion points to the same thing: this person who wrote endlessly of faraway places never managed, in the end, to get over the mountain inside her own heart.

六、写给2026年的我们

VI. To Us in 2026

今天，我们说“去远方”太容易了。一张机票、一个假期，就能抵达任何地方。可三毛说的“远方”，其实是另一种东西——是一个人敢于离开所有人替你规划好的轨道，去一个没有人认识你的地方，重新定义什么叫做“家”。

Today, it is all too easy to say “go somewhere far away.” One plane ticket, one holiday, and you can reach anywhere. But the “faraway” Sanmao spoke of is something else—it is a person daring to leave the track everyone else has laid out for you, to go where no one knows you, and to redefine what “home” means.

她并不完美。她在感情里受过伤，也在感情里伤过人。她的婚姻里有甜蜜，有争吵，甚至有家暴的传闻。她笔下的撒哈拉，可能比真实的撒哈拉要明亮一些。

She was not perfect. She was wounded in love, and she wounded others in love. Her marriage had sweetness and quarrels, and even rumors of domestic violence. The Sahara she wrote about was perhaps a little brighter than the real one.

但这些都是真的。一个把梦写成了书、把痛苦写成了诗的女人，她的人生不是童话，是一份极其诚实的记录。

But all of it was real. A woman who turned her dreams into books and her pain into poetry did not live a fairy tale; hers is an utterly honest record.

她真正留给我们的，不是“流浪”这个浪漫的标签，而是一种活法：哪怕身处荒漠，也要认真地给自己布置一间小小的家；哪怕心里空了一块，也还要给别人留下一点亮。

What she truly left us is not the romantic label of “wandering,” but a way of living: even in a wasteland, arrange yourself a small home with care; even with a piece missing in your heart, still leave a little light for others.

这，或许就是三毛在2026年仍然被记住的原因。

Perhaps that is why Sanmao is still remembered in 2026.

史料来源与辨析

Sources and Notes

1. 生平与卒年：三毛（陈平）1943年3月26日生于重庆，1991年1月4日在台北荣民总医院去世，享年48岁。此为通行的权威记载（台湾方面资料与大陆《三毛全集》编者年表一致）。

1. Life and year of death: Sanmao (Chen Ping) was born in Chongqing on March 26, 1943, and died at Taipei Veterans General Hospital on January 4, 1991, at the age of forty-eight. This is the standard authoritative record (Taiwanese sources and the editor's chronology in the mainland Complete Works of Sanmao agree).

2. 少年被老师羞辱一节：三毛本人在《一生的战役》《匪兵甲》等散文及多次访谈中回忆过此事（老师用墨汁在她脸上画圈）。因系本人自述，细节无法完全旁证，但多个不同时间的自述相互印证，可信度较高。

2. The episode of her humiliation by a teacher as a youth: Sanmao herself recalled it in essays such as “A Lifelong Battle” (Yisheng de Zhanyi) and “Soldier A” (Feibing Jia), as well as in numerous interviews (the teacher drew circles on her face with ink). Because it rests on her own account, the details cannot be fully corroborated, but multiple accounts given at different times corroborate one another, so it is fairly credible.

3. 与荷西的相遇、六年之约：出自三毛散文《结婚记》《去年的冬天》等自述。学界对其文学真实性有过讨论（有研究者质疑部分细节经过文学加工），此处按作者自述呈现，不作绝对化表述。

3. Her meeting with José and their six-year promise: from her own essays such as “A Record of Marriage” (Jiehun Ji) and “Last Winter” (Qunian de Dongtian). Scholars have discussed the literary truth of these accounts (some researchers question whether certain details were literary embellishment); here they are presented as the author's own account, without absolute claims.

4. 撒哈拉生活与《撒哈拉的故事》：1976年出版，为其成名作。文中“棺材板做家具”“旧轮胎做坐垫”等细节出自三毛本人的散文，属实。

4. Life in the Sahara and Stories of the Sahara (Sahala de Gushi): published in 1976, it was the work that made her famous. Details in the text such as “furniture made from coffin boards” and “cushions made from old tires” come from Sanmao's own essays and are accurate.

5. 荷西去世：1979年9月30日，荷西·马利安·葛罗（José María Quero）在拉帕尔马岛潜水时溺亡，为确证史实。

5. José's death: on September 30, 1979, José María Quero drowned while diving off the island of La Palma—a confirmed historical fact.

6. 死因：现场无遗书，存在“自杀”与“服药后意外”两种推断，均无决定性证据。本文并列呈现，不武断下结论。需说明的是：她去世前确实长期处于抑郁状态，这是各方较为一致的看法。

6. Cause of death: with no note left at the scene, there are two inferences—“suicide” and “an accident after taking medication”—and neither has decisive evidence. This essay presents both side by side and does not draw a hasty conclusion. It should be noted that she was indeed in a long-term state of depression before her death, a point on which all sides broadly agree.

7. 《滚滚红尘》：1990年上映，三毛编剧，1990年第27届台湾电影金马奖获最佳剧情片等多项奖，为其实。此处按通行说法表述，未采信“她因此片受政治压力”等未经证实的传闻。

7. Red Dust (Gungun Hongchen): released in 1990, written by Sanmao; at the 27th Taiwan Golden Horse Awards in 1990 it won Best Feature Film and several other awards—this is fact. Here it is stated according to the common account; unverified rumors such as “she came under political pressure because of this film” are not accepted.

8. 关于三毛生前身后的争议：她去世后，有学者（如马中欣）对其部分散文的真实性提出质疑，也有不少人反对这种“考据式”的解构。本文的态度是：承认其散文有文学加工成分，但不因此否定她作为作家与“远方”象征的意义。

8. On the controversies surrounding Sanmao in life and after death: after her death, some scholars (such as Ma Zhongxin) questioned the truthfulness of parts of her essays, while many others opposed this kind of “textual-criticism” deconstruction. The stance of this essay is this: it acknowledges that her essays contain elements of literary shaping, but does not on that account deny her significance as a writer and as a symbol of the “faraway.”
